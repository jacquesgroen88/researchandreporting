How we work

Local knowledge, and a straight answer

The Garden Route is not one market. What a house is worth in Heuwelkruin has very little to do with what it is worth in Dana Bay, and the person selling it should know the difference without having to look it up.

Every one of our offices is staffed by people who live in that town. They know which streets flood, which estates have levies worth paying, which schools people move for, and what the house down the road actually transferred for rather than what it was listed at.

We would rather tell you a property is wrong for you and keep you as a client for the next twenty years than sell you something that does not fit. If we are not the right agency for what you need, we will say so.

What that looks like in practice

  • We film the properties properly. Thirty full walkthroughs on our channel, so buyers travelling from Gauteng or the Cape can rule houses in and out before they book a trip.
  • We answer on WhatsApp. Most property enquiries arrive in the evening and over weekends, which is when people actually look. That is when we reply.
  • We value honestly. A valuation that flatters you and then sits on the market for eight months has cost you money, not made it.
