The thesis in one line

RedirHub has 10,533 registered users, roughly 92 paying accounts, and three of those accounts produce about half the revenue. It does not have a traffic problem or a product problem. Seven figures comes from forty-two more enterprise accounts, which is under two a month, and every document here exists to make those forty-two possible.
