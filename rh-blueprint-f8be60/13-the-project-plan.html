The project plan

Six workstreams over twelve months, with the first ninety days broken to the task. The binding constraint is not budget or ideas. It is five hours a week of marketing capacity and a small engineering team with its own roadmap, so almost everything here is sequenced to be small, independent and non-blocking.

Capacity, honestly

The real constraint

Five hours a week is roughly twenty hours a month. The ninety-day plan above assumes about that from the marketing lead, plus an intermittent engineering commitment of perhaps twelve to fifteen days spread across the quarter. If engineering cannot commit that, the plan still works but the sequence changes: workstreams A, C and D are almost entirely marketing-side and can run without engineering, while B and E cannot.

If everything slips, the order that survives contact is: chase the invoice, build the form, open the pipeline, consolidate the pages, publish the certificate pillar. That is roughly three weeks of the marketing lead's time and half a day of engineering, and it captures most of the near-term value in this Blueprint.
