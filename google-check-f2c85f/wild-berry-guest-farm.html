Why this decides your bookings

Found: Google ranks local results on relevance, distance and popularity, and says "more reviews and positive ratings can help your business's local ranking." Distance you can't change. Reviews you can grow every week.

Picked: 68% of people want at least a 4-star rating, and 74% look for reviews written in the last 3 months.

Booked direct: a guest who doesn't find you, or isn't convinced, often books you through a booking site instead, and that booking pays commission.

Sources: Google Business Profile Help, "Tips to improve your local ranking on Google". BrightLocal Local Consumer Review Survey 2026 (international survey).
