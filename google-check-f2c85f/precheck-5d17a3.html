Pre-call Google check

About a minute per guesthouse with the Google button (4 minutes by hand), done the day before or the morning of the walkthrough. It tells you what to lead with, gives you a note to paste into GHL, and saves a row you export to Jacques so their page gets rebuilt with live numbers.

The Google button (set up once)

The button reads Google Maps for you: both searches, the top 3, rating, prices, the newest Google reviews and owner replies. Then it fills this page in.

  1. Show your bookmarks bar: press Ctrl+Shift+B (Mac: Cmd+Shift+B).
  2. Drag this onto the bookmarks bar: RT Google check
  3. That's it. It works in incognito windows too. When Jacques says the button has changed, drag it again and delete the old one.

Clicking it on this page does nothing useful: it only works on Google Maps.

Why we check this way

  1. Searching with the town in the words ("guest houses in Port Edward") gives the same results wherever you are, so checks are comparable. A guest searching from inside the town can still see a slightly different order.
  2. Google Maps lets guests filter accommodation by guest rating (3.5+, 4.0+, 4.5+). A place at 4.4 disappears for anyone who taps 4.5+. Checked live on 2 Nov 2026.
  3. Reviews from the last 3 months are what 74% of people look for (BrightLocal 2026, an international survey; never call it South African).
  4. Don't promise rankings. Google says local results are mainly based on relevance, distance and popularity, and that more reviews and positive ratings can help.
