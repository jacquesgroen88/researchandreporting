04
First test

The first thing I'd test: sell the refill date, not the discount

Most subscription offers lead with "save 15%". Someone who's just paid isn't thinking about savings. They're thinking about whether this works, and the answer needs more than one bottle. So I'd offer them not running out.

A · control

Subscribe & save 15%

Get this every month and save 15% on every order. Cancel anytime.

Add subscription No thanks
B · refill date

Your bottle lasts about 30 days

Want the next one to arrive on 14 November, before you run out? Same price as today, then 15% off every refill after that. Skip or cancel by email, any time.

Yes, send it on the 14th No thanks
Win means

Higher subscription take rate and no drop in second-order rate. Take rate alone can be bought with a bigger discount.

How big a test

To see 5% go to 7% reliably takes about 2,200 orders per side. assumption Your monthly volume decides whether that's two weeks or two months.

Holdout

10% of orders see no post-purchase offers at all, so the revenue we report is extra revenue and not just moved revenue.
