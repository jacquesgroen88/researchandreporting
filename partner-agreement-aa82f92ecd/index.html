Together, "the Parties."

1.Purpose

The Parties will collaborate to deliver and grow the Leasing Conversion System (the "System") for salon-suite operators. Rachel leads sales, onboarding, and client relationships. JCE leads build, implementation, and the platform. This agreement governs the rollout of additional locations and all new clients brought in under the collaboration.

2.Background and Scope

  • The System includes: conversion landing pages, lead and member email and SMS journeys, the CRM / leasing dashboard, integrated calendars, instant lead response, call bridge, and the supporting automation.
  • The original My Salon Suite Blaine pilot was delivered under a separate prior agreement between JCE and My Salon Suite, and is not part of this collaboration.
  • This collaboration covers: (a) the rollout of additional My Salon Suite locations beyond the pilot, and (b) all new operators and clients brought in under the collaboration.

3.Roles and Responsibilities

Rachel (Growth Partner):

  • Identify, approach, and sell to prospective clients
  • Onboard clients and gather requirements, content, and brand assets
  • Own client communication and the ongoing relationship
  • Manage expectations, scheduling, and approvals

JCE (Implementation Partner):

  • Build, configure, and launch the System for each client and location
  • Maintain and update the delivered systems
  • Provide the platform, technical guidance, and reporting
  • Provide Rachel with the assets and links needed for client review

Both Parties will act in good faith, keep each other reasonably informed, and protect each other's reputation.

Standard pricing, adjustable by mutual agreement:

5.Revenue Split

Net Revenue under this collaboration is split as follows:

50 / 50
Setup (one-time, per location)
Rachel 50% • JCE 50%
60 / 40
Monthly (recurring, per location)
Rachel 60% • JCE 40%

"Net Revenue" means total fees actually collected from clients under this collaboration, less direct third-party costs incurred specifically for that client (platform / software, telephony where not passed through, and advertising spend). The split applies to Net Revenue, not gross.

6.Costs

Each Party covers its own operating costs, including its own time and software. Client-specific third-party costs (platform, telephony, advertising) are recovered from the client or taken off the top as direct costs before the split is applied.

7.Invoicing and Payment

The Party holding the client billing relationship collects client payments and pays the other Party its share within 10 business days of funds clearing, in USD via Wise or an equivalent service. Each Party is responsible for its own taxes in its own jurisdiction. Confirm which Party invoices and collects.

8.Term and Termination

This agreement takes effect on signature and continues until ended by either Party on 30 days written notice. Clients and projects already in progress at the time of notice will be completed, and revenue from them shared in line with this agreement. Platform access for live clients runs month-to-month.

9.Intellectual Property

The System, its frameworks, templates, code, and platform configuration (the "JCE System") remain the property of JCE. Client-specific deliverables are licensed to the relevant client under that client's own agreement. Rachel's client relationships and contacts remain Rachel's. Neither Party acquires ownership of the other's pre-existing intellectual property or contacts.

10.Confidentiality

Each Party will keep confidential the other's non-public business information, client details, pricing, and systems, both during the collaboration and after it ends.

11.Non-Circumvention

During the collaboration and for 12 months after it ends, neither Party will deliberately bypass the other to take the full economic benefit of a client or opportunity introduced through the collaboration, without the other's written agreement.

12.Relationship of the Parties

The Parties are independent contractors. This agreement does not create an employment relationship, a legal partnership, or a joint-venture entity, and neither Party may bind the other without written consent. Each Party is responsible for ensuring its participation does not conflict with its other obligations.

13.Status and Resolving Differences

This agreement records the Parties' commercial understanding. The revenue-share, confidentiality, intellectual-property, and non-circumvention sections are intended to be binding. Any disagreement will first be worked through in good-faith discussion. Confirm governing law / jurisdiction. Each Party should take its own legal and tax advice before signing.
