What we would run

One programme.
Every site.

  • Branded units per restaurant, each pointing at its own Google profile
  • One dashboard across both brands, live
  • Monthly report per site, not per group
  • Automatic review replies, approved by you first
  • Alerts under three stars, same day
  • New-opening kit, so each launch ships ready

What we will not do

We do not sell or generate reviews, and we do not screen guests to send only the happy ones to Google. Gating breaches Google's policy, and for a 35-site listed group the exposure is the whole estate, not one listing.

We also never lock hardware to a subscription. Stop the programme and the units keep working.
