Finding 02 · why timing matters
1,208

consecutive five-star reviews to move Hussar Grill GrandWest from 4.2 back to 4.5.

Casa Bella GrandWest needs 634.

Volume cuts both ways.

Once a site carries two thousand reviews, every new one barely moves the average. Volume protects a good rating and it locks in a poor one.

Which is the case for acting across the estate now. Most of your restaurants are still light enough to move quickly. GrandWest is what it looks like when a site is left long enough for the maths to harden.

What this adds up to

No group view.
No group standard.

  • Thirty-five separate Google profiles, checked one at a time, if at all
  • No alerting, so a bad run at one restaurant is invisible until it has already moved the average
  • Replies fall to whoever has time that week, so most reviews go unanswered
  • Two brands never compared side by side, which is exactly how GrandWest stayed hidden
  • No group pricing and no group standard, so each restaurant solves it alone or not at all
What we would run

One programme.
Every site.

  • Branded units per restaurant, each pointing at its own Google profile
  • One dashboard across both brands, live
  • Monthly report per site, not per group
  • Review replies, approved by you first
  • Alerts under three stars, same day
  • New-opening kit, so each launch ships ready

What we will not do

We do not sell or generate reviews, and we do not screen guests to send only the happy ones to Google. Gating breaches Google's policy, and for a 35-site listed group the exposure is the whole estate, not one listing.

We also never lock hardware to a subscription. Stop the programme and the units keep working.
