If it is happening right now

Three things, in this order. Everything else can wait.

  1. Stop paying. There is no final fee. Whatever you have already lost, the next payment does not bring it back.
  2. Phone your bank's fraud line using the number on your bank card, not one from any message. Under an hour, recovery is genuinely possible.
  3. Open a SAPS case and get a case number. Your bank will ask for it, and it is what makes patterns visible.

The shape of every scam

You do not need to recognise the specific scam. You need to recognise the machinery, and it is almost always the same six parts.

1. Who started this?

They found you.

2. Why must this happen right now?

Urgency is manufactured to stop you thinking.

3. How am I being asked to pay?

Irreversible payment methods are chosen deliberately.

4. What are they asking me to reveal or install?

Nobody legitimate needs your OTP, PIN or your screen.

5. Can I verify this on a channel they did not give me?

Verification only counts if you choose the channel.

6. Does the money make sense?

Ask where the profit actually comes from, and insist on an answer.
