Identity Theft & Document Fraud

Your ID number and personal documents are used to open accounts, take out credit and commit fraud in your name, often for years before you notice.

Typical loss R5 000 – R600 000

SIM Swap Fraud

Criminals convince your mobile network to move your number onto a SIM card they hold, which hands them every OTP your bank sends.

Typical loss R10 000 – R500 000

WhatsApp Account Hijacking

Someone tricks you into forwarding your WhatsApp verification code, takes over your account, and then scams your contacts while pretending to be you.

Typical loss R500 – R40 000

AI Voice Cloning & Fake Emergency Calls

A few seconds of a person's voice is enough to clone it convincingly, and the clone is used to fake an emergency and demand immediate money.

Typical loss R2 000 – R150 000
