Where to Report a Scam in South Africa

Reporting does two things: it gives you the case number you will need for any bank dispute or insurance claim, and it feeds the pattern data that eventually shuts these operations down. Report even when you did not lose money, and even when you feel foolish. Especially then.

Do these first, in this order

  1. 1

    Phone your bank's fraud line

    From the number on your bank card, not one from a message. Do this within minutes, not hours. Speed is the single biggest factor in whether money is recovered.

  2. 2

    Freeze or block

    Freeze cards and accounts in the banking app while you wait to be answered. You can always unfreeze.

  3. 3

    Change passwords from a clean device

    Start with the email account that can reset everything else. Use a device that was not involved in the incident.

  4. 4

    Open a SAPS case

    Go to any police station and get a case number. Your bank, insurer and credit providers will ask for it.

  5. 5

    Preserve the evidence

    Screenshot chats, profiles, emails, payment confirmations, URLs, wallet addresses and phone numbers before anything is deleted.

  6. 6

    Refuse all recovery offers

    Anyone who contacts you afterwards offering to recover your money is running the second half of the scam.

Who handles what

South African Police Service (SAPS)

All criminal matters. Your first stop for a case number.

Any police station, in person. Ask for a case number and the investigating officer's details.

Your bank's fraud line

Unauthorised transactions, payment recalls, card blocks, account freezes.

Use the number printed on your bank card or on your statement. Never a number from a message.

National Financial Ombud Scheme (NFO)

Complaints against banks, insurers and credit providers when you are unhappy with their response.

Free to consumers. Complain to the institution first and get their final response, then escalate.

Financial Sector Conduct Authority (FSCA)

Unlicensed financial services, investment scams, fake trading platforms, unregistered advisers and insurers.

Check the register before investing, and report unlicensed operators to them.

Southern African Fraud Prevention Service (SAFPS)

Identity theft. A free protective registration forces extra verification on credit applications made in your name.

Apply for protective registration if your ID documents have been exposed.

National Credit Regulator (NCR)

Loan scams, unregistered credit providers, reckless lending, advance fee loans.

Verify any lender on the NCR register before applying, and report unregistered ones.

National Consumer Commission (NCC)

Fake online stores, misleading advertising, prize and competition scams, subscription traps.

Lodge a consumer complaint under the Consumer Protection Act.

Directorate for Priority Crime Investigation (Hawks)

Large-scale, organised and syndicate fraud, including major investment schemes.

Report through the SAPS or the Hawks' published channels. Usually engaged for high-value or multi-victim cases.

Information Regulator

Misuse of your personal information and data breaches under POPIA.

Lodge a complaint if an organisation exposed or mishandled your personal data.

Property Practitioners Regulatory Authority (PPRA)

Estate agents and property practitioners, including unregistered agents and trust account issues.

Verify an agent's registration and Fidelity Fund Certificate before paying any deposit.

SARS

Tax-related phishing, fraudulent returns filed in your name, diverted refunds.

Report through the official SARS fraud reporting channels and correct your eFiling profile immediately.

SASSA

Grant fraud, diverted grant payments, fraudulent agents at pay points.

Report on the official SASSA toll-free line or at a SASSA office.

SADAG

Emotional support. Scam victims frequently experience serious distress, shame and depression.

Free counselling helplines, open daily. There is no threshold of seriousness you need to meet to call.

Verify before you pay

Six checks, all free, most of them under a minute. Between them they stop the majority of what is on this site.

Is this financial firm licensed?

FSCA register

Anyone giving financial advice or taking investments in South Africa must be licensed. This one check stops most investment scams.

Is this lender registered?

NCR register

Registered credit providers must assess affordability and may not charge fees before disbursing a loan.

Is this estate agent registered?

PPRA

Confirms the agency exists and holds a valid Fidelity Fund Certificate covering trust money.

Is this company real?

CIPC

Confirms registration, directors and status. A trading name with no registration behind it is a warning.

Are these photographs stolen?

Any reverse image search

The fastest single check available. It exposes fake stores, fake rentals, fake profiles and fake pets in seconds.

Has anyone else reported this?

Search the name, number or account plus the word scam

Victims post quickly and specifically. If a bank account number returns results, you have your answer.
