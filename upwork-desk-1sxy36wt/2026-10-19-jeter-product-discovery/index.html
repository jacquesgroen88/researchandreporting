COVER LETTER (paste into the proposal box)

Hi,

You asked for judgment rather than a free strategy, so I haven't written one. I've set out how I'd run Jeter's discovery instead, with the hours, what I'd need from you, and the frame I'd use to compare your four directions: https://jcereports.netlify.app/jeter-discovery-notes-44qoua/

An example where I decided what to build next. On my own product, ReviewTap (NFC review cards for hospitality and retail), the obvious next build was a self-serve setup so devices could ship "unclaimed". I looked at what it would fix. Programming took about a minute per device, we'd had 1 refund in 729 orders, and links were already editable after shipping. So it solved a problem we didn't have and added new ones: devices dead out of the box, customers picking the wrong business, and support load moving onto them. I chose not to build it, and parked it as the onboarding step for our subscription product, the one place it would pay. That was entirely my call: I did the analysis and made the decision.

On a client's travel business, the instinct was more leads and faster quotes. The pipeline showed 54 quoted leads and one booking, with quotes going out inside 20 minutes, so I pointed the work at follow-up and qualification instead of lead volume.

Structure. Four weeks, about 60 hours, $5,100 fixed in three milestones. Week 1 is the product and data review, week 2 is 10 to 12 interviews (users, non-finishers, attendees, and two or three professional matchmakers), week 3 compares the options and drafts the recommendation, and week 4 delivers the 90-day roadmap, the developer brief and the measurement plan. The detail is in the link.

What I'd need. A 60-minute kickoff, 30 minutes a week, help recruiting users, access to analytics, feedback and the admin side, and an hour with your developer in week 3.

Who does it. Me, personally. No agency. I use AI tools for research and synthesis. The interviews and the recommendations are mine.

Samples. A public, redacted version of my strategy plan format: https://jcereports.netlify.app/seven-figure-blueprint/
