Four things changed between the build and what shipped

1. Free delivery is R1,000, not R2,500. Doc 05 proposed R2,500 as an upsell mechanic. The live store already runs free domestic shipping at R1,000, confirmed from the delivery profile. The pages state the live figure, because publishing a threshold the checkout does not honour is a customer-facing error. Doc 05's recommendation is superseded by what is already live.

2. One About page, not two. An unpublished About page already existed and was good — it already named the confusable overseas companies and held the real Google Business Profile CID and socials. A second at /pages/about-reviewtap would have created two About pages competing for the same entity, which is exactly what doc 08 says to avoid. They were merged and published once at /pages/about. The pre-existing page also corrected a guessed Facebook URL in the draft.

3. The fleet enquiry block ships functional. The build called for a GHL form. Rather than publish a dead anchor, #enquire now carries the store's real WhatsApp with a prefilled message and a mailto with the four qualifying questions pre-typed. The GHL form remains the upgrade; the field spec is in the page source.

4. The About page Organization schema was slimmed after first publish. The theme already emits a site-wide Organization with the same @id and a better one (it has a logo). The draft also carried aggregateRating on Organization, which Google treats as self-serving review markup and can action manually. The live block now declares only what the theme does not.

Do not switch these on yet — the evidence got stronger this morning

Google non-brand recorded R0 of conversion value on R2,938.74 of spend between 24 July and 4 August, while Shopify shows orders on almost every one of those days.

The re-check on 10 August makes this close to conclusive. Brand Search — the one campaign that is actually enabled — has now spent R273.82 across 4 to 10 August and recorded zero conversions and zero value, over a week in which the store took fourteen orders. Brand search is the highest-intent traffic a business has: people typing your name. It converting at literally zero is not a performance result, it is a measurement failure.

Every ROAS gate in doc 09's staging table reads from this tag, and so does every judgement about whether to reallocate budget from Meta. Fire a test purchase and confirm it lands in the account with the correct value before restarting anything. Until that is done, the account is spending real money and reporting nothing back.
