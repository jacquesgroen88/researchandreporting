A correction on how this page is produced

The first version of this record reported "no evidence" against the bulk page, the fleet page, the guides, the group outreach and the benchmark report. That was wrong. All of them existed. They were checked against the live systems and against committed history, and the work was sitting in the working tree untracked, which neither check reaches. Built-but-unshipped is invisible to a live-systems check by definition, and on this project it is the single largest category of work. This page now checks the working tree as well.
