SEO strategy: review devices and smart business cards

ReviewTap is currently renting through Google Ads the exact traffic it could own. This document maps two keyword universes against a site architecture that does not yet exist, in the order the pages should be built, with the technical and link work that makes them rank.

Demand evidence comes from ReviewTap's own Google Ads search terms report rather than third-party estimates. See the method note below for why that is stronger.
Method note, stated up front

The Google Ads account holds explorer-level API access, so Keyword Planner volumes and traffic forecasts are not retrievable. Every demand signal in this document is therefore taken from ReviewTap's own impressions, clicks and click-through rates over the last thirty days. This is better evidence than an estimate: it is the actual auction this business competes in, in this country, at this moment. Where a query shows impressions and zero clicks, that is a page that does not exist, not a traffic guess.

Page template for a commercial hub

  1. H1 matching the query exactly. "Google Review Cards" not "Our Products".
  2. Answer in the first 60 words. What it is, what it costs, how fast it ships. AI assistants and featured snippets both take from here.
  3. Price and the volume ladder visible. Hiding price loses the bulk buyer, who is qualifying suppliers before they will talk.
  4. The formats, linked. Passes authority to spokes.
  5. Proof. Named clients, real review count, order volume.
  6. The integrity paragraph. Answers the compliance question every serious buyer has.
  7. FAQ block with schema. Built from the actual question queries, worded as asked.
  8. Two exits: buy, and enquire for multiple locations. Never only a buy button.

Page template for an industry spoke

These rank easily because nobody is writing them, and they convert well because the visitor sees their own business described. Structure: the problem in that industry's language, where the review moment actually happens in that business, the recommended configuration and quantity, the ICP-specific objection answered, a matched case study, and the volume ladder. Roughly 900 to 1,200 words. Twelve of these is the single highest-leverage content block in the plan.

The rule: informational pages link down to commercial pages, never sideways. A guide that links to another guide leaks the visitor. A guide that links to the hub converts them.

Schema markup

  • Product with price, availability and aggregateRating on every product page, using the real 4.9 across 29 figure and never the unverified 4.8
  • FAQPage on every hub and guide, built from the actual question queries
  • HowTo on the setup and QR-code guides
  • Organization with sameAs pointing at the real socials (review_tap, not the incorrect reviewtapsa), plus the Google Business Profile, to fix entity confusion
  • BreadcrumbList across the new hierarchy
  • LocalBusiness where applicable, to reinforce the South African entity

An honest expectation

SEO is the slowest lever in this Blueprint. Meaningful organic revenue arrives in months four to nine, not weeks. It is included at this priority for two reasons: the bulk page answers demand that is already appearing and costs one day, and organic is the only channel that keeps working after the advertising budget is switched off, which has happened to this business once already. Judge months one to three on pages shipped and impressions gained, not on revenue.
