14 · Content and assets

Asset reuse audit

ReviewTap has built far more than it is using. This document inventories every existing asset against the new strategy and classifies each one: reuse as-is, recut, or rebuild. Most agencies skip this step and quote for new production. Doing it properly removes roughly two thirds of the build cost in doc 15.

Classification key: Reuse works unchanged · Recut needs adaptation · Rebuild or retire · New does not exist yet
The headline

Roughly 70% of what this plan needs already exists in some form

The platform is built. The winning creatives are identified and measured. The fulfilment pipeline for a 25-card bulk order has already been run end to end. The brand kit and product photography exist. What is missing is not capability, it is packaging: the same assets pointed at a group buyer instead of an individual one.

Worth stating plainly

The bulk fulfilment problem, which is the usual reason a small hardware business cannot chase group orders, has already been solved and documented. The pipeline that produced 25 personalised cards for KMG Gauteng scales to 200 with the same steps. Delivery capability is not the constraint on this strategy. Demand generation is.

What to stop maintaining

  • Dormant product lines. Autotrader and Instagram variants have produced R3,668 combined across the full year and all four are out of stock. Keep the pages indexed for long-tail search, but do not restock, photograph or promote them.
  • Retired creatives. Begging Video, Card Spotlight, Hypermotion variants. Leave them off and stop revisiting.
  • Any claim that cannot be evidenced. Covered in doc 06, repeated here because it appears across several assets at once.
  • Parallel competitor research. Consolidate the existing analyses into one working document rather than adding a third.
