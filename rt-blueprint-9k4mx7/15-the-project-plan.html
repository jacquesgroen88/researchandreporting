The project plan

Six workstreams across twelve months, with the first ninety days broken down to the task. Every item carries an owner, an effort estimate, a dependency and the doc it comes from. Sequenced so that revenue-producing work lands before infrastructure work.

Starts Monday 10 August 2026. Note that 10 August is a public holiday (Women's Day observed) and 24 September is Heritage Day.

The sequencing principle

WS1 and WS4 carry the revenue. WS2 carries the compounding. Everything else supports those. If the plan slips, it slips in WS2 and WS5, never in WS1 or WS4. The plan is deliberately built so the first two weeks produce revenue effects without depending on anything else being finished.
