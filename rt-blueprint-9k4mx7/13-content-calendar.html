13 · Content and assets

The 90-day content calendar

Thirteen weeks, dated, from Monday 10 August to Friday 6 November 2026. Every asset named with its hook, channel and owner, sequenced so the highest-value pieces land first and partial execution still delivers the ones that matter.

Public holidays accounted for: Monday 10 August (Women's Day observed) and Thursday 24 September (Heritage Day). Pillars are numbered per doc 12.
The seasonal spine

This ninety-day window sits directly ahead of the South African festive season. The reviews that fill a lodge in December are being written in August and September. That is the single most persuasive line available to ICP 2 in this exact period, and it gets progressively less true every week that passes. Hospitality content is front-loaded for that reason.

If time runs short

The cut order, decided in advance

Fifteen hours a week across content and outbound is a real constraint. Decide now what gets dropped, rather than dropping things by accident.

  1. Never cut: the group audit page, /bulk/, /branded-fleet/, /smart-business-cards/, and the claim corrections in week 1. These unlock revenue directly.
  2. Cut first: Facebook and Instagram frequency. Drop to twice a week. These are the lowest-value posts in the plan.
  3. Cut second: guides. Valuable but slow, and they can be written any time.
  4. Cut third: industry pages beyond the first four.
  5. Protect above all: LinkedIn Monday and Wednesday, and photographing every bulk order. Those two habits produce the proof that makes group selling possible, and they take twenty minutes a week combined.
