What can you balance?+

Almost anything that rotates: fans, blowers, turbine rotors, pump impellers, crankshafts, motor armatures, vacuum pump rotors, cutter heads, rollers, brake discs, propellers and flywheels.

How big can the job be?+

From small components in the workshop up to 20-ton rotors balanced in place on your plant.

Do you work on site?+

Yes. When a rotor is too big or too critical to remove, we bring the equipment to you and balance it in place.

What about breakdowns?+

Call Dana directly on 082 874 9855. We respond to breakdowns around the clock across the Vaal Triangle and beyond.

Do you sell balancing machines?+

Yes. BRG is the South African agent for JP dynamic balancing machines, with sales, installation and local support.

Where is the workshop?+

20 Fairbanks Street, Vanderbijlpark. We serve the Vaal Triangle, Gauteng and plants further afield.
