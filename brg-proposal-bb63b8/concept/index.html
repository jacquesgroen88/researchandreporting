What We Do

Balancing, drivetrain and machine supply, under one roof.

From a single pump impeller to a 20-ton rotor, and the machines to balance them yourself.

Dynamic Balancing

Fans, turbines, pumps, rotors, impellers and crankshafts balanced to spec on calibrated machines. Optional unbalance correction by drilling, milling or welding.

On-Site Balancing

We bring the capability to you. On-site balancing up to 20 tons, so a critical rotor never leaves your plant longer than it must.

Propshafts

Industrial and commercial propshafts manufactured, repaired and balanced for smooth, reliable drive under load.

Gearboxes

Gearbox stripping, inspection and repair to get your drivetrain back in service, fast and properly.

CV Joints

CV joint supply and repair for industrial and commercial applications, matched to your drivetrain.
