The Problem

Online, BRG is invisible, and what does show up works against you.

When a potential customer goes looking for BRG, two things happen. Neither helps you win the job.

1. Your website is a dead end

brgtechnical.co.za currently shows one line: "Has been registered and is still under construction", on a page the browser flags as Not secure. To a plant engineer doing his homework before handing over a 20-ton rotor, that reads as inactive, unfinished and not serious. It quietly costs you trust before you have even spoken.

2. A Facebook page is all there is

The only public information about BRG is a Facebook page with a short "We specialise in" list. No depth, no proof, no easy way to compare you to the next supplier. Your real strength, on-site balancing up to 20 tons and being the SA agent for JP machines, is nowhere to be seen.

The work is serious. The shopfront is missing. Every search that ends on that holding page, or on a thin Facebook profile, is a job that could have been a phone call.

Give BRG a proper shopfront, and make sure people find it.

Five moves turn that dead page into a working sales tool.

The Engagement

Everything that gets done

01

Strategy & Onboarding Call

One session to map your services, your best customers and the words they search, and to collect everything we need: logo, photos, service details and how you want enquiries to reach you. We leave with a clear plan, and the site gets built to win work, not just to look good.

02

Website Design & Build

A fast, modern, mobile-first website covering your balancing, propshaft, gearbox and JP machine work, with clear ways to call, WhatsApp or request a quote.

03

Go-Live & Deployment

We design, build and deploy the site and get it live on brgtechnicalservices.co.za, secured with HTTPS and loading fast. Hosting is arranged separately with Squirrel Media, who also host Izi Travel, and billed directly by them.

We set up or claim and optimise your Google listing, so BRG shows on Google Search and Maps when people look for balancing and engineering services nearby.

Google Ads Management

MONTHLY

We build and run your Google Ads campaigns: keyword research, ads written for balancing, propshaft and gearbox work, and conversion tracking so every enquiry is counted. Then we manage it every month, cutting waste, adjusting bids and reporting what came in. Ad spend is paid by BRG directly to Google.

Free Smart Business Card

FREE

A ReviewTap NFC smart business card, on us. Tap it on any phone to share your details instantly and to collect Google reviews, which push your listing higher. Yours free with this project.
