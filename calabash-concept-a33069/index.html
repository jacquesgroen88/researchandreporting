Welcome

Your home away from home, near the airport

Calabash sits on a quiet smallholding in Jatniel, Benoni Small Farms, about 12 km from OR Tambo International Airport. It is built for people who need a good night's sleep and a working day: business travellers on short stays, families visiting the East Rand, and guests landing late or flying out early.
