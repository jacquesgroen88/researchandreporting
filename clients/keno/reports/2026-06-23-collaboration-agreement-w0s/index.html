01 Purpose

The Studio and the Creator are partnering to build Keno Rossi's personal content brand, boxing, fitness, and the training journey, across social platforms. The Studio turns the Creator's raw footage into published content, grows the audience, and monetises it. This is a revenue-share partnership, not employment, and not work-for-hire.

02 What each party brings

The Creator (Keno) is responsible for:
  • Training, competing, and filming raw footage, footage from 3–4 training sessions + 2–3 short talk-to-camera clips / week, filmed so it can be atomised into many posts (vertical, to simple agreed guidelines).
  • Dropping footage into the shared folder within 48 hours of filming.
  • Responding to shot-lists and approving posts within 24 hours.
  • Being the face, voice, and authentic personality of the brand.
The Studio (JCE Media) is responsible for:
  • All strategy, editing, branding, captioning, scheduling and publishing across platforms.
  • Analytics, optimisation, and the content calendar.
  • Sourcing and negotiating brand deals / monetisation (with Creator sign-off).
  • Providing and running the production tooling and systems.
  • Target output: ramping from 5–7 to 10–14 posts / week (1–2 per day) across TikTok + mirrored platforms, via atomisation (one footage drop → many posts).

03 Revenue covered ("Content Revenue")

Money earned from the content brand, including:

  • Brand sponsorships & paid partnerships tied to the content / channels
  • Platform creator payouts, funds & monetisation
  • Affiliate income, merchandise, and paid products (programs, community, coaching) built off the brand
  • Appearance / booking fees that come through the content brand

04 Revenue excluded, 100% the Creator's

To keep this fair and clean, the partnership does not touch Keno's athletic career:

✓ Always 100% Keno's, never shared
Boxing & fight purses · prize money & competition winnings · athletic sponsorships secured independently of the content brand · his employment & other personal income.

Rationale: the Studio monetises the content, not Keno's career as a fighter.

06 Costs

  • The Studio covers its own base production tooling (editing / AI / transcription subscriptions), not reimbursed.
  • Shared costs agreed in advance (paid ad spend, videographer, gear, photographer) are recouped off-the-top from Content Revenue before the split.
  • Neither party incurs a shared cost without the other's written approval.

07 Ownership & IP

Review this section with a lawyer
Personal-brand & likeness ownership is the most important clause to get right.
  • Keno's name, likeness, and personal brand belong to Keno, always.
  • The primary handle(s) are registered in the Creator's name; the Studio has full operational / management access during the term.
  • Content produced under this agreement is jointly owned for the term; the Studio retains the right to its revenue share from content it produced / deals it sourced (per the Term and exit section).
  • The content machine is the Studio's asset. The production system, methodology, editing & AI workflows, templates, formats, and all know-how of how the content is produced are the exclusive intellectual property of JCE Media, used to benefit the brand during the term, but never transferred to or owned by the Creator.

08 Term & exit

  • Initial term: 12 months, then auto-renews month-to-month.
  • Either party may exit with 60 days written notice.
  • On exit, the handles & audience go with Keno (his name, his brand). In-flight brand deals are honoured and split at the then-current rate.
  • The Studio continues to earn its split on revenue from content, products & deals it built or sourced during the term for a 12-month tail (see Commitment and protections).
  • Neither party may use the other's confidential information or disparage the other.

09 Decisions & voice

  • Creative & operational control of posting & strategy: the Studio (that's the service).
  • Final say on anything personal, his likeness, what's shared about his life, tone: Keno.
  • Brand deals & paid partnerships: require both parties' sign-off.

10 Good faith

Both parties commit to acting in good faith, communicating openly, and reviewing this agreement together every 3 months as the brand grows.

12 Commitment & protections

The Studio invests months of unpaid work to build the brand and its revenue engine. These terms protect that investment, and reward the Creator for staying.

  • Own the engine, not just the edits. The audience & handles are Keno's. But the monetisation infrastructure, digital products, the email/SMS list, store, community platform, funnels, and sponsor relationships, is owned by the partnership and operated by the Studio.
  • Exclusivity. During the term, the Creator will not engage another content team or manager for this content brand.
  • Non-circumvention. For 12 months after exit, the Creator won't deal directly with sponsors / partners the Studio introduced without the Studio's agreed share.
  • The split rewards loyalty. The rebalancing toward the Creator (the split table above) applies only while the partnership continues, leaving early forfeits the improved share. Staying literally pays more.
  • Tail. On exit, the Studio keeps its split on revenue from products, deals & assets it built or sourced, for 12 months.
Honest note on lock-in
No clause can force a person to stay. The real protection is a system the Creator can't easily replace + a share that grows by staying. For larger scale, consider forming a joint-venture company that owns the brand business (both as shareholders per the split), the strongest protection, and worth a lawyer's input.

13 Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure (NDA)

This agreement doubles as the NDA. By signing, the Creator agrees to protect the Studio's confidential information, so a separate NDA is not required.

"Confidential Information" means:
  • The content machine itself: the production system, methodology, editing and AI workflows, templates, formats, prompts, and the know-how of how the content is produced.
  • Strategy documents, content plans, analytics, performance data, pricing, and revenue figures.
  • Sponsor, partner, and supplier relationships and contacts introduced through the partnership.
  • Anything shared in confidence, or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential.
The Creator agrees to:
  • Keep it confidential and not disclose it to any third party, including another agency, manager, or editor.
  • Use it only to benefit this partnership, and not to build, copy, or reverse-engineer a competing content system.
  • Return or delete confidential materials on request once the partnership ends.

Carve-outs. This does not cover information that is already public, that the Creator already lawfully knew, or that must be disclosed by law.

Survival. These confidentiality obligations continue for 3 years after the partnership ends. The content machine and its methodology are the Studio's trade secret and stay protected for as long as they remain confidential.

Why this is here
Keno will see the inner workings of the system that makes the content. This clause lets us share it openly while protecting the engine that is the Studio's core asset (see the Ownership and IP section).
