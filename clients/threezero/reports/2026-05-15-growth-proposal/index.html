Site Audit

What's holding ThreeZero back

01

No analytics — you're flying blind

Critical

There's no Google Analytics, no Google Tag Manager, and no Facebook Pixel installed on the site. You have no visibility into where your traffic comes from, which pages convert, how visitors behave, or which campaigns generate actual leads. Every decision is being made without data.

Fix: GA4 + Facebook Pixel installed in day one. Immediately unlocks conversion tracking, audience insights, and retargeting.

02

Trial forms submit into silence

High Impact

When someone books a trial, nothing is triggered on the backend. No WhatsApp notification to you, no confirmation to them. A warm lead who just raised their hand goes cold before you've had a chance to respond. At premium price points, speed of follow-up is everything.

Fix: Instant WhatsApp trigger on form submission — to you and a confirmation to the lead. Response time drops from hours to seconds.

03

Blog content earns traffic but loses visitors

Medium Impact

ThreeZero has 12 solid blog posts on topics people actively search — Ozempic and muscle loss, longevity training, women's fitness. These earn real organic traffic. But not one article ends with a CTA. Interested readers have nowhere to go.

Fix: A clear "Book a free trial" CTA on every post, plus 6 new SEO articles per month to compound traffic over time.

04

The site's first impression underplays the product

Brand Impact

ThreeZero is running the most advanced 30-minute workout studio in South Africa — AI-guided machines, Technogym hardware, premium pricing. The current homepage doesn't signal any of that. A new visitor landing on the site doesn't immediately understand what makes this different. Member stories and transformation results — the most powerful conversion assets — aren't given enough visual weight.

Fix: Homepage refresh with a design that leads with the technology, surfaces member results, and builds trust on the first scroll.
