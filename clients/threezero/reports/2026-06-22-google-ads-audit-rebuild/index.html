Core Problems: Summary
1
Smart Campaign = no control, no data

Google runs everything. No keyword visibility, no search term data, no audience control. Can't optimise what you can't see. The account has been effectively operating as a black box for its entire life.

2
Conversion tracking is broken, all decisions based on false data

177 "conversions" over 90 days sounds healthy. In reality these are map direction clicks, website taps, and Google-modelled store visit estimates, each worth R1. No trial bookings, no membership sign-ups, no calls have been tracked at real value. ROAS of 0.03x is not a real number.

3
Performance declining month over month

Spend fell from R1,685 to R1,229 while the CPA climbed from R17.45 to R32.40. Whether this is a budget issue, quality score issue, or seasonal shift cannot be determined without search term data. The Smart Campaign has no tools to diagnose or fix it.

✓
Trial booking form is live and connected to the Reviewtap System

The form at the bottom of the homepage passes each submission to the Reviewtap System (live since 15 Jun 2026, tagged as a trial booking, verified). This is the ideal primary conversion to track in Google Ads. We just need to add the Google Ads conversion tag to the form on submission.

Why two campaigns, not four: the keyword reality

An earlier draft leaned on Threezero's marketing language: "30 minute gym", "biocircuit", "gymtimidation", "gym that tells you what to do". Those phrases are excellent in ad copy but almost nobody in South Africa actually searches them. Real local search volume sits in "gym near me", "gym cape town" and "gym membership cape town". So we target the demand that already exists and sell the 30-minute USP inside the ad copy. On a R3,000 budget, two focused campaigns also concentrate the conversion data. Four campaigns split the budget too thin for Google's bidding to ever learn. Exact search volumes will be confirmed from real search-term data within the first week of launch.

Implementation

Step-by-Step Timeline

Six-phase rollout. Do not skip Week 1. Launching campaigns without fixing conversion tracking first means the new campaigns will optimise toward the same meaningless signals as the old one.

📋
Before starting: one thing needed from Andrew

The ad copy has a placeholder "Membership from R[X]/month." Confirm the membership price with Andrew before Week 2 builds. Everything else can proceed without it.

Week 1
Pre-launch
Fix Conversion Tracking: Do This Before Anything Else
  • Add Google Ads conversion tag to trial booking form submit handler (homepage JS, handleFormSubmit function, add gtag event after the fetch POST succeeds). Value: R300.
  • Update web purchase conversion action from R1 to actual membership price.
  • Update call conversion actions to R250. Set minimum call duration: 60 seconds.
  • Demote map directions, website visits, store visits to secondary conversion status.
  • Remove "Local actions – Other engagements" from conversion tracking.
  • Verify trial booking form tag is firing: submit a test form entry, confirm it shows in Google Ads conversion report within 24hrs.
  • Confirm Andrew is not running 24-hour access (keep "24 hour" as negative). Confirm activity types they don't offer.
Week 2
Build
Build Both Standard Search Campaigns
  • Create shared negative keyword list (6 categories, ~55 terms from Section 2). Apply to both campaigns.
  • Campaign 1 Local Demand Capture (R80/day): two ad groups: "Local Intent" (gym + place keywords) and "USP Differentiator" (circuit / 30-minute). Exact + phrase match. RSA per ad group using the copy in Section 2. Location targeting: 8km radius from Cape Quarter (27 Somerset Road, Green Point).
  • Campaign 2 Brand Defence (R20/day): exact + phrase match on branded terms, bid to hold top of page, short brand-focused RSA.
  • Bidding: both campaigns start on Maximise Clicks (no CPA target yet, need 30 conversions first to switch to smart bidding).
  • Add all ad extensions: sitelinks, callouts, call extension (+27 76 588 0709), structured snippets.
  • Set ad schedule if Andrew confirms peak enquiry times (e.g. 7am–8pm Mon–Sat).
Week 3
Launch
Go Live: Pause Smart Campaign
  • Launch both Standard Search campaigns.
  • Immediately pause "Threezero Fitness Studio" Smart Campaign (do not delete yet, keep for 60 days as data reference).
  • Monitor search terms daily for the first 7 days, this is the first time actual query data will be visible. Add irrelevant terms to the shared negative list aggressively in the first week.
  • Check impression share on Campaign 1: target 70%+ search impression share. If lost IS (budget) is high on the Local Intent ad group, shift spend from the USP ad group toward it.
  • Verify conversion tags are firing on real traffic (check conversion report in Google Ads after 48hrs).
Week 4
Optimise
First Optimisation Pass
  • Search terms audit: pull the full search term report for the first 7 days. Add any new irrelevant queries to the shared negative list.
  • Kill any keywords with 50+ clicks and zero conversions, pause them and review match type.
  • RSA asset performance: check which headlines/descriptions are rated "Good" or "Best" in Google Ads. Pin the top 2 headlines if a clear winner has emerged.
  • Compare the two ad groups in Campaign 1 (Local Intent vs USP Differentiator) on cost per conversion. If the USP ad group's CPA is more than 3x the Local Intent ad group's and has 15+ clicks, pause it and reallocate that budget to Local Intent.
  • Report to Andrew: first 7-day snapshot (spend, clicks, trial form submissions, calls).
Month 2
Scale
Smart Bidding Switch + Budget Rebalance
  • Once Campaign 1 reaches 30 real conversions (trial form + calls), switch it from Maximise Clicks to Target CPA. Set initial Target CPA at 1.5x the observed real CPA to give Google flexibility.
  • Rebalance budget: shift spend away from the ad group with high CPA or low volume toward the best performer.
  • Add RLSA audiences: bid +20% on past website visitors in Campaign 1 (warm audience, more likely to convert).
  • Build a second RSA per ad group in Campaign 1: use an alternative angle ("longevity fitness", "science-backed", ties in with Threezero's positioning vs a commoditised gym).
  • Install GA4 on Threezero site if not yet done, required for audience building and cross-channel attribution.
Month 3
Review
Full Review Against Baseline
  • Compare real trial booking CPA and call CPA vs Smart Campaign baseline of R30/conv (which was false). The new numbers will be the first real benchmark this account has ever had.
  • Decision point: if real CPA per trial booking is under R200 and leads are converting to members, increase budget beyond R3,000/month. Recommended next threshold: R4,500/month.
  • Assess Performance Max: if GA4 is installed and purchase data is flowing, a Performance Max campaign for display/YouTube retargeting could make sense as a fourth channel (separate from the R3k Search budget).
  • Full monthly report delivered to Andrew with: spend, clicks, trial form submissions, calls, estimated member conversions, cost per acquired member.
