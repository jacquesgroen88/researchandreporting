02 · Website Audit · 43/100

The first screen sells a R2.50 core cap, not a R2,288 moving kit

Screenshots of the live site taken on 14 Nov 2026. On mobile, the first thing a visitor sees is a cookie panel that covers the whole screen. Behind it, the first products are core caps and graduation tubes.

Findings

Critical
No real online checkout
The Terms page says online orders get "a pro-forma invoice & banking details along with a delivery fee". The customer has to wait for an email, do an EFT and send proof of payment. Every extra step loses buyers, especially movers on a deadline.
Take card, Instant EFT and SnapScan at checkout through PayFast, Yoco or Ozow, with the delivery fee worked out live from the suburb.
Critical
"Free shipping" appears on every product, but delivery is charged
Every product card carries the Jimdo default "Free shipping to the following countries: South Africa". The T&Cs say a delivery fee is added. When a customer sees that mismatch at the invoice stage, it damages trust and invites disputes.
Remove the default label and show a clear rule instead, for example "Free delivery over R1,500 in our zones, otherwise from R__".
Critical
Phone and WhatsApp aren't tappable
The live site has 0 tel: links and 0 WhatsApp links. The numbers are plain text or part of a banner image, so mobile visitors have to copy them by hand.
Add tap-to-call and a click-to-WhatsApp link with a pre-filled message, plus a sticky bar on mobile.
Warning
The navigation repeats itself
"Products" and "Shop Online" are two near-identical lists of about 55 items each, in alphabetical order rather than by need. A mover has to know that "Stock 6 SWB" is the box they want.
Organise the menu by need: Moving · Boxes · Protect · Tape & Seal · Gift & Retail · Business/Bulk. Add search.
Warning
Every page loads hundreds of products
The homepage loads 204 resources and 159 images, and the Moving Boxes page is 932 KB of HTML. Each page also carries the full product carousel, so pages are heavy and very long on mobile.
Show only relevant products on each page, lazy-load images, and use WebP images that are around 30% of the current size.
Warning
Nothing shows customers what other customers think
There are no reviews, star ratings or "since 2011" claim, and the 13K Instagram following isn't mentioned. The registration shows 15 years in business, which a new visitor never finds out.
Add a Google review widget, "Trusted since 2011", the Instagram follower count and photos of the delivery fleet.
Warning
The logo is still in Christmas mode, and the store's look is dated
The Santa-hat logo is live in mid-November and stays up year-round unless someone swaps it. The image-text "Buy Now" buttons date from about 2015 and can't be read by screen readers.
Make seasonal elements a scheduled banner, and turn image buttons into real HTML buttons.
Warning
A broken widget is throwing errors on the homepage
The browser console on the live homepage logs: eapps.Platform … WIDGET_NOT_FOUND. An Elfsight widget, typically a reviews or Instagram feed, has been deleted or expired but is still embedded. Visitors see an empty block, and the page wastes a request.
Remove the embed, or replace it with a native Google reviews block (included in the redesign).
Working
Real prices and real product photography
Box Shop publishes clear VAT-inclusive prices and its own branded product shots. Many competitors only say "call for a quote". That transparency is a real advantage and should be made much more visible.
03 · Conversion Rate Optimisation · 36/100

8-point CRO assessment

Each point is scored out of 10. Together they show how easily a cold visitor (a mover who googled "moving boxes Bedfordview") can turn into a paid order.

PointScoreKey finding
1. Five-second test4/10The logo makes the business clear, but there's no headline promise, no "who it's for" and no single next step. Two competing image buttons.
2. CTA audit4/10The CTAs are images ("Buy Now", "Shop Now") pointing to similar places. There's no "Get my moving kit" and no CTA after the content sections.
3. Trust signals2/10No reviews, years in business, delivery-fleet proof or guarantees. The address only appears in the footer.
4. Contact friction3/10No tap-to-call, no WhatsApp link and no email address. The contact form sits on a separate page.
5. Mobile experience4/10No horizontal scroll, but the cookie panel takes the whole screen, the page is 16,940px long and product text is tiny.
6. Page speed signals4/10204 requests, 159 images, no lazy loading, two legacy analytics libraries, and a carousel on every page.
7. Value proposition4/10"Affordable", "competitively priced" and "friendly" are generic. The real advantages (own drivers, transparent prices, ready kits, 15 years) are never stated.
8. User journey4/10"I'm moving a 2-bed flat, what do I need?" takes 3+ clicks and reading SKU lists. There's no kit finder, no size guide and no "add tape and bubble wrap" prompt.
Overall CRO score29/80 = 36/100

Top 3 CRO fixes (biggest revenue impact)

1. Turn moving kits into the hero. Add a 2-question "Kit Finder" (home size and fragile items) that recommends a kit and offers add-ons: mattress bags, TV box, glass box. The average order is likely to move from tens of rands to thousands.
2. Replace the pro-forma step with a real checkout. Card, Instant EFT, delivery-or-collect, and a delivery fee shown live, with a free-delivery threshold to lift basket size.
3. Add cross-sells everywhere. "Frequently bought together" on every box (tape, marker, fragile labels, bubble wrap) and "Don't forget" prompts in the cart. These are low-cost, high-margin items customers already need.

The tags are installed, but almost nothing useful is measured

We scanned the page source and the live Google Tag Manager container. The tags exist, but there are no sales events, the GTM container is empty, and one tag is from a product Google shut down years ago.

Critical
No sales data reaches GA4 or Meta
Because the "checkout" ends with an emailed pro-forma, no tool knows when a sale happens. Box Shop can't answer "which channel made us money last month?"
Move all tags into GTM and send GA4 e-commerce events plus the Meta Conversions API for view_item, add_to_cart, begin_checkout and purchase.
Warning
Calls and WhatsApps aren't counted
The most common customer action, a call or WhatsApp to 082 633 1367, leaves no trace in any report.
Track tel: and wa.me clicks as GA4 key events and import them into Google Ads.
Warning
Old scripts still load
UA, a hard-coded gtag and an empty GTM container all load on every page view. They slow the site and record nothing.
Remove UA and the hard-coded gtag, and run everything through one GTM container with Consent Mode v2.
05 · SEO · 49/100

Strong local intent, but the pages don't make the most of it

The sitemap lists 115 URLs. Many are keyword-stuffed paths that read as spam to people and add little for Google.

Critical
Keyword-stuffed URLs
Example: /packaging-boxes/die-cut-boxes/corrugated-boxes/cardboard-boxes/box-shops-gauteng/cheap-moving-boxes/cardboard-boxes/packaging-stores/. That's 8 folder levels for one product page. Google discounts this, and the links look untrustworthy when shared on WhatsApp.
Use clean URLs like /moving-boxes/ and /moving-kits/2-bedroom/ with 301 redirects from every old URL.
Critical
135 of 157 homepage images have no alt text
That's 86% of images without descriptions. It costs Google Images traffic for searches like "TV box for moving" and fails accessibility.
Write descriptive alt text from the product name and size.
Warning
Duplicated title tag
Box Shop Johannesburg | Moving Boxes | Box Shops - Box Shop Johannesburg | Packaging Store | Moving Boxes: 98 characters with the brand repeated, so Google cuts it off.
"Moving Boxes & Packaging Bedfordview | Box Shop – Delivered in Joburg" (about 60 characters).
Warning
No LocalBusiness schema
Only Jimdo's old Product microdata is present. There's no LocalBusiness/Store, opening hours, geo or review markup, which weakens the Map Pack and rich results.
Add JSON-LD for Store, Product with Offer, and FAQPage.
Warning
Two H1s, neither with a keyword
"WHATEVER YOU'RE PACKING" and "BOX SHOP" are both H1s. Neither says moving boxes or Bedfordview.
One H1: "Moving Boxes, Moving Kits & Packaging in Bedfordview".
Warning
robots.txt slows crawlers
Crawl-Delay: 5 slows Bing and others (Google ignores it), and AhrefsBot and MJ12bot are blocked completely.
Remove the crawl-delay. Leave the bot blocks if the owner prefers.
Working
Sitemap, meta description and Search Console
A sitemap exists, the homepage meta description names the products and phone number, and Search Console is verified. These are a good base to build on.
