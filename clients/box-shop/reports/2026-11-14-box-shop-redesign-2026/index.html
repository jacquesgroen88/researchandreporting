Good to know

Questions movers ask us

When do you deliver?

Our own drivers deliver Monday to Friday between 9:00 and 16:30 to our service areas. We don't deliver on weekends. Outside our zones, you're welcome to collect in-store or send your own courier.

Can I collect my order?

Yes. Order online and collect from Unit 1B, 11 North Reef Rd (cnr George Allen Rd), Wilbart, Bedfordview.

How many boxes do I need?

As a rule of thumb, allow about 15–20 boxes per bedroom plus 10 for the kitchen. Our Kit Finder above does the maths for you, and our team is happy to check your list on WhatsApp.

What's the difference between SWB and DWB?

SWB (single wall board) is lighter and cheaper, fine for most household items. DWB (double wall board) has twice the corrugated board for heavy items, stacking and storage.

Can I return unused boxes?

We exchange goods that are still in good, resalable condition. Special-order and manufactured items can't be exchanged. See our terms for the details.

Box size guide

Which box for what?

The rule movers use: heavy things go in small boxes, light things in big boxes. Your back will thank you.

Stock 5 · Small

450 × 300 × 300 mm

Books, tins, kitchen cupboards, tools and files.

Best for: heavy items

Stock 6 · Medium

616 × 450 × 300 mm

Linen, toys, shoes, pots and small appliances.

Best for: medium weight

Stock 7 · Large

450 × 450 × 500 mm

Duvets, pillows, lampshades and folded clothes.

Best for: light, bulky items

TVL 12 · Tall

500 × 400 × 780 mm

Lamps, mirrors, plants, rugs and sports gear.

Best for: tall, light items
SWB
Single Wall Board

Lighter and cheaper. Right for most household packing and items under about 15 kg.

DWB
Double Wall Board

Twice the board. Choose it for heavy items, storage, stacking, and anything going into long-term storage.
