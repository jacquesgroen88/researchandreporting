Ann's ReviewTap System Checklist

This is Ann's complete daily and weekly operating guide for the ReviewTap System. Every item is specific and actionable — no guessing, no interpretation needed. Once the AI bot is live, many of these tasks will be handled automatically; Ann's role shifts to monitoring, exceptions, and human conversations that require personal judgment.

ℹ️

Note for Charissa: Please review this checklist with Ann together. Any tasks that don't apply, need to be reworded, or need additional steps — add your notes in the Approval section at the bottom of this document.

🌅
Morning Routine — Every Working Day (8:00–8:30am)
Do this before anything else. Should take no more than 30 minutes.
Check all new WhatsApp conversations in the ReviewTap System
Open the inbox. Look for any conversations that came in overnight or since yesterday. Any contact that has sent a message and has not received a reply within 2 hours needs to be responded to first.
Daily
Check the "Needs Attention" contacts — look for the human handover tag
Filter contacts by the tag human handover. These are people the AI bot could not assist and flagged for a human. They need a personal response from Ann today, not tomorrow.
Urgent
Check the Public Cruise pipeline — update any stages that changed
Open the Public Cruise pipeline. Move any contacts forward if they have paid, confirmed a booking, or cancelled. Stages: New Inquiry → Quoted → Deposit Paid → Confirmed → Completed → No Show/Cancelled.
Daily
Check the Private Functions pipeline — same process
Move private function inquiries forward if they have progressed. If someone has confirmed but not yet paid a deposit, flag them and let Charissa know. Do not move to "Confirmed" until deposit is received.
Daily
💬
Responding to Conversations — Rules Ann Must Follow
These rules apply every time Ann handles a conversation manually.
Always reply within 2 hours during business hours (8am–5pm)
If a message comes in after 5pm, reply first thing the next morning. Do not leave any unanswered message for more than 24 hours.
Daily
For public cruise bookings — always send the direct booking link
Do not send people to the general website homepage. Send the direct link to the specific cruise they are interested in on the Shopify store. If they don't specify a cruise, ask which one they want first.
Daily
For private functions — collect all required information before quoting
Required: Guest count, type of function, preferred date (if known), catering requirements, time of day. Only send a quote once all of this is collected. If they don't have a date yet, that's fine — proceed with a quote based on what they do know.
Daily
If someone goes quiet after receiving info — follow up once after 24 hours
Send a short, friendly follow-up: "Hi [name], just checking in — did you get a chance to look at the options? Happy to answer any questions or check availability for you." Only follow up once manually. The automated sequence will handle further follow-ups.
Daily
Never move a pipeline stage if it will trigger an automated message you don't want sent
Stage automations are currently switched off. Jacques will confirm when any stage-based automation is reactivated. Until then, move contacts freely without risk of sending messages.

AI Bot Conversation Flow

The bot handles all incoming WhatsApp messages. It routes each person to the correct agent based on what they're asking. It never sends someone in circles — if it cannot confidently help, it hands over to Ann or Charissa immediately.

Flow Architecture
Entry Point
Routing Bot — Every New Incoming Message
Reads the message. Greets the person. Asks one simple question: "Are you interested in booking a cruise, enquiring about a private function, or do you have a question about Liquid Lounge?" Maps their reply to the correct agent below.
↓ routes to one of three agents
Agent 1
Public Cruise Agent
Triggered when someone wants to book a cruise. Collects: cruise type preference, preferred date, group size. Sends the direct booking link. Follows up if no booking after 24 hours.
Outcome A
Booking Link Sent
Direct Shopify link sent for the specific cruise chosen. Bot monitors for confirmation. If no booking after 24h, sends one follow-up nudge.
Handover Trigger
Date Not Available / Special Request
If the requested date is not available or the contact has a non-standard request (e.g. wheelchair access, large group 20+, media inquiry), bot flags as "human handover" and notifies Ann.
Agent 2
Private Function Agent
Triggered when someone wants to enquire about a private event. Collects: type of function, approximate guest count, preferred date (if known), catering requirements. Sends standard private function info pack. Flags for Charissa to follow up with a custom quote.
Outcome B
Info Pack Sent + Charissa Notified
Bot sends the private functions info document. Internally notifies Charissa: "New private function inquiry — [name], [guest count], [date if given], [function type]. Awaiting your quote."
Handover Trigger
All Private Function Inquiries
All private function leads always end in a human handover to Charissa for quoting. Bot collects the info — Charissa closes the deal.
Agent 3
FAQ / General Query Agent
Handles general questions: pricing, location, parking, age restrictions, accessibility, what's included, dress code, cancellation policy, etc. Pulls answers from the knowledge base. If the question is outside the knowledge base, escalates immediately.
Outcome C
Answer Provided — Convert to Booking
After answering the question, bot always asks: "Would you like to book a cruise or find out about a private function?" Re-routes them into Agent 1 or 2.
Handover Trigger
Unknown / Sensitive Questions
Complaints, refund requests, supplier inquiries, media requests, legal matters — immediate handover to Ann or Charissa. Bot does not attempt to answer these.
Agent 4
Post-Experience Review Request Flow
Triggered separately — not from inbound messages. Fired by Jacques loading the guest list every two weeks. Sends a warm post-experience message, asks for a Google review via the ReviewTap card/link. Filters: only sends to guests who had a positive interaction (screened via a quick 1-question rating first).
Outcome D
Google Review Link Sent
If guest gives a 4 or 5 rating: sends direct Google review link. If 1-3: sends internal flag only — no public review requested. Charissa or Ann follows up privately to understand what went wrong.

Handover Events & Escalation Rules

These are the exact conditions under which the AI bot stops handling a conversation and passes it to a human. When a handover fires, the contact is tagged "human handover", assigned to the correct person, and Ann receives a notification in the ReviewTap System inbox.

🚢 All Private Function Enquiries

Every private function inquiry ends in a handover to Charissa. The bot collects the info — Charissa does the quoting and closing.

  • Assign to: Charissa
  • Tag: human handover
  • Notification: full inquiry summary sent internally
  • Expected response time: within same business day
❓ Question Not in Knowledge Base

If the bot cannot answer from its knowledge base, it immediately hands over rather than guessing or making something up.

  • Assign to: Ann
  • Tag: human handover
  • Bot message: "Let me get one of our team to answer that properly for you!"
  • Ann responds within 2 hours during business hours
👥 Large Group (20+ people) on a Public Cruise

Groups of 20 or more may require special arrangements. The bot cannot confirm this — it must be checked by a human.

  • Assign to: Ann or Charissa
  • Tag: human handover, hot lead
  • Bot message: "For groups your size, our team will personally check availability and options for you — we'll be in touch shortly!"
😞 Negative Experience / Complaint

Any contact who indicates a poor experience, raises a complaint, or requests a refund must be handled personally. Never by the bot.

  • Assign to: Charissa
  • Tag: human handover
  • No review link ever sent
  • Feedback notes captured internally for management review
💬 Abusive / Inappropriate Message

The bot ends the conversation politely and flags it. No engagement, no escalation to argue.

  • Assign to: Ann (to review and decide action)
  • Tag: human handover, blacklist (if warranted)
  • Bot message: "Thanks for getting in touch. If you'd like assistance, feel free to message us again."
🔄 Contact Breaks Out of Flow

If a contact sends a message that doesn't match any expected response in the flow (e.g. types a random sentence mid-booking), the bot attempts to re-route. After 2 failed re-route attempts, it hands over.

  • Assign to: Ann
  • Tag: human handover
  • Bot message: "I want to make sure I help you properly — let me connect you with our team directly!"
📦 Media, Supplier, or Partnership Inquiry

Press enquiries, supplier pitches, partnership proposals, or sponsorship requests must be routed to Charissa — not handled by the bot or Ann.

  • Assign to: Charissa
  • Tag: human handover
  • Bot message: "That sounds like something our management team should handle personally. We'll be in touch!"
⏰ No Response After 24 Hours (Human Conversations)

If Ann has taken over a conversation and the contact has not responded for 24 hours, the system sends Ann a reminder to follow up once more before closing the conversation.

  • Assign to: Ann (reminder notification)
  • Ann decides: send one more follow-up or close conversation
  • If closed: tag not_ready for future broadcast re-engagement
