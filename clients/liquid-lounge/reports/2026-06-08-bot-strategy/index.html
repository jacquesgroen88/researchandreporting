What's Been Built — Status Overview

This section summarises everything currently in the system as of June 8, 2026. The WhatsApp bot has been live since early 2026 and has handled 386 contacts from the Facebook ad campaign. Three workflows are published and active. Nineteen workflows are built but sitting in draft — these need to be reviewed and activated.

The Critical Gap

This means: when someone fills in the houseboat booking form, the public cruise booking form, or the private charter enquiry form — nothing happens automatically. No confirmation is sent to the customer. No internal notification fires to Charissa or Ann. Ann has to manually check the inbox and respond. Live WhatsApp Bot Flow

This is the exact flow a contact goes through when they message Liquid Lounge on WhatsApp — whether they clicked a Facebook ad or found the number organically. The bot uses WhatsApp interactive button menus, not free-text AI. If someone sends a free-text message at any point and doesn't select from a menu, they fall through to a human agent (Ann or Charissa).

Handover Events — When Ann Takes Over

These are the specific situations where the bot cannot (or should not) continue the conversation and a human needs to step in. Ann is the first line of response. Charissa handles anything requiring a decision, a quote, or a complaint.

💬 Free-text message (no button selected)
Contact asks a question in free text instead of using the menu buttons. The bot re-presents the menu once. If still no selection or a further free-text message arrives — Ann responds.
  • Who: Ann first, escalate to Charissa if needed
  • Target response: within 2 hours (business hours)
  • Common examples: "How much for a birthday party?", "Do you have availability on Saturday?", "Is there food?"
🎉 All Private Charter & Houseboat Enquiries (post-form)
After a contact submits the Private Charter Enquiry or Houseboat Booking Form, the bot cannot continue — it needs Charissa to check availability and create a personalised quote.
  • Who: Charissa (for availability & quoting)
  • Target response: same business day
  • Ann's role: flag to Charissa if internal notification hasn't been actioned by afternoon
🔢 Review Request — Negative Score (1–3)
When a post-cruise contact rates their experience 1–3, the bot collects their feedback. It must then be picked up by a human for a personal recovery conversation.
  • Who: Charissa personally
  • Target response: within 24 hours
  • Never send a Google review link to these contacts
😤 Complaint or Refund Request
If at any point in a conversation a contact mentions a bad experience, cancellation, or refund — the bot should not engage. Route immediately.
  • Who: Charissa personally
  • Ann's role: identify in inbox and flag immediately
  • Do not respond with templates — personal message only
👥 Large Group 20+ Guests (Private Charter)
After the bot sends the 20+ guests info pack and the form is submitted, this requires a custom quote and potentially multiple vessels. Always a manual conversation.
  • Who: Charissa
  • Ann's role: confirm form was received in CRM pipeline
📆 Availability Check — Specific Dates
A contact asks about a specific date that they are unsure is available (e.g. weekday charter, public holiday, peak season houseboat). The bot cannot check live calendar availability.
  • Who: Ann checks calendar, responds
  • Escalate to Charissa if the date has complications
🤔 Question Not Answered by Bot
Any question the bot cannot answer from its current message set (e.g. dietary requirements, accessibility, what entertainment is on, group specials, gift vouchers).
  • Who: Ann first
  • If Ann unsure: escalate to Charissa
  • Common recurring questions should be added back to the bot's knowledge base
⏱️ No Booking After 24-Hour Follow-Up
The bot sends an automated morning follow-up after 24 hours if no booking has been made. If the contact still hasn't booked or responded after that follow-up — Ann sends one final personal message before closing.
  • Who: Ann
  • One personal follow-up only — then mark as "not ready" and leave for future broadcast
Ann's ReviewTap System Checklist

This is Ann's complete operating guide for the system. Everything is specific and actionable. As more draft workflows get published, the manual steps reduce — but until then this covers all the gaps the automation doesn't yet fill.

Do this before anything else. Should take no more than 30 minutes.
Check the inbox for any unanswered messages
Open the ReviewTap System inbox. Look for any conversation that has an unread message or no reply. Sort by "unread" if possible. Any message from a contact that has been waiting more than 2 hours during business hours needs to be answered first.
Daily
Look for any free-text messages the bot couldn't handle
These are contacts who sent a free-text question instead of selecting from the menu. They'll often have a message like "how much is it for..." or "do you have availability..." without any bot reply. These need a personal response from Ann.
Priority
Check the pipeline stages — move anything that changed overnight
Open each active pipeline: Public Cruises, Private Charters, Houseboat. If someone paid, confirmed, or cancelled — move them to the correct stage. Until the booking confirmation workflows are published, this is all manual.
Daily
Check the houseboat inbox specifically — same-day response is critical
Houseboat enquiries are high value. If someone submitted the Houseboat Booking Form, let Charissa know immediately so she can check availability and respond. Do not leave a houseboat enquiry overnight without a response.

Responding to Conversations — Rules to Follow Every Time
These apply every time Ann handles a conversation manually.
Public cruise inquiries — always send the direct booking link, not the homepage
Leisure cruise: vaalrivercruises.co.za/products/leisure-cruises — Lunch cruise: vaalrivercruises.co.za/products/lunch-cruises. If they ask about both, send both links.

Before sending any pricing, ask: "How many guests are you looking to host?" The answer determines which boat to recommend (Lady Elizabeth = 1–12, El Shaddai = 13–20, full private function = 20+).
Always
If a contact went quiet after the bot — do one manual follow-up, then leave it
The bot already sends an automated morning follow-up. If they still haven't responded after Ann's one manual follow-up, tag them not_ready and leave it. They will be picked up in a future broadcast campaign. Do not keep chasing.
Always
Negative reviews or complaints — flag to Charissa immediately, don't reply yourself
If a contact expresses anything negative — bad experience, complaint, request for refund — tag the conversation, note what they said, and message Charissa directly. Do not send a template reply. These need Charissa's personal response.
Always
Moving pipeline stages is safe — no automations will fire unexpectedly
The 19 draft workflows are not published yet, so moving a contact between stages will not trigger any automated messages. Move freely and keep the pipelines accurate. Jacques will confirm before any stage-based automation goes live.
