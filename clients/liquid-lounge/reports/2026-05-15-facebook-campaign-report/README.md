# Liquid Lounge — Facebook Campaign Report

**Type:** Platform Audit / Campaign Analysis  
**Period:** 24 March – 11 May 2026  
**Published:** 15 May 2026  
**Analyst:** JCE Media

---

## Summary

Comprehensive performance report for the Liquid Lounge Facebook ad campaign running across two ad sets (Evergreen and Upcoming Public Holidays). Covers all key metrics, placement breakdown, lead generation funnel, CRM automation workflows, and a 90-day action plan.

## Key Metrics

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Ad Spend | R1,158.87 |
| Total Impressions | 71,306 |
| Accounts Reached | 38,525 |
| WhatsApp Conversations | 371 |
| Cost per Result | R3.12 |
| CRM Contacts Generated | 386 |
| Confirmed Booking Leads | 13 |

## Notable Findings

- Facebook Feed drove 313 of 371 results (84%) and 88% of spend
- Facebook Reels (Public Holidays) most cost-efficient at R1.77/result
- 3.37% lead-to-booking conversion rate from WhatsApp conversations
- 386 warm contacts in CRM represent significant retargeting opportunity
