Campaign Overview

Liquid Lounge ran two Facebook ad sets between 24 March and 11 May 2026, targeting South Africans interested in riverside leisure experiences on the Vaal River. The campaign objective was WhatsApp conversation starts, routing prospective guests directly to the booking team via WhatsApp.

  • LL Public Cruises — Upcoming Public Holidays Ad Set Ran 24 Mar – 17 Apr 2026. Targeted specific upcoming public holiday dates with time-sensitive creative. Strong cost-efficiency results.

Review Campaign Recommendation

The post-cruise review workflow is already the top driver of Google reviews, yet only 100 contacts have entered it. Every guest who books and cruises should be marked as "Won" in Reviewtap CRM to trigger this workflow automatically. In addition, run a manual broadcast-style review campaign at least twice per month targeting all contacts who have been on a cruise but have not yet received a rating request. Even a 20% response rate from the existing 386 contacts could generate 77+ new Google reviews.

After every cruise, mark all guests as "Won" in Reviewtap CRM immediately — this auto-triggers the 24h review request.
Run a manual review campaign on the 1st and 15th of each month to any past guests not yet in the flow.
Monitor the 1–3 star feedback responses closely — these contain actionable service improvement data.
Use 4–5 star reviewer names (with permission) in ad creative and on the Shopify/booking page for social proof.

Where & How to Deploy the Cards and Stands
🚢
Public & Sharing Cruises Highest Volume
The guest services crew member or cruise operator holds the Reviewtap card as guests disembark at the end of the cruise. Guests scan the QR code while the positive emotion of the experience is still fresh — this is the single most effective moment to capture a review.
💡 Tip: Crew should say: "Loved having you on board — would you mind leaving us a quick Google review? It takes 10 seconds, just scan here."
⚓
Houseboat Accommodation
Place the Reviewtap stand on the dining room table or in a visible common area of the houseboat. Guests will notice it during their stay and can scan at any point. The extended stay gives multiple opportunities for a natural, unprompted scan.
💡 Tip: Add a small handwritten card alongside: "Enjoying your stay? Let us know — scan to leave a review. Your feedback helps us improve."
🎉
Private Functions & Events
Place a stand on the bar or catering table during the event. As the event winds down, the host or crew can also do a personal hand-held card scan prompt with the group organiser. Groups are socially motivated — one person scanning often prompts others.
💡 Tip: Ask the event organiser in advance to encourage their group to scan — mention it can be done during the cruise, not just at the end.
