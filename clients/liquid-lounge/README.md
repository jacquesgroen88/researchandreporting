# Liquid Lounge — Client Research Hub

**Liquid Lounge** operates leisure and dining cruises on the Vaal River in Vanderbijlpark, South Africa. The business offers Sunday leisure cruises and public holiday sailings, with services including 1-hour leisure cruises (R250/adult) and 2-hour lunch cruises (R680/adult).

## Key Business Details

**Positioning:** Relaxed Vaal River cruise experience for families, couples, and private charters on the Vaal River, Vanderbijlpark.

**Service Range:**
- 1-Hour Leisure Cruise — R250/adult, R125/child (U12) — Sundays & public holidays 10:30–11:30
- 2-Hour Lunch Cruise — R680/adult, R340/child (U12) — Sundays 13:00–15:00
- Houseboat hire
- Private charters

**Advertising:** Facebook & Instagram campaigns managed by JCE Media. WhatsApp conversation-start objective. CRM managed via Reviewtap CRM.

**Research Status:** Initial campaign audit and strategy document complete (May 2026). Ongoing campaign optimisation recommended with increased budget (R2,500–R3,500/month).
