08 / Questions to Ask Them
Strategic Questions That Make You Look Sharp

"How do you see the relationship between Magos Media and VeliTech developing — is there a digital services overlap you're planning to build?"

Signals: strategic awareness of his multi-venture play. Forces him to think about you in both contexts.

"What's the current biggest friction point between the creative team and client delivery — where does it break down?"

Signals: you're thinking about operational reality, not just what looks good on a job brief. You want to actually fix things.

"What does success in this role look like at 90 days — and what does it look like at 12 months?"

Signals: you're already thinking about measurement and timelines. This is a performance mindset question.

"Is the head of digital expected to also drive new business development, or is that primarily coming from you?"

Signals: you understand that in a small agency, "Head of Digital" often means wearing multiple hats. Gets clarity upfront.

"I noticed your own social channels are more low-key than the brand's positioning — is there a strategy behind that, or is it something you'd want the Head of Digital to change?"

Signals: honesty + strategic awareness. If they have a reason, they'll explain it. If it's a gap, they'll be impressed you spotted it.

"How does reporting work for major clients like PnP or KFC — is that live dashboards, monthly decks, or something more custom?"

Signals: you're thinking about client retention and accountability infrastructure, not just campaign execution.

"What happened with the previous person in this role — or is this a new position?"

Signals: maturity. Understanding whether this is a backfill or an expansion tells you a lot about the agency's trajectory.
