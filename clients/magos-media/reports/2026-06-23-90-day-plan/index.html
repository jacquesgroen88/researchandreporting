Phase 01 · Days 1–30

Audit & Establish

Nothing gets changed until we know what we’re working with. This phase is diagnostic: audit the full 12+ brand portfolio, claim every unclaimed GBP, talk to every team member and client, and set the baselines every future metric will be measured against.

01
Audit and claim ALL unclaimed GBPs across the full Magos portfolio
The PnP audit already identified 47 unclaimed profiles, but PnP is one of 12+ brands. Run a full scrape across every account: KFC, Pick n Pay, Spar, Protea Hotels, African Alliance, Hotel Sky, News Cafe, Calisto’s, Petzone, Doppio Group, FutureDrum, Kream and more. Queue claim links sorted by review exposure for every unclaimed profile found. The 47 PnP profiles are the starting point, not the finish line. Priority: any location with 200+ reviews and zero brand response capability.
Week 1
02
Complete reputation and GBP baseline audit across every brand
Star ratings, review volumes, unclaimed rates, negative review exposure, documented for all 12+ brands. Run the same diagnostic across Protea Hotels, African Alliance, Hotel Sky, News Cafe, Calisto’s, Petzone, Doppio Group, FutureDrum, and Kream. By end of Day 30, every brand has a documented reputation baseline with a clear priority action list.
Audit
03
Full ad account audit across the entire portfolio
Structural review of every active paid media account in the Magos portfolio: campaign architecture, audience segmentation, creative rotation, bidding strategy, and targeting parameters. Document what exists before touching anything. Identify immediate inefficiencies and flag accounts where spend is likely wasted on broken structure or unverified tracking.
Audit
04
Verify conversion tracking on every active account
Broken tracking means broken optimisation. Audit GA4, Meta Pixel, Google Tag Manager, and any server-side tracking implementations across all accounts. Build a tracking health scorecard, one row per brand. Every account running without verified tracking is making budget decisions on incomplete data. The scorecard becomes the fix log in Phase 2: every row must be green before any spend recommendation is made.
Data
05
Interview every member of the Magos digital team
One-on-one sessions with Sasha, Shoko, Remmert, Itumeleng, Tracy and any incoming team members. Goal: understand their current workflows, what tools they use, what’s frustrating them, where they feel underutilised, and what they think the agency does well vs. where it falls short. The team’s honest assessment is the fastest map to what needs fixing. People who feel heard in week one perform differently at week twelve.
People
06
Meet every client stakeholder across all 12+ accounts
Scheduled calls or visits with the day-to-day contacts at every brand in the portfolio. The goal is to understand their perception of the relationship: what’s working, what frustrates them, what they wish existed. Client perception gaps are renewal risks. Knowing them by Day 30 means they can be addressed before they become the reason a contract does not renew. One stakeholder meeting per brand, minimum.
Client
07
Establish performance baselines across the full portfolio
Lock in the Day 1 numbers for every account: GBP response rate, average star rating trend, ROAS by campaign, impression share, and review volume growth rate. Every QBR, roadmap and client conversation from here forwards measures against these numbers. Without a baseline per brand, there is no proof of progress and no commercial case for renewal or expansion.
Data
08
AI visibility audit: search every brand on ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini
Search each brand across every major AI interface: “best KFC near me”, “Protea Hotels Johannesburg”, “restaurant in Sandton.” Screenshot and document what surfaces for every brand in the portfolio. This is your agentic discovery baseline. As AI buying agents become the new search layer, brands that do not appear in AI responses lose the first touchpoint entirely. Nobody at Magos is tracking this today.
AI Readiness
Phase 02 · Days 31–60

Build & Fix

Fix the foundation. Build the systems. Run the first QBR cycle across every account. By the end of this phase, the work is visible to clients, the portfolio is running profitably, and the team has a shared operating rhythm it can hold independently.

01
Deploy review response system across the full portfolio
Templated response library by category and vertical: food safety, service, wait time, facilities, pricing, compliments, tailored for QSR, hospitality, retail, and professional services. 24-hour SLA on all 1★ and 2★ reviews across every brand in the portfolio. Response rate is a Google Maps local ranking signal. Improving it from zero to 40%+ benchmark is both a reputation and an SEO win. Automated alerts for negative reviews across all claimed profiles.
System
02
Fix all broken conversion tracking across every account
Every account bidding blind is money wasted. Work through the tracking health scorecard built in Phase 1. Implement fixes, test each conversion event end-to-end, and set up conversion confirmation reporting per brand. No campaign optimisation recommendation will be made on any account with unverified tracking. The scorecard closes green before Phase 2 ends.
Data
03
Run the first QBR cycle across all anchor accounts
Performance vs. the Day 1 baselines set in Phase 1. Forward recommendations per account. The difference between a QBR and a report: clients leave with a decision, not just a document. Cover every account in the portfolio, not just the biggest three. Clients who have not had a structured QBR are the ones most at risk of quiet attrition. Each session ends with a signed-off priority list for the next quarter.
Client
04
Standardise client dashboards across all accounts
One format. Live data. Consistent KPIs. Every brand gets the same dashboard structure: reputation (response rate, rating trend, review volume), paid media (ROAS, CPR, impression share), and local (GBP impressions, direction requests, call clicks). No more monthly PDF decks that are outdated before they’re read. Consistent dashboards also make the team’s output scalable. Any team member can run any account report without rebuilding from scratch.
System
05
Margin audit: which accounts are profitable and which are not
Map actual team time spent vs. retainer value per account. Identify which accounts are delivering healthy margins and which are consuming more than they’re paying for. A portfolio of 12+ accounts is only worth having if it’s running profitably. Right-size scopes where there is a consistent mismatch. Propose scope adjustments where needed. The goal is a sustainable digital function that does not depend on heroic hours to deliver. Present the margin picture to Petro before the roadmap conversation in Phase 3.
Profitability
06
GBP completeness pass: every claimed profile filled to 100%
Claiming was step one. Step two: fill every field on every profile across all brands. Hours, photos, Q&A, description, product categories, business attributes. AI agents rank GBP completeness as a data quality signal. A claimed but half-empty profile is nearly as invisible as an unclaimed one when an AI decides which location to recommend. One brand per week, systematic, not ad hoc.
AI Readiness
07
Weekly response rate and reputation tracking live across all brands
Response rate, average rating, and review volume reported weekly for every brand in the portfolio. Tracked against the Day 1 baselines and the 40%+ industry benchmark. This becomes the standing reputation metric in every client update. Simple, consistent, and something clients can actually see moving week on week.
Data
Phase 03 · Days 61–90

Lead & Scale

Move from execution to strategy. The foundations are solid, the QBRs are done, the accounts are running profitably. Now present the roadmap, define how the agency measures itself, and plant the flag on what’s coming next for every client in the portfolio.

01
Present 6-month digital roadmap to Petro and leadership
Strategic, not executional. The roadmap covers: where each account is today vs. industry benchmark, the three highest-impact initiatives per account over the next 6 months, and the agency capability investments needed to deliver them. Includes a profitability layer: which accounts need scope renegotiation, which are candidates for expansion, and how the team’s capacity maps to the portfolio’s growth. This is the plan that justifies the hire and sets the direction for Petro’s transition to Venteri Capital.
Strategy
02
Build the agency digital onboarding checklist for every new client
Every new client onboarded from this point follows the same protocol: GBP audit on day one, conversion tracking verification before any spend, AI visibility baseline, competitor benchmark, margin model signed off before work begins. This is how you prevent the next 47 unclaimed profiles from ever happening again. A single checklist that becomes a non-negotiable standard, enforced without exception, regardless of account size.
System
03
Define agency-wide digital KPIs, including margin per account
Four metrics that measure the health of the digital function across the full portfolio: average GBP response rate (reputation health), tracking quality score (data integrity), client retention rate (commercial health), and margin per account (profitability health). Reported to leadership monthly. Makes the invisible work visible and gives the team a shared definition of what a well-run portfolio actually looks like.
System
04
Surface cross-sell opportunities across the full portfolio
Map each of the 12+ brands against Magos’s full capability set. Identify the single most logical gap per client where Magos could expand scope: a channel not being run, a capability gap, a competitor doing something they aren’t. Cross-sell from a relationship of demonstrated results is the lowest-cost revenue available to any agency. Present as a data-backed recommendation from the last 60 days, not a pitch.
Strategy
05
Identify one net-new revenue opportunity from within the existing base
Based on 90 days of data and client conversations, identify one commercially viable new service or product that could generate incremental revenue from clients already in the portfolio. Could be a retainer expansion, a project, a productised service, or a new channel rollout. Present to Petro with a revenue estimate, a margin model, and a delivery plan.
Strategy
06
AI readiness section in the 6-month roadmap
Where each brand currently appears when an AI agent is asked a relevant question. What needs to change per brand: GBP completeness, structured data, first-party data strategy, AI ad buying readiness. Position Magos as the agency thinking about this before clients start asking. That positioning is worth more than any campaign in a retention conversation, and more than any pitch in a new business one.
