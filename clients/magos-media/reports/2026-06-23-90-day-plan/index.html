Phase 01 · Days 1–30

Audit & Establish

Nothing gets changed until we know what we’re working with. This phase is diagnostic — map the landscape, fix what’s bleeding, and set the baselines every future metric will be measured against.

01
Claim all 47 unclaimed Pick n Pay Google Business Profiles
Direct Google Maps links already identified and sorted by review exposure. This is a queued task list, not a project. Priority order: Maponya Mall (1,155 reviews), Sanbury Square (1,158 reviews), Richmond Corner (852 reviews). Maponya is Soweto’s biggest mall — zero brand presence on 1,155 public reviews is not acceptable.
Week 1
02
Complete Spar full network audit: 44 → 900+ locations
Current data is a 44-location sample — insufficient to report on a 900+ store franchise. Run a full Apify scrape of the national network to establish unclaimed rate, star distribution and negative review volume. Based on PnP precedent, expect 20%+ unclaimed. Sets the baseline before any client engagement metrics are agreed.
Audit
03
Audit all ad accounts across KFC, Pick n Pay and Spar
Full structural review of every active account: campaign architecture, audience segmentation, creative rotation, bidding strategy, and targeting parameters. Document what exists before touching anything. Identify immediate inefficiencies and establish the performance starting point.
Audit
04
Verify conversion tracking on every active account
Broken tracking means broken optimisation. Audit GA4 setup, Meta Pixel, Google Tag Manager, and any server-side tracking implementations. Every account running without verified tracking is making decisions on incomplete data. Flag and fix before any spend recommendations are made.
Data
05
Meet every client stakeholder — not just the brief
Scheduled calls or site visits with the day-to-day contacts at KFC, PnP and Spar. The goal is to understand their perception of the agency relationship — what’s working, what frustrates them, what they wish existed. Client perception gaps are renewal risks. Know them before the QBR.
Client
06
Establish performance baselines across all accounts
Lock in the Day 1 numbers: GBP response rate, average star rating trend, ROAS by campaign, impression share, review volume growth rate. Every QBR, roadmap and client conversation from here forwards measures against these numbers. Without a baseline, there is no proof of progress.
Data
07
AI visibility audit — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini
Search each brand on every major AI interface: “best KFC near me”, “Pick n Pay store Cape Town”, “Spar vs PnP.” Screenshot and document what surfaces. This is your agentic discovery baseline — as AI buying agents become the new search layer, brands that don’t appear in AI responses lose the first touchpoint. Nobody at Magos is tracking this today.
AI Readiness
Phase 02 · Days 31–60

Build & Fix

Fix the foundation. Build the systems. By the end of this phase, the work becomes visible to clients — dashboards are live, responses are happening, and the audit findings have been presented back with evidence of what changed.

01
Deploy review response system across all three accounts
Templated response library by category: food safety, wait time, service quality, facilities, pricing, compliments. 24-hour SLA on all 1★ and 2★ reviews. Response rate is a Google Maps local ranking signal — improving it from zero to 40%+ benchmark is both a reputation and an SEO win. Set up automated alerts for negative reviews across all claimed profiles.
System
02
Fix all broken conversion tracking
Every account bidding blind is money wasted. Implement verified tracking fixes identified in Phase 1. Test each conversion event end-to-end. Set up conversion confirmation reporting so every client sees tracked vs untracked traffic in their dashboard. No campaign optimisation recommendation will be made on an account with unverified tracking.
Data
03
Standardise client dashboards across all accounts
One format. Live data. Consistent KPIs. Every client gets the same dashboard structure: reputation (response rate, rating trend, review volume), paid media (ROAS, CPR, impression share), and local (GBP impressions, direction requests, call clicks). No more monthly PDF decks that are outdated before they’re read.
System
04
Present audit findings back to every client
The “here’s what we found, here’s what we fixed” conversation. Not a report — a meeting. Makes the work visible before the QBR and resets the relationship baseline. Clients who understand what changed are clients who renew. Each presentation includes: what the data showed, what was actioned, and the before/after metrics.
Client
05
Identify one upsell or scope expansion per account
Based on the audit and the stakeholder conversations, surface the single most logical expansion for each account: a channel not being run, a capability gap, a competitor doing something they aren’t. One specific proposal per client by end of day 60. Not a pitch — a data-backed recommendation with projected impact.
Client
06
Weekly response rate tracking live across all brands
Response rate, average rating, and review volume reported weekly for KFC, PnP and Spar. Tracked against the Day 1 baseline and the 40%+ industry benchmark. This becomes the standing reputation metric in every client update — simple, consistent, and something clients can actually see moving.
Data
07
GBP completeness optimisation pass
Claiming was step one. Step two: fill every field on every profile. Hours, photos, Q&A, description, product categories, business attributes. AI agents rank GBP completeness as a data quality signal. A claimed but half-empty profile is nearly as invisible as an unclaimed one when an AI decides which location to recommend. This is future-proofing, not housekeeping.
AI Readiness
Phase 03 · Days 61–90

Lead & Scale

Move from execution to strategy. By day 90 the foundations are solid — now we present the roadmap, run the first QBR, define how the agency measures itself, and plant the flag on what’s coming next.

01
Present 6-month digital roadmap to Petro and leadership
Strategic, not executional. The roadmap covers: where each account is today vs industry benchmark, the three highest-impact initiatives per account over the next 6 months, and the agency capability investments needed to deliver them. This is the plan that justifies the hire — it shows the function running autonomously, exactly as the Venteri Capital transition requires.
Strategy
02
Run the first QBR cycle across all three anchor accounts
Performance vs the Day 1 baselines set in Phase 1. Forward recommendations for the next quarter. The difference between a QBR and a report: clients leave with a decision, not just a document. Each session ends with a signed-off priority list for Q2. This also builds the renewal case six months before renewal happens.
Client
03
Build the agency digital onboarding checklist
Every new client onboarded from this point follows the same protocol: GBP audit on day one, conversion tracking verification before any spend, AI visibility baseline, competitor benchmark. This is how you prevent the next 47 unclaimed profiles from ever happening. A one-page checklist that becomes a standard agency process, not a one-off project.
System
04
Define agency-wide digital KPIs
Three metrics that measure the health of the agency’s digital function across all accounts: average GBP response rate (reputation health), tracking quality score (data integrity), client retention rate (commercial health). Reported to leadership monthly. Makes the invisible work visible and gives the team a shared definition of what good looks like.
System
05
Surface cross-sell opportunities across the current client base
Map each client’s current scope against Magos’s full capability set. Identify the single most logical gap per client where Magos could expand. Cross-sell from a relationship of demonstrated results is the lowest-cost revenue available to any agency. Present options — don’t pitch, recommend.
Strategy
06
Identify one net-new revenue opportunity from the existing base
Based on 90 days of data and client conversations, identify one commercially viable new service or product that could generate incremental revenue from clients already in the portfolio. Could be a retainer expansion, a project, or a productised service. Present to Petro with a revenue estimate and a delivery plan.
Strategy
07
AI readiness section in the 6-month roadmap
Where each brand currently appears when an AI agent is asked a relevant question. What needs to change — GBP completeness, structured data, first-party data strategy, AI ad buying readiness. Position Magos as the agency that was thinking about this before clients started asking. That positioning is worth more than any campaign in a retention conversation.
