Account structure audit

What's actually live, how it's wired, and where it fights the algorithm.

META OPPORTUNITY SCORE

Meta's own verdict: the account is well-aligned with best practice (86/100). That's an important check on this audit — the strategy isn't broken. Meta surfaces just two levers, and the bigger one points at budget, not structure:

  • +11 pts · "Budget-limited" ad sets — Meta flags the live campaign's ad sets as constrained: "your current budget may be limiting performance — consider increasing it." This independently corroborates the volume-starvation finding below.
  • +3 pts · Conversion optimisation — guidance to ensure delivery is optimised for the purchase event (est. 17% lower cost per result).
1

Three near-identical ad sets inside one campaign High impact

The live campaign holds ad sets named "Women with kids 3-8 – Experiment / – Copy / Winner." All three carry identical targeting (South Africa, women, 23–65, Advantage Audience ON, optimising for purchases). Only "– Copy" is currently live. This is legacy manual A/B-testing logic: duplicating a "winner" into parallel ad sets that chase the same people, splitting learning history into three thin pools.

Verified with Meta Honest correction to my first draft: I expected live auction overlap / self-competition, but Meta's auction-ranking diagnostic did not flag it — because only one ad set is active right now, so there's nothing to overlap today. The real cost is fragmented learning history and the risk the moment the duplicate sets are switched back on. So this is a clean-up-and-prevent issue, not a live fire.

Fix → keep one broad ad set as the permanent engine; archive the duplicates rather than toggling them.
2

The account is volume-starved for its CPA Structural

At a ~R900 cost-per-purchase on a ~R950/day budget, the live ad set produces roughly 1 purchase a day — about 7 per week. Meta's optimiser wants ~50 conversions per ad set per week to stabilise. This ad set will likely never fully exit the learning phase at current budget/CPA, which explains the volatility and the slide.

Verified with Meta Meta's Opportunity Score names this its single biggest lever (+11 pts): the ad sets are "budget-limited." Its prescription is "increase budget." Mine differs in sequence: consolidate to one engine first so added budget compounds in one signal pool — and only scale spend if margin supports a 2.4× ROAS. Same diagnosis, disciplined order.

The single most important truth in the account: it is volume-starved for its CPA. Consolidate, then feed it (budget and/or a richer conversion signal).
3

14 paused legacy campaigns & messy naming Hygiene

15 campaigns exist; 14 are paused (multiple "Completed -" builds, duplicate "Advantage+", "March/April 2026", "MFB 23 April"). It's not hurting delivery, but it makes the account hard to read, hard to hand over, and easy to mis-edit. A real "Advantage+ Sales" campaign sits paused — relevant, because that's the format Meta now steers spend toward.

Fix → archive/relabel with a clean convention (e.g. [ASC] Prospecting | Sales | 2026-06).

The pattern: CTR and ROAS are inversely related here. The "Reaction" and "Unboxing With Tears" videos are scroll-stopping (8–12% CTR, sub-R0.65 clicks) but pull curiosity clicks that don't buy — Reaction sends 4,270 people to the site and converts 8; "With Tears" sends 148 and converts none. CBO is doing exactly what it's told: chase cheap conversions-signal, which on a thin pixel skews toward the cheap-click creative. The optimiser is being misled by the wrong creative, not malfunctioning.

Copy review all live creatives pulled

Winner's hook (3.13×): "👩‍👧 'Mommy, she's real!' … a soft, lifelike doll that feels just like a real baby sister in her arms." Strong emotional + scarcity framing.

  • Hooks are genuinely good — emotional, benefit-led, local trust cues (free SA delivery, SA-mom reviews)
  • Trust mismatch (now confirmed across multiple creatives): several ads promise a "7-day refund guarantee" while the site says "14-Day Returns." Fix everywhere — inconsistency erodes trust on a R2,000 buy.
  • Destinations are inconsistent: most body links point at the bare homepage (MyForeverBaby.co.za, weak — no reviews above fold), while some CTAs go to the stronger /pages/shop. Standardise on /pages/shop.
  • Duplicate primary text: two live video ads share identical copy — that's wasted creative diversity Andromeda could be using.
  • One "ad" is a thin boosted post ("Thank you mommy for this unboxing 🩷" — a 1-line status, no offer/CTA). Fine organically, weak as paid cold-traffic.
  • Every ad runs a website link and a WhatsApp link — two funnels competing; some "sales" are messaging threads, muddying attribution.

Where the 99% cart-leak lives. Two destinations are in play, and they are not equal — yet ads point at both.

  • Clean hero, clear product, free delivery + 14-day returns stated
  • No reviews / star ratings / social proof above the fold

  • No price justification — cold traffic can't judge if R2,099 is fair
Conversion thesis: the product, price and checkout work — the account has banked nearly R690k in tracked sales. The cart leak is a belief gap, not a checkout bug: cold buyers of a R2,000 niche item need review density, objection-handling ("is it appropriate / safe / worth it?"), and a single consistent high-trust destination. Tighten those and the existing 6.67% CTR converts far harder — without touching ad spend.
07

Reading this through Meta's Andromeda update

Andromeda is Meta's ML retrieval engine (built with NVIDIA) that lets the ad system sift a vastly larger pool of ad–person matches per impression. The practical consequence for advertisers: stop hand-segmenting audiences and start feeding the machine consolidated budget, broad targeting and creative volume — then let retrieval find the buyer. Here's how MyForeverBaby maps to that.

✓ Already aligned

Broad targeting + Advantage Audience, CBO, purchase objective, video-first library. The instincts are modern — the account isn't fighting Andromeda on strategy.

✗ Fights the model — fragmented signal

3 identical ad sets split the conversion signal Andromeda needs to retrieve well. Consolidate to one broad ad set so 100% of the (already thin) signal compounds in a single pool.

✗ Fights the model — creative starvation

Andromeda rewards creative diversity — it matches the right ad to the right person. The good angles are stranded in paused ad sets. Load 5–8 diverse live creatives into the one engine and let retrieval allocate.

↗ Untapped — Advantage+ Sales

An ASC campaign sits paused. ASC is the native Andromeda vehicle (minimal manual structure, model-led). It's the logical destination once the single broad ad set proves out.

↗ Untapped — Advantage+ Creative

Advantage+ Creative is opted out. Turning it on (with brand-safe guardrails) gives the model more creative permutations to test per person — more retrieval surface.

⚑ The hard constraint

Andromeda still needs signal volume. At 7 sales/week this account is below the threshold for clean optimisation. Feeding the model (consolidation + creative + a richer conversion signal) is the unlock — not more micro-management.

08

The 90-day plan

Sequenced so the cheap, reversible, high-certainty moves come first. Nothing here requires a rebuild — it's consolidate, redirect, and reinforce.

Week 1 · quick wins (do today)

Stop the bleeding & fix trust

  • Fix the refund mismatch — change ad copy "7-day" → "14-day" to match the site. Zero cost, protects trust.
  • Point every link to /pages/shop (the high-trust page). Kill the bare-homepage body link.
  • Cap or pause the Reaction Video's dominance — it's eating 51% of budget at 1.91×. Don't kill the angle (great hook), but stop it monopolising spend.
  • Increase budget share to "Video Ad October English" (3.13×) and re-enable "Hypermotion – Hope" (4.82×).
  • Archive the 14 dead campaigns and apply a clean naming convention.
Weeks 2–4 · structural reset

Consolidate into one engine

  • Collapse the 3 ad sets into 1 broad ZA / Advantage-Audience ad set. End the self-competition; pool the signal.
  • Stack 5–8 diverse live creatives in that one ad set: the proven October English + Hope, the best Reaction cut, an Afrikaans variant, plus 2 new angles (testimonial, price-justification/handcrafted).
  • Turn on Advantage+ Creative with brand guardrails.
  • Add a value/price-justification creative aimed squarely at the cart leak.
  • Hold budget at ~R950/day; let the consolidated set re-enter learning once, cleanly.
Weeks 4–8 · conversion-rate offensive

Close the 99% cart leak

  • Landing page: review density above the fold, an FAQ that handles the "is this appropriate/safe/worth it" objection, security/guarantee badges, fix the "From R1,299" pricing contradiction.
  • Broaden the framing beyond mum→daughter (collectors, grandparents, therapeutic/companionship).
  • Decide the volume play: either lift budget to push toward ~50 conv/week, or add an Add-to-Cart optimisation event / value rules to give Andromeda denser signal. Model both against margin before committing.
  • Launch the paused Advantage+ Sales (ASC) campaign as the scaling vehicle, fed by the winning creatives.
Weeks 8–12 · scale what works

Compound & protect

  • Shift primary spend to ASC once it beats the manual broad set on ROAS.
  • Creative cadence: 3–4 fresh concepts/week into the engine to outrun fatigue (frequency is already ~3–4.5 on a finite ZA audience).
  • Build a retention/retargeting layer (site visitors, ATC, past buyers) — currently no lower-funnel campaign exists despite a R690k buyer base.
  • Target: restore ROAS to 3×+ at ≥ current spend, then scale budget against a defended CPA.
