Overview

Executive Summary

My Forever Baby is a South African Shopify store selling lifelike reborn baby dolls, priced from R699 to R4,999. The store operates on the Dwell theme and has a strong product selection with an active Facebook and Google review presence.

The audit identified five high-impact, low-effort CRO opportunities in Phase 1. The most critical gaps were: no WhatsApp conversion channel, a disabled marquee section showing stale promotional text, globally shared accessories block content showing incorrect items per product, no on-site social proof (zero reviews), and sold-out products showing dead "Sold out" buttons with no alternative action.

Findings

Issues Identified

🔴 No WhatsApp conversion channel

WhatsApp is South Africa's #1 support channel. Uncertain buyers message instead of bouncing — WhatsApp conversations convert at 40–60%. No button existed on the store. Buyers who needed reassurance had no easy contact path and were likely bouncing.

🔴 Zero on-site reviews

No review app was installed. The review block was wired into the product template but rendering nothing. Despite 19 Facebook recommendations and 4 Google reviews, none of this social proof appeared on product pages — the highest-trust signal at the point of purchase was completely missing.

🟡 Marquee section disabled with stale text

The homepage marquee section (announcement ticker) was disabled. The text it contained was stale Christmas copy. This is free urgency and trust messaging that costs nothing to enable.

🟡 Accessories block showing wrong content per product

The Travel Package (R879) and Pamper Pack (R699) are high-revenue accessory SKUs. Both shared a global accessories text block showing the default doll accessories (birth certificate, bottle, toy, blanket) — completely wrong for these products. Customers seeing incorrect included items lose confidence and abandon.

🟡 Sold-out dolls showing dead "Sold out" button

Nine dolls show a greyed-out "Sold out" button with no alternative action — no pre-order option, no notify-me, no WhatsApp to enquire. This is a complete dead end for interested customers. A pre-order template exists in the theme but was not assigned to any sold-out products.

Priorities

Phase 1 Priority Matrix

1
WhatsApp Button
Fixed position, pre-filled message, 58px green button. SA's highest-converting support channel.
2
Review App + Import
Install Judge.me, import 23 existing reviews from Facebook & Google across top-selling in-stock dolls.
3
Enable Marquee
Enable marquee section and update text to current offers: free delivery, returns, new stock, WhatsApp.
4
Fix Accessories Blocks
Create per-product templates for Travel Package and Pamper Pack with correct accessories lists.
5
Pre-Order Template
Assign sold-out dolls to pre-order template: keeps conversion path open with 2–3 week delivery message.
6
Best-Seller Badges
Apply best-seller tag to top 5 dolls. Badge code already in theme — just needs the tag applied in Admin.
