Overview

Executive Summary

In 6 days since the Phase 1 audit, My Forever Baby has gone from a store with zero on-site social proof, no WhatsApp channel, a disabled marquee, and incorrect accessories content — to a fully CRO-optimised Shopify storefront with live reviews, an active WhatsApp conversion path, a running marquee, correct per-product templates, and a brand new TikTok presence.

The biggest wins are the Google reviews — growing from 4 to 11 (a 175% increase) — and the Judge.me integration, which now surfaces 23 five-star customer reviews directly on product pages. A new May promotion with countdown urgency is also live. The store is in the best shape it has ever been.

🏆 Standout move: 11 Google reviews at 5.0 ★

Going from 4 to 11 Google reviews in under a week is significant. Google reviews are the highest-trust signal in South African e-commerce — they appear in Google Search, Google Maps, and Google Shopping. At 11 reviews and a perfect 5.0 average, My Forever Baby now has the social proof needed to win searches and first impressions from cold traffic.

New Since Audit

Initiatives You've Launched

🎁

May Promotion with Countdown Timer NEW

A promotional banner is live at the very top of the site: "FREE Magic Milk and Juice Set with any Baby + Travel Deluxe Package! Valid 1–31 May 2026" with a live countdown timer ("Offer ends in 10d 15h 42m"). This is textbook urgency-and-value marketing — a free gift bundle with a hard deadline is one of the most effective AOV boosters in e-commerce. It also naturally cross-sells the Travel Deluxe Package by bundling it with the free item.

💰

Sitewide Sale Pricing Applied NEW

Sale prices and strike-through original prices are now active across most products (16%–24% off). This creates visible value anchoring on collection pages — buyers see the original price crossed out, immediately perceive value, and are more likely to add to cart. Baby Hope (-16%), Baby Hannah (-22%), Baby Summer (-19%), Baby Nila (-24%), and Baby Bonnie (-18%) all benefit from this. Note: If the sale is meant to be temporary, set an end date so discounts don't become the perceived "real" price.

📹

Video Embed on Homepage NEW

A "Play video" element is now embedded on the homepage in the "The Gift She Will Never Forget" section. Video on a product/brand homepage dramatically increases time-on-site and emotional connection — essential for a product that relies on that "wow" reaction. This is exactly the right content for a doll brand: mothers and daughters, unboxings, and gift moments. Make sure this is a short, high-emotion clip (under 60 seconds) with no audio required to understand it.

🏷️

Best Seller Badges Active NEW

Best Seller badges are now showing on Baby Hope, Baby Ashley, Baby Hannah, and Baby Elizabeth in the collection view. These were already coded in the theme from Phase 1 — the tags have since been applied correctly in Shopify Admin. This is a strong collection-page signal that guides buyers toward your top products without needing any copy.

Still To Do

Remaining Action Items

🔴 HIGH PRIORITY

Assign Pre-Order Template to Sold-Out Dolls

11 sold-out products still show a dead "Sold Out" button. Assign product.preorder-babies in Admin → Products → each product → Theme template. Affects: Hailey, Grace, Emma, Blake, Melissa, Amelia, Nila, Samantha, Elizabeth, Chloe, Summer.

🔴 HIGH PRIORITY

Judge.me Post-Purchase Email Flow

Turn on automated review request emails in Judge.me → Settings → Review requests. Set delay to 7–10 days after fulfilment. This is what grows your on-site review count from 23 to 100+ organically over the next 3 months without any manual work.

🟡 MEDIUM PRIORITY

TikTok Content Strategy

3 followers is normal for a brand new account — growth comes from content frequency and hooks, not waiting. Post 3–5× per week. Best-performing formats: unboxing reveals, daughter's first reaction, gift wrap close-ups, "what's inside" content. Use trending SA audio. Always end with the store link in bio.

🟡 MEDIUM PRIORITY

Add Reviews to Baby Aaliyah

New product with no reviews yet. Once first 2–3 orders arrive, import those reviews via Judge.me CSV. In the meantime, consider moving 1 generic positive review from a lower-priority product to seed Baby Aaliyah's page.

🟡 MEDIUM PRIORITY

Google Business Profile — Complete Info

Google flagged "Complete your profile" and "Fix product details" in your Business dashboard. Completing missing fields (categories, business description, hours confirmation, product listings) directly improves how you appear in Google Search and Maps — especially important now that you have 11 reviews pulling eyes to your profile.

🟢 PHASE 2

Essential Upsell App — Travel Package + Pamper Pack

Confirm the Essential Upsell app is offering Travel Package and Pamper Pack as add-ons on doll product pages. The upsell widget is already installed — verify it's appearing and correctly configured for all in-stock doll pages. This is free AOV without any extra traffic.

🟢 PHASE 2

TikTok Pixel + Meta Pixel Audit

Now that you have a TikTok channel, install the TikTok Pixel on your Shopify store (via TikTok app in Shopify App Store). This tracks which TikTok content drives store visits and purchases — essential for any paid TikTok ads later. Also verify Meta Pixel is firing correctly so Facebook traffic is being tracked for retargeting.

🟢 PHASE 2

Review the Sale End Date

Multiple products show sale pricing with no visible end date on collection pages (the promo banner has a May 31 deadline but the individual product sale badges don't). If the sale ends May 31, make sure the discounts revert on June 1 — otherwise R2,499 stops feeling like the "real" price and the discount loses its power.

🟢 PHASE 2

Restock Strategy for Sold-Out Dolls

Baby Chloe and Baby Summer sold through since the audit — proof the CRO changes are working. Build a restock plan and consider adding an email capture ("Notify me when back in stock") to sold-out pages alongside the pre-order option. Every sold-out page is a warm lead list waiting to be built.
