Good to know

Questions moms ask

How quickly will my baby arrive?
In-stock babies ship in 2-4 business days, with free delivery anywhere in South Africa. Pre-order babies are made to order and arrive in 10-14 business days.
What age are the dolls suitable for?
Our babies are perfect for little ones aged 3 and up, and they are loved by collectors too.
Can she go in the bath?
Babies made from full vinyl are waterproof and can join bath time. Each baby's page says whether her listing describes her as waterproof. If it doesn't, keep her dry and wipe her clean, or WhatsApp us to check.
How big is she?
Our standard babies measure about 47-49cm and weigh around 0.9-1kg. Each baby's page shows her size where it has been measured.
Is she ready to give as a gift?
Yes. Every baby arrives beautifully gift-boxed with her accessories, ready to give.
What if something isn't right?
You're covered by our 14-day returns policy. You can also WhatsApp us on 072 236 5134 and a real person will help you.
