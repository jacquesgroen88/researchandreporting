Overview

Introduction

Wiesenhof's brand strength is built on consistency across every franchise location. However, customer feedback and online reputation are often fragmented across multiple platforms and individual store profiles — making it difficult for head office to maintain full visibility and control over brand perception.

We propose the development of a centralised Franchise Reputation Intelligence System — a custom web-based application that provides Wiesenhof with a single, unified view of customer feedback, ratings, and performance across all franchise locations.
