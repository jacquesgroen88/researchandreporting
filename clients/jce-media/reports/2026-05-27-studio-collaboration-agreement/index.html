Parties to This Agreement

Lead Partner

Jacques Groenewald, trading as JCE Media ("the Lead Partner"), brings existing client relationships, the ReviewTap device and service infrastructure, an active and funded sales pipeline, brand, and strategic direction to this collaboration. ReviewTap's existing subscriber base is the primary lead source for studio upsell opportunities — these are warm, qualified relationships built and maintained at the Lead Partner's cost before this agreement began. Ongoing operational spend through JCE Media and ReviewTap (tooling, infrastructure, client management) constitutes a recognised capital contribution by the Lead Partner to this studio from inception. Technical Partners benefit materially from this pre-existing foundation.

Technical Partners

Samuel Rencken and Giorgio van Rooyen ("the Technical Partners") bring digital product development skills including web application development, SaaS tooling, AI-powered builds, content creation, and sales support.

Each party enters this agreement as an independent contractor. Nothing herein creates an employment, agency, or joint-venture relationship unless explicitly upgraded by a separate Shareholders Agreement signed by all parties.

02

Purpose of the Collaboration

The parties agree to collaborate on three complementary revenue streams, all anchored to the existing ReviewTap client base:

Every system, tool, and automation the studio builds is first deployed and proven inside the Lead Partner's own businesses — JCE Media and ReviewTap — before being packaged and sold to clients. This internal-first model means every studio product has been validated in a live commercial environment. Once proven, these systems become resellable, scalable assets that can be offered across the full ReviewTap client base and beyond.

Equity Pathway — Phase 2

Crystallisation Trigger

Phase 2 is triggered when the studio achieves combined net revenue of R90,000 or more per month for three consecutive months. Once triggered, a Shareholders Agreement must be executed within 60 days.

How the TP Equity Pool is Divided

The combined Technical Partner equity pool is fixed at 40% of the studio. JCE Media (Jacques Groenewald) holds the remaining 60% permanently and unconditionally. Within the 40% pool, Samuel's and Giorgio's individual equity percentages are not split equally by default — they are determined by their relative contribution scores calculated at the Phase 2 crystallisation date.

Formula: Partner Equity % = (Partner's Contribution Score ÷ Combined TP Contribution Score) × 40%

Example: If Samuel's contribution score is 700 and Giorgio's is 300 at crystallisation, Samuel receives 28% and Giorgio receives 12% — still totalling 40% between them. The Lead Partner's 60% is unaffected regardless of how the pool divides. Contribution scores are calculated and locked at the Phase 2 trigger date.

13

Exit Terms

Voluntary Exit

Any party may exit this agreement on 30 days written notice. Upon exit:

  • All studio IP remains with the Lead Partner / studio entity
  • Revenue share ceases at end of notice period
  • Sales pipeline built by the exiting partner transfers to the studio at no additional cost
  • Unvested equity (if in Phase 2) is forfeited
  • Vested equity (if in Phase 2) is subject to a buy-back at fair value, negotiated within 60 days
Early Exit — Before Month 12

A Technical Partner exiting before Month 12 forfeits all equity entitlement and receives only revenue share earned to the exit date. No goodwill payment or additional compensation is owed.

Wind-Down Clause

If the Lead Partner terminates the collaboration (not for cause) before the Month 12 review, each Technical Partner receives a one-time payment equivalent to 3 months of their average monthly revenue share as a goodwill settlement.

Termination for Cause

The Lead Partner may terminate a Technical Partner's involvement immediately — without notice or payment — for material breach of this agreement, client solicitation, IP theft, or conduct that materially damages the studio or its clients. Where the breach is capable of remedy, the Lead Partner will provide written notice specifying the breach and allow 10 business days for remedy before termination takes effect. All equity entitlement is forfeited on termination for cause.

Single Technical Partner Exit

If one Technical Partner exits while the other remains active, the following applies:

  • The remaining Technical Partner's revenue share and equity path continue unchanged — their equity is determined solely by their own contribution score relative to the full TP pool at Phase 2 crystallisation
  • The departing partner's unvested equity entitlement returns to the Lead Partner's pool — it does not automatically transfer to the remaining Technical Partner
  • Any vested equity held by the departing partner is subject to the buy-back clause in this section — fair value negotiated within 60 days, with CA(SA) valuation as fallback
  • The Lead Partner may recruit a replacement Technical Partner via a separate written addendum; that partner's equity entitlement is carved from the available pool by agreement at that time
  • The remaining Technical Partner is not automatically required to absorb the departing partner's quotas in full; revised targets must be agreed in writing within 30 days of the exit date
  • All other exit obligations — IP transfer, non-solicitation, confidentiality — apply to the departing partner in the same manner as a standard voluntary exit
14

12-Month Review

At the 12-month mark all parties conduct a formal review covering:

  • Studio revenue vs the R90,000/month Phase 2 threshold
  • Actual contribution inputs per partner (time, IP, revenue) vs projected
  • Code of conduct compliance and minimum involvement standards
  • Whether to proceed to Phase 2, amend terms, or wind down

If the revenue threshold has not been reached and there is no credible path to it within a further 6 months, the collaboration will be wound down by mutual consent. All earned revenue share is paid in full. IP remains with the Lead Partner.

Independent Contractor Status & Tax

Contractor Status

Each Technical Partner is an independent contractor. Nothing in this agreement creates an employment relationship between the Technical Partners and JCE Media, and the studio has no obligation to deduct PAYE, UIF, or SDL on revenue share payments — provided each Technical Partner operates in a manner consistent with independent contractor status under South African law and SARS guidelines.

If SARS determines that a Technical Partner qualifies as a "personal service provider" under the Fourth Schedule of the Income Tax Act No. 58 of 1962 — for example, where substantially all of their income derives from services rendered to this studio — JCE Media reserves the right to withhold employees' tax at the applicable rate from revenue share payments without prior notice. Technical Partners are strongly advised to seek independent tax counsel to assess their personal service provider exposure and to consider operating through a registered company (Pty Ltd) to reduce this risk.

Invoicing

Revenue share payments are made only against a valid tax invoice issued by each Technical Partner (or their registered entity) by the 3rd business day of each month for the preceding month's earnings. JCE Media will provide each Technical Partner with a monthly earnings statement by the 1st business day of each month to facilitate invoicing. No payment will be released without a valid invoice on file.

Provisional Tax

Each Technical Partner is solely responsible for registering with SARS as a provisional taxpayer (IRP6) and submitting provisional tax returns twice annually in accordance with SARS deadlines (typically August and February). JCE Media accepts no responsibility for any penalties, interest, or assessments arising from a Technical Partner's failure to meet their own SARS obligations.

Value-Added Tax (VAT)

Each Technical Partner must monitor their cumulative annual turnover from all sources. Where turnover exceeds R1,000,000 in any 12-month period, VAT registration with SARS is compulsory under the Value-Added Tax Act No. 89 of 1991. Each Technical Partner is solely responsible for their own VAT registration, returns, and remittances. Where a Technical Partner is a registered VAT vendor and charges VAT on invoices issued to the studio, JCE Media will pay the VAT component in addition to the revenue share amount, provided a valid tax invoice is supplied.

Annual Earnings Certificates

JCE Media will provide each Technical Partner with a written summary of total payments made during the tax year by 30 April following each financial year end. This summary supports each Technical Partner's personal income tax filing but does not constitute a formal IRP5 or IT3(a) certificate unless the studio is required by law to issue one.

Tax Indemnity

Each Technical Partner indemnifies JCE Media and the studio against any SARS assessments, penalties, interest, or third-party claims arising directly from that Technical Partner's failure to meet their own tax obligations as an independent contractor. This indemnity survives termination of this agreement.
