01

Purpose of This Addendum

This addendum forms part of, and supplements, the Studio Collaboration Agreement between Jacques Groenewald (JCE Media) and Giorgio van Rooyen. It defines Giorgio's specific responsibilities within the sales and operations function of JCE Media — activities that fall outside the standard Technical Partner scope and which carry their own compensation structure.

All terms of the Studio Collaboration Agreement continue to apply in full. Where this addendum conflicts with the parent agreement, this addendum governs for the specific responsibilities described herein.

This addendum does not alter Samuel Rencken's rights or obligations under the Studio Collaboration Agreement. Giorgio's compensation under this addendum is separate from and in addition to his revenue share as a Technical Partner.

02

Sales Support Responsibilities

Giorgio will actively support the sales pipeline and customer acquisition function for JCE Media. Compensation for this role is contribution and results-based — tied to the value delivered, not hours spent. Sales activities include:

📞
Inbound & Recovery
Abandoned Carts & EFT Pending Payments

Giorgio phones and follows up on customers who have initiated a purchase or order but not completed payment — including abandoned checkout processes and outstanding EFT (Electronic Funds Transfer) payments. Objective is to recover these transactions and convert them to completed sales.

  • Phone outreach within 24 hours of a flagged abandoned cart or pending EFT
  • Follow a scripted conversation flow agreed with the Lead Partner
  • Log outcome in ReviewTap CRM immediately after each call
  • Escalate disputes or payment queries to the Lead Partner within the same business day
🔍
Lead Handling
New Enquiries

Giorgio is the first point of contact for new inbound enquiries received through JCE Media's and ReviewTap's channels. This includes web enquiries, WhatsApp messages, and referral introductions.

  • Respond to all new enquiries within 4 business hours
  • Qualify the lead against the agreed ICP (Ideal Customer Profile) criteria
  • Book qualified leads into the Lead Partner's calendar for a discovery call
  • Log all enquiries in ReviewTap CRM with qualification status and next action
  • Handle basic pricing and package queries; escalate complex commercial questions to the Lead Partner
🎨
Client Approvals
Mockup Approvals

Giorgio manages the mockup and design approval cycle with clients — sending mockups, following up for feedback, capturing revisions, and obtaining written sign-off before production begins.

  • Send mockups to clients within the agreed delivery SLA
  • Follow up on outstanding approvals after 48 hours of no response
  • Capture and communicate client revisions clearly to the production team
  • Obtain written (email or message) approval before any job goes to print or live
  • Log approval status in ReviewTap CRM against each job
03

Operations Responsibilities

🧾
Admin & Finance
Invoice Creation

Giorgio assists with the creation and sending of client invoices using JCE Media's agreed invoicing system. This includes generating invoices from approved quotes or completed jobs, verifying amounts and line items, and ensuring invoices reach clients promptly.

  • Create invoices from approved quotes or job cards — no invoice to be raised without client approval on file
  • Verify line items, amounts, and VAT treatment before sending
  • Send invoices to clients within 24 hours of job completion or monthly billing date
  • Follow up on overdue invoices at the 7-day and 14-day marks
  • Report outstanding debtors to the Lead Partner weekly

Giorgio does not have authority to amend pricing, grant discounts, or issue credit notes without explicit approval from the Lead Partner.

⭐
Social Proof
Testimonial Collection

Giorgio is responsible for collecting at least one verified client testimonial per month. A testimonial may take the form of a written review, a WhatsApp voice note, a short video clip, or a recorded screen-share — whatever the client is comfortable providing. The content must reference JCE Media, ReviewTap, or a specific product or service by name and be usable in marketing materials.

  • Identify satisfied clients from fulfilled orders, approved mockups, or resolved enquiries as the primary source pool
  • Request the testimonial personally — via phone, WhatsApp, or in person during a delivery or pickup
  • Collect the testimonial in a usable format (written, audio, or video) and submit it to the Lead Partner
  • Log the testimonial in ReviewTap CRM against the relevant client record
  • Minimum 1 verified testimonial per calendar month — this is a standing requirement, not optional

First testimonial: required, unpaid. The first testimonial Giorgio collects under this addendum is a mandatory baseline — no bonus applies. From the second testimonial onward, the R500 bonus structure applies, subject to the monthly minimum being met first.

04

Fulfilment Responsibilities

Giorgio assists with the physical fulfilment side of JCE Media's product operations. This includes collecting printed materials from production partners and assisting with the preparation and delivery of client orders.

🖨️
Collection
Printer Pickups

Giorgio collects printed units from JCE Media's print suppliers when instructed to do so. Collection trips are scheduled by the Lead Partner and Giorgio is notified in advance.

  • Collect printed units from the designated printer(s) as scheduled
  • Verify quantity and quality of the print run before leaving the printer — flag any defects immediately
  • Deliver collected units to the agreed staging or despatch location
  • Confirm collection in writing (WhatsApp or email) to the Lead Partner upon pickup
📦
Delivery
Fulfilment Assistance

Giorgio assists in the packing, preparation, and physical delivery of client orders where required. Fulfilment activities are scheduled and coordinated by the Lead Partner.

  • Pack and prepare orders according to the job specification provided
  • Deliver orders to clients or collection points as scheduled
  • Obtain proof of delivery (signature, photo, or message confirmation) for every delivery
  • Report any delivery issues, damages, or client complaints immediately to the Lead Partner
  • Submit a weekly fulfilment log (hours worked + km driven) by close of business each Friday
05

Compensation Structure

Giorgio's compensation under this addendum is structured across four layers: a guaranteed monthly base retainer, results-based recovery commissions, an operational hourly rate for fulfilment work, and a mileage reimbursement for kilometres driven on authorised fulfilment trips.

Base Retainer

Giorgio receives a guaranteed base retainer of R1,500 per month for being available and responsive as the operations first-responder — covering enquiry handling, mockup approval management, and invoicing support. This retainer is paid regardless of activity volume and is not conditional on results. It is paid within 15 days of month end against a valid tax invoice.

The base retainer does not reduce or offset results-based commissions, fulfilment rates, or mileage reimbursements earned in the same month — all compensation components are additive.

Recovery Commission

Recovery compensation is results-based — Giorgio earns on the value he actually recovers, not on call time.

Abandoned Cart & EFT Recovery
10% of the recovered transaction value for each abandoned cart or pending EFT payment that Giorgio personally followed up on and converted to a completed payment. Applies to both checkout abandonment and outstanding EFT transactions. Recovery is attributed to Giorgio in ReviewTap CRM at the time payment is confirmed.
New Enquiry Conversion
Covered under the 7.5% closed-deal bonus in the parent Studio Collaboration Agreement — applies where Giorgio sources, qualifies, or nurtures a lead to close, as attributed in ReviewTap CRM. (How attribution is handled when Giorgio qualifies and the Lead Partner closes is to be agreed separately.)
Mockup Approvals & Invoicing
Covered by the monthly base retainer and Giorgio's broader Technical Partner contribution score. No additional per-task fee applies.
Testimonial Bonus
R500 per verified testimonial collected, subject to the following conditions: (1) the first testimonial ever collected is unpaid — it is a mandatory baseline requirement; (2) from the second testimonial onward, the R500 bonus applies per testimonial; (3) the monthly minimum of 1 testimonial must be met before any bonus is paid — the bonus is not paid if the minimum was missed in that calendar month; (4) testimonial must be verified and approved by the Lead Partner before bonus is processed.
Payment Timing
Recovery commissions calculated monthly and paid within 15 days of month end against a valid tax invoice from Giorgio.

06

Reporting & Accountability

Reporting is kept lean — two progress updates per week and one monthly invoice.

Progress Updates
2 updates per week (e.g. Monday and Thursday) covering: enquiries handled, carts and EFTs followed up, approvals status, invoices sent, and any fulfilment trips or hours logged that week. Shared via the agreed channel (WhatsApp, Notion, or equivalent). No formal template required — a clear, brief summary is sufficient.
Monthly Invoice
One consolidated tax invoice submitted by the 3rd business day of each month covering: base retainer + recovery commissions + fulfilment hours + mileage. Lead Partner provides a monthly earnings summary by the 1st business day to facilitate this.
Escalation
Any client complaints, payment disputes, print defects, or delivery failures reported to the Lead Partner within the same business day — not held for the next progress update.

Failure to submit the monthly invoice on time may result in payment being deferred to the following month's cycle. The Lead Partner is not obligated to chase overdue invoices.

07

General Terms

Independent Contractor

Giorgio performs all activities under this addendum as an independent contractor. JCE Media is not responsible for PAYE, UIF, SDL, or any other employment-related deductions on payments made under this addendum. All tax obligations remain Giorgio's sole responsibility, consistent with Section 16 of the parent Studio Collaboration Agreement.

Authority Limits

Giorgio does not have authority to commit JCE Media to any contractual, financial, or operational obligations on behalf of the business. Specifically:

  • Giorgio may not offer discounts, credit notes, or pricing variations without written approval from the Lead Partner
  • Giorgio may not make representations about delivery timelines, product capabilities, or service terms beyond what has been pre-approved
  • Giorgio may not authorise print runs, production orders, or supplier commitments
  • All commitments to clients must be confirmed by the Lead Partner before being communicated
Amendments

This addendum may be amended by written agreement between Jacques Groenewald and Giorgio van Rooyen without requiring Samuel Rencken's consent, provided the amendment does not alter the terms of the parent Studio Collaboration Agreement.

Termination

Either party may terminate this addendum on 14 days written notice without affecting Giorgio's standing as a Technical Partner under the parent Studio Collaboration Agreement. Termination of the parent agreement automatically terminates this addendum. All accrued, unpaid compensation under this addendum is paid in full within 30 days of termination.
