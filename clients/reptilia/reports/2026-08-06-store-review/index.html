What we need from you

1

How should live animals be sold online?This week

This is the big one and it has not changed. Right now every live animal on the site can be bought online by anyone, paid for by card, with a R160 courier fee applied automatically. We tested it: a R7,000 ball python goes through to payment like any other product.

We do not think that is what you intended, and you did ask us back in June to look at removing livestock. Before we change anything we want your call, because there is more than one sensible answer.

Options, any of these work
  • Enquiry only. Animals stay visible with photos and prices, but the button becomes "Enquire" and it comes to you to handle personally.
  • Collection in store only. Buyable online, but no courier option. The customer pays and collects from Vanderbijlpark.
  • Fully orderable, with a permit and transport notice on each animal and a separate livestock delivery rate that reflects what live transport actually costs.
2

Is Reptilia VAT registered?This week

We are asking because of something on your own price list. The header carries a tax number, 4620299232, and the list is titled "Price List - Inclusive Price", which reads as prices that already include VAT.

The website is currently charging no VAT at all on any order. If that number is a live VAT registration, then every order taken since launch has been recorded as if there were no VAT in the price, when there is.

That is an accounting question rather than a website one, so we would rather you and your bookkeeper confirm it than us guess. If you are registered, we will configure it properly. If that number is an income tax reference rather than a VAT number, then nothing needs doing and we will stop worrying about it.

How often can you send that price list, and what system is it from?

Two practical questions that decide how we build the rest.

How often can you send it? If it is something you can print weekly or monthly, we will build the website to take it properly, so stock levels and prices update in one go instead of by hand. If it is a once-off, we will approach it differently.

What system does it come out of? If your POS can connect directly to the website, stock and prices could update automatically and neither of us would have to think about it again. Even just the name of the software would let us check.

5

Some page content, and one quick question

Several pages on the site are still showing placeholder text because we do not have the wording from you. We need:

Your returns policy. What you already do in store, and whether it works differently for live animals versus dry goods. South African law gives online buyers a seven day cooling off right, so this one does need to be published.
Shipping terms, once you have told us your courier costs.
A short About Us, a few paragraphs on the shop in your own words.
Care sheets, if you have any written already. This is the kind of content that brings people to a reptile shop's website from Google, so it is worth doing properly at some point even if not right now.

And the quick one: are you open on Fridays? The contact page lists Monday to Thursday, then Saturday and Sunday. Friday is missing, so a customer cannot tell whether you are trading.

One thing we no longer need to ask: your price list shows info@reptilia.co.za already exists, so we will switch the site over from the Gmail address.
