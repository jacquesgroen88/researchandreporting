Reptilia Online Store

Your store is live

A few decisions now need to come from you before we go further. Six questions, most of them quick.

Where things stand

reptilia.co.za is live and taking orders. Card payments through PayFast are working, the site is running clean with no errors, and it displays properly on phones. All 926 products are up, and stock levels are showing on the product pages.

We are working through a technical punch list on our side in the background, so nothing below is something you need to action yourself. These are the calls that only you can make, because they depend on how the business actually runs.

The one that matters most

1

How should live animals be sold online?This week

Right now all 132 live animals on the site can be bought online by anyone, paid for by card, with a R160 courier fee applied automatically. We tested it: a R7,000 ball python goes through to payment like any other product.

We do not think that is what you intended, and you did ask us back in June to look at removing livestock. Before we change anything we want your call, because there are good arguments for more than one approach and it depends on how you actually want to handle these sales.

Options, any of these work
  • Enquiry only. Animals stay visible with photos and prices, but the button becomes "Enquire" and the enquiry lands with you to handle personally.
  • Collection in store only. Buyable online, but no courier option. The customer pays and collects from Vanderbijlpark.
  • Fully orderable, with a permit and transport notice on each animal and a separate livestock delivery rate that reflects what live transport actually costs.

Related to this: 71 of the 132 animals have no description at all on their page. A R7,000 python is currently just a photo, a price and a buy button. Whichever route you pick, those pages need words.

Questions we need answered to finish the build

2

Where does the product list actually come from?

This is the one blocking the biggest chunk of remaining work. Around 299 products have no description, 22 have no photo, and roughly 100 have raw stock codes showing in the product name where a proper name should be, things like "Pet Toy 616442".

We can fix all of it. What we need to know first is whether the products come out of your POS system on an ongoing basis, or whether they were loaded once from a spreadsheet. If the POS pushes updates, anything we type by hand gets wiped the next time it syncs, and we would be doing the work twice.

Once you confirm this, the whole catalogue clean-up and the category tidy-up you walked us through in July can start properly.

3

What is your returns policy?

The site has shipping, terms and privacy pages, but nothing published on returns or refunds. South African law gives online buyers a seven day cooling off right on distance sales, so this needs to be on the site.

Tell us what you already do in store, and whether it works differently for live animals versus dry goods, and we will write it up and publish it.

4

What does delivery actually cost you?

Everything currently ships at a flat R160, whether it is a R35 pack of artificial vines or a R7,000 order. On small orders that fee is bigger than the product, which is one of the most common reasons people abandon a basket.

Give us your real courier costs and we will set up sensible rates: free delivery over a certain order value, banded rates below that, and collection in store made more visible for local customers.

5

Is Reptilia VAT registered?

The store is currently charging no VAT on any order. If Reptilia is registered, that needs configuring before this runs much longer. If not, no action needed, we just want it confirmed rather than assumed.

6

Two quick ones

  • Are you open on Fridays? The contact page lists Monday to Thursday, then Saturday and Sunday. Friday is missing, so a customer cannot tell whether you are trading.
  • Do you want a proper Reptilia email address? The site currently lists a Gmail address. Now that you own reptilia.co.za we can set up something like info@reptilia.co.za, which reads better to a customer spending R7,000.

What happens next

Question 1 is the one we would like to settle this week, because the store is live and selling while it stays open. Question 2 unlocks the rest of the catalogue work. The other four can come whenever suits you. A voice note on WhatsApp is perfectly fine, no need to write anything up.
