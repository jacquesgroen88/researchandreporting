Website report for Willem

Recovery after the new site launch, and what comes next

Prepared 6 September 2026  |  Covers 5 August to 4 September  |  reptilia.co.za

The short version: moving to the new website cost us Google visibility, which is normal for a site migration, and it was made worse because the old site was offline for two and a half weeks before the new one went live. Google search traffic dropped to about a third of what it was.

Over the past month we rebuilt the connections between the old site's Google listings and the new site. The recovery is now clearly under way: search visits are up 71% from the low point, and the site's Google rankings are back at pre-migration levels. The plan below finishes the job and then pushes past where the old site ever was.

Work completed

What we did to get the recovery moving

  • 590 redirects from old product pages to the new site. Every old product listing Google still holds now lands on the right new page instead of an error.
  • A bridge for the old site's link format. The old site used a different web address style. Google's entire memory of the old site now flows through to the new pages. This was the single biggest fix.
  • Sitemap submitted to Google and the robots file rebuilt. Google now has a proper map of the new site and re-reads it faster.
  • Care sheet downloads reconnected (6 Sep). The old care sheet PDFs (Ball Python, Leopard Gecko and eleven more) were getting roughly 2,000 Google appearances a month and all pointed to dead links. They now land on the right care sheets again.
  • Site search fixed. 41% of product searches on the site returned nothing (for example "snakes" or "lizard" found zero products). That is now down to 14%, and what remains is mostly products not yet loaded.
  • Site speed: every page is now 75 to 80% lighter. Compression and browser caching switched on, and the logo file cut by 94%. Pages arrive much faster, especially on mobile data.
  • Navigation rebuilt. The menu now has a full Shop dropdown with every category, the footer carries proper link columns, and the broken layout on tablets (the big empty gaps and squeezed footer) is fixed.

Coming up

The plan from here

  • Reconnect the last group of old product links. About 19 old product pages still point nowhere. They were worth roughly 830 Google visits a month, we are recovering their destinations from the old site's database.
  • Permanent animal pages for the animals you always stock. Searches like "hamster for sale" and "ferret for sale" used to bring steady traffic to the old site. Ongoing pages for these animals (rather than one-off listings that disappear when sold) win those searches back for good.
  • Proper sales tracking in analytics. The new site currently reports visits but not sales. Once tracking is in, we can report exactly what search traffic is worth in rand, not just in visits.
  • Product descriptions and catalogue growth. Most products have almost no description, and a large part of the full range is not on the site yet. Every product added and described properly is another page Google can send customers to. This is the phase that takes the site past where the old one ever was.
Timing expectation: at the current pace, search traffic should be back around pre-migration levels within 4 to 6 weeks. The items above are what carry it beyond that.
