Reptilia · Post-Launch Site Audit

What to fix, in what order

Site went live 5 August. Audited 6 August against the live production store. This is the build list, ordered by what you can start today versus what is waiting on Willem.

Launch worked

Both blockers from the July staging audit are dead. PayFast is live and charging, the production domain resolves, and the redirects are correct. Mobile carousel clipping is fixed, amphibians are gone, and stock levels now display. Zero console errors, zero broken images, no mixed content. That is a real result and it is worth saying out loud before the list below.

Three things need attention now, and only one of them is a big job.

Do this week · nothing here is blocked

1

Take the staging site down

The old 10Web staging site is still live, still public, and still serving a complete copy of all 926 products. It canonicals to itself and publishes its own sitemap, so Google can index both stores and split the ranking signal between them. This is the single fastest thing on this list to fix.

While you are in there: production is serving a placeholder robots.txt, the commented example template, with no sitemap line. Staging has the correct one. They are the wrong way round.

Evidencesquare-horse.10web.cloud returns 200 with X-WP-Total: 926, no noindex, no X-Robots-Tag, canonical set to itself. Production /robots.txt contains only a bare Disallow: and no Sitemap: line, though /wp-sitemap.xml does exist and works.

2

Install analytics. The site is currently measuring nothing

There is no GA4, no Tag Manager, no Meta pixel and no conversion tracking anywhere on the site. Every visitor since launch is invisible: no traffic baseline, no idea where anyone came from, no funnel, no cart-abandon signal. When Willem asks how launch went, there is currently no answer.

This is the cheapest fix on the list and the most expensive one to leave, because the data we do not collect this week is gone permanently.

EvidenceChecked in the live rendered page: gtag, dataLayer, fbq and ga are all undefined. The only script host on the page is reptilia.co.za itself.
DianeThis week~half a day
3

Fix the Contact Us page

Four separate problems on one page, and it is the page that turns a browser into a lead:

No enquiry form. The only form on the page is the theme search box. The sole contact route is a mailto link. Because Reptilia is on the Reviewtap System, that means no lead capture into the CRM from the store's own contact page.
The map renders as an empty box. The iframe ships with an empty src and the lazy-loader never swaps in the real Google Maps URL, even after scrolling it into view. Two other lazy elements are stuck the same way.
Friday is missing from the opening hours. The page lists Monday to Thursday, Saturday and Sunday. A shopper cannot tell if the store opens on a Friday.
The contact address is a Gmail account, not an @reptilia.co.za address, on a business that now owns the domain.

4

Shipping: R160 flat on everything, no free-shipping threshold

Tested at both ends of the range. A R35 cart quotes R160 shipping, which is 457% of the item value. A R7,035 cart also quotes R160. Local pickup at R0.00 exists but is not the selected default, on a store with a physical Vanderbijlpark shopfront where a good share of buyers would collect.

We have watched this exact pattern kill a checkout before. A fixed courier fee that dwarfs small baskets is one of the most reliable ways to lose an order.

SuggestedFree shipping over roughly R750 to R1,000, value or weight banded rates below that, and consider making local pickup the default. Needs Willem to confirm real courier costs first.
DianeNeeds courier costs
5

Metadata and sharing basics

The homepage has no meta description, no favicon at all, and no Open Graph or Twitter tags. That last one matters most here: every time someone shares the store on WhatsApp or Facebook it renders as a bare link with no image and no title. For a South African retail business where WhatsApp sharing is the main organic spread, that is a real loss and a quick win.

Two smaller items on the same pass: there are two H1s on the homepage, and the page title carries no geography. Nothing on the site targets "reptile shop Vanderbijlpark" style intent.

Diane~2 hours
6

Two housekeeping items

Two competing shop pages are both live and indexable. /shop-all-now/ at 149KB with no H1, and /shop-all/ at 180KB with an H1. Pick one and 301 the other. This was in the July audit too.

The users sitemap is public. /wp-sitemap-users-1.xml exposes /author/dianec/, which publishes a valid WordPress username for brute-force targeting and puts your name on the client's store. Disable the users sitemap.

Live animals are freely orderable, and this is the one to raise first

All 132 livestock products are purchasable right now, with a combined shelf value of about R199,620. I verified it end to end: adding a R7,000 ball python to a guest cart returns a R7,195 total with a R160 flat courier rate selected by default and PayFast ready to charge the card.

71 of the 132 have no description at all. The R7,000 python page is one image, a price, a quantity box and an Add to cart button. Only 2 of the 132 mention a permit, collection or transport condition anywhere.

Willem asked for livestock removal back in his 30 June feedback. It is now not just still listed, it is fully transactable on a live domain with a live payment gateway. Whatever the commercial intention was, a flat courier rate applied to a live snake is the wrong default, and this is a decision only he can make.

The options to put to himEnquiry only, collection in store only, or fully orderable with a permit notice and a livestock-specific shipping class. Any of the three is defensible. Leaving it as it is, is not.

8

No returns or refunds policy is published

Shipping policy, terms and privacy pages all exist and load. Returns and refunds both 404. South African online sales fall under the ECT Act and the Consumer Protection Act, which give buyers a seven-day cooling-off right on distance sales. A store selling live animals with no published returns position is carrying avoidable risk.

Ask Willem what the in-store policy actually is, and whether it differs for livestock versus dry goods, then publish it.

Speed

Worth being precise about where this sits. The 10Web optimizer plugin is still installed and its headers confirm it is serving these pages from cache, but hosting has moved to plain Apache on PHP 8.1. Questions to put to Willem

  1. Should live animals be orderable online at all? If yes, on what terms: collection only, permit confirmation, deposit? This gates the biggest item on the list.
  2. Is the catalogue POS driven or a one-off CSV? Still unanswered since 30 June. The entire clean-up waits on this.
  3. What is the actual returns policy, and does it differ for livestock versus dry goods?
  4. Is the store VAT registered? Cart totals return R0.00 tax on a R7,195 basket. If Reptilia is registered, tax is not configured in WooCommerce.
  5. Is Friday a trading day? It is missing from the published hours.
  6. Should the contact email move to @reptilia.co.za now the domain is live?

What I could not check

  • No order was placed. The payment finding is read from the cart API, not from a completed PayFast transaction. Someone should put through one real low-value test order and confirm the money lands and the order email fires.
  • Admin side. I only had the public front end and Store API. Analytics could in theory be configured in WP admin but not firing. Worth confirming from inside.
  • Whether staging is actually indexed in Google, as opposed to merely crawlable and duplicated. Check Search Console for both properties.
  • Whether the missing images and descriptions are an import artefact or genuine gaps. That is the POS question again.
  • Whether R160 is deliberate courier pricing or another placeholder like the old R15 was.
