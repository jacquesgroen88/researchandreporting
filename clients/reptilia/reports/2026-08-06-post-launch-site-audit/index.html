Launch worked, and the catalogue picture just changed

Both blockers from the July staging audit are dead. PayFast is live and charging, the production domain resolves, and the redirects are correct. Mobile carousel clipping is fixed, amphibians are gone. No console errors, no broken images. That is a real result.

And a closer look at the content pages found something the first pass missed. Both are below.

8

Shipping: R160 flat on everything, no free-shipping threshold

A R35 cart quotes R160 shipping, which is 457% of the item value. A R7,035 cart also quotes R160. Local pickup at R0.00 exists but is not the selected default, on a store with a physical Vanderbijlpark shopfront where plenty of buyers would collect.

We have watched this exact pattern kill a checkout before. A fixed courier fee that dwarfs small baskets is one of the most reliable ways to lose an order.

SuggestedFree shipping over roughly R750 to R1,000, value or weight banded rates below that, local pickup made the visible default. Needs Willem's real courier costs first.

9

Metadata, sharing and housekeeping

The homepage has no meta description, no favicon, and no Open Graph or Twitter tags. That last one matters most: every WhatsApp and Facebook share renders as a bare link with no image or title, and for a South African retail business WhatsApp sharing is the main organic spread.

Two H1s on the homepage, and the title carries no geography, so nothing targets "reptile shop Vanderbijlpark" intent.

Two competing shop pages are both live and indexable: /shop-all-now/ at 149KB with no H1, and /shop-all/ at 180KB with one. Pick one, 301 the other.

The users sitemap is public and exposes /author/dianec/, publishing a valid WordPress username for brute-force targeting. Disable it.

The POS export changes the catalogue plan

10

The blocker is answered: it is a manual POS export, not a live sync

Willem's file is a legacy Excel .xls, not a CSV, printed by hand on 2 August at 07:50 by "Anemarie". A person pressing print, not an automated feed.

So nothing on the site is being silently overwritten. The risk is different from what we assumed: a wholesale re-import wiping manual work. That is an engineering problem with a known fix, not an open question.

The import ruleMatch on Product Code. Write Category, SOH and Price only. Never touch descriptions, images or SEO fields. Read product codes as text. Exclude Non Website Related and Not Linked.

Full breakdown in 2026-08-06-POS-Export-Analysis.md in the client folder.

13

A pipeline bug that will recur on every import

Five live products have SKUs corrupted into scientific notation: 6.93575E+12 (Infrared Thermometer), 6.00969E+12 (Montego Classic Kitten), 6.0611E+11 (JUAVA Adult Dog), 6.00951E+12 (Daro Hamster Tonic), 6.00107E+12 (Bobtail Gravy).

The POS export itself is clean. Zero scientific-notation codes in the source file. So the corruption happened in the import pipeline, almost certainly by opening the file in Excel and letting it reformat 13-digit barcodes before saving.

This is also the root cause of the two broken R0.00 zero-variation products flagged in both audits. Their barcodes were destroyed, so they matched nothing. Fix the process, not the two products, or it happens again next time.

Duplicate names are inherited. Do not merge them

The site has 17 duplicate-name groups. The POS has 47. These are genuinely different products sharing a description:

Questions to put to Willem

  1. Should live animals be orderable online at all? Collection only, permit confirmation, deposit? This is still the biggest single decision.
  2. Is that a VAT number on his price list? The export header shows Tax Number 4620299232, which is SA VAT format, and says "Price List - Inclusive Price". The site charges R0.00 tax on a R7,195 basket. If he is registered, every order since launch is mis-recorded.
  3. Are the site prices wrong or deliberately discounted? Start with Small Animal Cage, R530 on the site against R2,850 in the POS.
  4. How often does he send this export? This decides whether we build a one-off load or a repeatable process.
  5. What POS system is it, and does it have an API? An API would remove the Excel corruption problem permanently.
  6. What is the actual returns policy, and does it differ for livestock versus dry goods? Plus the content for shipping, terms, privacy and about.
  7. Are Casino and Zooperdoos brands or store sections? They read like brands, but guessing puts 107 products in the wrong place.
  8. Is Friday a trading day? Missing from the published hours.

What I could not check

  • No order was placed. The payment finding is read from the cart API, not a completed PayFast transaction. Someone should put through one real low-value test order and confirm the money lands, the order email fires, and where the customer lands afterwards, given /payment-complete/ is a placeholder.
  • Admin side. Public front end and Store API only. Analytics could in theory be configured but not firing. Worth confirming from inside.
  • Whether staging is actually indexed in Google, as opposed to crawlable and duplicated. Check Search Console for both properties.
  • Whether R160 is deliberate courier pricing or another placeholder like the old R15.
  • Which stock figure is authoritative for livestock, given animals sell in store and online off the same count.
