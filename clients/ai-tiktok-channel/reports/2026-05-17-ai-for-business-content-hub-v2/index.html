Business angle: create lifestyle product images, swap backgrounds for seasonal campaigns, remove unwanted elements.

  • Available in Adobe Photoshop (Firefly tab) and Adobe Express
  • Sketch/rectangle selection — both work

  • Lighting and shadow adaptation is automatic
  • No Photoshop experience required for basic edits

⚡ Algorithm Rules

7 Rules
1

Completion Rate Is Everything

TikTok's #1 signal. Target >70%. If a video hits >70% in the first 4 hours, film a follow-up immediately — the algorithm is already showing it to more people and you can ride the wave.

2

Hook in 3 Seconds or You've Lost Them

The first 3 seconds determine if someone continues watching. Lead with the biggest claim, the most surprising visual, or the clearest promise. Never start a TikTok with "Hey guys" or any setup. Start with the hook — immediately.

3

Saves and Shares Beat Likes

Saves signal "I want to come back to this" — the highest quality engagement signal. Include an explicit "Save this" CTA in every video. Shares signal the algorithm to distribute beyond your current followers.

4

Trend-Jack Within 24–48 Hours

New AI feature drops → post within 24–48 hours. You'll rank in TikTok search for "[tool name] tutorial" before anyone else has posted. After 72 hours, you're competing against established videos. First-mover advantage decays fast.

5

Quality Signals Matter

Blurry videos are downranked 40–60%. Minimum: 1080p, good lighting, clear audio. You don't need studio equipment — a window on a cloudy day + a $30 mic + a clean background beats $5,000 of studio gear in bad lighting.

6

Pattern Interrupts Every 3–5 Seconds

Cut to a different angle, add a text overlay, change the screen recording — anything that prevents the thumb from reaching the swipe. Dead air (>0.4 seconds of silence or static shot) is a completion rate killer. Cut it all in edit.

7

Hashtag Formula: 3–6 Max

Use: 1 broad (#AItools) + 1–2 niche (#AIforBusiness, #ContentCreator) + 1 tool-specific (#CanvaTips). More than 6 hashtags dilutes the signal. Place hashtags in the first 3 lines of your caption for maximum indexing.
