Algorithm Rules

Non-Negotiable
1

Completion Rate >70% is your only early metric

If viewers scroll before finishing your video, the algorithm buries it. Every edit decision (trim dead air, add pattern interrupts, cut anything boring) serves this number. Check it 4 hours after posting.

2

Shares and saves beat likes 3:1

The algorithm weighs saves heavily — they signal "I want to come back to this." Always include a "save this" CTA. Never just ask for likes.

3

Pattern interrupt every 3–5 seconds

Add a text overlay, zoom cut, B-roll clip, or on-screen graphic every few seconds. This resets the viewer's attention and prevents scroll-aways mid-video.

4

Trend-jack within 48 hours or it's too late

When a new AI tool or feature drops, post within 24–48 hours. You'll rank in TikTok search before the competition crowds in. First-mover gets 10x the views.

5

If a video hits >70% completion in 4 hours → film a follow-up immediately

A video riding the algorithm wave lifts your next video too. Film a Part 2 or "more detail" video the same day and post it within a few hours.

6

1080p minimum, good lighting, clear audio — no exceptions

Blurry or re-encoded clips are algorithmically downranked 40–60%. Film in front of a window (natural light) or invest in a cheap ring light. Use your phone's back camera.

7

Stagger cross-posting by 15–30 minutes

TikTok first. Then Instagram Reels (remove watermark first — Instagram downranks TikTok-watermarked content). Then YouTube Shorts with a keyword title.
