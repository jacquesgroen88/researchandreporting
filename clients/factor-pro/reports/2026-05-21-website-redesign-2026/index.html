What We Supply

Electrical & Mechanical Solutions

We supply electrical and mechanical spares and offer engineering advice and project support — making it easy for both Procurement and Floor staff to get the job done.

Cable Management

Full range of cable management solutions for industrial and commercial installations — trays, conduit, cable glands, and accessories from leading brands including Hellermann Tyton and Cabstrut.

⚙️

Electric Motors & Geared Motors

Standard and geared electric motors for every industrial application. We stock a comprehensive range from WEG, Siemens, SEW, Bonfiglioli, and Actom.

Brake motor supply and complete motor repair services. Our on-staff engineers can attend to motor, gearbox, and drive requirements on-site.

Lighting Solutions

Industrial, commercial, and mining lighting from Osram, Philips, Eurolux, Radiant Lighting, and PIOLED — suitable for every environment and lux requirement.
