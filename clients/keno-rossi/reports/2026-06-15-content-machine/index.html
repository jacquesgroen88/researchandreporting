The first 60 minutes

The Golden Hour is Keno's, not ours

Early engagement velocity is what pushes a post from your followers out to strangers. The single biggest lever in that window is Keno replying to his own comments in his own voice. A managed agency reply kills the debate. Keno's reply extends it. So the rule is simple: we do not reply in the first hour, he does. We schedule, we monitor, and then we turn his best replies into the next posts.

0–2 min

Post, then drop the seed question as your own first comment and pin it. (e.g. "Who wins, [Fighter A] or [Fighter B]? Change my mind.")

0–15 min

Reply to every comment, fast. End each reply with a question so they comment again. This is the highest-leverage window of the whole day.

15–30 min

Escalate on the spicy ones. Disagree, challenge, defend your Fight IQ take. Debate is the goal, and only the real you can credibly throw punches back.

30–45 min

Take the best comment and start a reply-with-video. That becomes a brand new post that reaches the commenter's network and yours again. One idea, a second at-bat.

45–60 min

Final sweep. Answer stragglers, re-pin if a better debate comment appeared, nudge a save or a send.

📱 Only post when you can sit on your phone for an hour. The hour you spend in the comments is worth more than the hour spent making the clip. The studio's job in that window is to watch, capture, and feed your replies back into the machine.

Current intelligence

What's Working Right Now (June 2026)

This machine is tuned to the platforms as they behave today, not last year. The signals we engineer every post around:

⏱️

The 60 to 90 second band is rewarded again

After years of favouring 7 to 15s clips, all three platforms now reward a longer watch session. A 45s clip at 70% completion beats a 15s clip at 40%. Edit for completion, not length.

📤

Saves and sends are the new currency

On Reels in 2026, a DM send is weighted 3 to 5× a like, a save about 3×. They beat comments for reach, which is why the CTA mix leans on them.

🔥

Mic'd-up and breakdowns overperform

For combat content, raw mic'd-up training and technique breakdowns get the most rewatches, and rewatch is the strongest quality signal the algorithm reads.

📊

The window for a small account is open

Small accounts (1k to 5k) are currently growing roughly 8× faster than 100k+ accounts. A sub-50k fighter who ships consistently can scale fast right now.

🎤

Sound-first, with original audio as the power play

Build clips to a trending sound on Reels and TikTok, but lead with original raw audio on mic'd-up and breakdown content. The breath and the pad smack are the audio.

🧾

What a hit looks like

Solid = 1 to 3× your follower count in views. Breakout = 5 to 10×+ with 60%+ completion and a visible save or share spike. That is the algorithm expanding you past your own audience.
