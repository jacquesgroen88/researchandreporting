The core idea

Atomisation: one drop → many posts

The machine doesn't need Keno filming constantly. It needs him filming smart — a few rich sessions a week — which we then break into many posts. This is how we hit volume without burning him out.

1 Training session bag / spar / strength
→ up to 5 posts Montage (The Grind)Form Check (technique)POV clipMic'd Up (raw audio)Human moment / blooper
1 Talk-to-camera or sit-down interview
→ 6–8 posts The Why ×2The Climb ×2Fight IQ ×2Ask Keno
1 Run road / trail
→ 2–3 posts POV + candid VO1% Better progress noteMic'd Up thought
1 Fight / event his or one he reacts to
→ 4+ posts Walk-inCorner / aftermathReactionRecap / lessons

⚙️ The same post is then mirrored to TikTok + Reels + Shorts — so 1 cut = 3 platform posts. Plus stills → IG carousels & Stories.
