The platforms

The Stack

Four platforms, four distinct jobs. We don't post different content to each, we produce once and mirror, tuned per platform.

🔴 Primary · the growth engine

TikTok

Where we win the audience first, best organic reach for a cold start, best home for the short-form journey.
Cadence 4–5/wk → dailyAll 5 pillars
  • Hook in 2 to 3 seconds, open on the payoff
  • Keno fields his own comments in the first hour
  • Trending audio on montages, original audio on mic'd-up
  • Ask for the save and the send, not just the like
  • Post 6–7am / 6–8pm SAST + Sunday mornings
✓ Win: first 100k-view post → consistent 5-figure views
🟡 High · the business home

Instagram Reels

Same cuts, slightly more curated. Where brand-deal DMs land and where a polished profile matters.
Cadence Mirror TikTok+ Stories
  • Offset post times a few hours from TikTok
  • Stories for behind-the-scenes / polls
  • Clean grid identity · Highlights = pillars
✓ Win: a profile that converts visitors → followers and looks legit to a sponsor
🟢 Passive · free repurpose

YouTube Shorts

Zero-extra-work incremental reach + seeds a YouTube presence for later.
Cadence Auto-mirrorLow effort
  • Re-post the same verticals, don't overthink it
  • Builds a back-catalogue for long-form's foundation
✓ Win: a growing YouTube base before long-form starts
⚪ Later · depth & real money

YouTube Long-form

Where the deep fans and bigger money live (ad revenue, larger sponsors, "fight vlog" storytelling). Needs an audience to funnel, don't start cold.
Phase 2–3Trigger: short-form traction
  • First long-form = a fight vlog or "road to my next fight" doc
✓ Win: a monetised home base the short-form funnels into

Fresh start

Launch Setup Checklist

New accounts, consistent from post #1, no algorithmic baggage from low-traction history.

  • Pick the handle, identical across all three platforms for consistency.
  • Create new TikTok + IG + YouTube with MONO branding, identical profile pic & bio.
  • Bio = positioning in one line: "Amateur boxer documenting the climb to champion 🥊" + location.
  • Reserve / redirect Keno's old handles; pin a strong intro post.
  • Launch with the 10 banked posts as a 3-week runway.
Discipline

What We're NOT Doing (yet)

  • Spreading across X / Facebook / Threads early, niche concentration beats presence everywhere.
  • Posting different content per platform, one production, mirrored. Efficiency is the moat.
  • Long-form before short-form traction, it needs an audience to funnel.
