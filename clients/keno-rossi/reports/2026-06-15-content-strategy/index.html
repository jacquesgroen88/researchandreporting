The data

Signals — How to Win the Algorithm

2026 short-form is a watch-time game, and it now favours authentic human creators — which is exactly Keno's lane. The rules we'll engineer every post around:

⚡

Hook in 3 seconds

The algorithm measures "intro retention." Top creators keep 70%+ past 3s. Open on the payoff/action — never an intro. A strong hook cuts scroll-offs up to 20%.

⏱️

Watch time > views

2k views at 80% watched beats 10k at 30%. Don't chase length — chase completion. A gripping 40s story can out-rank a 15s clip.

💬

The first 60 minutes

Early engagement is weighted heavily. Be online at post time to reply fast. BOXING IQ takes are built to bait comments — that's the point.

🎯

Niche consistency

Posting across 3+ unrelated topics = ~45% less reach. Stay boxing/fitness. 80% core pillars, 20% experiments.

🔁

Build replay value

Complex moves get rewatched — and rewatches signal quality. Crisp combos, a clean KO-power shot, a satisfying deadlift lockout = loops.

📅

Cadence & timing

1–3 posts/day sweet spot, but consistency > volume. Fitness peaks: 6–7am, 6–8pm, and Sunday mornings. Cross-post TikTok + Reels + Shorts.

🫶

Authenticity is the meta

2026 algorithms down-rank generic AI content and reward real humans. Keno's raw "phone-in-the-car confession" footage isn't a weakness — it's the moat.

📈

Honest transformation

Progress content (60–90s) drives the highest engagement if credible. Document real markers over time — fake arcs get called out in comments.
