## Summary

A deep-dive into the global NFC/QR Google Review product advertising landscape, with a specific focus on identifying South African competitors. Ads were scraped from the Facebook Ad Library using 7 keyword searches across global and ZA-filtered results.

---

## Key Findings

### 🇿🇦 South Africa
- **No active SA competitors detected** as of May 2026 — a clear open window for ReviewTap
- **RoyalTap** (SA-native): Ran 3 video ads June 9–19, 2025 then stopped entirely. Analytics angle. Likely failed to convert.
- **ReviewBoost**: Tested ZA market Aug–Oct 2025, then stopped. Price/shipping disadvantage vs local ReviewTap offering.

### Winning Copy Patterns Identified
1. **Competitor shame stat** — specific numbers create urgency
2. **Emotional validation opener** — "your customers love you" before the pain
3. **5-second time claim** — concrete micro-commitment hook
4. **Broken system reframe** — "it's not you, it's your review system"
5. **Industry target list** — signals relevance to specific verticals
6. **Trust stack** — local credentials + guarantee + delivery speed
