WhatsApp Automation

How WhatsApp gets
built into the tap

WhatsApp is the highest-engagement channel in South Africa. ReviewTap makes it the destination — creating a conversation that starts the moment a customer taps.

1

The NFC card links to a wa.me URL

When programmed, the NFC chip or QR code encodes a special WhatsApp deep link with a pre-written message already loaded. No typing required from the customer.

https://wa.me/27XXXXXXXXX?text=Hi+ReviewTap!+I+just+visited+[Venue+Name]+🎉
2

WhatsApp opens on the customer's phone

The pre-filled message appears in a new WhatsApp chat — pointed at your brand's WhatsApp Business number. The customer taps Send. One tap. That's all they do.

3

The automation fires instantly

The incoming message triggers a Make.com scenario, GHL conversation automation or direct WhatsApp Business API webhook. The bot responds within seconds — no human required.

4

The conversation does the work

Based on the message content and customer replies, the bot routes to the appropriate flow — review request, competition entry, loyalty reward, lead qualification, or content delivery.

5

Data flows into your CRM

Every interaction is logged — phone number, name (if provided), location, timestamp, choice made. This feeds into GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Google Sheets or any connected platform.

🌍
Why WhatsApp in South Africa? WhatsApp has over 23 million active users in SA — a higher penetration rate than email or any other messaging app. Messages sent via WhatsApp have an average open rate of 98% vs. 20–25% for email. It's where your clients' customers actually are.

The Data Layer

Every tap is a
data point

ReviewTap doesn't just collect reviews — it builds a first-party data asset that feeds directly back into your clients' campaigns.

📞

Phone Numbers (Opted-In)

Every WhatsApp interaction captures a mobile number with explicit opt-in. This is first-party, consented contact data — the most valuable asset in modern marketing.

📍

Location & Timestamp

Every tap is tagged to a location and timestamp. Know which stores, events or activation zones are performing — and which need attention.

⭐

Sentiment Scores

Rating data per location, per campaign, over time. Real customer sentiment — not survey panels. Feeds directly into brand health reporting.

🔁

Repeat Visit Frequency

Phone number matching across taps reveals which customers are returning and at what cadence. Identify your best customers automatically.

🎯

Campaign Attribution

Each NFC card or QR code can carry a unique campaign ID. Know which activation, media placement or influencer drove the most engagement — down to the tap.

🤝

CRM & Retargeting Ready

All captured data flows into your CRM of choice. Phone numbers can be used for WhatsApp retargeting, Meta custom audiences, Google Customer Match — closing the attribution loop.
