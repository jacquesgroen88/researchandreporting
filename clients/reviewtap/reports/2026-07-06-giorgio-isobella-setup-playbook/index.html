4

"But do I ever touch GHL?"

Almost never — and never to build.

The only reason you'd open GHL is to look, not build — e.g. Diane shows you where guest conversations land, or you confirm the bot is live before testing. If you're ever staring at a workflow builder or a template editor wondering what to do, stop — that's Diane's screen, not yours. Ping her.

5

The Nightsbridge question (bookings → messages)

Nightsbridge is Ruan's booking system. The messages need to know when a guest arrives or checks out. You just find out how, then hand it to Diane.

Ask Ruan, word for word:
"Ruan, does Nightsbridge email you a confirmation when a booking is made or when a guest checks out — and do those emails land in your inbox?"
✅ If yes, emails work → Email parsing (recommended)

Diane sets it up automatically. No extra work from you. This is the default — aim for this.

🟡 If emails are unreliable → Manual trigger (fallback)

Ruan fills a 30-second form at checkout. Simple, no tech. Fine to launch on this and upgrade later.

🔵 If Ruan wants full automation → Nightsbridge API

Only this path needs you. Contact Nightsbridge as a ReviewTap partner for API credentials: +27 87 943 6035 or info@nightsbridge.com. Takes 1–3 business days, so don't wait on it — launch on email/manual first.
