01 — Context

Market Overview

The digital business card market is growing fast but fragmented. Here's where the industry stands and where ReviewTap fits.

📈
$242M → $600M+
Global digital business card market. Projected CAGR of ~11% through 2030 driven by hybrid work, sustainability mandates, and CRM integration demand.
🏷️
Two buyer types
SMB/Consumer: HiHello, Blinq, Tapni. Cheap or free, self-serve, profile-link focus. Enterprise: Mobilo, V1CE. Per-user pricing, SSO, CRM, admin control panels. ReviewTap sits between both with a physical NFC card as the entry point.
📱
NFC as a differentiator
Popl pioneered the "NFC card + app" bundle. Tapni now includes free NFC cards with Pro plans. Mobilo sells cards at $5–$139. ReviewTap's R649 all-in pricing (card + profile + setup) is competitive but the value story isn't told on the card page.
🤖
AI is the new battlefield
Blinq launched AI-powered contact enrichment and a "notetaker" that takes notes during calls. Popl added AI-assisted OCR for business card scanning. Whoever offers the smartest post-tap workflow will win enterprise accounts.
⚠️
The big shift in 2025: Linq (once NFC-card focused) has completely pivoted to AI messaging APIs. This is a warning sign — standalone digital card platforms that don't build deeper value (analytics, CRM, lead capture) risk becoming commoditised or irrelevant. ReviewTap must build stickiness beyond the physical card.

06 — UX Evaluation

UI / UX Audit: Client Pain Points

Issues a real client would experience from card creation to daily use. Rated by severity and impact.

🔴 Critical
No success screen after card creation
After submitting the intake form, the user is immediately redirected to their public card. There's no confirmation, no "here's your card link", no "here's how to update it later", no QR code to save. The client doesn't know what happened — this is a broken experience that causes instant support requests.
Fix (Phase 5)Show a full success screen with card URL, edit link, copy buttons, WhatsApp share, and a QR code of the card.
🔴 Critical
Edit link is generated but never shown
The edit_token UUID is created server-side and returned in the API response, but IntakeForm.tsx discards it and calls router.push(). Clients have absolutely no way to update their card without contacting admin. This defeats the "self-service" promise and creates support overhead for every single update request.
Fix (Phase 5)Show the edit link prominently on the success screen with a "Bookmark this" prompt and copy button. The token IS the auth — share it once, securely.
🔴 Critical
No card analytics for the owner
Clients have zero visibility into whether anyone has tapped or viewed their card. This is the #1 reason people churn from digital card platforms — the card feels like a dead-end product. Without analytics, there's no reason to come back, no success metric, no "wow this is working" moment. Every competitor offers this.
Fix (Phase 6)Track page views + contact button taps + link clicks in a card_events table. Show a simple stats card on the /edit/[token] page. Even basic numbers create massive engagement uplift.
🟡 High
QR code points to localhost in development
The QR code generated in CardPage.tsx uses window.location.origin which is correct — but the NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL env var is not set on Netlify, so any server-rendered path defaults to localhost. Clients sharing their QR code link to a dead URL. This is a live bug affecting all deployed cards.
Fix (immediate)Set NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL=https://reviewtap.netlify.app on Netlify. Confirm QR codes on all 5 demo cards resolve correctly after deploy.
🟡 High
Links have no visual identity
Custom links display as plain text with a generic external link icon. On Linktree and competitors, links use icons, thumbnails, or emojis to create visual hierarchy. Plain link lists get lower click-through rates because nothing draws the eye to any specific link. A business website link looks identical to a booking link.
Fix (Phase 5)Add optional emoji field per custom link. Show emoji as visual prefix before the label. 16 common emojis in a one-tap picker. Zero infrastructure — stored in existing JSONB field.
🟡 High
"Save Contact" is buried and unclear
The primary call-to-action (saving the contact to phone) is a glassmorphism button that visually competes with the action tiles above it. On mobile, recipients often don't scroll far enough to see it. Industry research shows "Save Contact" is the most failed action on digital card pages — people tap Call or leave instead.
FixTest placing "Save Contact" ABOVE the Call/Email/WhatsApp tiles, or make it sticky at the bottom as a floating pill. It should be the #1 visual action — not the 4th element in a list.
🟡 High
No way to share the card mid-conversation
No mobile app means clients can't pull up their digital card quickly when networking. Competitors let users open their app and show their QR code in 2 taps. With ReviewTap, the user has to navigate to a URL they may not have bookmarked. The NFC chip solves this at close range, but across a table or via messaging requires a saved link.
Fix (Success screen)Make the WhatsApp share and "copy link" the primary actions on the success screen so clients immediately save the link to themselves / their contacts.
🔵 Medium
Intake form has no progress persistence
If a client fills 4 steps of the intake form and then closes the browser (accidentally or due to distraction), all their data is lost. They have to start over. On mobile this is common. Competitors save draft data to localStorage or the user's account automatically.
FixSave form data to localStorage after each step change. Restore on next visit with a "Continue where you left off?" banner. This dramatically reduces incomplete submissions.
🔵 Medium
URL is reviewtap.netlify.app — unprofessional
Clients share their card as reviewtap.netlify.app/cards/your-name. This immediately reads as a demo/prototype rather than a finished product. The custom domain cards.reviewtap.co.za was planned but not yet configured. Netlify provides this for free — this is a Netlify DNS configuration task, not a code change.
Fix (immediate — 10 mins)Configure cards.reviewtap.co.za as a custom domain in Netlify. Add CNAME record in DNS. All existing card URLs auto-redirect.
🔵 Medium
Google Review button not prominently positioned
ReviewTap's most unique feature — the Google Review direct link — is buried in the links list as a generic link item with a gold star. It should be a hero element. Clients buying ReviewTap are often local businesses who live and die by Google reviews. The review CTA should be impossible to miss on the card.
FixGive the Google Review link a dedicated, visually distinct section above the custom links. Star icon, amber colour treatment, larger tap target. Consider a "Leave us a ⭐ review" badge with distinctive styling separate from the links list.
🔵 Medium
Photo upload UX is friction-heavy on mobile
Photo upload uses a standard file input which on mobile shows "Choose File" — not camera-optimised. Competitors open the native camera directly. The drag-to-reposition feature (photo_x/photo_y) also has no visual affordance that it's draggable — users who accidentally skip it get poor photo framing.
FixAdd accept="image/*" capture="user" to the file input to trigger the front camera by default on mobile. Add a visual "drag to reposition" label/indicator over the photo preview.
🟢 Low
No accessibility baseline
Contact buttons rely on icon + label but have no aria-label for screen readers. Colour contrast on subtext (rgba 60% opacity) may fail WCAG AA on light theme. No focus-visible styling on interactive elements means keyboard navigation is broken. Unlikely to be a legal concern now, but worth addressing as the product matures.
Fix (low priority)Add aria-label to all icon-only or icon-led buttons. Audit colour contrast ratios. Add :focus-visible ring styles to all interactive elements.
07 — Gaps

Key Gaps to Close

Ranked by business impact. These are the features that clients notice are missing and that competitors use to win deals.

📊
#1 Analytics
Every single competitor offers card view counts and link click tracking. Without this, clients have no reason to stay engaged. Analytics is the #1 driver of subscription upgrades and renewal intent across the entire category. This is the retention engine.
✏️
#2 Edit Link (self-service)
The edit_token exists but is never shown to the client. This is a completed feature stuck behind a UX bug. Every support request for "can you update my card?" is admin cost. Showing the edit link on the success screen is a 30-minute fix that eliminates this entirely.
🎉
#3 Success Screen
Zero wow moment after card creation. No sharing options, no edit link, no QR. This is the first impression the client has of their finished card — it should be a celebration. HiHello and Blinq nail this with animated success states that immediately drive sharing.
🌐
#4 Custom Domain
cards.reviewtap.co.za is planned but not configured. The Netlify URL reads as a demo. This is a 10-minute DNS + Netlify configuration — no code change needed. Every day without this is a missed opportunity to look professional.
✉️
#5 Email Signature Generator
HiHello and Popl both offer this as a key upsell. An email signature with a "View my digital card" link drives daily impressions and new card views passively. Generates a copyable HTML block — straightforward to build.
🔗
#6 Link Visual Identity
Plain text links get lower engagement. An emoji picker per link (zero DB changes, zero uploads) is the fastest visual upgrade. Already in the Phase 5 plan. Consider also adding link click tracking as part of the analytics feature.
08 — Roadmap

Recommended Priority Roadmap

Ordered by impact-to-effort ratio. Quick wins first, then the features that drive long-term retention.

1
Custom domain — cards.reviewtap.co.za
Configure Netlify custom domain + DNS CNAME record. No code change. Makes every existing and future card instantly look more professional. This is a 10-minute task that affects every client card currently live.
High Impact Effort: 10 mins
2
Success screen + edit link reveal (Phase 5)
Replace the post-creation router.push() with a rich success screen showing: card URL with copy button, edit link with copy button and "keep this safe" notice, WhatsApp share, and a QR code. The edit_token already exists — it just needs to be surfaced. Fixes two critical issues in one change.
High Impact Effort: 2–3 hrs
3
Link emoji picker (Phase 5)
Add optional emoji field to CustomLink type and StepLinks. No DB migration — JSONB stores it automatically. Render emoji prefix in LinksList. One-tap emoji popover with 16 common emojis. Small change, big visual improvement to the card.
High Impact Effort: 2 hrs
4
Card analytics (Phase 6 — most critical retention feature)
Create a card_events table (card_id, event_type, created_at). Track: page_view, save_contact_tap, call_tap, email_tap, whatsapp_tap, link_click. Expose a simple stats view at /edit/[token] or /stats/[token]. Even "142 views this month" creates massive engagement and gives clients a reason to share more. This is the feature that prevents churn.
High Impact Effort: 1–2 days
5
LocalStorage draft save for intake form
Save form state to localStorage after each step. Restore on next visit. Banner: "You have a draft card — continue?" This reduces lost submissions (likely 30–50% on mobile) and creates a better experience for clients interrupted during the setup process.
Medium Impact Effort: 2 hrs
6
Email signature generator
A page at /edit/[token]/signature (or a tab in the edit flow) that generates a copyable HTML email signature block with: name, title, phone, and a "View my card" button linking to their card URL. Drives passive card views from every email sent. HiHello treats this as a top feature.
Medium Impact Effort: 4 hrs
7
Google Review section — dedicated visual treatment
Move the Google Review CTA out of the links list and into its own visually distinct section. Amber/gold badge, star icon, larger tap target, copy like "Leave us a Google Review ⭐". This is ReviewTap's unique differentiator — it should be impossible to miss, not buried in a list. Lead with it in all marketing too.
High Impact Effort: 1 hr
8
Subscription / Pro tier monetisation
Introduce a R99/mo "Pro" tier that unlocks: analytics dashboard, lead export CSV, email signature generator, unlimited links, custom accent themes. This converts ReviewTap from a once-off R649 sale into an ARR business. Analytics is the gate — build it first, then monetise around it. Free tier = 1 card, basic links, no analytics.
High Impact Effort: 1 week+
🎯
Bottom line: ReviewTap is already ahead of most competitors on design quality and unique features (Google Review link, lead capture). The gaps are almost entirely in post-creation experience — analytics, success screen, edit link visibility, and domain. These are all solvable in Phase 5 and 6. The Google Review feature alone is a strong enough differentiator to build an entire SA SMB marketing story around. Lead with that, close the post-creation gaps, and add analytics — then the product is defensible.
