Strategy: The Risk Email

Awareness → Problem → Solution. Email 1 was awareness. This is the problem email. The reader must feel the exposure before they want the solution. The scenario format puts them inside the situation rather than describing it from outside.

  • "You never had a chance to fix it. You never even knew it was coming." — two short sentences that land the full weight of the risk. Do not trim or soften this line.
  • Subject A is story-framed ("just tapped your stand") and present-tense. Present tense creates urgency that past-tense copy cannot. Test both across 50/50 but Subject A will likely win with service businesses.
  • For hospitality and restaurants (Segment B and C high-value accounts), the 1-star review risk is existential. Subject A resonates most with this group. Subject B works better with retail and trades where revenue framing is more relevant.
  • The CTA is a direct upgrade CTA for the first time. By Day 2, the reader has seen the teaser and Email 1. The curiosity has been building for 2 days. This is the right moment for a direct ask.
