Under The Hood

How the AI agent
actually thinks

The AI isn't just a chatbot following a script. It's a context-aware agent inside the ReviewTap System — trained to qualify leads, recommend products, and drive Google reviews without human involvement.

📱

WhatsApp (Meta Business API)

The customer-facing channel. All messages route through here — inbound and outbound.

🧠

ReviewTap AI Agent

The brain. Reads every incoming message, understands intent, picks the right response and action from its trained knowledge base.

⚡

ReviewTap System Automations

Triggered by conversation events: new contact → assign to pipeline, payment detected → fire delivery sequence, no reply in 24h → send follow-up.

🔗

Automation Layer

Connects the ReviewTap System to Shopify, email, and other tools. Passes order data, triggers post-purchase flows, handles edge cases.

📦

Shopify + Fulfilment

Order placed, device picked, packed, and dispatched. Tracking number fed back into the ReviewTap System → sent to customer via WhatsApp automatically.

What the AI
agent actually does

🎯
Intent Detection

Reads every message to determine what the customer wants — pricing, product info, custom branding, delivery time, or support. Routes accordingly.

🔍
Dynamic Qualification

Asks targeted questions to understand business type and platform (Google, Autotrader, TripAdvisor, Instagram) — then recommends the exact right product.

💬
Objection Handling

Trained on common objections: "Is it too complicated?", "What if it doesn't work?", "Can I see reviews?". Responds naturally with real proof and the 14-day guarantee.

📲
Contextual Memory

Remembers what was said earlier in the conversation. If a customer mentions they own a salon, the AI doesn't ask again — it keeps context throughout.

🚨
Human Escalation Trigger

If the AI detects a complex or sensitive query it can't confidently handle, it flags the conversation and notifies a team member to take over.

⏰
Re-engagement Sequences

If a lead goes cold — no response for 24h — the AI sends a friendly follow-up. If still no reply after 48h, another message goes out. Fully automated drip.
