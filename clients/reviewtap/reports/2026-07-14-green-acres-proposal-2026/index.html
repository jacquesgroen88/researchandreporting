System Two · Deep Dive

The whole guest journey.
Handled automatically.

📲
What this does
  • Reads the booking confirmation emails from Nightsbridge and Booking.com, so no change to how the team already works and no manual data entry
  • Fires a pre-check-in message once a booking is confirmed: check-in time, directions, parking, Wi-Fi, house rules
  • Adds a mid-stay check-in so small issues get fixed before they become a bad review
  • Sends a warm post-checkout follow-up that thanks the guest and invites a review
  • Sent via WhatsApp, the highest open-rate channel available at 95%
  • Reads as a message from Green Acres, and cancellations or changes are handled so nobody gets a message meant for a guest who is no longer coming

System Four · Deep Dive

Own the guest relationship.
Bring them back direct.

🔁
How the repeat-guest engine works
  • A simple digital check-in step captures each guest's real name, mobile number and email as they arrive
  • This matters because Booking.com hides guest emails behind relay addresses that expire after the stay, so without capture there is no way to reach them again
  • Every captured guest joins the group's own contact database, owned by Green Acres, not the OTA
  • Post-stay flows thank them, invite a review, and later send a return offer to book direct
  • Repeat-guest campaigns bring past guests back at zero commission, straight to the group
  • This becomes the foundation of a proper loyalty and rewards programme over time
