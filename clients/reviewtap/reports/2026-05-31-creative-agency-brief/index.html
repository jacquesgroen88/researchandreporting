👋 Context for the Brief

I run two South African e-commerce brands and need your help creating paid ad variations — primarily for Meta (Facebook & Instagram), with TikTok as a secondary channel. Each brand has a distinct voice, audience, and creative strategy, broken out in detail below.

For MyForeverBaby specifically, I have a large library of unused UGC footage that has never been used in paid media. The Google Drive link to the full folder is included in the brief. Turning this footage into structured ad variations is the single biggest creative priority for this brand.

💡 What is UGC?

UGC (User-Generated Content) is real video footage captured by actual customers — not produced by a studio or agency. Think phone-filmed unboxing videos, genuine reactions when a child opens a gift, or a mom showing the doll to her daughter for the first time.

UGC consistently outperforms polished studio ads on Meta and TikTok because it looks and feels organic — it blends into the feed, builds instant trust, and drives higher click-through and conversion rates. For MyForeverBaby, the emotional reactions in this footage are the single most powerful asset we have.

Please use this brief to develop ad concepts, copy variations, hook ideas, and UGC edit direction. Feel free to reach out with any questions before you begin.

Ad Angles to Develop
01
The Reaction Angle ⭐ UGC Priority
Lead with a child's genuine face the moment they first see the doll. Zero words needed for the first 3 seconds. The hook IS the reaction. Pull directly from UGC footage.
02
The Unboxing ⭐ UGC Priority
Show the full unboxing — packaging, reveal, doll in her hands. Works extremely well as Reels/TikTok-style ad. Look for the best unboxing sequences in the UGC folder.
03
The Gift Moment
"She's going to lose her mind." Target moms actively searching for the perfect birthday/Christmas gift. Solve the "what do I get her?" problem head-on.
04
The Realism Hook
"She thought it was a real baby." Lean into how lifelike the doll is — shock + delight. Works especially well if the UGC captures this moment naturally.
05
The Nurture / Play Angle
Show the child playing "mommy" — feeding, bathing, pushing in a pram. Deeply relatable to moms. Also creates a natural upsell into the Pamper Pack and Travel Package.
06
The Value Justification
"The gift they'll still talk about at 21." Justify the R2,099 price vs. a toy that breaks in a week. Free delivery + 14-day return = low perceived risk. Use for retargeting.

Proven Ad Angles — Build Variations Around These
01
Pain — The Competitor Angle
"Your competitor has 200 reviews. You have 4. Customers pick them, not you." Lead with the daily sting of being outranked. This is the highest-performing cold angle.
02
Mechanism — Show the Tap
5-second demo: someone taps the device → phone instantly opens Google review page. Make it look effortless. This IS the aha moment — the product sells itself visually.
03
Proof — Real Customer Results
"18 five-star reviews in 3 weeks." Before/after of a Google listing. Screenshot of a notification flood. Social proof that overcomes "does this actually work?"
04
Price — Once-Off Investment Frame
"R299 once. More reviews forever." Zero monthly fees. Frame it vs. what one new customer is worth — the ROI angle for business owners.
05
Urgency — Sale Pricing
These products carry steep discounts (R1,299 → R499). Flash-sale framing. "Was R1,299 — get it today for R499." Works well as a retargeting layer.
