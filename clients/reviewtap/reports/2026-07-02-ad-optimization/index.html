Mobile is the leak

Mobile is 87.5% of traffic (1,560 sessions) but converts at just 2.18%, versus desktop at 6.64%. Same traffic on a desktop-grade mobile checkout would roughly triple mobile revenue.

55% abandon at checkout

Of 107 sessions that reach checkout, only 48 complete. Express pay, fewer form fields, and visible trust and delivery cues are the standard fixes and the highest-leverage work in this report.

What to do next

Eight moves, ranked by leverage.

Each is tied to a number above. Effort is marked S (quick, in Ads Manager or minor store edit), M (a session of focused work), L (a build). Do them roughly in order.

1

Fix the mobile checkout leak

M

Mobile is 87.5% of traffic (1,560 sessions) but converts at 2.18% versus desktop 6.64%. Only 44.9% of mobile checkouts complete. This is the single biggest revenue unlock: add express pay (Apple Pay, Shop Pay), shorten the mobile form, and surface trust and delivery info above the fold. Closing half the mobile gap is worth more than any ad change.

2

Rebalance budget out of the CBO Prospecting engine

S

RT Prospecting Engine (CBO) takes 47.6% of spend at 1.24 ROAS while RT Creative Testing sits at 2.65 ROAS on 27%. Shift 20-30% of daily budget from Prospecting into Creative Testing and the proven winners. Same spend, higher return.

3

Exclude Facebook Reels, Audience Network and junk placements

S

Facebook Reels burned R1,886 at 0.41 ROAS. Audience Network, Rewarded Video and Marketplace show inflated CTR with zero tracked purchases (bot and misclick traffic). Move campaigns to manual placements and switch these off. Recovers roughly R2k of wasted spend per two weeks.

4

Tilt spend toward Instagram

S

Instagram returns 3.14 ROAS versus Facebook 1.11, yet gets under a third of the budget. Instagram Stories (5.97) and Instagram Reels (2.95) are the standout positions. Add an IG-weighted ad set and feed the Stories placement specifically.

5

Kill the Begging Video and Card Spotlight Static

S

Begging Video has the highest CTR (3.02%) but 0.51 ROAS: it is clickbait that pulls the wrong audience. Card Spotlight Static loses money at 0.67. Turn both off and redirect budget to the winners below.

6

Scale the starved winners

M

Custom Branded Stand UGC Video (3.27), Reviews Used to Be Hard Static (3.52), and the 10 Seconds (4.40) and 2in3 (4.14) statics are all proven but running on R140 to R2k. Duplicate them into the Creative Testing campaign with real budget and let them find scale.

7

Tighten age targeting

S

45-54 (2.40) and 35-44 (1.95) carry the account. 55-64 spends R2,970 at breakeven 0.99 and 25-34 sits at 1.24. Set bid or exclusion adjustments to lean into the 35-54 core and trim the two weak bands.

8

Refresh the account's biggest ad

M

Reviews Used to Be Hard Static [Prospecting] is the single largest spend at R4,509 but its 0.95% CTR is below the 1.30% account average and ROAS is only 1.46. Refresh the creative or cap its budget so it stops setting the account's blended ceiling.

Net: the media is already profitable at 4.32x blended. The three fastest ad wins (reallocate budget, cut wasted placements, tilt to Instagram) cost nothing and lift return on the same spend. The single biggest revenue unlock is not in the ad account at all: it is the mobile checkout.
