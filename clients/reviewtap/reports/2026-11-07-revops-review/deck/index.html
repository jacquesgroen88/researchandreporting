Step 8b · Cost of poor quality
Cost of poor quality · what it says

Only R9,700 is cash out the door. The rest is time and lost sales, in three places

42%

Missing setup info

Orders arrive without the logo or review link. One fix: the /setup link in the order email asks for both, with a "find my Google profile" search.

23%

Unanswered messages

59 customer messages ended unanswered in six months. Fix: an alert on anything older than 15 minutes.

19%

Fulfilment errors

A three-tick check before sealing each parcel: print vs mock-up, every line packed, tap-test every NFC unit. About R2,000 a year to stop R13,800.

Step 8b · Cost of poor quality
A3 problem solve · ask why five times

89% of buyers never come back. Why?

Why 1

Nobody contacts them after delivery. No second-purchase message has ever reached a buyer.

Why 2

Half the buyers aren't in the CRM (437 of 840), and its email was dead from 19 August.

Why 3

Shopify isn't connected to the CRM, and the email failure raised no alert.

Why 4

Nobody owned the post-sale stage, and no weekly number showed repeat rate or failures.

Root

ReviewTap was built as an acquisition machine. Everything after checkout was handled case by case, never designed with an owner, a promise and a scorecard.

Step 8c · A3
A3 · targets

What good looks like, and by when

First, by 31 Dec 2026
  • 70% of custom orders arrive with logo and review link, no chase
  • 100% of parcels pass the pre-dispatch check
  • 90% of messages answered within 15 minutes
  • All 840 buyers in the CRM as customers
Then, by 30 Apr 2027
  • Repeat rate 11.3% to 18%
  • Cost of poor quality halved, under R37,000 a year
  • Lifetime value to acquisition cost at or above 3:1
  • Reviews of ReviewTap itself 40 to 100

Expect a short dip: dispatch may slow by about a day while the check becomes habit.
