Why the rating lags

The happy majority stays silent.

Unhappy guests post, happy guests leave

The one table that had a slow starter writes a one-star review that night. The forty tables that loved it never think to. Over time the public average drifts below reality.

No easy way to actually leave a review

Asking a guest to find you on Google, search, scroll and type is too much friction. Almost nobody does it, even when they had a great time.

Problems go public before you hear them

By the time a complaint appears on Google, it is too late to fix. You never got the chance to make it right at the table.

No single view of both venues

Reviews land across Google and Tripadvisor with no alerts, no replies, and no reporting. Reputation is left to chance.

How ReviewTap works

Tap the phone. Leave the review. Done.

A branded ReviewTap stand sits on each table or at the pay point. When a guest has had a great meal, one tap or scan takes them straight to your Google review page for that venue. No app, no searching, no typing.

1

Guest taps or scans

The stand uses NFC and a QR code, so it works on every phone. One touch opens your Google review page instantly.

2

Happy guests go to Google

Delighted diners are guided straight to a public five-star review, exactly where new customers will see it.

3

Unhappy feedback stays private

An optional smart step routes anyone less than thrilled to a private message that reaches your team first, so you fix it at the table instead of on Google.

4

Your team stays in the loop

Every new review triggers a WhatsApp alert, an AI-assisted reply, and a monthly report, so both venues are managed, not left to chance.
