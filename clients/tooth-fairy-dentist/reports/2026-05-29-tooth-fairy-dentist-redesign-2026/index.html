Meet Your Dentist

Dr. Josephine Lekota

Dr. Lekota founded Tooth Fairy Dental Clinic with a single, powerful mission: to make dental care stress-free, anxiety-free, and pain-free for every patient who walks through the door.

A qualified Bachelor of Dental Science (BDS) practitioner with over 10 years of experience, Dr. Lekota has treated thousands of patients across Pretoria — from children seeing a dentist for the very first time to adults seeking life-changing cosmetic transformations.

Her warm, patient-centred approach and modern dental practice equipped with the latest technology have earned her a trusted media platform as a dental expert.
