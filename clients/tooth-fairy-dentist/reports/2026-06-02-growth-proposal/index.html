Site & Growth Audit

What's holding
Tooth Fairy back

Four gaps standing between where you are and a fully-booked appointment book.

01
Critical

Your website doesn't convert visitors into patients

A new patient landing on your site has one question: "Can I trust these people with my teeth?" The answer needs to come through instantly — with professional photography, visible qualifications, real patient reviews, and a frictionless booking flow. Right now, visitors arrive, browse briefly, and leave without booking. Every departure is a lost patient.

Fix: A modern, conversion-focused website designed to answer trust questions on the first scroll — with visible credentials, prominent reviews, clear treatment pricing, and a booking form that works on any device.
02
Critical

No Google presence — you're invisible to new patients

When someone in your area searches "dentist near me", "emergency dentist", or "teeth whitening [your suburb]" — you don't appear. These are high-intent patients ready to book right now. Without Google Ads and a properly optimised local presence, every one of those searches results in a booking for a competitor.

Fix: Google Search campaigns targeting high-intent dental keywords in your catchment area. You appear at the top of search results the moment a patient is actively looking — not before, not after.
03
High Impact

Enquiries go unanswered — warm leads go cold

When a patient fills in your contact form or sends a WhatsApp message, what happens next? If the answer is "we get to it when we can", you're losing patients. Research shows that leads responded to within 5 minutes are 9x more likely to convert. A manual process at a busy front desk simply can't compete with that expectation.

Fix: A WhatsApp AI system that responds instantly to every new enquiry, qualifies the patient, collects their details, and either books the appointment automatically or flags urgent cases to your team — 24/7, including weekends.
04
Growth Opportunity

Existing patients aren't being recalled or re-engaged

Your best source of new revenue is sitting in your patient database. Patients overdue for a 6-month checkup, those who haven't been back in over a year, people who enquired but never booked — these are warm relationships that should be generating appointments every month. Without an automated system, they quietly drift to whoever reminds them first.

Fix: Automated WhatsApp recall campaigns and re-engagement sequences that bring lapsed patients back — personalised by name, treatment history, and time since last visit. Set up once, runs every month.
