What We Found

What's holding
Tooth Fairy back

Four gaps between where you are now and a fully-booked appointment book.

Critical 01

Your website doesn't convince new patients to book

When someone lands on your site for the first time, they're asking one question: "Can I trust these people with my teeth?" That answer has to come through immediately — through professional photos, Dr. Lekota's qualifications, patient reviews, and a simple way to book. Right now the site doesn't do that. Visitors arrive, look around briefly, and leave without booking.

What we'll do: A fresh, modern website that builds trust on the first scroll — showing Dr. Lekota's credentials, featuring real patient reviews, making every treatment easy to understand, and giving people a simple way to book straight from their phone.
Critical 02

No paid ads — invisible when patients are actively searching

When someone in Pretoria CBD searches "dentist near me", "emergency dentist Pretoria", or "Invisalign Pretoria" right now — Tooth Fairy doesn't appear. These are patients who are ready to book today, not tomorrow. Without paid search ads, every one of those searches ends with a booking at a competitor. Your Google Business Profile exists, but it's not enough on its own.

What we'll do: Google search ads that put Tooth Fairy at the top of results when someone in your area is actively looking for a dentist — for emergency appointments, teeth whitening, implants, Invisalign, and general check-ups.
High Impact 03

Enquiries get lost — and warm leads go cold fast

When a new patient fills in your contact form or sends a WhatsApp message, what happens next? If the front desk is busy, that response might come hours later. But research is clear: leads reached within 5 minutes are far more likely to book. By the time most practices reply, the patient has already booked somewhere else. It's not a people problem — it's a systems problem.

What we'll do: A WhatsApp assistant that replies to every new enquiry straight away — day or night, weekends included. It collects the patient's details, asks about the treatment they need, and either gets the appointment booked or passes the conversation to your team. No more leads slipping through.
Growth Opportunity 04

No reviews on your website — patients have no reason to trust you yet

Dr. Lekota has real patients and a Google Business Profile — but a new visitor to your website sees no star rating, no testimonials, and no patient stories. For a healthcare decision, people need to see that others have been happy before they'll book. Without that social proof on your site, they leave and find someone with 50 reviews instead.

What we'll do: A live Google reviews section on your homepage so patients can see your reputation the moment they arrive. After every appointment, patients are automatically asked for a review — with a gentle follow-up if they don't respond first time.
