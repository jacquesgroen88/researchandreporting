Questions

The things people ask before signing up

Do I have to move my DNS to RedirHub?
No. You can point a single hostname with a CNAME record and leave the rest of your DNS exactly where it is. Nameserver delegation is available if you would rather hand over a whole domain, but it is optional — unlike edge platforms that require you to move your zone.
What happens to SSL certificates?
RedirHub provisions and renews certificates automatically for every source hostname, 30 days before expiry, at no extra cost. Wildcard SSL is available so you issue one certificate for *.domain.com rather than one per subdomain. With certificate lifetimes dropping to 45 days, this is the single biggest operational reason teams move off a self-hosted stack.
How fast are the redirects, really?
90ms average global response, routed through the nearest of 10+ points of presence. Speed matters here beyond user experience: a slow redirect adds latency to every crawl and can erode the SEO value the redirect exists to protect.
Can AI agents manage our redirects?
Yes. RedirHub publishes an MCP server, so Claude, Cursor, Codex and any MCP-compatible assistant can create, monitor and repair redirects directly. It is the practical answer to the deployment gap between an AI-generated landing page and a live URL that people can actually reach.
What do you provide for a security review?
A signed Data Processing Addendum, a published subprocessor register, our security and compliance documentation, and a contractual 99.99% uptime SLA. Request the pack through the form above and it is sent before the call, not after it.
Can I change a destination after it is live?
Any time. Updates propagate across the global network within seconds with no downtime, which is what makes dynamic QR codes and campaign links work — print once, point anywhere, change it later.
