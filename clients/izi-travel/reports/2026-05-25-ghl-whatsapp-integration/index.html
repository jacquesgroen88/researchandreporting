Section 04

AI Qualification Bot

Configure GHL's Conversation AI to qualify leads automatically after they confirm their details.

🤖
GHL Conversation AI (formerly "AI Employee") Go to Settings → AI Employee → Conversation AI. Enable it on your WhatsApp channel. The bot handles the qualification conversation; when complete, it tags the contact and hands off to your team.

AI Bot System Prompt

Paste this into the Conversation AI → Custom Prompt / Instructions field:

## Identity
You are Zara, a friendly travel consultant for Izi Travel Agency, based in
Vanderbijlpark, South Africa. You help South Africans plan affordable luxury
holidays to Mauritius, Maldives, Zanzibar, and Thailand.

## Your Goal
After a lead confirms their travel details, ask 3–4 qualifying questions
in a warm, conversational tone. Never ask more than 2 questions per message.

## Qualifying Questions (ask in order)
Q1: "What's the main vibe you're going for? 🌴 Beach & relax, 🤿 Snorkel & adventure,
🌹 Romantic getaway, or 👨‍👩‍👧 Family fun?"

Q2: "Are your travel dates flexible, or do you have specific dates in mind?"

Q3: "Have you ever been to [destination] before? Any must-haves like a pool villa,
all-inclusive, or beachfront room?"

Q4: "Last one 😊 — how did you hear about Izi Travel?"

## Lead Scoring Rules (set internally, don't tell the lead)
- Budget R25k+ → add 30 points
- Budget R15k-25k → add 20 points
- Travel within 6 months → add 25 points
- Travel 6-12 months → add 15 points
- Answered all 4 questions → add 20 points
- Score 60+ = HOT 🔥 | 40-59 = WARM 🌡️ | <40 = COLD ❄️

## After Qualification
Thank them warmly and say: "Wonderful! I'm passing your details to one of our
travel experts who will prepare a personalised quote for you within 24 hours. ✈️
Is there anything else you'd like us to know?"

## Rules
- Keep messages SHORT — WhatsApp, not email
- Use 1-2 emojis per message max
- Never discuss pricing or make promises
- If asked something outside travel: "I'll get our team to assist you with that!"
- If lead is aggressive or asks to stop: immediately apologise and stop messaging

Section 05

GHL Workflow Automations

Build these 4 workflows in GHL → Automations → Workflows. Each handles a distinct stage of the lead journey.

W1

New Lead — Instant WhatsApp Confirmation

Trigger: Contact Created

Fires the moment Make.com creates a contact in GHL. Sends the WhatsApp template confirmation within 2 minutes.

#ActionDetail
1⏱️ Wait2 minutes (avoids spam flag)
2💬 Send WhatsApp Templateizi_lead_confirmation · Map {{1}}=firstName, {{2}}=izi_destination, {{3}}=izi_travel_month, {{4}}=izi_travel_year, {{5}}=izi_travellers, {{6}}=izi_budget
3🏷️ Add Tagwhatsapp-sent
4📊 Move to PipelineIzi Travel Holidays → "AI Qualify"
5🤖 Enable AI BotActivate Conversation AI on this contact
W2

Hot Lead — Immediate Agent Alert

Trigger: Tag Added = hot-lead

When the AI bot scores a lead as HOT (60+), instantly alert your sales agent so they can call within the hour.

#ActionDetail
1🔔 Internal NotificationPush notification + email to assigned agent
2📋 Create Task"CALL NOW — Hot Lead: {firstName} {lastName} — {izi_destination} {izi_travel_month}" · Due: 1 hour
3📊 Update Pipeline StageMove to "CONTACTED"
4💬 Send Agent WhatsAppInternal alert to your personal number with lead summary
W3

No Response — Re-engagement Sequence

Trigger: Tag = whatsapp-sent · No reply 2h

If a lead hasn't replied to the confirmation message after 2 hours, re-engage via a different channel.

#DelayAction
1+2 hours📧 Send email: Subject "Your Izi Travel enquiry — did you get our message?" · Friendly reminder with link to contact page
2+24 hours💬 Send WhatsApp Template: izi_followup_24h
3+48 hours📋 Create Task: "Manual call attempt — {firstName}" · Assign to agent
4+7 days🏷️ Add Tag: cold-lead · Move to nurture sequence
W4

Quote Sent — Follow-up & Booking Push

Trigger: Pipeline Stage = "QUOTE SENT"

Once an agent manually moves a contact to "Quote Sent", this workflow drives them to booking.

#DelayAction
1+24 hours💬 WhatsApp: "Hi {firstName}, just checking you received your {izi_destination} quote? Any questions I can answer? 😊"
2+3 days📧 Email: "Don't let your dream holiday slip away" — urgency email with scarcity angle (limited spaces for peak months)
3+5 days💬 WhatsApp: "We have 2 spots left for {izi_travel_month} {izi_travel_year} — shall I hold one for you? ✈️"
4+7 days📋 Task: "Final call — {firstName} quote expires" · IF still no booking → tag as cold-lead

Full 30-Day Follow-up Map

The complete touchpoint timeline from first enquiry to booking close — across WhatsApp, email, and phone.

⚡
Day 0 · Minute 2

WhatsApp Confirmation Template

Automated. Sends the izi_lead_confirmation template with their details prefilled. AI bot activates on reply.

🤖
Day 0 · On reply

AI Qualification Conversation

Zara (AI bot) runs 4-question qualification. Takes ~5 minutes. Tags lead as hot/warm/cold and notifies agent accordingly.

📧
Day 0 · +2 Hours (if no reply)

Email Re-engagement

Subject: "Did you get our WhatsApp? Your Izi Travel quote is waiting 🌍" — Friendly nudge with a direct link to the contact page.

💬
Day 1

WhatsApp Follow-up (24h Template)

Uses izi_followup_24h template. Shows the destination name and starting price. Soft CTA: "Reply QUOTE to continue."

📞
Day 2

Agent Manual Call Attempt

GHL creates a task for your agent to call. Log the call outcome in GHL. If no answer → leave a voicemail and send a follow-up WhatsApp.

💬
Day 5

Value-add WhatsApp Message

Non-sales touchpoint. Share something useful: "Did you know {destination} is best visited in {month}? Here's why..." — builds trust without pressure.

📧
Day 7

Urgency Email

"Limited spaces available for {month} {year}" — scarcity-based email with social proof (testimonials from the Testimonials section of your site).

💬
Day 14

WhatsApp Check-in

"Hi {firstName}, still thinking about {destination}? We have some new packages for {month} you might love. Want me to send details? 🌊"

📧
Day 30

Final Re-engagement + Nurture

Last active follow-up. After this, move to a monthly newsletter nurture list. Never fully abandon — travel bookings have long consideration cycles.

Add a GoHighLevel → Create/Update Contact module after the Router. Map these fields:

firstName→First Name
lastName→Last Name
email→Email
phone→Phone
destination→izi_destination
travelMonth→izi_travel_month
travelYear→izi_travel_year
travellers→izi_travellers
budget→izi_budget
specialRequests→izi_special_requests
formType→izi_form_type
Source = "website"→Lead Source
⚠️
Use "Create or Update" (Upsert) not "Create" Set the lookup field to Phone or Email so duplicate contacts aren't created if someone submits the form twice.

GHL API Connection in Make.com

  • In Make.com → Connections → Add → GoHighLevel
