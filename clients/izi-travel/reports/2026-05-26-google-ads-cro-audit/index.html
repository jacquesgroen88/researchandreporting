🚨 Critical CRO Issues for Google Ads

P0 — Critical

No Google Ads Conversion Tracking

No Google Ads tag, Google Tag Manager, or conversion pixel detected. You can't measure which ads drive enquiries — you're flying blind.

🔧 Fix: Install Google Tag Manager + Google Ads conversion tracking on form submissions
P0 — Critical

No Thank You / Confirmation Page

The "Request Pricing" button submits but there's no visible thank-you page. Google Ads needs a conversion event on a separate thank-you page URL.

🔧 Fix: Create a /thank-you page and fire the conversion tag there
P0 — Critical

No Click-to-Call Tracking

The phone number is a plain link with no call tracking. For a local travel agency, phone calls are likely the #1 converter.

🔧 Fix: Add Google Ads call tracking number or use CallRail to track which ads drive calls
P1 — High

Form Has Too Many Required Fields

8 required fields creates friction. Every extra field reduces conversion rate by ~5-10% for Google Ads traffic.

🔧 Fix: Make Month, Year, Budget, and Travellers optional. Only require: Name, WhatsApp/Email, Destination
P1 — High

No WhatsApp Floating Button

The form asks for WhatsApp but there's no direct WhatsApp chat button. South African users heavily prefer WhatsApp for business communication.

🔧 Fix: Add floating WhatsApp button → https://wa.me/27829672060
P1 — High

No Customer Reviews / Testimonials

Zero social proof beyond the "#1 Travel Agent" claim. Google Ads Quality Score improves with trust signals on the landing page.

🔧 Fix: Add Google Reviews widget, testimonials with photos, or TripAdvisor badges
P2 — Medium

Rotating Headline Hurts Ad Relevance

The hero heading rotates between "Island Holiday", "Wedding Getaway", "Birthday Escape", "Honeymoon". If someone clicks a "Thailand Holiday" ad but lands on "Honeymoon", that's a Quality Score mismatch.

🔧 Fix: Create dedicated landing pages for each ad group, or use a static broad headline
P2 — Medium

No Urgency or Scarcity Elements

No "Limited time offer", "Only X seats left", or booking deadlines. Travel purchases are emotional — urgency drives action.

🔧 Fix: Add seasonal urgency like "Book before [date] for free airport transfers"
P2 — Medium

Missing Trust Badges

No BBBEE, IATA, or ASATA membership badges visible. Travel is a high-consideration purchase — trust badges reduce anxiety.

🔧 Fix: Add industry accreditation badges near the CTA buttons
P3 — Nice to Have

No Exit-Intent Popup

No mechanism to capture leaving visitors who don't convert on the first visit.

🔧 Fix: Add exit popup offering a free travel guide or discount in exchange for email

💡 The Bottom Line

The biggest bang for your buck: set up conversion tracking first (all 3 P0 items). Right now you're spending on Google Ads with no idea which clicks turn into customers. Once tracking is in place, the form optimization and landing page tweaks will significantly lower your cost per lead and improve your Google Ads Quality Score.
