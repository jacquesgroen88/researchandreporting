Opportunities Pipeline — Quick Reference

Keep at your desk

The one idea: A Conversation is just talking. An Opportunity is a conversation with a deal attached — so nothing and no one gets forgotten.

The 6 Pipeline Stages

1
New Enquiry — they've messaged asking about a trip
2
Quote Requested — gathering dates, pax, destination, budget
3
Quote Sent — proposal delivered, waiting on them
4
Follow-Up / Negotiating — chasing & adjusting the package
5
Booking Confirmed (Won) — deposit paid 🎉
6
Lost / Cold — went elsewhere or went quiet

Turn a Chat into an Opportunity

  1. Open the conversation in your inbox
  2. Go to the contact → Add Opportunity
  3. Pick pipeline: Travel Enquiries
  4. Set stage: New Enquiry
  5. Name it: Surname + Trip + Month
    e.g. "Smit Family – Mauritius July"
  6. Add estimated value (rough rand value of the trip)
  7. Save ✓

Move a Deal Forward

  • Open Opportunities → see your board
  • Drag the card to the next stage as things progress
  • Sent a quote? → drag to Quote Sent
  • Deposit paid? → mark Won
  • Gone cold? → mark Lost (this is useful data, not failure)
  • The total at the top = money live on your board right now

The Two Rules That Make It Work

① Every new enquiry becomes an opportunity BEFORE you close the chat.
② The moment you send a quote, drag the card to "Quote Sent."

Your 5-Minute Morning Habit

Each morning: open the pipeline → look at Quote Sent and Follow-Up → chase anyone sitting there more than 2–3 days. That's it. That single habit is where the bookings come from.
