Why It Leaks

Five reasons, pulled from your real lead notes

These are not guesses. Every quote in the pipeline was read. Here is what is actually killing the bookings, in the clients' own words.

1

Price-shopping — you're one of three quotes

The single biggest killer. Clients are collecting quotes and going quiet, treating Izi as a commodity line item.

"client has received quote and is awaiting on other quotes" · "awaiting quotes from other agencies"
2

Quotes literally not arriving

Email quotes are landing in spam. The client never sees it, Terri assumes it arrived, and the booking dies silently.

"Client came back to say she never got the email so i have resent it to her"
3

Dead leads clogging the quote queue

Several "Quote Sent" leads can't actually be quoted — no dates, no answer — eating Terri's 20-minute cycles.

"tried to contact client but not able to whatsapp and doesnt answer - need more info"
4

No follow-up rhythm

Follow-up is manual and inconsistent. Several quotes are 7–8 days old with no next step. Travel sells on the 3rd to 5th touch, not the 1st.

3 quotes 8 days old with zero follow-up notes recorded
5

The pipeline doesn't tell the truth

One client accepted a quote but still sits in "Follow-Up". Another has an invoice sent but sits in "Quote Sent". The CRM shows 1 booking; you have 5. You can't fix what you can't measure.

The Solution

Six fixes that plug the leak

In priority order. The first three cost almost nothing and attack the leak directly.

1

Win on value, not price

Biggest lever

You can't always be cheapest, so stop competing only on price. Terri already gives away room upgrades and travel insurance. Make them stated, named reasons to book, plus your 5-star reviews, right at the top of every quote. (Example below.)

2

Install a 5-touch follow-up cadence

Fastest win

Travel quotes are won on the 3rd to 5th touch. A fixed, mostly-automated rhythm the moment a quote goes out. Applied to the 36 open quotes today, this is your highest-ROI move this week.

3

Deliver quotes WhatsApp-first

Stops silent losses

Email is where quotes die. Flip the default: WhatsApp is the primary channel, email is the backup, and message #1 confirms it arrived. The "never received it" loss disappears entirely.

4

Add urgency the quotes lack

Quotes are valid 24 hours but nothing pushes the decision. Add two things:

  • Provisional hold — "I can hold this room for 48 hours, just say the word." Turns a big decision into a small yes.
  • Deposit-to-secure — a small deposit locks the price against forex movement.
5

Qualify before quoting

An automated message collects confirmed dates, budget and a reachable WhatsApp number before a lead hits Terri's quote queue. No more 20-minute quotes burned on un-quotable enquiries.

6

Make the pipeline reflect reality

So we can measure

Auto-move the stage when a booking event fires (accepted, invoiced, deposit paid). Until this is done you cannot see your true quote-to-booking rate, which means you can't tell if any of the above is working. Do this so the rest is measurable.

House Rules

When to retire a quote

Two different stale situations, two different clocks. The golden rule: almost never delete, just move to nurture so it can be requoted later.

A · Can't be quoted yet
No dates / no info / unreachable
Day 0–2
3 contact attempts (WhatsApp + call) to get dates & budget
Day 2
Still nothing? Move to "Awaiting Info" out of the active queue
Day 7
No response → long-term nurture (not lost)
B · Quoted but gone quiet
Real quote sent, no reply
Day 0–8
Run the full 5-touch cadence
Day 10–14
Zero engagement → move to nurture
"Lost"
Reserved only for an explicit no ("booked elsewhere")

Why nurture, never delete: when travel dates shift closer or forex moves, a refreshed quote reopens a cold lead at near-zero cost. Filed as "Lost", you never touch them again. That nurture bucket is found money against the R50k goal.
