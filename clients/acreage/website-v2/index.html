The Restaurant

Country cooking, fresh from the kitchen.

Open Tuesday to Sunday from 7am, our kitchen serves generous country-style breakfasts and lunches built on fresh, honest ingredients. Settle in indoors or take a table on the lawn with views across the estate.

  • Country-style breakfasts from 7am
  • Generous, seasonal lunches until 3pm
  • Indoor and outdoor seating with sweeping views
  • Pet-friendly, so the whole family is welcome
