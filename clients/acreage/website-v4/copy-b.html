Welcome to The Acreage

A country estate you can book, and a restaurant you can walk into.

The Acreage sits on the Randjesfontein racecourse, twenty minutes from Sandton and five from the N1. The lawns and the lapa are hired for weddings, year-end functions, birthdays and markets, from fifty guests up to three thousand. The restaurant is open to everyone, Tuesday to Sunday, from seven in the morning.

03 The Restaurant

Breakfast and lunch, Tuesday to Sunday.

Open from seven for country breakfasts, with lunch until three. Reviewers keep naming the same three things without being asked: the steak sandwich, the cheesecake and the coffee. Sit inside, or take a table on the lawn with the track in front of you. Dogs are welcome, and there are blankets on the terrace when the mist is still on the track.

  • Breakfast from 7am, Tuesday to Sunday
  • Lunch until 3pm, bar until 10pm
  • Tables inside and on the lawn
  • Dogs welcome
