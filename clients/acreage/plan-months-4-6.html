Why these three months and not some other three

Year end is 48% of Good & Proper's annual value. It is chosen in August and September and delivered in October, November and December. That has one blunt consequence: the year end season cannot be fixed during the year end season. It is won in the quiet months or it is not won.

December through February is the quiet stretch. It is also the only window in which the demand engine for the next Q4 can be built without taking people off live events. Three months from now that window has closed and the next one opens in 2028.

The Good & Proper ads mandate has never been settled. Until it is, the audit of 455-376-7888 is a document rather than a plan, and months five and six assume the pages, the ads and the tracking are ours to change. If they stay with Launch Digital, say so today and we will rescope those two months around The Acreage alone.

Worth knowing before you decide: the conversion goals for that account sit inside the managing agency's manager account, not inside your own. Changing agency moves or breaks the signal the bidding depends on, so this is a decision with a switching cost attached and it gets more expensive the longer it waits.

How you will know it is working

One report a month, on the numbers that pay for it rather than the ones that flatter it.

  • Enquiries, by source. How many, from where, and how many were answered inside an hour.
  • Booked value, not clicks. Rands confirmed against rands spent, per channel.
  • Quote turnaround. Enquiry in to proposal out, which is the number the quote desk exists to move.
  • Conversion rate on enquiries. Four of the last ten were lost to silence. That number should go to zero before anything else improves.
  • Restaurant covers on the nights the programme targets, against the baseline in your own till data.

Baselines are set at the ninety day review on 8 December, so month four is measured against a line we both agreed rather than one chosen afterwards.
