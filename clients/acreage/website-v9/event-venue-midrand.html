Functions & Events

One Estate, Six Spaces

The Acreage is an event and function venue in Randjesfontein, Midrand, twenty minutes from Sandton. The estate has a restaurant, a permanent stretch tent, a lapa by the water and four lawned areas, each available on its own or together.

Corporate functions, markets, fairs and expos, celebrations and weddings all fit, because the venue shapes itself around the event rather than the other way round. Catering and bars are in-house, with back-up power and ample secure parking.

  • Six hireable spaces, from 30 to 5,000 guests
  • Indoor, under canvas and outdoor options
  • In-house catering and fully serviced mobile bars
  • Back-up power and secure on-site parking
  • Room for markets, fairs and expos
  • Five minutes off the N1

Good to Know

Questions We Are Often Asked

How many guests can The Acreage host?

Up to 5,000 across the whole venue. On their own, the restaurant takes 150, the stretch tent 100 to 150, the restaurant and entrance lawns 300 to 400 each, and the lapa 30 to 90 with lawns around it for up to 3,000.

Can I hire one space on its own?

Yes. Each space can be hired individually or combined. The top lawns are the only exception: they extend larger events rather than being hired alone.

Do you host public events like markets and expos?

Yes. Acres of green and room for thousands make the estate a good fit for markets, fairs and expos.

Do you provide catering?

Yes, in-house: plated meals, platters and canapés, braais, spit braais, food trucks and mobile bars.

Can we view the venue?

Yes. Send us your date and we will arrange a walk-through with our events team.
