Section E

Good & Proper

Price per head

RBy a single price per head, we do not mean that Good & Proper has one fixed per-person price. When dealing with multiple food-truck vendors directly, a client would normally receive separate quotes containing numerous individual line items — portions, travel, staffing, consumables, truck hire etc. Good & Proper consolidates all of those costs into a simple per-head price for the client, for example, X per head for mains, X per head for starters and X per head for desserts. The actual price varies from event to event depending on the vendors selected, number and type of portions, staffing and other requirements. The benefit to the client is that the complexity is removed and they can easily understand what each element of their catering is costing per person. The price per head then becomes the same accross all the vendors for mains, all the vendors for dessert etc.
