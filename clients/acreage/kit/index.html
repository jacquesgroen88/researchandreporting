06 — Your website, twice

You said you struggle to find designers whose work you like.

That is the one thing a proposal cannot answer, so we did not try. We built The Acreage in two deliberately different directions, using your monogram, your setting, your rating and your own photographs. Neither is a template and neither is a variation of the other.

Direction one

The warm one

Deep forest greens and cream, full-bleed photography, a softer and more traditional hospitality feel. On desktop the hero plays a short film of your restaurant in service.

Open direction one →
Direction two

The editorial one

Oversized type, a great deal of white space, numbered sections, near-monochrome with a single gold accent. Colder and more confident, closer to a design magazine than a venue brochure.

Open direction two →

The typefaces came from your own logo, not from taste. Your submark is a high-contrast didone "A" and your wordmark is wide-tracked geometric capitals. The display face answers the monogram and the text face answers the wordmark. That is something you can check rather than a preference you have to trust.

If you like neither, say so. It costs you nothing and it is useful. We would rather do the strategy well and have you take the build elsewhere than sell you a site you quietly dislike.
