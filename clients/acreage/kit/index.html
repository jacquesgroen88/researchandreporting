05 — What you receive

The Seven Figure Blueprint, step by step.

You said it had only ever been described in high level terms. Fair. Below is the whole process, what you are holding at the end, and, more to the point, what you will be able to answer afterwards that you cannot answer now.

What it actually is

A two week strategy engagement covering The Acreage and Good & Proper together. It is not a report we write at you. It is two working sessions with the accounts open, plus the analysis in between, ending in a plan specific enough to hand to a supplier and have them quote off it.

The reason both businesses go in together is that they share a customer. Someone who books a food truck through Good & Proper is a candidate to hold their function at The Acreage, and vice versa. Planned separately, that overlap is invisible and you pay twice to reach the same person.

The process

1

Access, before anything is written

Read-only access to the Google Ads account, the Google Business Profiles, whatever analytics exists, and the enquiry inbox. If a claim in the final document cannot be traced to something we looked at in here, it does not go in.

2

Kickoff call, roughly ninety minutes

Recorded and transcribed so you are not taking notes. We go through how enquiries actually arrive today, who touches them, what happens when nobody replies, what a good month looks like versus a bad one, and what you want the business to be doing in a year.

3

The analysis week

Where the work happens. We go through the ad spend line by line, the enquiry path end to end, your reputation position against the venues you actually compete with, and the gap between what people find online and what the venue is really like. This is also where the free Good & Proper ad audit gets done.

4

Presentation call

We walk you through the findings and the plan, argue about the order, and change it while you are on the call rather than afterwards. Also recorded.

5

Final documents, yours to keep

Delivered as a set. You own them outright. If you never work with us again you can hand them to any other agency and they will know exactly what to do.

The five things you end up holding

01

Diagnostic

Where both businesses leak money and attention right now, with the evidence attached to each finding. Written after we have been inside the accounts, not before. Expect it to include things you already suspect and at least one you do not.

02

Three month roadmap

Every action, sequenced, with the reason it sits where it does and what it depends on. Split into what you can do yourselves, what needs a supplier, and what needs us. This is the document you would still be working off in October.

03

Brand voice and visual language

The identity work you have been sitting with for weeks, finished and written down: how the two businesses speak, what they look like, what they never do. The point is to close the loop so it stops being a decision you carry around.

04

Website creative direction

The direction, the structure, and what each page has to achieve. In your case a first look already exists in two versions, which is not usually the case at this stage.

05

Prioritised systems rollout

Which systems to build and in what order, what each costs to run monthly, who operates it once it is live, and what happens if you build the first one and stop. Costed so you can decide to stop.

What is actually different when it is finished

Documents are not an outcome. This is the test: these are questions you cannot answer with confidence today, and can at the end. If any of them are still open when we hand over, we have not finished.

What it is not. It is not a retainer, it does not auto-renew, and nothing in it obliges you to use us for the build. The roadmap is deliberately written so another supplier could execute it. If the honest answer at the end is that you should do three things yourselves and spend nothing further, that is what it will say.
