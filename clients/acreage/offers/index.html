Why this version is different

Your own trading figures pointed somewhere else.

The first sheet gave every day of the week a discount. Then your till report arrived, and it made a much stronger case for programmed events than for everyday price cuts. Four numbers did most of the persuading.

46%of restaurant revenue comes from Saturdays alone
R38,547taken on the house music Saturday, against about R10,750 on a normal one
R2,160total trade after 16:00 on Tuesdays, Wednesdays and Thursdays across eleven weeks
4.3%of sales are wine and bubbly, on a list that is almost entirely one estate
One programmed night earned more than three ordinary midweek days put together. That is the lever, and it is already proven in your own numbers.

There is a second reason for the change. Your brand foundation document says promotions should attract people through the quality of the experience rather than through price, and that Acreage should never become known for cheap drinks. I agree, and the old sheet was heading that way. Nothing on this page is a discount. Every item is either an occasion people buy into, or something new to sell.

What I dropped, and why

Two of the old offers should not run.

Tuesday breakfast

The old sheet had a discounted Farmhouse Breakfast on a Tuesday morning. Your figures show Tuesday between 8:00 and 10:00 is one of your strongest midweek windows already. Discounting it would cost you money on tables you are getting anyway.

Removed.

Thursday evening ribs

Thursday night is empty, but so is every midweek night, and there is no evidence anyone wants to be here on a Thursday yet. Building a Friday evening first is the better bet, and if Sunset Sessions works we can look at Thursday afterwards.

Held, not cancelled.

What this could be worth

About R99,000 over ninety days, on careful assumptions.

I want to be straight with you about this number, because the last sheet carried a bigger one and I no longer stand behind it. Before your till report I had no baseline, so I was guessing. Now I have one.

  • The events carry most of it, roughly R69,000. That assumes the new events draw about half of what your house music night drew, which is deliberately cautious, plus one repeat of that night at what it actually took.
  • The three quiet windows add roughly R30,000, most of it from Monday, because a closed day currently earns nothing at all.
  • The menu additions are not in that total. Dessert, wine and the cheese board raise what an existing table spends rather than bringing a new one, and I would rather show you real attach rates after a month than guess at them now.
The one number here that is not an assumption is R38,547. That is what a single programmed Saturday actually took, against about R10,750 on an ordinary one. Everything else on this page is an estimate and should be treated as one.
