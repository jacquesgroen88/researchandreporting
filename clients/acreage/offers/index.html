Things I got wrong last time

Three offers from the first sheet are gone.

Tuesday breakfast

I had a discounted Farmhouse Breakfast on Tuesday morning. Your figures show Tuesday between 8:00 and 10:00 is one of your better midweek windows already. I would have been handing money back on tables you were getting anyway.

Removed.

Coffee and cake on Wednesday

Wednesday afternoon is quiet, that part was right. But nobody drives to Midrand for a discounted slice of cake. The tasting now sits on Wednesday instead, because that is worth the drive.

Replaced.

Thursday ribs and a draught

Thursday night is empty. So is every midweek night, and there is no sign yet that anyone wants to be out here on a Thursday. Build Friday first. If Sundowners works, Thursday is the next conversation.

Held, not cancelled.

And one naming correction

I had a Champagne brunch on the first version. You don't stock Champagne, you stock MCC, and in South Africa those are not interchangeable words. Anything we run has to say Cap Classique or MCC unless you are actually pouring French.

Worth checking across your menus too.

What it could be worth

Somewhere around R110,000 over ninety days.

I want to be straight about this figure, because the last sheet carried a bigger one and I don't stand behind it any more. I had no baseline then. Now I have eleven weeks of your trading.

  • The events carry about R77,000 of it. That assumes the three new ones draw roughly half what your house music night drew, which is deliberately pessimistic, plus one repeat of that night at what it actually took.
  • Monday and Sunday morning add about R43,000, and most of that is Monday, for the simple reason that a closed day earns nothing at all right now.
  • I have knocked ten percent off the total because the first run of anything underperforms the fourth. People need to hear about something two or three times before they come.
  • The menu additions are not in the number. Dessert, wine and the board raise what an existing table spends. I would rather show you real figures after a month than invent them today.
The only number on this page that isn't an estimate is R38,547. That one already happened. Everything else is my best reading of your figures, and you should treat it that way.
