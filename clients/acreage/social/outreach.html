An Email and Four WhatsApp Messages

The people who've already booked with you are the easiest to book again. The full Open Day sequence, from invitation to follow-up, is on the Open Day page. This is one email to past clients and four short WhatsApp versions, timed to go out on Tuesday 13 October, the same day the Open Day invitation goes on social.

Leave a note on any word you'd change, the same way as on the other pages.

The Email

Open Day, Year-End Dates and Corporate Mondays

One email with three reasons to reply, the invitation first. It's built to look right in Gmail, Outlook and on a phone.

SubjectYou're invited: Open Day at The Acreage, Thursday 29 October PreviewA tour of the estate on Thursday 29 October, then breakfast on us. Plus year-end dates and a new Monday breakfast for teams. FromThe Acreage <events@acreagevenue.co.za> ToPast function and catering clients

The Open Day invitation now includes a buffet breakfast after the tour. Two gaps to fill before it goes: the Open Day time and the Corporate Mondays price a head. Everyone on the list can unsubscribe from the link at the bottom.

Short Versions for WhatsApp

Shorter, warmer and sent from a person. Use them one-to-one, as a broadcast list, or on your WhatsApp Status. Press copy and paste it straight into WhatsApp; the stars become bold.
