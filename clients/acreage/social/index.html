The Idea

Social Has Two Jobs Here

The restaurant and the venue sell to different people on different timelines. We keep them in one feed but plan, measure and advertise them separately.

Job One

Fill Tables This Week

A decision made the night before or that morning, usually over WhatsApp, by someone who lives or works within about twenty kilometres. This is breakfast, lunch, the bar and the weekend.

46%of restaurant takings come in on Saturdays, from your own till report. Saturday gets the strongest post of the week.
Job Two

Fill the Diary for Next Year

Year-end functions, team days, weddings and milestone birthdays. Decided over weeks by someone comparing three or four venues, so they need to see the spaces and the numbers.

30 to 5,000guests, from the lapa to the whole estate. Most venues never say how many people fit. We say it every time.

The two jobs feed each other. The family who comes for breakfast in October is walking past the lapa and the lawns, and that is often how a wedding or a year-end function starts. So every restaurant post is also a quiet venue viewing.

Formats

What Works at Your Size

  • Carousels are the weekly anchor. For accounts with one to ten thousand followers they reach nearly twice as many people as a Reel, and people save them, so a menu carousel keeps working for weeks.
  • Reels buy reach. They are how new people find you, and they're the best material for adverts. Your drone footage is cut to vertical for these.
  • Stories for the moments. A Story reaches around three times as many of your followers as a feed post, so we save them for the days that matter: the Open Day, match days, the competition draw.
  • No links in Facebook posts. Posts with links are shown to far fewer people. The link lives in the bio, in Stories and in the adverts.
  • Every post ends with a next step. A WhatsApp number or "link in bio", never "DM us for info".

How We'll Know

What We Measure

  • Table bookings that came from social. The simplest way to know: whoever takes a WhatsApp booking asks "where did you hear about us?" and notes it.
  • Function enquiries from social and the adverts, counted by the events team, with the space and the headcount.
  • Saves and shares on each post, which tell us what people want to come back to.
  • Followers within twenty kilometres, because a follower in Midrand is worth more than a hundred anywhere else.
