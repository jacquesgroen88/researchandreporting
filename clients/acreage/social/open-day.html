The Plan

What the Morning Is For

Past Clients

Bookings

A venue sells best in person. Past clients already trust you, so getting them back on the lawn, with a coffee in hand, is the shortest path to a year-end or 2027 booking.

Creators

Reach

Each creator brings a local audience of 16,000 to 64,000 people who have mostly never heard of The Acreage. One good video does more for the estate than a month of posts.

The Breakfast

A Reason to Stay

The tour shows the spaces; the breakfast shows the food and gives people time to talk dates. Without it, guests walk the lawns and drive off. With it, they sit down for an hour.

The Morning

How It Could Run

A suggestion for you to change. The times are the main thing we need from you.

  • 08:30Guests arrive. Coffee at the restaurant.
  • 09:00Tours in small groups of eight to ten: the restaurant, the stretch tent, the lawns and the lapa by the water. About forty minutes.
  • 09:45Buffet breakfast on the terrace.
  • 10:30Time with the events team to talk dates, numbers and quotes. Creators film on the lawn while the light is still good.
  • 11:30Close. Everyone leaves with the function pack.
What we need from you
  • The start time, and whether this run of the morning works.
  • How many guests the kitchen can do a buffet breakfast for. We'd suggest around 40 past clients and 6 creators, each with one guest.
  • Your list of past clients and planners to invite, with a phone number for each.
