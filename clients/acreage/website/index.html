Welcome to The Acreage

Where the country meets the city.

A refreshing retreat for Johannesburg and Pretoria, The Acreage pairs relaxed country charm with genuine polish. Sweeping lawns, versatile event spaces and a stylish restaurant make it the natural setting for everything from a family breakfast to a three thousand guest celebration.

The Venue

Room to gather, on a grand scale.

An expansive outdoor event space with a stylish restaurant venue and a lakeside lapa bar at its heart. Weddings, corporate days, private functions and large public events all find their place here.

  • Outdoor event space for up to 3,000 guests
  • Stylish restaurant venue and lakeside lapa bar
  • Ample secure parking and reliable back-up power
  • A trusted network of suppliers for every detail

The Restaurant

Country cooking, fresh from the kitchen.

Open Tuesday to Sunday from 7am, our kitchen serves generous country-style breakfasts and lunches built on fresh, honest ingredients. Settle in indoors or take a table on the lawn with views across the estate.

  • Country-style breakfasts from 7am
  • Generous, seasonal lunches until 3pm
  • Indoor and outdoor seating with sweeping views
  • Pet-friendly, so the whole family is welcome
