The same rule as Part 1, carried through all five

Every claim is tagged. Observed means we saw it ourselves and the way for you to see the same thing is written next to it. Modelled means it is arithmetic, with the assumption printed in the open. Assumption is new in these parts: it marks a recommendation that rests on a number we do not have yet, and it names the number.

There are more assumptions in Parts 2 to 5 than there were in Part 1, and that is deliberate rather than sloppy. Part 1 could be built entirely from the outside. A roadmap cannot. Every assumption tag in these four documents corresponds to a question in the onboarding questionnaire, and the kickoff call on 20 August exists to replace them with your figures.

What is still open

These four documents are drafts, and they say so on purpose.

Part 1 was built to be checked without you. Parts 2 to 5 cannot be finished without you. The gaps are named in each document and gathered in the questionnaire, and the kickoff call is where they close. Anything still tagged Assumption at the presentation call is a decision we made on your behalf that you should overturn if it is wrong.

The single largest gap is your calendar.

Finding 5 in Part 1 was the biggest line in the document and the least visible, and it stays a mechanism rather than a rand figure until we see calendar utilisation and average function value. Phase 3 of the roadmap is built around it and is deliberately last, because it is the one that cannot be designed from the outside.
