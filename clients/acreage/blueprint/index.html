The same rule as Part 1, carried through all five

Every claim is tagged. Observed means we saw it ourselves and the way for you to see the same thing is written next to it. Modelled means it is arithmetic, with the assumption printed in the open. Assumption is new in these parts: it marks a recommendation that rests on a number we do not have yet, and it names the number.

There are more assumptions in Parts 2 to 5 than there were in Part 1, and that is deliberate rather than sloppy. Part 1 could be built entirely from the outside. A roadmap cannot. Every assumption tag in these four documents corresponds to a question in the onboarding questionnaire, and the kickoff call exists to replace them with your figures. Many of them now have been, see The Numbers.

If you read three pages

The argument in four sentences

You own the best-rated venue in Midrand and almost nobody can find it, book it, or see the rating. Nothing in the plan tries to change your product, because your product is the one thing already working.

The first two weeks are free, reversible and do not need us. Everything after that is worth more because those two weeks happened first.

What is still open

Anything still tagged as an assumption is yours to overturn.

Part 1 was built to be checked without you. Everything since has been rebuilt on your own documents, and the evidence trail for every figure sits in The Numbers under "The paper trail". Where a recommendation still rests on a judgement call rather than a counted number, it carries the Assumption tag, and the strategy call is where you confirm or overturn each one.

Updated 3 September: every open question now has an answer.

Jade has supplied the calendar, the enquiry counts, the conversion rates, the invoice distributions, the full six-figure invoice ledger, the live event schedule, real proposals with their email threads, and Letitia's review of the last ten lost enquiries. The plan's sequencing is confirmed. What remains is decisions, and they belong to the second strategy call. Start with The Briefing, built for that call.
