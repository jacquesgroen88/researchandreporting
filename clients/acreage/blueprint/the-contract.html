The First 90 Days

Everything else in the Blueprint is analysis. This page is the only one that changes anything. Fourteen moves across three months, each with a person and a date against it, because an action without an owner is just a conversation.

The Acreage + Good & Proper · agreed

How to use this page

The owner and start-date columns are deliberately blank. They get filled in live, on the call, by the two of you, because we cannot assign your people and a name written in by an agency is a name nobody agreed to. Type straight into the dotted fields, they save in this browser, and the print button produces a one-page record.

Everything in week one is free and reversible. Nothing in it needs us, a build, or a budget. The point of the first month is not to finish the plan. It is to prove the manual version works before anything gets automated, which is exactly what the Monday.com build skipped. Months two and three only make sense if month one actually happened.

Fill it in as you go. Entries save automatically to this browser.

The commitments

Fourteen moves, owned and dated.

Ordered by what the year-end booking window rewards, not by what is most interesting. The first month is about proving the manual version works. The second and third are about building on something that already runs.

MoveOwnerStartsDone meansNumber watched weekly
Days 1 to 7 · free, reversible, needs nobody outside the business
Follow-up rule on every open quote
Four of the last ten losses were silence. One nudge closed a 24-day-silent enquiry the same day.
 Every open quote has two scheduled touches, at 48 hours and 7 days, and no quote sits untouched. Quotes followed up, as a percentage of quotes sent
One shared enquiry sheet, both businesses
Nothing currently records why an enquiry failed, so nothing below can be judged.
 Date, channel, guest count, event date, estimated value, outcome, and one word for why if lost. Logged within one business day. Enquiries logged, against enquiries received
Reactivate every 2025 year-end client
76% of invoices are repeat, and etv came back unprompted after a long silence. The list already exists.
 Every client who ran a year-end function in 2024 or 2025 has been contacted personally about 2026. Replies, then enquiries, then booked value
Harvest the sixteen converting search terms
61 of your 205 conversions came from terms that are not keywords. Free, and it needs nobody's permission.
 All sixteen added as exact match, the singular "food truck johannesburg" among them, and the duplicate "food truck for hire" resolved to one campaign. Cost per conversion, blended
Quote validity and date-release terms
Letitia proposed this herself. It creates a reason to follow up and a reason to decide.
 Every quote carries a validity period and a tentative-hold release, and both are enforced. Quotes expiring untouched
Days 8 to 30 · inside the year-end booking window
Sign off and publish the function pack
The one loss with a recorded reason went to a venue with a more inclusive package. Prices in the worked example are ours and need your numbers.
 Per-head tiers agreed, priced by you, live as a page and forwardable as a PDF. Packs sent, and pack-to-booking rate
The Showcase open day
The 58-name ledger is the invitation list. You ran this once already and called it worth doing.
 Date set, invitations out, RSVP target agreed, photographer booked, follow-up sequence written before the day. RSVPs, attendees, then enquiries created
Repoint the corporate ads
71% of spend buys the crowd you are trying to leave, and the corporate campaign was cut on the wrong number.
 Corporate landing page live first, then the corporate terms switched on behind it, with conversions weighted by qualification. Not the other way round: corporate clicks cost R83 and have nowhere to land today. Qualified enquiries and their estimated value, not lead count
Decide the below-minimum policy
Accolades surcharges small bookings instead of declining them. A 20-guest enquiry still became R14,940.
 A written rule: decline, or accept with a surcharge, and at what threshold. Applied consistently by whoever answers. Below-minimum enquiries converted rather than declined
Days 31 to 60 · building on something that already runs
The functions page and forwardable pack, live
Your buyer has to forward something to whoever signs. Right now there is nothing to forward.
 Functions page live with capacity per space, what is included, an indicative per-head range, and a one-page pack that downloads. Pack downloads, and enquiries that mention it
Fix the big-proposal assembly line
Single-vendor quotes ship in hours. The six-figure ones take days, through a spreadsheet and a Word document.
 A multi-vendor proposal can be produced in under an hour by someone who is not Andrew, from a template that costs and builds itself. Hours from enquiry to proposal, on jobs over R100,000
Cross-referral rule in operation
134 of 147 Good & Proper events ran at someone else's venue, and 42 of 55 Acreage functions were catered by someone else.
 A written guest-count band and date test, applied to every enquiry in both businesses, with the internal venue price agreed. Events where both businesses were quoted
Review engine running properly
4.7 stars on 68 reviews beats the neighbour's 4.4, but 29 Good & Proper reviews loses to a single food truck's 110.
 Every completed event triggers a review request, and every review gets a reply. New reviews per month, per business
Days 61 to 90 · proving it, then compounding it
Apply the new pricing to the next booking window
Peak and dead dates cost the same today. Nothing already sold gets touched.
 Peak, shoulder and quiet rates published internally and quoted consistently from an agreed date forward. Average value per booking, by season
Automate only what the manual version proved
The R80,000 CRM failed because it was built before anyone knew what the process was.
 The enquiry sheet, the follow-up rule and the acknowledgement are automated. Nothing else, yet. Time from enquiry to first reply
The 90-day review, and the next quarter
Everything above either moved a number or it did not. This is where you find out and decide what happens next.
 Conversion, response time and booked value compared against the starting position. Next quarter's moves agreed, including the second Showcase. All four numbers, against day one
