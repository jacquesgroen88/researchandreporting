The Seven Figure Blueprint

A short, intensive strategy process that turns two good businesses running on manual effort into two businesses built to scale. We do this first, on purpose. Pushing traffic to a website that does not convert, or automating a process that is broken, only makes the problem faster. The Blueprint fixes the foundation so everything we build on top of it actually compounds.

What it is, and what it isn't

It is a paid, structured strategy intensive that produces the actual operating plan for both businesses: who you serve, how you sound, what we build, in what order, and what it should return. It is not a sales call, a generic proposal, or a document that ends up in a drawer. It is the thinking that every future rand of build and ad spend depends on, done properly once.

Why it exists

Right now the strategy lives in your head. The Blueprint gets it out, onto paper, measured and structured, so that when we build your booking automation, your review engine, your AI email assistant and your new website, every one of them is pointed at the same clearly defined customer, in the same voice, chasing the same numbers. By the end there should be no open questions about who you serve, how you sound, or what we are building next.

Everything included

  • Onboarding questionnaire to map both businesses before we speak
  • Two strategy calls: a kickoff and a full presentation of the finished Blueprint, both transcribed and kept on record
  • A full diagnostic of both businesses with the gaps and leaks costed
  • The written Blueprint: your three-month roadmap with priorities and metrics
  • A brand voice and visual language guide for each business
  • A first-look direction for your new website, reflecting the venue's quality
  • A prioritised systems rollout plan: booking automation, review engine, AI email assistant
  • A live presentation of your Blueprint, walked through decision by decision, not just emailed over
1

Deep dive & data

You complete the questionnaire in this pack, then we run a 45 to 60 minute onboarding call. Every call is fully transcribed and stored, so nothing gets lost and every future conversation builds on the last.

2

Diagnosis

We map both businesses end to end, find the gaps and the leaks (manual booking, stagnant reviews, repetitive enquiries, a website that undersells you) and attach real numbers to each one.

3

We build the Blueprint

We turn the diagnosis into a structured three-month roadmap: your target audience, your brand voice and visual language, the systems rollout in priority order, and the direction for your new website with a first look at what it becomes.

4

The presentation call

We present the finished Blueprint back to you on a dedicated call, walking through every decision and the reasoning behind it, and align on priorities together. You see the whole strategy laid out and explained, not just emailed a document.

5

Rollout

You leave with the full scope and a phased plan, so we either build it for you immediately or you have the clarity to run pieces yourself.

What you walk away with

  • A documented ideal customer for each business, who you want and who you do not
  • Your brand voice and visual language, written down, so every future asset sounds and looks like you
  • A three month roadmap with priorities and metrics, not a vague wish list
  • A first look at your new website direction, built to reflect the quality of the venue
  • A prioritised systems plan: booking automation, review engine, AI email assistant, in the order that pays off fastest

By the end you should have no open strategic questions left. That is the whole point. The Blueprint is the part that makes every build after it actually land: it turns guesswork into a plan you can measure, it is a once-off rather than a retainer, and most of what it saves in wasted ad spend and redone work pays it back inside the first few months.
