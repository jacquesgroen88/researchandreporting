✦ The offer

The Seven Figure Blueprint

A short, intensive strategy process that turns two good businesses running on manual effort into two businesses built to scale. We do this first, on purpose. Pushing traffic to a website that doesn't convert, or automating a broken process, only makes the problem faster. The Blueprint fixes the foundation so everything we build on top of it compounds.

What it is, and what it isn't

It is a paid strategy intensive that produces the actual operating plan for both businesses: who you serve, how you sound, what we build, in what order, and what it should return. It is not a sales call or a document that ends up in a drawer. It's the thinking every future rand of build and ad spend depends on, done properly once.

Everything included

  • Onboarding questionnaire to map both businesses before we speak
  • Two strategy calls: a kickoff and a full presentation of the finished Blueprint, both transcribed
  • A full diagnostic of both businesses with the gaps and leaks costed
  • The written Blueprint: your three-month roadmap with priorities and metrics
  • A brand voice and visual language guide for each business
  • Creative direction and a first look at your new website
  • A prioritised systems rollout plan: booking automation, review engine, AI email assistant
