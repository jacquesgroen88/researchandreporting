The spaces

From Intimate Gatherings to Events for Thousands.

The Acreage offers a number of distinct spaces across the estate, each available individually or as part of the whole venue. From the restaurant and its adjoining lawns to the lakeside lapa and expansive open grounds, you are afforded the flexibility to shape the venue around your event.

01

Full Venue

Full venue hire provides you with access to a beautiful restaurant and adjoining lawns, the lakeside lapa and surrounding lawns, as well as two additional expansive lawned areas, suitable for large-scale events.

With capacity for up to 5,000 guests, the full venue gives you the freedom to create an event across the entire estate.

CapacityUp to 5,000 guests
02

Restaurant Venue

An elegant terraced space overlooking the racecourse, with the option to extend onto the adjoining tented lawned area.

The restaurant and tent can each be hired separately or together.

Restaurant150 guests
10 × 12 m stretch tent100 to 150 guests
03

Restaurant Lawns

Rolling lawns cascade down from the restaurant, which can be used to extend the restaurant area or hired as a separate event space.

Capacity300 to 400 guests
04

Lakeside Lapa

A private lapa and bar overlooking the water, opening onto expansive lawns for larger gatherings. Intimate on its own, with plenty of space around it to host a much larger event.

Can be extended with hired stretch tents.

Lapa30 to 90 guests
Surrounding lawnsUp to 3,000 guests
05

Entrance Lawns

A generous lawned area beneath established trees, offering plenty of shade and open space for an event.

Capacity300 to 400 guests
06

Top Lawns

An additional lawned area available to extend the footprint of larger events, providing extra space for activities, activations or other event requirements, or for additional parking.

Not available for hire as a standalone event space.
