What the current copy does

  • It sells hire, but Good & Proper only does full catering. Someone wanting a single truck for a 30-person party reads it as a fit, clicks, and costs money.
  • No guest minimum appears anywhere, so nothing filters out small events before the click.
  • The corporate ad repeats one idea five ways (“Food Trucks: Corporate Events”, “Corporate Event: Food Trucks”, and so on) and lands on the homepage, not a corporate page.
  • Two of your lines do most of the work: We’ll Handle Everything and Single Price Per Head. Both are kept.

What we need from you

  1. The guest minimum. Is 80 guests right all year, with 150 from October to December? The ads say 80+; the season figure can go on the corporate page.
  2. Truck-only hire. Confirm you never hire out a truck without the catering. Two lines say so plainly, which is what stops paying for the wrong clicks.
  3. 15+ years. Your current ad claims it. Keep it only if it is accurate.
  4. Several trucks, one invoice. Every proposal we have seen bills the client once for all vendors. Confirm that is always the case.
  5. Where you travel. Gauteng only, or Pretoria and further? This decides which area searches to exclude.
  6. A price per head. Would you show a starting price per head? Ads with a price filter hardest, but only with your yes.
  7. Weddings and birthdays. They sit on the exclude list. Remove them if you want 80+ guest weddings and parties.

Once you approve: we load both ads and the exclusion list, pause the old ads after a two-week overlap, and report on qualified enquiries (80+ guests, in your area, full catering) rather than on clicks. Our access to the Good & Proper ads account is view-only today, so loading it needs edit access.
