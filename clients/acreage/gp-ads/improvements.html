The audit found an account that is tidy but aimed at the wrong target. This document sets out what to change, in the order it should change, who does each part, and how to tell whether it worked. Nothing here asks for more budget until the measurement is fixed.

The three changes that matter most

Count real enquiries, not tapsThe bidding currently learns mostly from taps on the phone number. Until it learns from submitted forms and real calls, every other change is judged against the wrong number.
Sell catering for 80+ guestsThe ads say "hire". The business sells catering with a guest minimum. Saying so in the ads stops paying for clicks the website then turns away.
Give corporate its own pageCorporate events are the most valuable bookings and have no page of their own. Both current pages take about 15 seconds to show on a phone.

Phase 1 · Week 1

Fix the measurement

Everything after this depends on the account counting the right thing. These changes are small and should be done together.

  • Reconcile 90 days of enquiries

    Good & Proper1 hour

    Export every enquiry received from 12 Aug to 9 Nov (form, phone, WhatsApp, email), with date, source if known, guest count, and whether it booked.

    Why: GA4 recorded 60 form submissions and the Google Ads tag recorded 17 for the same period. The real list settles which one is right and gives the first true cost per enquiry.

  • Change which conversions the bidding learns from

    Ads manager30 min

    Make primary: the website form (counted once per person), calls from ads of 60 seconds or more, and calls to the website forwarding number of 60 seconds or more. Move to secondary: the phone-link tap, the GA4 form event, and the individual staff call-click events.

    Why: 112 of the 172 primary conversions in 90 days were taps on a phone link. Google's own call tracking logged 1 call from ads in the same period. The setup counts every tap, so one person tapping twice is two conversions.

  • Add a thank-you page and fire the form conversion on it

    Web developer2 hours

    Send every form submission to a /thank-you/ page and trigger the Google Ads conversion from that page load. Then submit one test enquiry and confirm it appears in Google Ads within 24 hours.

    Why: The form currently shows an inline message, which is harder to track reliably and is the likely reason the two form counts disagree.

  • Add a WhatsApp button and track it as secondary

    Web developer1 hour

    A floating WhatsApp button on every page, wired to the WhatsApp event already set up in the tag manager.

    Why: Many South African buyers prefer WhatsApp to a form. The tracking for it already exists and is waiting for a button.

  • Move the conversion goals into this account

    Ads managerGood & Proper1 hour

    The account shows its conversion goals are managed from a manager account. Ask for them to be set at the account level, so the setup belongs to Good & Proper.

    Why: If the manager account is ever unlinked, the conversion setup and the learning the bidding has built on it go with it.

Phase 2 · Weeks 1 to 3

Point the ads at the right buyer

  • Rewrite the ads around catering for events of 80+ guests

    Ads managerHalf a day

    Put the guest minimum and "catering" in at least one pinned headline or the first description. Keep the word "hire" in some headlines, because buyers use it loosely to mean catering. Let the copy do the filtering. Example copy is further down.

    Why: "Food truck hire" is the biggest keyword the account has ever had (R67.7k). The website says Good & Proper only does catering, does not hire out trucks on their own, and needs at least 80 guests (150 from October to December). A searcher planning a 30-person party pays for nothing and costs a click.

  • Add a negative keyword list for searches that can never book

    Ads manager1 hour

    Shared list across both campaigns: rental, buying and building trucks, weddings, small private parties, single-product trucks and out-of-area places. Starting list further down.

    Why: In 90 days, 128 search terms with no conversion cost R6,148, about R2,000 a month. That is only the visible part; Google hides about a third of search-term spend.

  • Keep the corporate campaign to corporate searches

    Ads manager1 hour

    Add the generic hire and food-truck terms as negatives in the corporate campaign, so those searches go to the main campaign. Add corporate keywords: year-end function catering, company family day catering, conference catering Johannesburg, team building catering, product launch catering.

    Why: The corporate campaign's biggest search term is "food truck for hire" (R1,558 in 90 days, about R36 a click). The main campaign buys the same search for about R20. Searches that say "corporate" cost only R123 in the same period.

  • Let Google pick the better ads

    Ads manager15 min

    Change ad rotation from "rotate indefinitely" to "optimise". Replace the four ads rated Poor, three of which are in the corporate campaign. Remove the age, gender, device and schedule bid adjustments that Maximize Conversions mostly ignores. Keep the location exclusions.

    Why: The Poor-rated corporate ads spent R3,732 in 90 days. Forced even rotation stops the bidding from favouring the ads that work.

  • Fill out the ad assets

    Ads manager1 hour

    Sitelinks to Corporate events, Menus, Our trucks and Request a quote (once those pages exist). Callouts such as "Minimum 80 guests", "Gauteng-wide" and "Full-service catering". A structured snippet listing event types. Check what is already live first; the audit did not review assets in detail.

    Why: Assets make the ad bigger and pre-qualify the click at no extra cost.

Phase 3 · Weeks 2 to 6

Build landing pages that match the search

  • Speed up the two existing pages now

    Web developer1 day

    Compress and resize images, serve them in a modern format, defer non-essential scripts in the tag manager, and remove render-blocking files. Target: main content visible in under 3 seconds on a phone and page weight under 1.5 MB.

    Why: Both pages score 38 and 39 on mobile Lighthouse, take about 15 seconds to show their main content on a throttled phone, and weigh 3.3 MB. Google rates landing page experience Below average on 8 of the 9 scored keywords, which raises the cost of every click.

  • Build a corporate events page

    Web developerGood & Proper3 to 5 days

    Its own URL, for example /corporate-events/, aimed at year-end functions, family days, conferences and launches. Point every corporate ad here. Spec further down.

    Why: Corporate is the highest-value booking and currently lands on the general homepage, with the form near the bottom.

  • Build a food truck catering Johannesburg page

    Web developer2 to 3 days

    Aimed at the main campaign's searches: food truck hire, food trucks Johannesburg, food truck catering. Says plainly what is offered, the minimum, and the area served. Replaces /event-catering/ as a landing page.

    Why: /event-catering/ is a near copy of the homepage, so the site effectively has one page for every search. A page that repeats the searcher's words in its heading helps Quality Score directly.

  • Shorten the form

    Web developer1 hour

    Move it near the top. Ask for name, phone, email, event date and guest count. Make Company optional except on the corporate page.

    Why: The current form has 5 required fields including Company, which puts off private buyers. A guest-count field qualifies the lead before anyone calls back.

Phase 4 · From week 6

Then grow it

  • Feed booked value back to Google Ads

    Good & ProperAds managerMonthly, 30 min

    Each month, upload booked events with their value against the click that produced them (offline conversion import, using the click ID captured by the form). Once there are enough, move the bidding to value-based.

    Why: Today a 30-person enquiry and a 1,200-person family day count as the same one conversion. Values teach the bidding to prefer the corporate search.

  • Decide on budget with clean numbers

    Good & ProperDecision

    After four to six weeks of clean tracking, compare cost per real enquiry and cost per booking by campaign. Raise budget only where the cost per booking is acceptable.

    Why: The main campaign loses 20% of its possible impressions to budget, so there is room. Budgets have not changed since November 2024. More money into today's setup would mostly buy more phone-number taps.

  • Plan the year-end season early

    Ads managerGood & ProperYearly

    Start corporate year-end ads in August, when functions are being planned, and switch copy to the 150-guest minimum from October. Consider a separate year-end budget for August to November.

    Why: The minimum rises in the busiest season, so the copy has to change with it or the account pays for enquiries it cannot take.

What to track each month

  • Real enquiries from the enquiry list, split by campaign
  • Qualified enquiries: 80+ guests, in the service area, catering not truck-only hire
  • Booked events and booked value, corporate and private separately
  • Cost per qualified enquiry and cost per booking, the two numbers that decide budget
  • Impression share lost to budget on the main campaign, as the signal for when more budget would help

Questions for Good & Proper

  1. Can you export every enquiry from 12 August to 9 November, with how it came in, the guest count and whether it booked?
  2. Are the minimums still 80 guests, and 150 from October to December? Can they appear in the ads?
  3. Which areas will you travel to? Pretoria searches are currently getting clicks and no enquiries.
  4. Is there a price-per-head range you are comfortable showing on the site or in the ads?
  5. Where are bookings recorded, and what is a typical booking worth for corporate and for private events?
  6. Are you happy for the conversion goals to be moved into your own Google Ads account?
  7. Who builds and maintains the website, and can they take on the speed work and two new pages?

What this does not promise

  • No forecast of extra bookings. Without the real enquiry list there is no reliable baseline to forecast from.
  • The R2,000 a month on non-converting searches is measured against conversions that are mostly phone taps. Some of those searches may have produced real calls that were never tracked.
  • Landing page speed figures come from one lab test on a throttled mobile profile, not from real visitor data.

Based on Google Ads account 455-376-7888 and the website goodandproperfoodtrucks.co.za, both reviewed on 10 Nov 2026. Full evidence is in the companion audit.
