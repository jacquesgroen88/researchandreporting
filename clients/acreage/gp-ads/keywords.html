Where they work against you

  • “party” and “parties” are excluded on both campaigns. That blocks every “year end party catering”, “office party” and “staff party” search, which are the buyers in October to December.
  • The shared list is a general template, not one written for a caterer. It excludes “conference”, “staff”, “office”, “work” and “group”, so “conference catering” and “work function catering” never see an ad.
  • The corporate campaign excludes its own buyers. “office party catering”, “hiring a food truck for an event” and “food truck catering” are all on its exclusion list.
  • 113 exclusions sit in the paused Mobile Food Trucks ad group, many of them other trucks by name, so they protect nothing. A name excluded in one ad group still shows in the others.
  • Trailer hire gets through. “food trailer hire”, “mobile food trailer for hire” and “food trailers in johannesburg” cost R79 last week.
