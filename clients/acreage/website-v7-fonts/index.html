Ten pairings to choose from

Every one of these is the full homepage with nothing changed but the type, so you are comparing letters and not layouts. They all work the same way: a readable serif carries the sentence, and one word in each section is set in a cursive.

Open any card to see the whole page. The bar at the top of each one lets you jump straight to another.
