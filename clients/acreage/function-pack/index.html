Welcome

An Estate Made for Gathering

The Acreage is an event venue and restaurant set across acres of lawn, trees and water in Randjesfontein, Midrand. It is close enough to Sandton and Pretoria to be easy for everyone, and far enough from the city to feel like a day out.

Each space can be hired on its own or combined, so the estate shapes itself around your event, whether that is a team lunch under a stretch tent or a festival on the lawns.
