Where the Budget Goes

Mostly Functions, Some Restaurant

Functions

A single year-end function or wedding is worth more than several months of advertising, so functions get most of the budget. These clicks cost more, about R14 to R59 for a year-end search, because other venues want them too. That's fine: one booking covers the spend.

The Restaurant

"Restaurants in midrand" is searched 8,100 times a month and a click costs R1 to R11, so a small amount buys a lot of local visibility. We'd only show these ads near the estate and only while the kitchen is open, so nobody clicks at 8pm and finds you closed.

You pay Google directly for the clicks, with no mark-up from us. Click numbers are estimates from Google's bid ranges; the first fortnight will give us the real cost.

As a rough guide, if one in twenty function clicks becomes an enquiry, the starting budget brings in two to four function enquiries a month, and the recommended level six to twelve.

The new-account credit

Google offered a R6,000 credit for a new account opened through its offer link. It comes with a minimum spend, so we'll confirm the terms when the account is opened and use it in the first month.

Measuring It

What Counts as a Result

  • Function enquiries, weighted by size. The enquiry form asks how many guests are expected. A 1,500-guest enquiry is worth far more to you than a lunch for twelve, so it counts for more, and Google learns to look for more of the big ones.
  • Calls from the ads that last longer than a minute.
  • WhatsApp and email taps on the website.
  • Cost per function enquiry. If it climbs too high, we change the ads or the searches before spending more.
Timing

The First Eight Weeks

  • Before launchThe account opened, the new website live on acreagevenue.co.za, the enquiry form set up to record enquiries, and your Google listing linked so the ads can show your address.
  • Week 1Your name and functions go live, led by year-end functions and the Open Day on Thursday 29 October, with weddings and team days alongside.
  • Week 3We go through every search that triggered an ad, block the ones that wasted money and move budget to whatever is bringing enquiries.
  • Mid NovemberThe year-end ads change to "a few December dates left". Christmas lunch is added if you open on the 25th.
  • DecemberFunctions spending eases off as companies finish booking. Restaurant ads cover the school holidays.
  • January2027 year-end functions, and weddings for couples who got engaged over the holidays.
