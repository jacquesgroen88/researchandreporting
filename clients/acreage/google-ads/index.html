The Google Ads Plan

Social puts The Acreage in front of people who weren't looking. Google Ads does the opposite: it answers people who are already searching for a year-end venue, a team-day venue or breakfast in Midrand, at the moment they're deciding. This is how we'd set it up, what it would cost, and what we need from you to start.

It runs in its own account in The Acreage's name, separate from the Good & Proper ads, and you own it outright. Leave a note on anything you'd change.

The Idea

Buy the Searches That Turn Into Functions

The money is in functions. One booked year-end or team day pays for months of advertising, while a breakfast table is worth a few hundred rand. So most of the budget goes where the bookings are worth the most.

What We Learned From the Good & Proper Account

Since January 2024, most of that spend bought cheap, general "food truck" searches, and every enquiry counted the same: a birthday phone call and a 2,000-guest corporate enquiry were both "one conversion". So the system optimised for volume, not value. We won't repeat that here.

What We'll Do Differently

Every enquiry is scored by its guest count, straight from the dropdown on the enquiry form, so a 1,500-guest enquiry counts many times more than a table of twelve. Over time Google learns to find the bigger events, not just more of them.

Searches a month, South Africa, September 2025 to August 2026. The rand range is Google's estimate of a top-of-page bid. Two findings: Corporate Mondays has no search volume at all (nobody searches "corporate breakfast venue"), so it's sold through email, WhatsApp and social rather than Google. And weddings are the biggest venue searches on the list, so they run from launch rather than waiting for January.

"Conference" is blocked on purpose: the venue doesn't offer conference set-ups or AV, as we agreed on 21 September, so those clicks would be wasted. "Food truck" searches stay with the Good & Proper account, so the two accounts never bid against each other.

How We'll Know It's Working

What Counts

  • Function enquiries, scored by size. The enquiry form's guest-count answer sets the score, so bigger enquiries count for more.
  • Calls from the ads that last longer than a minute, to Letitia or the restaurant.
  • WhatsApp and email taps from the website.
  • Cost per enquiry for functions, kept well below what a booked function is worth. If it rises too high, we change the ads or the searches, not the budget.
