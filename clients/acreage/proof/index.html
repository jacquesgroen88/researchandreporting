Question two

Why the reviews you found were foreign and anonymous

Two separate things were going on, and one of them is genuinely our fault.

The reviews are foreign because the work was foreign

For most of the last decade the client base was United States and United Kingdom. A UK engineering plastics manufacturer, a US housing organisation, US franchise groups. The South African client base is the newer half of the business. So the testimonials genuinely are from overseas companies. That part is not a trick, it is just the history.

They are anonymous because of how the site was built, and that is a mistake

The case studies on our website are labelled things like "Financial Services" and "B2B Services" with no company attached. We did that out of caution about naming clients. The effect on you was the opposite of what we intended. It read as though we had something to hide, and a prospect doing exactly the diligence you did has no way to verify any of it. That is a fair hit and we are fixing it.

Where the real, named, third-party review history is. There is a public work history with more than sixty completed contracts going back to 2018, most of them rated five out of five by the client, with written feedback attached. It sits on Upwork, which is why it does not show up when you search for JCE Media reviews. That was our oversight, not a gap in the record.

The direct link is in the covering email. Nothing on it is written by us, and none of it can be edited by us.

Also true. The follower counts are low and it does look odd for a marketing business. The things that would actually tell you whether we can do this are the live sites above, the client references, and the public contract history. We would rather be judged on those.

Question seven

Whether it is worth paying us instead of finishing it yourself

You said you have been working through your visual and verbal identity for weeks and need to know it is worth spending on rather than just finishing it yourself. That deserves a straight answer rather than a sales one.

When you should just finish it yourself

If what is left is mostly taste, and you already know what you want it to look and sound like, then you do not need us for that part. You are clearly particular, you have been thinking about it for weeks, and a person who knows their own taste that well usually gets there. Paying someone to arrive at the answer you already have is a waste of your money.

When it is worth paying for

The identity work is one of five deliverables, and on its own it is not the reason to do this. The reason is the other four. Specifically, you have two businesses, one of which is spending roughly ten thousand rand a month on Google Ads with unclear return. Nothing about finishing your own brand guidelines tells you whether that spend is working, where the enquiries are leaking, or what to build first.

If the audit finds that the ad spend is underperforming by even a quarter, that single finding pays for the Blueprint within a few months. If it finds the spend is fine, you will have paid to learn that it is fine, which is worth knowing before you scale it.

A genuine alternative. If the full Blueprint is more than you want to commit to right now, we will run the free audit on the Good & Proper ad account first, on its own, with nothing attached to it. You get the findings whether or not you go further. That is a smaller way to test whether the thinking is any good, and it costs you nothing but access.
