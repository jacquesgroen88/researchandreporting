Weddings

A Country Wedding, Close to Home

The Acreage is a wedding venue in Midrand, Gauteng, set on a country estate of sweeping lawns, established trees and water. It feels a long way from the city, yet it is twenty minutes from Sandton and about the same from Pretoria, so guests arrive easily.

Hold the ceremony on the lawn and the reception in the restaurant or under a stretch tent, with the catering and bars handled by our own team. Tell us your date and how many guests you are planning for, and we will show you how the day could work.

  • Ceremony on the lawn, under the trees
  • Reception in the restaurant or under stretch tents
  • In-house catering and fully serviced mobile bars
  • Room for small weddings and very large ones
  • Back-up power and secure parking for guests
  • Twenty minutes from Sandton and Pretoria

Good to Know

Questions We Are Often Asked

How many wedding guests can you host?

The restaurant seats up to 150 and the stretch tent 100 to 150. Larger weddings can extend across the lawns, up to 5,000 across the whole estate.

Can we have the ceremony and reception in one place?

Yes. The ceremony can be on the lawn and the reception in the restaurant or under a stretch tent, all on the same estate.

Do you do the catering and the bar?

Yes, both are in-house, from plated meals and canapés to braais, food trucks and mobile bars.

Can we visit before we decide?

Yes. Send us your date and we will arrange a walk-through.

Are you taking 2027 bookings?

Yes. Send us your preferred date and guest count and we will come back to you with availability.
