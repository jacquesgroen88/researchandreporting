The Restaurant

Breakfast All Day, on a Country Estate

The Acreage is a restaurant in Midrand on a country estate in Randjesfontein, twenty minutes from Sandton. Breakfast is served all day, so there is no rush to arrive before eleven, and lunch can be on the terrace or out on the lawns.

Every coffee is a double shot, there is a kids menu for under-12s, and dogs are welcome. It is the kind of place you come for breakfast and stay for the afternoon.

  • Breakfast served all day
  • Tables inside, on the terrace and on the lawns
  • Dogs welcome
  • A kids menu for under-12s
  • Every coffee a double shot
  • Larger groups welcome, please book ahead

Good to Know

Questions We Are Often Asked

Do you serve breakfast all day?

Yes, breakfast is served all day, until the kitchen closes.

What are your opening hours?

Tuesday to Sunday and public holidays, from 7am, with the kitchen open until 5pm. Closed on Mondays.

Are dogs allowed?

Yes, dogs are welcome.

Is there a kids menu?

Yes, there is a kids menu for under-12s, and plenty of lawn to play on.

Do I need to book?

Larger groups are welcome but please book ahead on WhatsApp, 066 272 6862. We are cashless, so card payments only.
