Team Days

Room to Get Out of the Office

Team building days work best with space, and The Acreage has acres of it: rolling lawns, shade under established trees and a lapa by the water, on a private country estate in Midrand, near Johannesburg and Pretoria.

Use the lawns for games and challenges, then sit down to lunch under the stretch tent or in the restaurant. Tell us who is running the activities and what they need, and we will plan the space around them. The catering, the bar and the set-up are ours.

  • Open lawns for games, challenges and activities
  • Shade under established trees
  • Lunch under a stretch tent or in the restaurant
  • Catering from platters to braais and food trucks
  • A mobile bar for the end of the day
  • Ample secure parking on site

Good to Know

Questions We Are Often Asked

How many people can you host for a team building day?

From around 30 to 5,000. Smaller teams can have the lapa or a section of lawn; larger companies can take the whole estate.

Can we bring our own team building company?

Yes. Tell us who is running the activities and what they need, and we will plan the lawns and the timing around them.

Is there shade and somewhere to eat under cover?

Yes. There are established trees on the lawns, a permanent stretch tent and the restaurant.

Do you cater?

Yes, in-house: platters, braais and spit braais, plated meals and food trucks, plus mobile bars.

How far is it from Sandton and Pretoria?

About twenty minutes from Sandton and around the same from Pretoria, five minutes from the Samrand interchange on the N1.
