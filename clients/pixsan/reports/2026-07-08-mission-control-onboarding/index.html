What to have ready for your go-live session

I build your Mission Control on my end first. Then we run two short sessions together to switch it on and get you comfortable running it. This checklist is everything to have ready so those sessions go start-to-finish without a hitch.

How it works

Three steps, one live call

STEP 1

You prep

Complete the Mission Control Intake and make sure you have admin access to the accounts below. About 30 to 45 minutes of your time.

STEP 2

I build

I set up all the files, your command center, starter skills and routines on my end, loaded with your intake.

STEP 3

We go live

Two short screen-share sessions. First we connect your tools and switch on your routines; a few days later we review, tune and train you on daily use.

Before anything else, please complete the Mission Control Intake. It produces the two files that give your AI its memory. Everything else on this page is account access.

How your access stays yours

Every connection is authorized by you, in your browser, on the call. I never see or store your passwords.

Each tool connects through its official, revocable authorization. You can disconnect any of it at any time from that tool's settings. And the whole system is documented and handed to you, so it is yours to keep and run.
