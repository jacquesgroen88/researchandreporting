Copy this into Claude or ChatGPT
You are helping me build "Mission Control," an internal operating system for my digital marketing agency, Pixsan Solutions. I am Sam Agia, the founder.

Your job in this conversation is to interview me and then produce two markdown files that will become the memory of my Mission Control:

1) pixsan-soul.md  — who Pixsan is
2) pixsan-goals.md — where Pixsan is going

Interview me ONE section at a time. Ask 2–4 questions per section, wait for my answers, ask a sharp follow-up if an answer is thin or vague, then move on. Keep it conversational and quick. Do not lecture me. When we have covered every section, generate both files as clean, well-structured markdown I can copy out.

Work through these sections in order:

A. IDENTITY — What is Pixsan in one line? What do you actually do for clients? What makes you different? What is the story of how and why you started it?

B. POSITIONING & VOICE — Who is your ideal client? How do you want to sound (words like: honest, plain-English, premium, friendly)? What do you never want to sound like? Any phrases or values you live by?

C. TEAM — Who is on the team, their names and roles, and who owns what? Who should the system route different kinds of work to?

D. CLIENTS — For each active client: name, industry, what you do for them, main contact, current status, and what a win looks like for them. (Go client by client.)

E. TOOLS & OPERATIONS — What platforms do you run on (CRM, ads, email, docs)? What does a normal week look like? What reports or check-ins happen, and how often?

F. GOALS & NORTH STAR — What is the one number or outcome that matters most right now? Revenue targets (month/quarter/year)? Growth goals? What does winning look like 12 months out?

G. PRIORITIES & CONSTRAINTS — What are the top 3 priorities this quarter? What is getting in the way? Anything the system should always watch, protect, or never do?

When finished, output pixsan-soul.md (sections A–E) and pixsan-goals.md (sections F–G) as two separate, clearly-labeled code blocks. Then ask me if anything needs adjusting.
