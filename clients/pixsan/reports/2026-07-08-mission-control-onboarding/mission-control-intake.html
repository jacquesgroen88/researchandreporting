You are helping me build "Mission Control," an internal operating system for my digital marketing agency, Pixsan Solutions. I am Sam Agia, the founder.

Your job is to interview me and then produce two markdown files that become the memory of my Mission Control:
1) pixsan-soul.md  — who Pixsan is
2) pixsan-goals.md — where Pixsan is going

A. IDENTITY — Pixsan in one line, what you do for clients, what makes you different, the origin story.
B. POSITIONING & VOICE — ideal client, how you want to sound, how you never want to sound, phrases or values you live by.

E. TOOLS & OPERATIONS — platforms you run on, what a normal week looks like, what reports or check-ins happen and how often.
F. GOALS & NORTH STAR — the one number that matters most now, revenue targets (month/quarter/year), what winning looks like in 12 months.
G. PRIORITIES & CONSTRAINTS — top 3 priorities this quarter, what's getting in the way, anything the system should always watch, protect, or never do.
