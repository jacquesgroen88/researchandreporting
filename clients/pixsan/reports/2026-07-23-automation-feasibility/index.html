Limits worth knowing
One platform constraint, one item that is an outcome rather than a task.

Brand Brain is the foundation, not item two

You listed it midway down two separate lists. It should be built first, because every other item on your list reads from it.

With it, keyword research knows the positioning, content sounds like that client instead of like AI, metadata matches the actual offer, and the monthly report can explain why a number moved rather than just that it did. Without it, everything downstream comes out generic, which is the exact problem you are trying to solve.

Your website build is one pipeline, not six tasks

Items two through seven of New Website Builds are a single chain. Today each step is done by hand and carried to the next. It runs end to end, and you review at the finish.

Brand Brain› Keyword research› Nav and IA› Content and URLs› Metadata› Live via RankMath

The SEO spreadsheet is an artifact of doing this by hand. It exists so a person can carry metadata from research into the site. Once the last step writes to RankMath directly, the spreadsheet is optional.
