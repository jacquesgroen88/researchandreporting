Skills & routines

It works while you sleep

Beyond answering when you ask, Mission Control runs on its own schedule. We install skills, the things it knows how to do, and routines, the things it does automatically on a timer. Work happens overnight and is waiting for you in the morning.

Morning brief

Every morning, a summary of every client, what moved, what needs attention and the day's priorities, sitting there when you log in.

Consistent client reporting

Pulls each client's Google and Meta numbers on schedule and drafts a clean, on-brand report, the same standard for every client, every month. Reporting you can stand behind and charge for, without the manual scramble.

Watchers & alerts

Keeps an eye on spend, performance drops and stalled work, and flags anything off before it turns into a problem.

Follow-up chasers

Nudges the open threads and tasks that are waiting on someone, so nothing quietly slips through the cracks.

You are asleep in San Diego while it runs. You wake up to the work already done.
