Pipeline Architecture

Two pipelines in GHL. We build the Cold Outbound feeder. The Mako main pipeline stays untouched except for the graduation trigger.

🔨

New — We Build This: Cold Outbound Pipeline (Kat manages)

1

Targeted

Loaded and assigned to Kat. No email sent yet. Starting point for every contact.

2

Email Sent

First outreach email sent from Kat's MS365 inbox inside GHL. GHL auto-logs the send.

–

Engaged / Opened (Removed)

Dropped per client approval. Open tracking pixel removed. Tracks replies only.

3

Follow-Up Sent

One follow-up after 5 business days. GHL reminder fires; Kat sends the email.

★

Replied — Positive

Manual by Kat. Triggers graduation into Mako pipeline + intro email + rep notification. See Section 6.

✕

Replied — Not Interested

Kat marks contacts who decline or ask to be removed. No further automation.

↻

No Response — Nurture

No reply after follow-up. Hold for future re-engagement waves in 3–6 months.

⊘

Bounced

Hard/soft bounces. Flagged by GHL or Kat. Excluded from all future sends.

✅

Existing — Untouched: Mako Main Pipeline (Matt and Johnny manage)

★

New Lead ← Graduates here

Positive reply from Cold Outbound creates an opportunity here with full contact context attached.

2

Rep Contact Made

Matt or Johnny makes first direct contact with the prospect.

3

Qualification and Sampling

Discovery and sample process as per existing Mako workflow.

4

Quote Sent

Proposal issued to prospect.

5

PO Received

Purchase order confirmed.

✓

Closed Won

Deal closed. Revenue realised.

➜

The pipeline stays clean. 4,034 outreach contacts stay in the Cold Outbound pipeline. The Mako deal pipeline only receives contacts who have replied positively — it never jumps from ~200 to ~4,200 open deals.

6

Positive Reply Handoff Workflow

Manual trigger by Kat, automated execution by GHL. GHL cannot detect reply sentiment automatically — Kat reviews and tags.

1
GHL

Prospect replies positively

Reply arrives in GHL via the connected MS365 inbox. GHL logs the reply and surfaces it on the contact record in the conversation tab.

2
Kat

Kat reviews the reply

Kat reads the reply in GHL and judges it as a positive response — interest, request for information, meeting request, etc.

3
Kat

Kat moves contact to "Replied — Positive"

Manual stage move by Kat inside GHL. This is the trigger that fires all automation below. GHL cannot make this call — Kat owns the judgement.

4
GHL Automation

Intro email fires from Kat's mailbox

Pre-written email sent automatically from Kat's connected MS365 mailbox. Warms the prospect and introduces Matt Jones and/or Johnny Lincoln as the next point of contact. We write this template. Keeps the relationship tone — no cold handoff.

5
GHL Automation

Opportunity graduates into Mako pipeline

GHL creates a new opportunity in the Mako main pipeline at "New Lead" stage. Full contact record, outreach history, custom fields (source, segment, tier, why-they-fit) travel with it.

6
GHL Automation

Matt and Johnny receive internal notification

GHL fires an internal notification (email or GHL notification) to Matt Jones and Johnny Lincoln with the prospect name, outreach context, and a link to the new opportunity. They know what to expect before they pick up the phone.

7
Matt / Johnny

Sales team picks up the graduated lead

Matt or Johnny works the lead from New Lead in the Mako pipeline. Kat's involvement in this contact ends. The prospect has already received a warm intro so the first sales contact doesn't feel cold.

What Success Looks Like (Kat's Model)

  • 1,500 emails sent in 90 days (25/day steady state)
  • 15–25% reply rate = 225–375 replies
  • ~22–27% of replies are positive = 50–100 positive replies
  • 40–80% convert to meetings = 20–40 meetings
  • 15–20% close rate = 3–8 new customers
  • We own system reliability — not the pipeline projections
