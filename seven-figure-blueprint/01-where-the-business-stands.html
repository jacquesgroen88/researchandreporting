Read this correctly

The January to April 2026 flat line is not decline. Advertising was switched off deliberately because cost per acquisition had run away, and the business recovered to its best month ever within eight weeks of turning it back on. The real signal in this table is not the dip, it is that units per order has never gone above three and is now under two. Revenue moves with traffic. It has never moved with basket size.

The product nobody is selling

Smart Business Cards earn 2.1x per order and sell four at a time

Review devices are bought per premises, so demand caps at one or two. Business cards are bought per person, so a ten-person firm buys ten. That is why the Smart Business Card produces R2,423 per order against R1,148 for the flagship stand, and 4.0 units per order against 1.83. It received twelve orders in twelve months, while the term nfc business card sits in the Google Ads account as an excluded negative keyword after clicking through at 33%.

The franchise signal

Nobody has ever called a head office

A national beauty franchise appears in the order history eight times, across eight different branches, from six buyer addresses (a single R5,841 ten-stand order, the rest between R624 and R948). Four of the eight are on the group's own corporate email domain, and four landed in the last four weeks, including two branches ordered back to back by the same franchisee on a single day. Eight uncoordinated retail transactions from a group with roughly 200 branches. a national QSR group (multiple household fast-food brands) bought 22 stands in one order and separately sits in the pipeline with no value assigned. a national estate agency franchise and a retail services franchise each bought a single branch's worth.

This is a validated demand pattern with no sales motion attached to it. The franchisee proves the product to themselves at branch level, and the group never finds out. South Africa has more than 800 franchisor brands and over 30,000 outlets. ReviewTap has proof inside at least four of those networks and has monetised none of it.

The recurring line

R15,548 per month of transferable recurring revenue across seven contracts, roughly R187,000 of annual recurring revenue. The open pipeline in the Reviewtap System holds about R381,000 across 158 opportunities, with roughly 30 named upsell deals stalled between nine and twenty-four days. This is the number the sale price is built on, and it is the number the rest of this Blueprint is engineered to move.

The signal that arrived on its own

On 4 August 2026, a national restaurant group made contact about two national restaurant brands, roughly 23 sites. That is the largest inbound in the store's history, it came from a multi-site group, and it arrived without a single piece of the strategy in this document being built yet. The market is already asking for the thing ReviewTap does not currently sell.

Figures deliberately not used

  • The 4.8 star claim. Unverified, based on roughly 21 Google and 19 Facebook reviews. The live Google profile shows 4.9 across 29 reviews. Do not put 4.8 in any asset.
  • "1,000+ South African businesses". Not supported by 817 lifetime orders. Currently live in three ad creatives and needs removing.
  • "One refund ever". A defensible commercial story about exchanges, but Shopify records R948 of returns in July 2026 and R499 in June. It cannot be used as a financial claim, particularly in front of a serious counterparty.
