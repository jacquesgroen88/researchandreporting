Part 2 · The walkthrough, minute by minute Internal

How we present it

The order follows Hormozi's offer construction: diagnose the pain first, put the core and the price on the table, then stack the bonuses, and hold one risk reversal back for when they hesitate. Never discount: add a bonus instead.

Giorgio · 10 min

Their Google today

Screen-share their own page from the Google check. Reviews next to the place down the road, what a guest sees when they check prices, what December could look like if every guest was asked.

"You have 27 reviews. The Edwardian, just down the road, has 349. Let me show you what a guest sees."
Never: "we'll put your price on Google." We don't fix that yet.
Giorgio · 5 min

Discovery

Three questions. The answers set the size of the problem in their own words, and confirm which offer they route to.

"How many bookings come through Booking.com, and what does that commission cost you a year?" · "After a guest checks out, what happens next?" · "How do you take bookings: NightsBridge, your own system, or email and phone?"
Jacques · the next 10 walkthroughs

The core offer and the price

The one-sentence promise, the three core things, the price. Nothing else yet. Let the price land.

"Every guest you host this December is asked for a Google review the day they check out, every review gets answered, and every WhatsApp question gets a reply, Sundays included. It's R10,000 to set up, half now and half once it's live, then R5,999 a month."
Jacques

Stack the bonuses

Only after the price is out. Google profile rebuilt, Booking.com listing tidied, January win-back. The homepage and SEO bonus is limited to the first two places, which is true and is the scarcity.

Jacques

The guarantee, then the ask

State the one guarantee. Then urgency (13 Nov to be live for December) and scarcity (two places before mid-December). Then ask.

"If you let us ask every guest who checks out and you don't have at least 16 new Google reviews by 31 January, we keep running it free until you do. We can take two more places before December. Shall we start this week?"
Jacques · only if they hesitate

The held-back reversal, then the step down

First the setup refund. If it's still no, step down to Reviews + WhatsApp rather than cutting the Sprint price. Offer the December prepay to anyone who says yes.

"And if reviews and WhatsApp aren't live within 7 days of you giving us access, you get the setup fee back in full."

Why 2 reviews per room

Mid-December to end of January is about 45 days. At 70% occupancy and a 3-night average stay, an 8-room place has about 84 checkouts. If 20% of guests who are asked leave a review, that's about 17. Two per room (minimum 8) sits just under that, so we only pay out when something is actually broken.

The 20% is our assumption. Iso'Bella Vita's real before-and-after count calibrates it. Pull that before the first walkthrough.

The first two clients: pay only in months it works (open)

The first 2 Sprint clients pay the R10,000 setup as normal, but the R5,999 is billed only for months they hit their monthly review target, counted on their public Google listing. In exchange: a filmed 60-second result at the end of January and permission to use their name and numbers. That gives us the guesthouse case study we don't have. Paying us per result is not paying guests for reviews, so Google's rules aren't touched.
