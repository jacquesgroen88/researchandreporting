Welcome to The Homestead

A guest house above the beach, not a resort block

From the balconies you can see from Lucien Beach all the way down to Southbroom. Have coffee while the sun comes up over the ocean, watch the boats head out from the pier, then walk down to the beach after breakfast.

  • Standard, executive and family rooms, plus a Honeymoon Suite
  • Swimming pool and garden looking straight out to sea
  • Lounge, dining room and patio, all facing the ocean
  • Secure parking behind the gate

Breakfast

A proper buffet breakfast with the ocean in front of you

You're on holiday, so leave the stove alone. Every morning there are cereals, fresh fruit, yoghurt and juices, then eggs, beef, pork and chicken off the hot buffet.
