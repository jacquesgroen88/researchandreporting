The call script

1. Open (warm, short)

"Juan, thanks for making time. Quick one today, and first, thank you. You've paid every month without fail and the system's been busy, which is exactly what we wanted. I want to show you what that busy looks like and sort out one thing on the billing side."

2. Show the usage (make it about success)

"The bot is now answering thousands of WhatsApp messages and running AI replies all day, every day. Out of every restaurant and business we run on the system, Dot Com is about two thirds of all the message and AI usage. That's a good sign. It means your guests are using it instead of phoning."

Pause. Let them react. If they're pleased, let them say so.

3. Explain the cost (plain, no apology)

"Every one of those messages and AI replies has a cost from WhatsApp and the AI provider, and we pay it in dollars. When we set the pilot up, the R3,000 covered that comfortably. Two things changed. Your volume has grown by about a third since August, and from October WhatsApp started charging for every reply message, which used to be free, and the AI got more expensive. The usage cost went from about R380 a month in August to about R1,200 a month now."

4. The ask (say the number, then stop talking)

"So here's what I'd like to do. Your R3,000 founder rate stays exactly as it is, that's locked. We add one fixed line for AI and WhatsApp usage of R1,200 a month, starting with the invoice at the end of this month. Fixed, so it never jumps around on you, even if the volume or the exchange rate moves. Are you comfortable with that?"

Then stay quiet. Let them answer first.

5. If they say yes

"Great, thank you. I'll put it in writing today so Brendon has it, and it'll show as its own line on this month's invoice."

Send the confirmation email below the same day.

Close (either way)

"Two other things while I have you. The website we built is still sitting ready for you, and the review automation from phase two would be a quick win for Google ratings. Happy to talk about either whenever you're ready."

One line only. Today is about the usage fee.

Objections

"You said the price was locked."

"It is. The R3,000 doesn't move. The proposal allowed for usage fees if things went well beyond normal, with notice. What I didn't plan for was WhatsApp starting to charge for messages that were free when we priced this. I'm passing that on as one fixed number instead of a variable bill."

"Why now / why this month already?"

"Because the usage has kept climbing and I'd rather raise it now than send you a surprise later. If starting this month is too quick for your side, we can start on 1 December. I'd just like us to agree it today."

Your fallback. Offer it only if they push. The proposal says you notify in advance, so 1 December is the cleaner position if they resist.

"That's a lot. Can you do less?"

"R1,200 is roughly what it costs us, not a margin on top. What I can do is look at making the bot use fewer AI replies per conversation. If that brings the cost down over the next two months, I'll lower the line to match."

Floor if you need one: R1,000 a month. Do not go below cost.

"Can we just turn some of it off?"

"We can, but the AI replies are what take the bookings off your staff. Turning them down means more calls back on the phones. I'd rather keep it working and keep the cost fixed."

"We need to check with the owner."

"Of course. Can we agree that if I don't hear otherwise by Friday, it goes on this month's invoice? I'll send it all in writing today so Brendon can take it to him."
