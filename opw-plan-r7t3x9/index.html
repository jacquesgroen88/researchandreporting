The verdict first

Why it is a good idea
  • It creates JCE's own customers. A paid buyer of a marketing workshop can be marketed JCE's similar services, with an opt-out, under POPIA. That fixes the consent problem the research exposed in the Blueprint plan.
  • It pays to be seen. Buyers who watch you teach for 90 minutes are the warmest X-Ray and Blueprint leads you can get.
  • Cash this month, and a repeatable monthly event after that.
The honest risk
  • Five days is too short for TikTok to carry the first run alone. A new account rarely gets reach in its first week, and TikTok LIVE needs 1,000 followers.
  • So 15 October is the pilot. Fill it mostly from warm channels, use TikTok to start the account and test hooks with a small paid boost, and make the second run (Thursday 12 November) the one TikTok fills.
  • Your toolkit page has had no signups since July because nothing sent people to it. This plan exists so the workshop does not repeat that.

3 · The TikTok strategy

Account setup (Saturday, 30 minutes)

  • Handle: your name, not the agency (@jacquesgroenewald or @jacques.jce). People follow people. Name field: "Jacques | Marketing for SA owners".
  • Business account, category Professional Services. Bio: "I help SA business owners find where they're losing money. Free workshop resources below." Link: jcemedia.com/workshop.
  • Profile photo: your face, close crop, bright background.
  • Post the same videos to Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels. Same work, three times the reach.

Five content pillars

How every video is built

  • First 2 seconds: the hook spoken and on screen at the same time. No intro, no "hi guys".
  • One idea per video. 20 to 45 seconds. Captions burned in, large, in the middle of the frame.
  • Give the whole answer. The workshop sells the system and the doing, not the secret. Hormozi's rule: give away the what, sell the how.
  • End line: "This is box [3] of the one-page plan. I'm teaching all six live on Thursday at 7. Link in bio."
  • Comment bait that is genuinely useful: "Comment your industry and I'll tell you which box to fix first." Then reply to every comment with a video reply. Replies are the cheapest content you will ever make.

Paid boost

Use TikTok's in-app Promote on the two best-performing videos from Monday, aimed at website visits, South Africa, age 28 to 60, about R300 a day for three days (R900). Promote has lower minimums than Ads Manager, where an ad group needs about $20 a day. Pick the videos by watch-through rate after 24 hours, not by views. TikTok's own LIVE feature needs 1,000 followers, so the workshop runs on Zoom.

9 · Rules

  • Consent: buyers become JCE customers. An unticked consent box at checkout covers marketing beyond the event. Every follow-up has an opt-out. Nobody is messaged who did not give their number.
  • Register JCE Systems Group as a direct marketer with the National Consumer Commission before December (already in the Blueprint plan).
  • Name: never "The 1-Page Marketing Plan" and never the nine-square grid.
  • Claims: no income promises, no invented results, no fake countdown timers. "Pilot price" and a real date are enough urgency.
  • Real businesses only appear in content with their permission. Your own businesses are fair game.
  • Zoom: waiting room on, attendees muted on entry, recording announced at the start.
