Cottages

Montagu self-catering, with a garden of your own.

Each of Glen Eden's five cottages sits in its own enclosed garden with a terrace, a splash pool, a wood-fired hot tub and a covered braai. Inside there is a fully equipped kitchen, an indoor fireplace for the cold Klein Karoo nights, a smart TV and fresh towels and linen.

For two

Fish Eagle is a one-bedroom cottage with an en-suite shower, made for couples.

For families and friends

Honeybee and House on the Hill sleep four. House on the Hill has a front stoep with the best mountain views on the farm. Sugarbird sleeps six, with a separate lounge. Blue Crane House is the biggest, sleeping eight, with three bedrooms, a wrap-around garden and a wooden deck.

Dogs are welcome in every cottage when arranged at booking, free of charge (more on our pet-friendly page). Children under two stay free.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Anything else? WhatsApp us on 071 100 2782.

Which cottage is the biggest?

Blue Crane House sleeps eight, with three bedrooms and a double sofa bed. Sugarbird sleeps six.

Are the cottages fully self-catering?

Yes. Every cottage has a fully equipped kitchen and a braai, and towels and linen are provided.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes, dogs stay free in the cottages when arranged at the time of booking, at the owners' discretion. Pets are not allowed on furniture or in the splash pools.

Do children pay?

Children under two stay free.

Are the cottages warm in winter?

Every cottage has an indoor fireplace, plus its own wood-fired hot tub.

What are check-in and check-out times?

Check in from 14:00, check out by 10:00.
