Pet friendly

Dog-friendly Montagu, with a garden for them too.

Travelling with a dog narrows the options fast. At Glen Eden Farm, 14 km from Montagu on Route 62, dogs are welcome in all five self-catering cottages, and they stay free when you arrange it at the time of booking.

Every cottage has its own private enclosed garden and terrace, so there is space for the dog to be outside with you while you braai, swim in the splash pool or sit in the wood-fired hot tub.

A few house rules

Dogs are accepted at the owners' discretion, so mention yours when you book. Please keep them off the furniture and out of the splash pools.

Camping with a dog

One campsite at Mountain View, called "Valley", is pet friendly, with its own private bathroom, covered stoep kitchen and hot tub. See all the camping options and prices. The glamping units and the Little Eden campsite are pet free.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Anything else? WhatsApp us on 071 100 2782.

Which units are pet friendly?

All five cottages (Fish Eagle, Honeybee, Sugarbird, Blue Crane House and House on the Hill) and the "Valley" site at the Mountain View campsite.

Do dogs cost extra?

No. Pets stay free of charge in the cottages when arranged at the time of booking.

Are there any rules?

Dogs are accepted at the owners' discretion. Please keep them off the furniture and out of the splash pools.

Can I bring my dog glamping?

Not at the moment: the glamping units are pet free.

Is the garden fenced?

Every cottage has its own private enclosed garden.
