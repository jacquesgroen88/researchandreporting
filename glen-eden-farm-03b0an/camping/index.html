Camping

Montagu campsites with space and privacy.

Glen Eden has two campsites on its 50 hectare farm on Route 62, 14 km from Montagu. Both are big enough for tents, campers and caravans, every site is fenced for privacy, and every site has its own wood-fired hot tub, electricity, water, a braai and outside lights. Free WiFi reaches the campsites too.

Little Eden Campsite

Six sites of 300 m², each with reed fencing, a shaded picnic table and benches, a braai and fire pit. Guests share an upmarket ablution block with three showers and three toilets, plus washing-up sinks, gas hobs and a large fridge and freezer. No pets.

Mountain View Campsite

Six sites of about 260 m², each with its own private bathroom and a covered stoep with a fridge/freezer, sink, built-in braai, table and benches. One site, "Valley", is pet friendly.

We only take direct bookings for camping: book online with live availability or WhatsApp us.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Anything else? WhatsApp us on 071 100 2782.

How much does camping cost?

Little Eden: adults R250 to R350, under 12s R150 to R250 per person per night. Mountain View: adults R350 to R450, under 12s R250 to R350. Rates change between winter and peak season.

How big are the sites?

Little Eden sites are 300 m² and Mountain View sites about 260 m². Each takes up to six guests.

Can I bring a caravan or camper?

Yes, the sites are big enough for tents, campers and caravans, with water and electrical hook-ups.

Is there a private bathroom?

At Mountain View, yes, every site has one. Little Eden shares an upmarket ablution block with three showers and three toilets.

Are dogs allowed?

One Mountain View site, "Valley", is pet friendly. Little Eden is pet free.

How do I book a campsite?

Camping is booked direct only: online with live availability, or by WhatsApp on 071 100 2782.
