Hot tub stays

Hot tub accommodation near Montagu, for everyone.

Most places with a hot tub (or a jacuzzi, as many people call it) keep it for the honeymoon suite. At Glen Eden Farm every single stay has one: all five cottages, all six glamping units and every one of the twelve campsites. It is private, it is yours for the whole stay, and it is heated by its own wood fire.

How a wood-fired tub works

Each tub has its own small fire that warms the water. Get it going in the afternoon, then climb in as the sun drops behind the mountains and stay until the Milky Way comes out. On a winter weekend in the Klein Karoo, there is no better way to end the day.

Pick your setting

Want a hot tub and a splash pool? Choose one of the self-catering cottages. Want the romance of a gypsy wagon or bell tent? Every glamping unit has its own tub beside the fire pit. Bringing the caravan? Even our campsites come with a private hot tub, from R250 per person per night.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Anything else? WhatsApp us on 071 100 2782.

Is the hot tub private?

Yes. Every cottage, glamping unit and campsite has its own hot tub, used only by the guests staying there.

Is it a jacuzzi?

It is a wood-fired hot tub: a deep tub of water heated by its own small fire, rather than an electric jacuzzi with jets.

Do the campsites really have hot tubs?

Yes. Every site at Little Eden and Mountain View has its own wood-fired hot tub.

Which stays have a hot tub and a pool?

The five cottages each have a private splash pool as well as a hot tub. Every guest can also use the farm swimming pool at the recreation area.

How far is it from Montagu?

14 km along Route 62, towards Barrydale.
