Route 62 stopover

A Route 62 stop worth slowing down for.

Route 62 is one of South Africa's great road trips, and Glen Eden Farm sits right on it: 14 km past Montagu, heading towards Barrydale. It makes an easy first night out of Cape Town, or a base for exploring the Klein Karoo.

There are five self-catering cottages, six glamping hideaways and twelve campsites for caravans and tents. Every one has its own wood-fired hot tub.

Coming from Cape Town

Drive through Robertson and Ashton into Montagu. On Long Street, pass the Spar and Caltex on your right, then continue 14 km on the R62. The reception gate is on the left, just before the green "Ruigtevlei" road sign; the cottage gate is the next one on the left.

Coming from Barrydale

Head towards Montagu on the R62 for roughly 55 to 60 km. Pass Poortjieskloof Dam on your right; the farm is about 10 km further, on the right, opposite the green "Ruigtevlei" sign.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Anything else? WhatsApp us on 071 100 2782.

Where exactly is Glen Eden Farm?

On the R62 at Ruigtevlei, 14 km from Montagu towards Barrydale, Western Cape 6720.

How far is it from Barrydale?

About 55 to 60 km along the R62 towards Montagu.

Is it a good stop on a Route 62 road trip?

Yes. You can stay one night in a cottage, glamping unit or campsite, all with a private hot tub, and eat at Beaney Peanut on the farm.

Which gate do I use?

The reception gate is directly before the green "Ruigtevlei" road sign. The gate to the cottages is the next one along, also on the left when coming from Montagu.

What time can I arrive?

Check in is from 14:00 and check out by 10:00.
