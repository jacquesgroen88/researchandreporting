Welcome to Glen Eden

A small farm with a lot of sky.

Glen Eden is a 50 hectare smallholding on the famous Route 62, a short drive from the hot springs and wine farms of Montagu.

Choose a cottage with a splash pool, a hand-made gypsy wagon or a fenced campsite under the trees, then spend your days with the animals, on the water or at the archery range.

The Glen Eden ritual

Your own wood-fired hot tub. Every stay.

Cottage, wagon or campsite, it makes no difference. Stoke the fire in the afternoon, slide in at sunset and stay until the Milky Way comes out over the Karoo.

Slow mornings. Long evenings.

Most of what makes a stay here is free and right outside your door.

First light on the stoep

Pets

Dogs are welcome in the cottages when arranged at booking, at the owners' discretion (not on furniture or in the splash pools). One Mountain View campsite, "Valley", is pet friendly. Glamping sites and Little Eden camping are pet free.

Children

Children under 2 stay free in the cottages. Camping rates for under 12s are reduced. Life vests for kayaking fit children from 3 years (10 kg).

Camping rates

Little Eden: adults R250 to R350, under 12s R150 to R250 per person per night. Mountain View: adults R350 to R450, under 12s R250 to R350. Rates vary between winter and peak season.

Getting here

R62 Ruigtevlei, Montagu

From Cape Town

Drive through Robertson and Ashton into Montagu. On Long Street, pass the Spar and Caltex on your right, then continue 14 km on the R62. The reception gate is on the left, just before the green "Ruigtevlei" road sign. The cottage gate is the next one on the left.

From Barrydale

Head towards Montagu on the R62 for roughly 55 to 60 km. Pass Poortjieskloof Dam on your right; the farm is about 10 km further, on the right, opposite the green "Ruigtevlei" sign.
