Glamping

Glamping in the Western Cape, done properly.

Glen Eden's glamping is camping without the compromises. Every unit has proper beds, electricity, its own bathroom and a kitchen, and every one comes with a private wood-fired hot tub. You still get the fire pit, the open sky and the quiet of a 50 hectare farm on Route 62, 14 km from Montagu.

Gypsy wagon pods

Lilly Pod, Protea Pod and Aloe Pod each pair a hand-made gypsy wagon with a furnished bell tent, Persian rug and all, around a fire pit with tree-stump seats. Aloe is the largest wagon, with a king bed, and the family favourite. Lilly and Aloe sleep five.

Cabins and micro-bedrooms

Fynbos and Spekboom are cosy cabins with a wood-burning fire, Netflix and a covered stoep with a built-in braai. Pixie has two hand-made micro-bedrooms whose doors fold completely open, so you can fall asleep looking at the stars.

Glamping sites are pet free. Travelling with a dog? Look at the pet-friendly cottages instead.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Anything else? WhatsApp us on 071 100 2782.

What kind of glamping is it?

Six units: three gypsy wagon pods (a hand-made wagon plus a bell tent), two cabins (Fynbos and Spekboom) and Pixie, two hand-made micro-bedrooms. All have proper beds and electricity.

Do the glamping units have their own bathroom?

Yes. Every unit has a private bathroom with a shower.

Is there a kitchen?

Yes. Each unit has its own kitchen or kitchenette and a braai.

Do they all have a hot tub?

Yes, every glamping unit has its own wood-fired hot tub.

Is it good for families?

Lilly Pod and Aloe Pod sleep five, and Aloe has the largest wagon with a king bed. The touch farm, pool and play area are a hit with children.

Can I bring my dog?

Pets are not allowed at the glamping sites. Dogs are welcome in the cottages when arranged at booking.
