Welcome

Every room is named after a herb or a flower

Pietersielie, Roosmaryn, Lavender, Aandblom. Serendipity is a garden guest house in the middle of Danielskuil, with rooms in the main house and private units around the lawn, each with its own bathroom and a tea and coffee tray.

Breakfast is served in the dining room, the pool is there for hot Kalahari afternoons, and your car sleeps behind a secure gate. Whether you are passing through between Gauteng and the Kalahari or working in town for the week, you will feel at home.
