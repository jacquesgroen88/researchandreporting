What you did well

  • You didn't give up when the wrong person answered. At Draadstoor you went from the son to the mom and still booked her. At Homestead you went from Marlene to her son. That's exactly how it's done.
  • You gave them two times to choose from. "Thursday at 10 o'clock or 3 o'clock" and "Tuesday or Thursday, between 10 and 3". Both bookings came from that. Keep doing it.
  • You stayed friendly when people were short with you. The owner's husband ended the call apologising for "shutting you down", and you told him it was the nicest call you'd had all day. That matters when you call back in two weeks.

What to change

01Lead with what they want, not with their website

Almost every call opened on the website being down or missing. That's why the answers were "we've got someone for that", "we just had it redone" and "it costs too much". The website is only part of it. What they actually want is more bookings, less commission to Booking.com and more reviews. Start there, and bring the website in as part of how we get them there.

"I see that I can't make any bookings through it. The website is actually down. Is that something that you guys might need any assistance with?"

"Hi, it's Giorgio from ReviewTap. How's your week going? The reason I'm calling: I help guesthouses get more Google reviews and more past guests booking direct. When people look you up they can't book or message you from your site, so a lot of them probably end up on Booking.com and you pay commission on it. Can I take 30 seconds to tell you what we do about that?"

The playbook has a section called "Where the website fits" with a line for every situation you hit on these calls.

02Check the facts before you dial

Egweni's website isn't down, it just has an old date at the bottom. Homestead had just redone theirs. If you get it wrong in the first ten seconds, they stop listening. Open the website before every call, and if what's in the sheet isn't true anymore, open on reviews instead.

03Say who you're with in your first sentence

Sherry asked you straight out, and you moved on without answering:

"Sherry, sorry, where are you calling from?"

"Hi, it's Giorgio from ReviewTap." Use ReviewTap for guesthouses, restaurants and holiday rentals. Use JCE Media for wedding venues and training companies.

04Drop "this isn't a sale"

You said a version of it on nearly every call. It makes people expect a sale. Just tell them what you want to show them.

"So, not a matter of urgency, not a sale or anything like that, but just to show you what that would look like for your business."

"I'd like to show you what that would look like for Bella Zoe. It takes 15 minutes. Tuesday at 10 or Thursday at 2?"

05Talk like an owner, not like software

Owners don't know what a CRM or a platform is, and they don't care. Tell them what changes in their week.

"So basically, we're basically going to be a platform, you guys are going to have all of your messages and inquiries coming through one space…"

"Every guest gets a message after check-out asking for a Google review, and every enquiry from Facebook or TripAdvisor gets an answer, even at night when you're not at your phone."

06Don't ask why, and don't argue a no

"Okay, not a problem at all. Can I ask you why?" … "Because we're not interested."

"No problem. Is it the timing, or is it just not something you need right now?" Ask once. If it's still no, thank them and move on.

07"Send me a WhatsApp" isn't the end of the call

Lauren at Vaal River Lodge and Francie at Kiepersol both asked for a WhatsApp, and you hung up without a time to call back.

"Will do, I'll send it now. When's a good time to call you back, after 4 today or tomorrow morning?"

08Get out of dead ends fast

Twin Rivers took two minutes to establish it had closed. As soon as you know it's closed or you've got the wrong person, wrap up.

"Thanks, that's really helpful. I'll take them off my list. Have a good day."

09Don't offer to build anything for free

You told Gerda you'd set her up with a website and not charge her. Never promise a website or a price on a call. If they ask, use this:

"Jacques quotes websites himself, because it depends what you need. Let's look at the whole picture first, then he'll give you a number."
