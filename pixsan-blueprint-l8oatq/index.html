01Why I'm suggesting this

Following up on our chat about the Blueprint

When we spoke about it a while back, I said I thought there was big potential in Pixsan. I still think that.

I've spent the last few months inside Pixsan's delivery on Mako and Lifeport, so I know how you work and what your team can carry. And as agency owners we both have the same habit.

We put most of our energy into growing other people's businesses, and our own gets whatever time is left over.

The Blueprint gives the growth of Pixsan the same structure you give a client's: a clear picture of where things stand, a decision on where to focus, and a plan with owners and dates. It isn't a report I go away and write. We work through the business together, I do the research in between, and every big decision gets made with you, not for you.

I only take on two Blueprints a month, because of the time each one needs from me. I'd like one of those slots to be Pixsan's.
