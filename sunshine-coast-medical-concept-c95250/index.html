How to see a doctor

Booking and same-day visits

The quickest way to see a doctor is to phone the rooms. Reception will tell you when the next doctor is available.

  • Phone 046 648 1256 during consulting hours to book.
  • Need to be seen today? Phone first and ask about same-day and walk-in consultations.
  • Questions about results or repeat scripts: phone the rooms during office hours.
  • Email: dr.ejgalpin@gmail.com

Here on holiday?

Visiting Kenton-on-Sea and need a GP

Kenton gets busy over the holidays. A few things make a visit to the rooms quicker.

Phone before you come

Call 046 648 1256 to check when a doctor can see you, so you are not waiting longer than you need to.

What to bring

  • Your ID or passport
  • Medical aid card, if you have one
  • A list of your current medication

Mind the lunch hour

On weekdays the rooms close from 13:00 to 14:00. On Saturdays the rooms close at 12:30, and Sundays are closed.
