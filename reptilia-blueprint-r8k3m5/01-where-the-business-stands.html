Revenue has grown every single year. 2025 was the best year the store has had, at R208,645. The number under the number

Average order value has fallen from R776 to R634, an 18% drop, across the whole of 2026. That predates the 5 August relaunch by seven months, so the relaunch did not cause it. Order count is holding up fine — 177 orders in eight months against 269 in twelve. People are still buying. They are buying less each time.

38.7% down to 12.3%, on an identical measurement window. Ninety-day value per customer down from R1,249 to R784. The store compensated by acquiring more people — and it worked, revenue kept rising — but it is the expensive way to run a shop whose best-selling product is a tub of crickets that a bearded dragon eats in a fortnight.

What we can and cannot say about the cause

The data proves the decline. It does not prove why, and there are at least four candidates, in rough order of how much of it each probably explains:

  • Nothing was ever built to bring people back. No reorder email, no post-purchase sequence, no loyalty. This is certain and it is the one thing entirely inside Willem's control.
  • Availability got worse. 1,481 website-flagged products sit at zero stock on hand and the site marks nothing as sold out, so customers hit "out of stock" or an empty page. A customer who comes back and cannot buy does not come back twice.
  • Competition professionalised. Jozi Bugs now shows 920+ reviews, Silkworm Shop offers free Gauteng delivery over R1,000 with per-product star ratings. See document 06.
  • Early customers were the community. The 2021–2022 cohorts likely came from people who already knew the Vanderbijlpark shop. Later cohorts are colder traffic and behave like it.

Only the first is worth acting on immediately, because it is cheap, fast and certain to help regardless of which of the four is true.

January to March is the season. Those three months carry R219,083, 30% of all revenue ever, off 25% of the orders — so they carry a higher average basket too. That is the school holidays and the back-to-school window, when a child gets a first reptile and a parent buys the setup with it.

April and October are the troughs. Both around R44,000 across five years, roughly 40% below January. April sits immediately after the season, October immediately before the run-up.

What to do with this

The starter-setup bundle in document 09 should be built and live by 1 December, not January. The season is already decided by the time January arrives. October, the weakest month of the year, is the right time to run the win-back campaign: it costs nothing, it fills the trough, and it warms the list before the season.

Reptilia is a national online business run out of a Vanderbijlpark shop, and it is not a Gauteng-only business: 57% of revenue comes from outside Gauteng. The pattern in the LTV column is worth reading twice — the further from the shop, the higher the lifetime value. Limpopo R1,602, Eastern Cape R1,562, Free State R1,509, against Gauteng's R1,258 and Western Cape's R986.

The most likely explanation is the honest one: in Vanderbijlpark and Johannesburg there is a shop you can drive to, and there are competitors. In Polokwane or Kroonstad there is nobody, so when somebody finds Reptilia they stay. Outlying provinces are the loyal customers and they are being served by the same generic site as everyone else. Western Cape is the outlier the other way — most customers after Gauteng, lowest LTV of any province — which is what a market with real local competition looks like.

6. When people order

By day

Tuesday is the biggest revenue day at R144,074 off 161 orders, an AOV of R895. Monday has more orders (188) but a lower average. Sunday is the quietest at 108 orders, though it still trades — R78,890.

Friday and Saturday are the weakest paired days. That matters for a shop whose frozen stock ships early in the week.

By hour

Orders cluster hard into 09:00 to 14:00: the top six hours are 10:00, 12:00, 11:00, 09:00, 14:00 and 13:00, together carrying 46% of all orders.

This is a daytime, weekday, at-work shopping pattern, not an evening browse. Send email at 09:00 on a Monday or Tuesday.

That is harder than it sounds and most of the competitors in document 06 make a marketing feature of getting it right. Reptilia does it silently.
