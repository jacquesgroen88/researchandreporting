Who buys

The core asset

Nine customers are worth R118,384 — more than the whole of 2022

Nine people, 1.6% of the customer base, carry 16.2% of five years of revenue at an average lifetime value of R13,154 each, which is 15.7 times what a one-time buyer is worth. A repeat customer is worth R2,483 against R836 for a one-timer. Every rand spent making a second order happen is worth roughly three rand.

Revenue concentration overall is healthy rather than dangerous — the top customer is 6.4% of lifetime revenue, the top 10 are 18.3%, the top 100 are 53.9%. No single relationship can sink the business. But eight of the top ten have already lapsed, which is a different kind of risk.

Look at the "silent" column

Only two of the top 25 customers have ordered in the last 90 days. Twenty-three of the twenty-five most valuable people this business has ever had are lapsed, and not one of them has received a single email, SMS or WhatsApp asking them to come back. Between them the top 25 have spent R217,000.

Bianca Jansen Van Vuuren deserves her own paragraph

42 orders. R46,403 — 6.4% of every rand Reptilia has ever taken online. Her own median gap between orders was 30 days. She is now 321 days past her own cycle. She bought Montego 40kg dog food 26 times, which is single-handedly why that product is the second-biggest line in the catalogue, and she paired it with superworms 21 times.

Somebody who orders every 30 days for four years and then stops does not usually drift away — they usually hit a problem, or someone else got easier. Either way, that is one phone call, and it is the highest-value phone call available anywhere in this document. It should be made this week, and it should be a call, not an email.

Account holders are worth 47% more

R1,460 against R994, and they order 1.96 times against 1.41. Some of that is selection — people who intend to come back create an account. But 249 people, 43% of the customer base, have only ever checked out as guests, which means Reptilia has their email on an order and nothing else: no account, no consent, no list. Nudging account creation at checkout, and capturing consent properly, is the cheapest retention work available.

The collection customers are the loyalists

74 customers have collected in store at least once. They order 2.89 times against 1.55 for everyone else, and are worth R1,712 each. Read carefully — collection almost certainly does not cause loyalty; these are local Vaal Triangle regulars who were already loyal and find driving in easier than paying R160. But it says something useful: the customers who have physically been to the shop behave completely differently. Whatever happens in that building — the reptile exhibition, the petting zoo, staff who know the answer — converts people into repeat buyers at nearly double the rate, and none of it exists anywhere on the website.

The "B2B" segment is mostly not B2B

173 customers filled in a company field, but reading the actual names — Hoopstad Pharmacy, Penguin Verkoeling Kroonstad, RJK Machines, Sonic Radiators, Harrismith Inn — most are people using their work delivery address, not businesses buying stock. Their LTV is R1,199, slightly below the consumer average. There is a genuine trade segment inside Reptilia (see ICP 4) but the company field does not identify it, and it should not be used for segmentation.

The failure rate ran 17% to 24% through 2023 to 2025 and has fallen sharply to 5.4% in 2026. Something improved and nobody knows what — that is worth establishing so it does not get undone by a future change. But 107 people got as far as creating an order and never came back, and nobody has ever emailed a single one of them.

One structural contributor: PayFast is the only payment method. Bank transfer, EFT and cash on delivery are all disabled. In South Africa a meaningful share of shoppers prefer direct EFT, and there is currently no option for them but to abandon.

7. What this tells you to do

Phone Bianca this week

R46,403, 321 days past her own 30-day cycle. Not an email. A call, from Willem.

Write to the 75 in the 6-to-12-month band

Highest average order of any lapsed band at R1,009, and recent enough to remember you.

Build the reorder reminder at day 25

32 customers are overdue right now, worth R19,595. That list regenerates itself every month and today nobody is looking at it.

Get the guest checkouts onto a list

249 people, 43% of all customers, exist only as an email on an order.

Put the shop on the website

The people who have been to Vanderbijlpark buy nearly twice as often. The exhibition, the petting zoo, the twenty years and the 4.6 Google rating appear nowhere on reptilia.co.za.
