The three ways this goes wrong

Watch for these
  1. The redirects get published without human review. The matcher sent frozen rats to frozen mice and a green iguana to a red one. Publishing 1,705 wrong redirects is worse than 1,705 404s, because a 404 tells the customer to search and a wrong redirect tells them the shop does not have what they want.
  2. The reorder reminder fires for out-of-stock products. 1,481 products show zero stock on hand and the site marks nothing sold out. Prompting somebody to buy something that is not there costs more trust than the email earns. Stock accuracy in week 9 is a prerequisite for the flow in week 7, and if week 9 slips, the flow must be limited to products with confirmed stock.
  3. Everything gets done except the content. The two biggest lines in the money model — the win-back at R31,688 and the livestock pages at R27,390 — are both writing. They have no technical dependency, no plugin and no developer, which is exactly why they are the ones that slip while easier technical tasks get ticked off.
