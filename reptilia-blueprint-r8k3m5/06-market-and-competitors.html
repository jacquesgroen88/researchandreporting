Market and competitors

Seven South African players sell what Reptilia sells. Every one of them is narrower. Every one of them does one specific thing better, and it is the same thing in six of the seven cases.

Desk research, 15 August 2026 · Public websites, published pricing and policy pages · Reptilia's own position from its site, Google listing and directory entries

The structural read

The South African market splits into feeder specialists (Jozi Bugs, Feeders For Africa, Repticube) and broad pet retailers with a reptile aisle (Silkworm Shop, PetProvo). Only Ultimate Exotics does what Reptilia does: sell the animal, the enclosure, the equipment and the food, from one place, with a physical facility behind it.

That is a good position to be in and Reptilia is not using it.

The pattern in one line

Every competitor sells reassurance. Reptilia sells a price and a photograph.

Reviews, live-arrival guarantees, dry-ice protocols, stated lead times, breeding credentials, care content. Six of the seven lead with some form of "you can trust us with a living thing in a box". Reptilia — which has a 0.4% refund rate over five years and 998 orders, the hardest possible proof that its packing and sourcing actually work — says none of it anywhere.

3. What Reptilia has that none of them have

A physical shop with an attraction inside it

Trading in Vanderbijlpark since December 2006. A reptile exhibition with white throated and bosc monitors, green iguanas, Madagascar day geckos, reticulated and carpet pythons, Brazilian rainbow boas and veiled chameleons. A free public petting zoo with guinea pigs, chinchillas, rabbits, ducks, peafowl, pot-bellied pigs and geese.

4.6 Google rating. None of the online-only competitors can answer this.

The full range under one roof

Reptiles, tarantulas, small exotic mammals (chinchillas, hedgehogs, sugar gliders, degus, skinny pigs), and all their food, housing, heating and accessories. Plus custom-built cages made to order, which is mentioned in directory listings and nowhere on reptilia.co.za.

A first-time keeper can buy the animal and everything it needs in one order. Only Ultimate Exotics can match that, and they are in KZN.

The asset that is being wasted

Customers who have collected in store order 2.89 times against 1.55 for everyone else and are worth R1,712 against R1,194. Something happens in that building that turns a buyer into a regular — staff who know the answer, animals you can see, the confidence that these people actually keep reptiles.

Zero percent of that reaches the website. No photos of the shop, no photos of the exhibition, no staff, no story, no "we have done this for twenty years", no Google rating, no reviews. reptilia.co.za reads like a catalogue that could belong to anybody.

4. Where Reptilia is genuinely exposed

  • On feeders, against the specialists. Jozi Bugs and Feeders For Africa do one thing. Their pricing is transparent and starts low (crickets from R29, mealworms from R35), their review counts are public, and they breed their own. Reptilia's feed range is its retention engine — and three of its best feed lines (Superworms 70, Frozen Mice Pinkies, Mealworms 300) are currently dead URLs.
  • On findability. A shopper searching "lizard" on reptilia.co.za gets zero results, because WordPress core search does not read categories and the seven products in the Lizards category do not have "lizard" in their names. "cat food" returns nothing. A competitor with working search wins that shopper by default.
  • On Gauteng delivery. Silkworm Shop offers free Gauteng delivery over R1,000 into Reptilia's biggest market (247 customers, 42.6% of revenue). Reptilia charges R160 flat with no threshold, and Gauteng has the second-lowest LTV of any province.
  • On trust for the big purchase. Ultimate Exotics guarantees live arrival on animals. Reptilia sells a R14,495 Fiji Banded Iguana from a page with a photograph, a price and no text.

5. Where Reptilia should compete, and where it should not

Compete here

  • The complete first setup. Nobody else in the country sells the animal, the enclosure, the heat, the substrate and the first month of food as one decision. This is ICP 2 and it is the whole differentiator.
  • Small exotic mammals. Chinchillas, hedgehogs, sugar gliders, degus. Barely contested online in South Africa, and Reptilia already stocks the animals and their food.
  • The Vaal Triangle and the outlying provinces. Limpopo, Eastern Cape and Free State customers are the highest-LTV in the book (R1,602, R1,562, R1,509) because nobody local serves them.
  • Being a real shop. Twenty years, an exhibition, a petting zoo, a 4.6 rating. Unmatched and unused.

Do not compete here

  • Cheapest crickets. Against three dedicated feeder breeders with their own colonies, this is a losing price war. Compete on reliability and reordering instead.
  • General dog and cat. Against Absolute Pets, Petshop.co.za and every supermarket. Dry-food first-buyers have the lowest LTV in the book at R838 and the range carries most of the never-sold dead stock.
  • Fish and bird accessories. 27 and 22 never-sold products respectively. Nothing needs deleting; it just should not get attention.

6. The four things to copy, this quarter

Turn reviews on and seed them

Jozi Bugs has 920+. Reptilia has zero and a 4.6 Google rating it has never asked to reuse. One WooCommerce setting, then an automated review request three days after delivery.

Publish a live arrival guarantee and a frozen shipping protocol

Copy the structure Ultimate Exotics uses, because it is the right structure: what we guarantee, which days frozen ships, how it is packed, what happens if something goes wrong. Reptilia's 0.4% refund rate over five years earns it the right to say this louder than they do.

State the delivery lead time on the product page

"2 to 3 working days, R160 nationwide" on every page. Right now shipping is a surprise that appears at checkout, on a store whose median order is R532.

Put the shop on the website

Photographs of the exhibition and the petting zoo, the year 2006, the Google rating, the staff. This is the one thing on this page that no competitor can copy back.

What this research could not establish

Ultimate Exotics does not publish shipping rates — they are calculated at checkout, so no direct price comparison against R160 flat was possible. Neither Feeders For Africa nor Repticube publish thresholds or guarantees on their home pages. No competitor's traffic or revenue was measurable. All competitor claims above are quoted from their own public pages as at 15 August 2026 and should be re-checked before any of it is used in customer-facing copy.
