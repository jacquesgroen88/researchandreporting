When the assumptions get replaced

Close rate on Diagnostic Calls is real after 15 calls. Blueprint-to-retainer conversion is real after 20 presentations. Churn is real after ten retainers have passed day 90. Before those points, report the actual numbers with the sample size next to them, and do not change the plan on three data points. The JCE foreign plan's lesson about seven Upwork bids applies here too.

What this plan deliberately does not do

  • It does not change what is inside the Blueprint. The decks and the Acreage delivery define the product. This plan is about selling it and what follows.
  • It does not replace the Revenue Systems Audit or Real Estate Hero. They are the route-down products for buyers who score under 14.
  • It does not pay to be listed on agency directories or buy LinkedIn ads in phase one.
  • It does not sell the Blueprint at a discount outside the founding cohort. After six, R30,000 is the price.
