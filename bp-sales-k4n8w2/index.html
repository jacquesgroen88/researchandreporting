The Blueprint Sales Blueprint

A Seven Figure Blueprint for selling the Seven Figure Blueprint. Fourteen documents covering every step from a stranger seeing an ad, or a ReviewTap customer opening an email, to a signed monthly retainer, and what keeps that retainer alive after day 90.

The whole thing in five lines

  1. The Blueprint is not the product. It is the paid front door to the retainer. Acreage paid R10,000 for one and then R24,000 a month.
  2. Two Blueprints a month, converting at 40% into an average R16,000 retainer, bills about R1.25m in twelve months and exits at ~R109k MRR.
  3. The cheapest leads you will ever get are the 1,227 businesses that already bought from ReviewTap. Work that pond before buying a single click.
  4. Lead with a free Business X-Ray, never with a call request. Give the finding, sell the fix.
  5. The binding constraint is delivery capacity, not demand. A delivery hire is due around month five, and the plan says so up front.

Read this before quoting any number from this plan

The thesis that Blueprint buyers convert into retainers rests on one clean case (Acreage) and one supporting case (Reptilia, already a client). That is enough to act on and not enough to forecast with confidence. Every conversion rate in this plan is tagged Assumption until twenty Blueprints have been sold and the real rate replaces it. Doc 12 sets the checkpoint.
