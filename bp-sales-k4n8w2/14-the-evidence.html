1 · Does a paid diagnostic turn into a retainer?

There is no independent published benchmark. Every high number comes from someone selling a course.

  • Agency Mavericks (Paid Discovery Method) claims an average of 85% of "Digital Roadmaps" priced $500 to $5K convert into $3K to $10K retainers, and 15 to 20% for proposals without paid discovery. No sample or method. Low source
  • A professional-services resource library says a well-run diagnostic converts "about 70%", with no citation. Low
  • Scopen 2025, 25 SA creative agencies: average pitch win rate 17%; only 24% of pitches paid, at about R50,000, with agencies saying R108,400 would be fair. Medium Bizcommunity, 11 Mar 2025

What it means: 40% sits between the 17% pitch baseline and the unaudited 70 to 90% claims. Those claims are for roadmaps at a fraction of R30,000, where the buyer has less reason to run the plan in-house. Plan on 25% and replace it with your own number after twenty presentations.

Crediting the fee

No study compares conversion with and without a credit. Common practice on small discovery fees is a full credit within 30 days. The roadmapping school argues the diagnostic should stand as a product in its own right, and the agencies claiming the highest conversion do not mention crediting at all. A one-third partial credit with a deadline is a defensible middle path. Opinion

Retention

  • AgencyAnalytics 2025 (220+ agency leaders): 43% report average client lifespans of 2 to 5 years. Medium source
  • CallRail 2026 Agency Outlook: most agencies expect 12 to 24 months, only 3% expect 36+. Medium
  • A 56-month retainer lifespan figure from one agency report has no disclosed method. Low, ignored

SA pricing

Marketplace quotes on ProCompare put SA retainers at about R12,000 a month for R5k to R10k budgets and R25,000 to R28,000 for R10k to R25k budgets, with strategy plus campaign management around R15,000. Clutch lists most small SA agencies at $25 to $49 an hour and strategy-led shops at $100 to $149. Low to medium Clutch ZA

2 · Cold calling

  • Gong, 300 million calls (2024): dials to conversations 5.4% on average, 13.3% top quartile; conversations to meetings 4.6% average, 16.7% top quartile. B2B sales teams, not SA owner-operators. Medium, vendor source
  • Openers (Gong): an honest permission opener with a reason ("this is a cold call, can I have 30 seconds, then you decide") scored 11.18%; "bad time?" was worst at 2.15%. In the 2018 study, stating "the reason for my call is" gave a 2.1x lift. Medium, correlational source
  • Cognism 2025: 93% of conversations happen within 3 attempts; Tuesday books the most meetings; 07:00 to 09:00 is among the weakest windows. Medium, vendor source
  • South Africa: Truecaller reports about 30% of unknown calls in SA flagged as spam or fraud in H1 2026. Medium

What it means: a free X-Ray is an easier yes than a meeting, but 10 owner conversations from 40 dials is above every published benchmark. Plan 4 to 6, and treat the callback time from staff as the lever.

3 · Speed to lead and no-shows

  • HBR, "The Short Life of Online Sales Leads" (2011): in 1.25 million leads, contacting within an hour made qualification about 7 times more likely than after an hour, and 60 times more than after 24 hours. Of 2,241 firms audited, 23% never replied at all. High, older source
  • MIT and InsideSales (2007): calling within 5 minutes rather than 30 gave up to 100 times higher contact rates. Medium, small and dated
  • No-shows: RevenueHero measured 16% for SMB meetings, cut about 28% by two to three reminders. A Cochrane review of seven trials found SMS reminders raise attendance (risk ratio 1.14), in healthcare. Medium

4 · Ad costs

  • SA agency estimates for Meta: services and B2B leads R40 to R250 (Juicy Designs, May 2026), R150 to R400+ for higher-value services (Launch Digital, Jul 2026). Medium to low, agency estimates
  • WordStream/LocaliQ 2025, 1,000+ campaigns, mostly US: Meta lead-campaign cost per lead $27.66 (about R480), up 21% year on year; Google business services cost per lead $103.54. High as a ceiling source
  • SA Google B2B professional-services clicks R12 to R45, and R800 to R2,000 per qualified B2B lead (Growth Pulse Media, Apr 2026). Medium to low
  • Inbound form to booked meeting: median 62% for warm B2B demo requests; 66.7% when booking is offered at form fill against 30% without (RevenueHero, Chili Piper). Medium, much warmer leads

The Google Keyword Planner could not be used for this research: the Google Ads API token on your account only has explorer access, which blocks keyword planning.

What did not change

  • The thesis. Selling a paid diagnosis before a retainer is a recognised, widely recommended model, and SA agencies win only 17% of conventional pitches.
  • The offer ladder and prices. They sit inside or below SA market ranges.
  • The gap in the market. Nobody local sells a revenue-level diagnosis that leads into a fixed-price strategy product.
  • Inbound first. The landing page with a consent box, referrals, and existing clients are the cleanest channels legally and the warmest commercially. Outbound becomes a supporting channel, done properly.
