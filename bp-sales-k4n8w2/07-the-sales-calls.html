The Sales Calls

Two calls sell a Blueprint: a 15-minute qualifying call that most people never notice was a filter, and a 45-minute Diagnostic Call that ends with a deposit invoice sent while they are still on the line. Everything around them exists to get the right people onto the second one.

Scans: who gets a full one

A full scan is 45 minutes of work. At ad volumes that is the most expensive step in the funnel. Full scans go to requests scoring 10 or more on the two form qualifiers plus the business's outside footprint. Under 10 gets a light version (the review and AI visibility checks only, about 10 minutes, mostly automated) with a route-down suggestion. Tier A and referrals always get the full scan.

The scan delivery message (WhatsApp, then email with the page)

"Hi [name], Jacques here. Your Business X-Ray for [business] is done, I've emailed the page. The short version: [leak 1 in one line, with the rand figure]. That one surprised me. Have a look, and if you want, I can walk you through all three in 15 minutes. Here's my calendar: [link]. Or reply with a time that suits."

Call 1 · The qualifying call (15 minutes)

Who runs it: you, every time. For cold calls, steps 4 and 5 of doc 13 have already asked most of it, so this call becomes the X-Ray walk-through that was booked on the cold call. For Tier A and referrals, fold it into the start of the Diagnostic Call. Its real job: fill the five score fields and get every decision maker onto the next call. It feels to the prospect like a walk-through of their scan.

Script

Open (1 min): "Thanks for making time. I've got your scan in front of me. I'll take you through the three leaks quickly, and then ask you a few questions so Jacques can tell you properly what they mean for you. Sound OK?"

Walk the scan (5 min): one minute per leak. After each: "Does that match what you see?" Note every correction. A corrected number is worth more than our estimate, because it is theirs.

The five questions (7 min):

  1. "Roughly what does the business turn over in a year? A range is fine." (Size)
  2. "What do you spend on marketing in a normal month, all in? Agency, ads, people." (Spend)
  3. "Is there anything coming up that makes this more urgent? A busy season, a new site, a launch?" (Urgency)
  4. "Who does your marketing day to day?" (Capacity gap)
  5. "Who else owns the business or signs off on spending like this?" (Decision makers)

Close (2 min), if 14+: "Based on what you've told me, I think it's worth a proper conversation with Jacques. He'll go deeper on the numbers and tell you honestly whether our Blueprint would help or not. It's 45 minutes, and it works best if [partner name] is on it too. What day works for both of you?"

Close, if under 14: "Thanks, this is really useful. For where you are, I think [route-down product] is the better fit, and I'll send you the details. The scan's yours either way."

Call 2 · The Diagnostic Call (45 minutes)

Who: you, with every owner present. On video, camera on, recorded and transcribed for the call-review routine. Before the call: Claude prepares a one-page brief: the scan, the qualifying answers, their Google profile, any ReviewTap order history, and three questions specific to their business.

Frame · minutes 0 to 3

"Thanks both. Here's how I'd like to use the 45 minutes. I want to understand the business properly, then go through what the X-Ray found with your real numbers, and at the end I'll tell you honestly whether I think our Blueprint is right for you. If it isn't, I'll tell you that too, and what I'd do instead. Is that OK?"

Permission to say no is what makes the later yes credible.

Current state · minutes 3 to 15

Let them talk. You need five things, in their words:

  • "Where do your best customers come from today?"
  • "What's working that you'd be scared to switch off?"
  • "What have you tried that didn't work, and what did it cost?"
  • "When in the year do you make your money, and when don't you?"
  • "If this year went perfectly, what would be different in twelve months?"

Write down their exact phrases. You will use them in the Blueprint and on the presentation call.

The scan, with their numbers · minutes 15 to 22

Go back through the three leaks using what they just told you. Re-do the rand figure live with their corrected inputs. "You said you get about 60 enquiries a month and your average function is R45,000. If even five of those go cold because nobody follows up in time, that's R225,000 a month walking out the door." Let them say the number back.

The cost of standing still · minutes 22 to 28

"If nothing changed for the next twelve months, what does that cost you?" Then be quiet. Their answer is the anchor the price will be compared with, so it has to be theirs, not yours.

Then: "And the leaks we found from outside are usually the smaller ones. The bigger ones are inside: how enquiries are handled, what happens to past customers, which spend actually works. You can't see them from outside, and honestly, it's hard to see them from inside a busy week either."

The Blueprint · minutes 28 to 36

"That's what the Blueprint is for. It's two weeks. We go through everything: your numbers, your enquiries, your competitors, your website, your ads. At the end you get the full diagnosis, a 90-day plan in order of what makes the most money first, and the hours each part takes. You own it. You can run it yourself, hand it to your team, give it to another agency, or ask us to help. Most people find out from it exactly how much help they need, and that's the point."

Share your screen on the public worked example for 90 seconds. Show the roadmap page, not the whole thing.

The price · minutes 36 to 40

"The full programme is normally priced as R15,000 setup plus R40,000, so R55,000. We do it as one payment of R30,000, 60% to start and 40% when we present. And there's a guarantee: if the diagnostic doesn't tell you something about your business you didn't already know, you don't pay the 40%."

Founding cohort buyers: "Because you're already a ReviewTap customer, and we're building our case studies this quarter, you'd come in at the founding price of R19,500. That's for six businesses and in exchange we'd record a short case study with you at 90 days."

Then compare it with their number from step 4. "Against the R225,000 a month we talked about, it's a small decision."

The close · minutes 40 to 45

"I start two Blueprints a month, so I can do the work properly. I've got one seat left in November. Would you like it?"

On a yes: "Great. I'll send the deposit invoice now, while we're talking, so you can see it's arrived. As soon as it clears we'll book the kickoff, and I'll send a short list of what I need from you." Send it from JCE Systems Group on the call. Do not end the call until they confirm it arrived.

On "let me think": "Of course. What specifically would you want to think through? ... Is it the money, the timing, or whether it will work?" Handle the real one (below). Then: "I'll hold the seat until Friday. After that it goes to the next person on the list." Only say this if it is true.

No-shows

Acreage was a two-time no-show. Under the call-first policy, and from what that deal taught:

  • Every booking is a calendar invite with date and time in the title, plus a WhatsApp confirming it in words.
  • WhatsApp reminder the day before and one hour before, from a person, not a template.
  • No-show: phone them within five minutes. Often they are just running late. Plan for 15 to 25% no-shows even with reminders; two to three reminders cut no-shows by about a quarter in vendor data, and SMS reminders reliably raise attendance in medical trials (doc 14).
  • Second no-show: the scan stays theirs, the card goes to nurture. For Tier A and anyone scoring 18+, you call personally the following week, owner to owner. That is how Acreage was won back.

Getting better every week

Every Diagnostic Call is recorded and transcribed. On Fridays, Claude reviews the week's calls against this script (the same Call-Reviews routine Giorgio's calls already go through, and it covers your cold calls from doc 13 too): where the step-4 number was never asked, where price came before value, where the close was skipped. One fix per week. After ten calls, rewrite this document from what actually works.
