What the research changed

ReviewTap's permission does not travel to JCE

The 567 subscribers agreed to hear from ReviewTap about ReviewTap. POPIA's existing-customer exception (s69(3)) covers a business marketing its own similar products. JCE Systems Group is a different legal entity, and a R30,000 strategy engagement is not similar to a review stand. The first draft of this plan sent from ReviewTap and named JCE only after a reply. That would fail the sender-identity rule (s69(4)), and a ReviewTap buyer's due diligence would find it. So the play is now one clearly labelled request, sent once, that asks whether they want to hear from JCE. Only a yes moves anyone across.

The rules

  1. One consent request per customer, ever. POPIA allows one approach to someone who has not consented, and it may only ask for consent. No three-email sequence, no "last call" follow-up to people who did not answer.
  2. It names JCE Systems Group (Pty) Ltd t/a JCE Media, says what JCE would send (the free X-Ray, then information about its marketing services) and which channels (email, WhatsApp, phone), and has a one-click "no thanks".
  3. It goes only to subscribed customers who have never unsubscribed. The other 660 hear nothing about JCE.
  4. Every yes is logged as consent for JCE: date, wording, channel. GHL tag jce-consent-yes plus the source tag jce-sourced-from-rt. Every no is logged too, and that customer is never asked again.
  5. ReviewTap's own marketing carries on unchanged and never mentions JCE again after the request.
  6. Fulfilment and dedupe first: drop anyone with an unfulfilled order, match against live Shopify.

The bridge story

The bridge

"Reviews get people to your door. What happens after they arrive is where most businesses leak."

You bought ReviewTap to fix one thing. It is one of seven places a business loses money: reputation, being found, speed to answer, follow-up, conversion, repeat business, and knowing which spend works. ReviewTap handles the first. Our sister company, JCE Media, looks at the other six.

Use the before-and-after review numbers only where they are real and favourable, and say "since your stand arrived", never "because of your stand". Where the stand has not worked, it is a support conversation first.

The consent email (Tier B and C)

Sent once, from support@reviewtap.co.za, signed by you, Tuesday to Thursday mid-morning.

Subject: Would you like a free Business X-Ray for [business name]?

Hi [first name],

You have been using ReviewTap since [month]. Thank you.

Reviews get people to your door. What happens after they arrive is where most businesses lose money: slow replies, no follow-up, ads nobody can connect to a booking.

Our sister company, JCE Media (JCE Systems Group (Pty) Ltd), does a free Business X-Ray. It is a revenue leak check: they look at your business the way a new customer finds and contacts it, compare you with your three nearest competitors, and send you one page showing the three biggest leaks, with a rand figure on each. It costs nothing and there is nothing to sign.

We will only pass your details to JCE Media if you say yes. If you do, they will send your X-Ray by email or WhatsApp and may contact you about it and about their marketing services. You can stop that at any time.

[Yes, send me a Business X-Ray]   [No thanks]

Either way, nothing changes with your ReviewTap account, and we will not ask again.

Jacques Groenewald
Founder, ReviewTap · JCE Media

ReviewTap, [physical address], [phone]. Unsubscribe from ReviewTap emails: [link].

The yes button goes to jcemedia.com/xray with their business name pre-filled, so they confirm their details and tick consent on JCE's own form. That gives JCE its own consent record, collected on its own page.

Tier A: your call

Five calls a day, Tuesday to Thursday, using the warm version of the script in doc 13. You open as ReviewTap's founder, check the stand is working, give them their review progress, and ask whether they would like JCE Media to send them a free X-Ray and contact them about it. That call is their one approach, so do not also send them the consent email.

  • Yes: the three questions, book the walk-through, log consent for JCE, build the X-Ray within 48 hours.
  • No answer twice: leave a callback-only voicemail (doc 13). If they never pick up, they get nothing further.
  • No: thank them, log it, and they stay a happy ReviewTap customer.

The multi-site groups

The 11 August method still applies after a yes: about 20 minutes of Apify per group for site count, group mean, the frozen site, the cheap win and one free finding. That analysis becomes their X-Ray. The Branded Fleet programme stays ReviewTap's offer; the Blueprint is JCE's, and a group can buy both.
