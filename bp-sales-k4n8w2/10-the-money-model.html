The shape of it

Blueprints pay for the first five months. Retainers pay for everything after.

Until March, Blueprint revenue is most of the line. From June, retainers are more than half, and by October they are R109,000 a month on their own. That is why doc 09 matters more than doc 05: at 10% churn instead of 6%, the base case exits roughly R24,000 a month lower.

The scan is the hidden capacity cost

Thirty-seven full scans a month is about 28 hours, close to a Blueprint's worth of time. Two levers keep it manageable: referrals skip the scan entirely (one referred Blueprint a month halves the scan load), and the scan must get to 30 minutes by January with Claude producing the draft from the scrapes. One full-time person has about 140 productive hours a month. On top of the existing book, this motion needs one and a half people by October 2027. Diane already carries back-end implementation for the RT System clients.

The hire trigger

When committed retainer hours pass 60 a month, or the third new retainer signs, whichever comes first, recruit a marketing executor (campaigns, landing pages, reporting) at about R30,000 a month cost to company, or a contractor at R350 an hour on the same scope. In the base case that is March 2027. Write the role in January so recruiting starts before the crunch, not during it. You stay on Blueprints, Diagnostic Calls, monthly strategy sessions and day-90 reviews: the parts only you can do.

If your own hours change

Every number above assumes your current weekly hours on JCE. If those drop materially, for example with a full-time role, the capacity clock falls to one Blueprint a month (the downside case's volume), the hire comes forward to the first retainer, and the person you hire needs to be able to run Diagnostic Calls, not only deliver.
