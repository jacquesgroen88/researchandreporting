The shape of it

Blueprints carry the first year. Conversion decides the second.

At 25% conversion, Blueprint revenue is the bigger line all year and retainers reach R68,000 a month by October. At 40% they reach R109,000. Every 5 points of conversion is worth roughly R13,500 a month of exit MRR, which is why doc 08 (the presentation and the three-paths close) matters more than doc 05. Churn matters as much: at 10% instead of 6% the exit MRR drops by roughly a fifth.

Thirty-seven full scans a month is about 28 hours, close to a Blueprint's worth of time. Two levers keep it manageable: referrals skip the scan entirely (one referred Blueprint a month halves the scan load), and the scan must get to 30 minutes by January with Claude producing the draft from the scrapes. One full-time person has about 140 productive hours a month. On top of the existing book, this motion needs about one and a third people by October 2027 in the base case, and one and a half at the target. Diane already carries back-end implementation for the RT System clients.

The hire trigger

When committed retainer hours pass 60 a month, or the third new retainer signs, whichever comes first, recruit a marketing executor (campaigns, landing pages, reporting) at about R30,000 a month cost to company, or a contractor at R350 an hour on the same scope. In the base case that is about June 2027. At the 40% target it is March. Write the role in January so recruiting starts before the crunch, not during it. You stay on Blueprints, Diagnostic Calls, monthly strategy sessions and day-90 reviews: the parts only you can do.

If your own hours change

Every number above assumes your current weekly hours on JCE. If those drop materially, for example with a full-time role, the capacity clock falls to one Blueprint a month (the downside case's volume), the hire comes forward to the first retainer, and the person you hire needs to be able to run Diagnostic Calls, not only deliver.
