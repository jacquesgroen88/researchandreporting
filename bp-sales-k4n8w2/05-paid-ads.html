Document 05 · Getting the lead

Paid Ads

Ads do not sell a R30,000 Blueprint. They sell a free Business X-Ray to the right owner, and the scan, the call and the Blueprint do the rest. Everything in this document is built backwards from that.

Index · Previous: 04 · Launch in week 7 (23 Nov 2026), only after two warm Blueprints have closed.

Why week 7 and not now

Ads multiply whatever the funnel already does. If the scan takes three hours, the call script is untested and the landing page is not built, R12,000 a month buys a lot of expensive confusion. The ReviewTap base (doc 04) is the test bed. Turn on ads when three things are true:

  1. At least ten scans produced, median under 45 minutes.
  2. At least two Blueprints sold through the Diagnostic Call script in doc 07.
  3. The landing page below is live, server-rendered, and its form lands in GHL with a speed-to-lead alert.

The landing page

URL: jcemedia.com/xray. Built server-rendered: the JCE foreign plan found jcemedia.com returns 54 characters of text to a crawler, which also hurts Meta's page quality checks and link previews. One page, one action.

Page structure, top to bottom

  1. Headline: "Find out where your business is leaking money. Free, in 48 hours."
  2. Sub: "We look at your business the way a new customer finds and contacts it, compare you to your three nearest competitors, and send you one page with the three biggest leaks and a rand figure on each."
  3. The form, above the fold: name, business name, website or Google listing, WhatsApp number, and two qualifiers: "Roughly how many enquiries do you get a month?" and "What do you spend on marketing a month?" (ranges). The qualifiers are the doc 03 score, collected for free.
  4. An example scan, blurred names, real layout. Seeing the deliverable doubles the trust.
  5. What happens next: "1. We build your scan. 2. You get it on WhatsApp and email within 48 hours. 3. If you want, we talk it through. There is nothing to buy."
  6. Proof: "The team behind ReviewTap, used by over 1,200 South African businesses." Plus the case study once Jade has agreed.
  7. Who it is not for: "Businesses under about R3 million a year will get more from ReviewTap Pro." Saying who it is not for raises the quality of who applies.

Speed to lead: every form submission triggers a WhatsApp to you within the minute and an automatic confirmation to them: "Thanks [name], your Business X-Ray is in the queue. Jacques will WhatsApp you personally when it is ready, within two working days." The bottleneck in every JCE client system so far has been follow-up. Do not make the same mistake on your own funnel.

Eight ad concepts

Four founder-led videos (your face, your voice, filmed on a phone and cut with the talking-head pipeline) and four statics. Launch with four, two of each, and add the rest as winners emerge.

Ad 1 · "The R10,000 a month nobody could explain"

Founder video · 45 seconds · launch set

Hook (first 3 seconds, on screen and spoken): "A business I worked with was spending R10,000 a month on ads, and nobody could tell me what it returned."

Body: "That is not unusual. Most owners I talk to are paying for marketing and running on gut feel. So we built a free Business X-Ray. We look at your business the way a customer finds it, compare you to your three nearest competitors, and send you one page: the three biggest leaks, with a rand figure on each. It takes us 45 minutes. It costs you nothing."

CTA: "Tap the link, tell us your business, and you'll have it on WhatsApp in two days."

Primary text: Free Business X-Ray for South African businesses. Three leaks, three rand figures, one page, 48 hours. No call needed to get it.

Headline: Where is your business leaking money?

Ad 2 · "Full on Saturday, empty on Tuesday"

Founder video · 30 seconds · hospitality cut · launch set

Hook: "If your restaurant is full on Saturday and empty on Tuesday, this is for you."

Body: "That gap is not a cooking problem. It's usually three things: how people find you, how fast you answer them, and whether anyone follows up. I've built review systems for over a thousand SA businesses, and these three leaks show up every time. We'll find yours, for free, and put a number on them."

CTA: "Link below. Two minutes to request, two days to get it."

Ad 3 · "Your agency sends a report. Do you read it?"

Founder video · 40 seconds · current-spend buyers

Hook: "If your marketing agency sends you a monthly report and you don't read it, you're not lazy. It's written for them, not for you."

Body: "The only numbers that matter are enquiries, bookings and money in the bank. Our free Business X-Ray looks at your business from the outside and tells you, in rands, where it's leaking. Then you can ask your agency better questions. Or not. Up to you." CTA: as Ad 1.

Ad 4 · "We built ReviewTap. Here is what we learned"

Founder video · 50 seconds · RT buyers and lookalike

Hook: "Over a thousand South African businesses bought ReviewTap to fix their Google reviews. Here's what I learned looking at all of them."

Body: "Reviews get people to your door. What happens after they arrive is where most businesses lose money. Slow replies. No follow-up. Ads that nobody tracks. So we started doing a free Business X-Ray for our customers, and now we're opening it up." CTA: as Ad 1.

Ad 5 · Static: the scan itself

Image · launch set

Visual: a real (anonymised) scan page, angled, three leaks visible with rand figures highlighted.

Overlay: "Three leaks. Three rand figures. One page. Free." Primary text: "This is what you get. We build it by hand for your business in 48 hours. Request yours in two minutes."

Ad 6 · Static: the speed test

Image · launch set

Visual: a WhatsApp chat screenshot, an enquiry at 10:14, no reply, the timestamp "Read".

Overlay: "We sent your competitors an enquiry. Some took 4 hours to reply." Primary text: "How long do you take? Speed to answer is one of the checks in our free Business X-Ray. Find out where you stand."

Ad 7 · Static: the ChatGPT question

Image · second wave

Visual: a ChatGPT answer to "best wedding venue near Midrand" listing three venues, none of them the viewer's.

Overlay: "Your customers are asking ChatGPT. Are you in the answer?" Primary text: "AI visibility is one of five checks in our free Business X-Ray."

Ad 8 · Retargeting: the case study

Founder video or client video · retargeting only

Once Jade agrees: 60 seconds, her voice if she will, yours if not. What the Blueprint found, what changed, what the first 90 days produced. CTA: "Start with the free Business X-Ray." This is the ad that moves someone who watched Ad 1 last week. Until the case study is approved, retarget with Ad 3.

Assumption SA B2B lead costs on Meta for a free, useful offer typically land between R150 and R500. R250 is a midpoint target, not a benchmark. The last row is the only one that matters: at R15,000 per Blueprint the ads still pay back on the Blueprint alone at full price, before any retainer. At more than that, stop and fix the funnel before spending more.

The scale rule

When cost per Blueprint holds under R8,000 for two consecutive months and the capacity clock has a free seat, raise Meta by 20% a month. Never raise spend when both Blueprint seats for next month are already sold: buy leads you can serve, not leads that sit in a queue going cold.
