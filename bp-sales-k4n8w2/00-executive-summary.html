Executive Summary

The Blueprint is the best-converting thing JCE Media sells, because it is the only offer where the client pays to discover how much help they need. This plan turns one accidental success into a repeatable machine.

Built 8 October 2026. All rand figures exclude VAT (JCE Systems Group is not a VAT vendor). Back to the index

The thesis

You have noticed something true. Cold prospects pitched a R14,500 retainer say "let me think about it". The same prospect, after paying for a Blueprint and spending two weeks watching you understand their business better than they do, chooses the retainer on the presentation call. The Blueprint does the selling that a discovery call cannot.

The proof case

Acreage: a R649 stand, then R10,000, then R24,000 a month

Jade Mann bought a ReviewTap stand in November 2025. She was a two-time no-show for Giorgio and was marked Lost. You re-engaged her directly on 28 July, sold the Blueprint at R10,000, delivered on 3 September, and on 11 September she paid R24,000 for month one of a 90-day retainer, the top of three tiers. If she stays twelve months, that one R649 customer is worth R298,000. She is now ~41% of live MRR.

Three things in that story are the plan in miniature:

  1. She came from the ReviewTap customer base. The warmest pond you own, and almost entirely unworked for anything except more cards.
  2. She was sold the diagnosis, not the service. Nobody asked her to commit R24,000 a month to people she had never worked with.
  3. The retainer was presented as one of three options inside the deliverable, with a page titled "what you should not pay us for". She chose the most expensive one.
The honesty box

This is one clean case. Reptilia also took a Blueprint but was already a paying client, so it proves expansion, not acquisition. 360now bought the startup version in 2025 and is not on record as converting. One case justifies building the machine. It does not justify a forecast, which is why every rate in this plan is marked as an assumption and doc 12 sets the point (20 Blueprints sold) at which real numbers replace them.

The five moves

Build the Business X-Ray as a 40-minute product

The free give that opens every conversation. It reuses the Revenue Systems Audit engine (reputation, speed to lead, follow-up, friction, database) and the AEO snapshot, cut to one page with three rand figures. Without it, every channel in this plan asks for a call. With it, every channel gives something first. Done when: three scans have been produced in under 45 minutes each.

Work the ReviewTap customer base before spending on ads

567 subscribed buyers, 14+ multi-site groups, and the RT System clients who already pay you monthly. Because they are ReviewTap's customers and not JCE's, each gets one request naming JCE: a personal call for Tier A, one email for the rest. Target: four Blueprints sold from the base by 31 December, at the founding cohort price. Doc 04.

Put the retainer inside the deliverable

Every Blueprint ends with a "Working Together" document and a 15-minute three-paths close on the presentation call, exactly as Acreage did. Add a R10,000 credit against month one for anyone who chooses a retainer within 14 days. Doc 08.

Turn on paid acquisition in week 7, not week 1

Ads amplify a funnel that works. Run them only once the scan, the landing page and the call scripts have closed at least two Blueprints from warm leads. Start at R9,000 a month on Meta plus R3,000 on Google search, with a hard kill rule. Expect R250 to R450 a lead on Meta and R600 or more on Google. Doc 05.

Plan the delivery hire before you need it

At three active retainers the hours run out. The base case reaches that in March 2027. Write the role, the cost and the trigger now so that success does not break delivery for the clients who made it possible. Doc 10.

Added 10 October: the compliance finding

South Africa's Information Regulator treats live sales calls, WhatsApps and emails to non-customers as direct marketing that needs consent, companies included. ReviewTap's customer permission does not extend to JCE. The national opt-out registry launched on 7 October 2026. Docs 04 and 13 are rewritten around one consent request per person, and registering with the National Consumer Commission by December is now task 1.2. Details and sources in doc 14.
