Why statics are built as HTML, not by an image model

Image models still misspell words and invent numbers, and these ads live on exact rand figures and the word "free". So P6 has Claude Code lay the ad out in HTML at exact pixel sizes and screenshot it, which gives correct text every time. Image models are used only for photographic backgrounds with no text in them (P7).

P1 · The brief block

Paste this above any prompt below. It stops the model inventing claims.

BRIEF: JCE MEDIA, FREE BUSINESS X-RAY ADS Advertiser: JCE Media (JCE Systems Group (Pty) Ltd), a South African marketing agency. Same team that built ReviewTap, the Google review stands and cards used by over 1,200 South African businesses. Offer: a free Business X-Ray, described as "a revenue leak check". We look at the business the way a new customer finds and contacts it, compare it with its three nearest competitors, and send one page showing the three biggest places it is losing money, with a rand figure on each and the working shown. Built by a person, not a tool. Delivered on WhatsApp and email within 48 hours (two working days). Nothing to buy, nothing to sign, no call needed to get it. The five checks: reputation against the top 3 competitors; speed to answer an enquiry (we send one real enquiry and time the reply); AI visibility (does ChatGPT name them); paid activity (Meta and Google, theirs and competitors'); the website on a phone. Landing page: jcemedia.com/xray. One action: request the X-Ray. Audience: South African owner-operators with real enquiry volume. Venues, restaurants, guesthouses, clinics, dental, service businesses, franchisees and groups with several sites. Usually spending on marketing already and unsure what it returns. Age 30 to 60. Positioning: SA competitors give away free website or SEO scans. This is not a website audit. It is about money: enquiries, bookings, rands. Never call it a website audit or SEO audit. Voice: Jacques Groenewald, the founder. Plain South African English, direct, warm, owner to owner. Short sentences. Rand amounts written as R36,000. No hype words (game-changer, skyrocket, unlock, supercharge, secrets, hacks). No emojis. NEVER use em dashes or en dashes; use commas, full stops or colons. Hard rules: 1. Never invent a client result, statistic or testimonial. The only facts you may state: 1,200+ SA businesses use ReviewTap; 48 hours; three leaks; three nearest competitors; one page; free. 2. Example rand figures must be labelled as an example, or not used. 3. Never promise outcomes ("double your bookings"). Promise the X-Ray. 4. Never imply the viewer has done something wrong. The tone is "this is easy to miss from the inside". 5. No fake urgency. If scarcity is mentioned, it is true: X-Rays are built by hand, so we do a limited number each week. 6. Meta policy: do not assert personal attributes ("Are you struggling...?" about the viewer's finances or situation). Talk about businesses, not the person.

P3 · Hook bank

The first three seconds decide whether anyone watches. Generate a bank, film the best five, let the data pick.

[Paste P1 here] Write 20 opening hooks for founder-to-camera Meta video ads promoting the free Business X-Ray. Each hook is spoken in the first 3 seconds and also shown as on-screen text. Mix these types, at least three of each: - A specific, true observation ("If your booking button goes to a page that won't load on a phone...") - A number ("Most businesses I look at take four hours to answer a website enquiry.") Only use numbers framed as what Jacques commonly sees, never as research. - A role call ("If you own a restaurant in Gauteng, this is for you.") - A contrarian line ("Your Google rating is probably not your problem.") - A question about the business, not the person ("When did your business last answer an enquiry in under five minutes?") - A story opener ("A venue owner told me they spend R10,000 a month on ads...") labelled as anecdote. Rules: under 15 words each, no em dashes, no hype, nothing that implies the viewer is failing. Then rank the top five for a cold South African owner audience and say in one line why each will stop the scroll.

P4 · Founder video scripts and shot list

For filming on your phone. One morning, natural light, four concepts.

[Paste P1 here] Write teleprompter scripts for four founder-to-camera Meta video ads, spoken by Jacques. Concepts: 1. "The R10,000 a month nobody could explain" (45 seconds) 2. "Full on Saturday, empty on Tuesday", hospitality cut (30 seconds) 3. "Your agency sends a report. Do you read it?" (40 seconds) 4. "We built ReviewTap. Here's what we learned." (50 seconds) For each script: - Hook (0 to 3 seconds), then body, then call to action ("Tap the link, tell us your business, and you'll have it on WhatsApp in two days.") - Written to be spoken: contractions, short sentences, natural South African phrasing. About 2.4 words per second, so check the length fits the time. - Mark [PAUSE] where a cut can go, and [CARD: text] where an on-screen text card should appear (max 6 words per card). - Give two alternative hooks to film as separate takes. Then give one shot list for the whole morning: - Framing (chest-up, eyes on the upper third, phone vertical at eye height) - Two locations at most (desk, and standing somewhere with depth behind) - Audio (lapel mic or phone close, no fan or aircon) - Take order, filming every hook variant back to back - A 10-second B-roll list per concept that can be filmed on a phone (scrolling a Google profile, a WhatsApp chat with no reply, a laptop with a spreadsheet) No em dashes anywhere.
