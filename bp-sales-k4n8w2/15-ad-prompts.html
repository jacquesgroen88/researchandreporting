Image Ad Prompts

Copy-paste prompts for an image model that produce finished ad images: the Business X-Ray ads for Meta, and the workshop promos and TikTok photo carousels. Each prompt is complete on its own.

Index · Previous: 14 · Copy, video scripts, edits and Google ads are handled by Claude directly, so they are not in this document.

Four rules for every image
  1. Check every word and number before using it. Image models still slip a letter or a digit. Zoom in on the rand figures. If a word is wrong, ask the model to fix only that word, or fix it in Canva.
  2. Generate at 4:5 for Feed and 9:16 for Stories, Reels and TikTok. Ask for both in separate runs. Keep text out of the bottom 20% of 9:16 images, where the app buttons sit.
  3. No real business names or logos except JCE Media. Add the JCE Media logo afterwards in Canva (models draw logos badly).
  4. Example numbers stay labelled. Every prompt that shows rand figures asks for the small word "Example". Keep it.

Brand reference, already written into each prompt: off-white background #f5f8fe, dark navy text #101a2e, a blue-to-indigo-to-violet gradient (#2f6bf6 to #6366f1 to #8b5cf6), an orange-to-amber call-to-action (#ff6a45 to #ffb020), a bold geometric sans-serif for headlines.

Before an image goes live

  • Zoom in: every word, every number, every date.
  • "Example" or "Illustrative" is still on any image with rand figures or a chat.
  • Logo added in Canva, bottom corner, small.
  • Text still readable on a phone held at arm's length.
  • Saved to Sales-Blueprint/ads/static/YYYY-MM-DD/ with the prompt code in the file name (X2-4x5.png).
