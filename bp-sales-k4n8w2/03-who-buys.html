Who Buys

The right buyer is not "anyone who will pay R30,000". It is someone who will pay R30,000 and can then afford R7,500 to R24,000 a month. Selling the Blueprint to anyone else is selling a strategy document at a loss of your time.

The buyer, in one paragraph

The converting buyer

An owner-operator with real revenue, real spend and no plan

Turnover above R5 million a year. Already spends R8,000 or more a month on marketing (an agency, ads, a junior marketer, a system nobody uses) and cannot say what it returns. Has a dated reason to act: a season, a launch, a second site, a partner asking questions. Has no senior marketer in the building. Answers their own WhatsApp. Acreage fits every word of this: R21.7m invoiced by Good & Proper in 2025, ~R10,000 a month on Google Ads with unclear return, R80,000 spent on a system nobody used, and year-end functions chosen in August and September.

The five segments, in priority order

A · Hospitality, venues and events

Priority 1 · proven · heavily represented in the ReviewTap base

Who: event venues, restaurants with a functions business, guesthouse and lodge groups, caterers, wedding venues. Why first: Acreage converted, DotCom and IsoBella Vita already pay you monthly, and ReviewTap's base is full of them. Their revenue is lumpy and seasonal, so a plan with dates in it lands. The pain they say out loud: "We are full on Saturdays and empty on Tuesdays", "enquiries come in and nobody follows up", "the agency sends reports I don't read". Retainer fit: Process and demand, or Full growth for groups.

B · Multi-site and franchise operators (3 to 30 sites)

Priority 2 · 14+ groups in ReviewTap order history buying one store at a time

Who: franchisees running several stores, regional groups, attractions, clinics with branches. Why: the 11 August outreach got same-day yeses from this group (Museum of Illusions, PostNet, RE/MAX Coastal) by opening with their own order history. The beauty franchise that placed two separate orders on one day because checkout could not handle a group is this buyer. The pain: every site markets itself differently and head office cannot see which works. Retainer fit: Full growth. Highest retainer value of any segment.

C · High-ticket owner-led services

Priority 3 · from the July niche shortlist

Who: medical aesthetics, dental, veterinary, solar installers, home improvement, motor dealers. Rated A+ to A on the July shortlist because a single customer is worth R5,000 to R150,000. Why: a small improvement in enquiry handling is worth a lot, so the Business X-Ray produces big rand figures. The pain: paying for leads, losing them in follow-up. Retainer fit: Process and demand. Watch: Giorgio's Revenue Systems Audit already sells to this group at R5,999. The scan score decides who gets which.

D · Established eCommerce (R5m+ online)

Priority 4 · Reptilia, and the MyForeverBaby experience

Who: Shopify and WooCommerce stores past their first R5 million. Why: you run two stores yourself and can talk contribution per rand, not ROAS. Reptilia's Blueprint found a R249,000-a-year leak. The pain: ad costs rising, ROAS reported by the platform not matching the bank. Retainer fit: Process and demand.

E · International startups and agencies (USD)

Priority 5 · 360now in 2025, the JCE foreign plan

Who: pre-launch and early-traction startups (go-to-market version, $2,700) and the agency partners in the JCE foreign plan. Why last: the foreign plan says agency partners first, and Blueprint-to-retainer conversion is unproven internationally. Use it when: an Upwork or agency conversation needs a paid first step. The Blueprint is the strategy anchor in that plan's ladder.

The hard no's

Each one is a lesson already paid for somewhere in Mission Control.

  • A partner will not be on the call. Acreage was a 50/50 partnership and Andrew was addressed nowhere in the deliverable. Ask on the qualifying call: "Who else owns this business or signs off on spend?" Book the Diagnostic Call only when all of them can attend.
  • They want the retainer and not the diagnosis. Fine if they score 14+ and you can see the plan already. Otherwise it is the cold retainer pitch this whole plan exists to avoid.
  • They will not share numbers. Offer the NDA (Acreage signed one on 30 August). If they still will not, the Blueprint will be generic and will not convert.
  • A ReviewTap customer with an open or unfulfilled order. Fix the order first. Pitching strategy to someone waiting for a stand is how you lose both.
  • Anyone who asks for a discount on the Diagnostic Call. The founding cohort is the discount. There is no second one.
