Document 06 · Getting the lead

Referrals, Partners and Content

The ReviewTap base gets the first Blueprints sold and the ads make it scalable. These three channels make it cheap: a referral costs nothing, a partner brings a buyer who already trusts someone, and content makes the Diagnostic Call shorter because they have already watched you think.

1 · The referral engine

Business owners know other business owners in the same position. A referred Blueprint buyer skips the scan, arrives trusting you, and closes faster. Referrals do not happen because the work is good. They happen because you ask at the right moment and make it easy.

The three moments to ask

On the presentation call, when they say "this is really useful"

"I'm glad. Can I ask a favour? Who else do you know who runs a business your size and is in the same spot, spending on marketing and not sure what it returns? I'd rather work with people like you than find strangers online." Write the names down on the call. Ask permission to mention them.

At the first visible win in the retainer

The first month enquiries go up, the first big booking traced to the plan. "This is exactly what we planned for. If anyone in your network would benefit from the same, I'd appreciate an introduction."

At the day-90 review

The review is also the case study recording (doc 09). End it with the ask, and with a ready-made WhatsApp message they can forward.

The forwardable message

"Hi [name], I worked with Jacques at JCE Media on our marketing plan and it was worth it. They start with a free Business X-Ray, no strings. Worth a look: jcemedia.com/xray. Tell him I sent you."

The referral reward

R3,000 off the referrer's next invoice for every referred Blueprint that is paid (10% of the full price). Referred buyers get the founding cohort price while seats remain, and skip straight to a Diagnostic Call. Keep it simple and pay it promptly: a referral reward that has to be chased stops the next referral.

2 · Partners who see the problem first

Three kinds of people hear "my marketing isn't working" before any agency does. None of them want to fix it themselves.

Accountants and bookkeepers

They see the revenue, the marketing line and the owner's frustration every month. A Blueprint makes them look good to their client, and it does not compete with anything they sell.

Approach: a 20-minute coffee or call. Bring an anonymised Blueprint. Offer a free Business X-Ray for any client they name.

Business coaches and EOS implementers

They work on strategy and accountability, not marketing execution. Their clients hit a marketing wall the coach cannot solve.

Approach: position the Blueprint as the marketing chapter of the plan they are already coaching. Invite them into the presentation call.

Non-competing suppliers

Web developers without a strategist, photographers and videographers (Acreage booked one in September), signage and print suppliers, POS providers. They meet owners at the moment of spending on marketing.

Partner fee for all three: R3,000 (10%) on every Blueprint they introduce, paid when the client pays the balance. No fee on retainers. This keeps it clean and avoids an ongoing commission obligation you would have to track.

Target: five partners signed by end of January 2027, one Blueprint a quarter from the channel by Q2. Partners are slow to start and then steady. Do not judge the channel before month four.

3 · Giorgio's handoff

Giorgio calls guesthouses, restaurants, holiday rentals, wedding venues and training providers every day. Most of them fit the Revenue Systems Audit at R5,999. A few are bigger. Those few should not get the audit pitch.

The handoff rule

If on a cold call the prospect mentions multiple sites, an agency, ad spend over R10,000 a month, or a second business, Giorgio does not pitch the audit. He books a scan: "For a business your size, Jacques does this himself. He'll build you a free Business X-Ray and WhatsApp you. What's the best number?"

His reward Needs your decision: suggested 10% of the Blueprint fee when it is paid, plus his standard 30% on any retainer for the first three months only. Decide it before the first handoff, in writing, so Acreage's "0% because he marked it Lost" is not repeated as an argument.

4 · Content that shortens the call

Content does not generate many Blueprint leads by itself. What it does is make every other lead warmer: a prospect who has seen three of your posts arrives at the Diagnostic Call already believing you know what you are talking about.

LinkedIn, from you, three posts a week

DayFormatExample
MondayA leak, with numbers"I sent an enquiry to 10 Midrand venues last week. Here's how long they took to reply." (from the scans you are already producing)
WednesdayA lesson from running your own businesses"Our best month ever and the bank account fell R21,000. Here's why I stopped trusting revenue." (the R105k correction, which is a strong story)
FridayA Blueprint teardown or client winAnonymised findings, with permission. "Almost half their bigger bookings land in Q4 and are decided in August."

Every post ends with the same soft line: "We do a free version of this for SA businesses. Link in the comments." Claude drafts from the week's scans and log; you edit for voice. No em dashes, no hype.

A monthly live teardown

Thirty minutes, last Thursday of the month, on LinkedIn Live or Zoom. Pick one willing business (a scan recipient who said yes), take it apart live with their permission, and give away the three fixes. Registrations become scan requests. The recording becomes four short clips for Meta retargeting. First one: Thursday 28 January 2027, after the holiday slump.

5 · International, when it comes up

The JCE foreign plan puts agency partners first and treats the Blueprint as the strategy anchor in its ladder. Use the go-to-market version ($2,700) when an Upwork or agency conversation needs a paid first step, and the traction version ($3,000) for established foreign businesses. Do not build a separate international acquisition channel for the Blueprint until the SA motion has 20 sales behind it.
