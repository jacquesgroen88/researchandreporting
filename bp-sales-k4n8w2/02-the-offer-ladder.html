Rung 0 · The Business X-Ray

The single most important new asset in this plan. Every channel leads to it. The July Hormozi teardown of Giorgio's motion concluded "a discovery call is an ask, not a give", and the Acreage no-shows proved it. The scan is the give.

What it is

A one-page, branded PDF or web page delivered within 48 hours of the request, titled with the business name. Three leaks, each with a rand figure and the working shown, and one free fix they can do this week without us.

Target: 45 minutes per scan. Claude produces the draft from the URL and the scrapes, you check and send. It reuses the Revenue Systems Audit five-leak engine and the /aeo-audit snapshot, so nothing here is built from zero.

The rule that makes the scan sell

The scan names what is leaking and how much. It never explains the full how of fixing it. The last line of every scan: "These are three of the leaks we could see from outside. The Blueprint finds the ones you can only see from inside, and puts them in order. If you want to talk it through, here is my calendar."

It also does a quiet second job: it qualifies. A business whose three leaks add up to R4,000 a month cannot pay R14,500 a month to fix them. Route those to the products below, never to the Blueprint.

Rung 1 · The Blueprint

The product as already defined in proposal-deck.html and gtm-startup-deck.html. This plan does not change what is in it. It changes how it is sold and how it ends.

What they buy

  • Deep analysis and goal alignment
  • 360 competitor teardown
  • Planning and funnel mapping, targeted traffic analysis
  • Awareness, nurturing and conversion blueprints
  • The marketing roadmap, with hours per phase
  • Two strategy calls (kickoff and presentation), transcribed
  • Two weeks, start to presentation

Terms

  • 60% deposit to start (R18,000), 40% on presentation (R12,000)
  • Invoiced by JCE Systems Group, no VAT
  • Kickoff is booked only when the deposit clears
  • Anchor on the investment slide: R15,000 setup + R40,000 programme, struck through, against the single R30,000
  • The client owns every document whatever they decide next

The founding cohort Proposed, needs your yes

R30,000 is a long step from a R649 stand. The first Blueprint you sold went at R10,000. Selling the next ones at R30,000 cold, with one case study, is the hardest possible version of this plan.

Recommendation

Six seats at R19,500 (R11,700 deposit, R7,800 on presentation), open only to ReviewTap customers and referrals, closing 31 December 2026 or when the sixth seat sells. The price of the seat includes permission to record a 10-minute case study call at day 90 and use the business name. The reason given to buyers is the true one: "We are building our case study library this quarter. Six businesses get it at the founding price in exchange for letting us tell their story." After six, R30,000 with no exceptions.

It does not break the 62% rule in PRICING.md: it is a named, dated, capped cohort with a stated reason, not a new price point. The anchor slide still shows R30,000 struck through.

The guarantee

Approved for Acreage on 5 August and never resolved since. Decide once and publish it everywhere or nowhere. Recommended wording: "If the diagnostic does not tell you something about your business you did not already know, you do not pay the 40% balance." It caps your risk at the balance, costs you nothing on a good Blueprint, and answers the objection every first-time buyer has without saying it out loud.

Price headroom

R30,000 is on the low side for the work. SA agencies that get paid to pitch receive about R50,000 on average and think R108,400 would be fair (Scopen 2025, 25 agencies). The retainer tiers sit inside SA market bands. Hold R30,000 until ten have sold, then test R35,000.

The capacity clock

Maximum two Blueprints started per calendar month. Not scarcity theatre: each one takes 25 to 35 hours of your time, and a third in the same month would come at the expense of the retainer clients. Say it plainly on the Diagnostic Call: "I start two a month. November has one seat left." When it is true, it is the strongest close you have.

The 14-day retainer credit Proposed

If a retainer is signed within 14 days of the presentation, R10,000 of the Blueprint fee is credited against month one. On the R14,500 tier, month one costs R4,500. It gives "let me think about it" a date, it does not touch the retainer price, and it makes the Blueprint feel like a down payment rather than a cost. Research note (doc 14): no study shows a credit lifts conversion, and the agencies claiming the highest conversion do not credit at all. A one-third partial credit is the sensible middle path. The common window is 30 days, so 14 is deliberately tight: if decisions keep landing on day 15 to 25, move it to 21. For founding cohort buyers the credit is R6,500 (one third of the fee, the same proportion).

What the credit costs

At the base case (40% conversion, 2 Blueprints a month), roughly R8,000 a month of credits given back, R96,000 a year. It is worth it only if it moves conversion by more than 6 percentage points. Track it in doc 12. If after ten presentations the conversion with the credit is no better than Acreage's without it, remove it.

Rung 3 · The refresh

A Blueprint is true the day it ships and decays the next morning. That line, from the way you run your own blueprints, is also a product. Every retainer includes a quarterly refresh against the plan. DIY clients are offered it at day 90 for R7,500, which is also the natural moment to ask whether they now want help running it.
