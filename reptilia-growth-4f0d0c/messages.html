Two rules that keep this safe.
  1. POPIA. You've said yes to emailing past customers. The law (s69(3)) allows it for similar products if they were given a chance to opt out when they bought, and in every message. Reptilia's checkout has never had a marketing opt-out box. So:
    • Every win-back email says plainly why they're getting it and has a one-click unsubscribe.
    • We start with recent buyers.
    • Anyone who unsubscribes is suppressed everywhere.
    • A marketing opt-in checkbox goes on the checkout from now on.
  2. Warm-up. The domain has never sent bulk email. Send win-back in waves of about 150 a day (Brevo's free tier allows 300 a day). Pause if bounces go over 3% or complaints over 0.3%.

4. How the abandoned checkouts work (and how you'll see them)

StepWhat happens
CaptureThe checkout asks for email and cell number first. Cart Abandonment Recovery (free) saves the basket the moment a valid email is typed, before any payment.
MarkIf there's no paid order within 15 minutes, the basket is marked "abandoned". A real server cron on the VPS runs the check; WordPress's own cron isn't reliable enough.
Seewp-admin › WooCommerce › Cart Abandonment shows every abandoned basket, with name, email, phone, contents, value, time and status (abandoned, recovered, lost). It's the first time Reptilia will be able to see any of this.
RecoverEmails AC1 (1 hour), AC2 (24 hours) and AC3 (72 hours, optional coupon). WhatsApp version in Stage 2. A recovered order stops the sequence automatically.
Act by handBaskets over R1,000 or with a live animal in them get a personal WhatsApp from the shop the same day, using /available.
Test firstTwo things to prove on staging before going live: that a shopper sent to PayFast who never returns is still recorded, and how unpaid orders behave. Since 5 August no unpaid order has been kept, even though WooCommerce's hourly "cancel unpaid" job ran. Diane checks this.
