The searches Reptilia nearly wins, and what it takes to win them

I took the top 1,000 search terms for the last 90 days and compared them with the last 90 days of the old site. Each term is grouped by topic, then sized by how many extra clicks it would bring if it moved up to position 3 or better, using a conservative click-through curve. Read-only: nothing changed in Search Console or on the site.

Three things the data says.
  1. The biggest prize is local and small animals, not reptiles. "Hamsters for sale near me" (1,645 impressions), "guinea pigs for sale near me" (1,588), "geckos for sale near me" (761), "snakes for sale near me" (584) and "pet shop vanderbijlpark" (518) all rank in positions 4 to 11 with click-through under 2%. Google answers these with the map, so they're won mainly through the Google Business Profile, then a local landing page.
  2. People search prices. 77 price questions ("how much is a hamster in rands", "ball python price south africa", "how much is a bearded dragon in south africa") total 3,036 impressions. The site doesn't answer any of them, but the new category and animal pages answer them from live stock.
  3. Whole topics fell out of Google after the move. Leopard gecko impressions dropped from 4,756 to 499, chinchilla and degu from 14,346 to 553, tarantula and spider from 2,929 to 491, substrate from 1,257 to 118, and heat and UVB from 682 to 9. Those were pages on the old site with no strong equivalent now.

2. What wins each one

Biggest · local

Google Business Profile programme

Wins: "near me" for hamsters, guinea pigs, geckos and snakes; "pet shop vanderbijlpark"; "reptile shop near me"

About 8,000 "near me" impressions a quarter. Google shows the map pack first, so the profile matters more than the website here. Add the categories Pet store and Reptile shop, all products, weekly photos of animals in store, and a steady flow of reviews from the post-purchase email and a QR card at the till. The weekly posts are written in extras.

Built · prototype

Hamsters and guinea pigs landing page

Wins: hamsters/guinea pigs for sale (near me), hamster price, how much is a hamster in rands

One page that answers price, location and what's in today, with a waiting list for guinea pigs. See the prototype. Roughly 220 extra clicks a month between hamsters and guinea pigs, the largest single SEO line.

Built · prototype

Category pages with a price guide

Wins: snakes for sale, ball python price south africa, how much is a ball python

Intro copy, live price ranges by species, beginner questions and filters. Snakes slid from position 6.3 to 8.9; that slide is what this fixes. Snakes prototype. Roll the same template out to geckos, lizards, chameleons and spiders.
