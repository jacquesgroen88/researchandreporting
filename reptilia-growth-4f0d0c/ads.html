Reptilia · Paid ads · plan and creative · 19 November 2026

The ads to run, what they look like, and how we stop them losing money

This builds on the 28 October proposal: R7,000 cap, R300-per-order stop line, ad accounts in Reptilia's name and paid on his card. What's new since then: Meta's policy now confirmed, a separate lead campaign for animals, and Shopping held back until the store's product data is ready. Nothing has been launched.

1. The order matters more than the budget

No rand gets spent until three things are true:
  1. Purchases are tracked, and a test order has proved it. GA4 has recorded zero purchases since launch. We need Google Ads conversion tracking (with enhanced conversions) and the Meta pixel firing Purchase, plus a separate "Lead" event for animal enquiries and WhatsApp clicks.
  2. The pages we pay to send people to actually sell. That means the animal page with reserve and enquiry (mockup A), the back-in-stock form, and the side-cart add-ons. Paying to send animal searchers to today's pages buys the same 4-orders-from-2,450-clicks result.
  3. Abandoned-checkout capture is live, so paid visitors who start a checkout and don't finish can still be recovered.
Realistic start date: week 3, after the Phase 0 and 1 build. Black Friday weekend (27–29 Nov) is too soon for paid ads. Use email and the in-store banner for that.

2. What to run, in phases

Phase A · weeks 3–4R2,500Google Search only: brand, feeders nationwide, local shop. These are the clearest buying searches and the cheapest proof.

Gate to B: tracking matches real orders within 10%, cost per order R300 or less on feeders, local calls coming in.
Phase B · weeks 5–8R6,500/moAdd the animal enquiry campaign on Google (Vaal plus 120km) and Meta: retargeting site visitors and abandoned baskets, plus local "new in store" video.

Gate to C: cost per enquiry R60 or less, at least 25% of enquiries turning into a sale (shop to log this), blended cost per order R300 or less.
Phase C · month 3+R9,000–12,000/moGoogle Shopping for supplies once Product markup and a Merchant Center feed are live (live animals excluded). Move 20% a week of budget into whatever is winning. Never raise budget and change ads in the same week.

3. How risk is capped

RiskControl
Spending without knowing what sellsTracking first (section 1). Only Purchase and Lead count as conversions. Page views and add-to-carts are recorded but never optimised for.
A runaway budgetA hard monthly cap per phase at account level, daily budgets as above, and Google's automatic recommendations switched off.
Paying for the wrong searchesA negative keyword list before launch: free, adopt, adoption, rescue, wild, venomous, pictures, images, care, how to, how long, lifespan, facts, cartoon, toy, gumtree, kijiji, petfinder, jobs, wholesale, export. Search terms reviewed weekly for the first 8 weeks.
Ads pointing at sold-out stockAds only point to category pages or in-stock items. Animal ad groups are paused when a species sells out. Shopping uses the live stock feed.
Policy problemsMeta: ads must clearly come from a legitimate business (allowed: "Promote live non-endangered animals offered by a legitimate business, including retail businesses"). Never peer-to-peer wording, never endangered species. Google: no threatened species; animals stay out of Shopping. Permit wording reviewed by Willem.
Animal ads attracting buyers who can't collectG4 and M2 are geo-limited to the Vaal plus 120km, and the copy says "collect in store" up front.
Losing money on small ordersThe stop line is set on contribution, not revenue: once Willem gives margins, R300 per order gets replaced by a margin-based number. Feeders get judged on 60-day value, not the first order.
We own the account, not the clientAd accounts in Reptilia's name on his card, with JCE as manager. Spend never passes through us.
Kill switchAny campaign over 2× the stop line after R1,000 spent is paused the same day. The whole account is paused if tracking breaks (daily check against WooCommerce orders).
