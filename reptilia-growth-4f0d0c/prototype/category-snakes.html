Choosing your first snake

Which snake is best for a beginner?
Normal ball pythons and corn snakes are calm, stay a manageable size and eat frozen mice readily. Come and handle a few in the shop before you decide.
What do I need before I bring a snake home?
An escape-proof enclosure, a heat mat or blanket with a thermometer, two hides, a water bowl and frozen food. See a full setup list.
Can you deliver a snake?
Live animals can't go by parcel courier. Collect in Vanderbijlpark, use our Vaal delivery, or send a specialist pet courier to collect.
Do I need a permit?
Some species need a provincial permit. Tell us where you live and we'll tell you. Willem to confirm
