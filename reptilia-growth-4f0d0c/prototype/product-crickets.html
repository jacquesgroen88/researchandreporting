Good to know

Which size?
No bigger than the space between your animal's eyes. Pinheads for baby geckos, large or extra-large for adult bearded dragons.
Keeping them alive
A ventilated tub somewhere warm, with cricket food or vegetables and a water gel. Use within a week or two.
Gut-loading and dusting
Feed the crickets well for a day before feeding them off, and dust with calcium.
