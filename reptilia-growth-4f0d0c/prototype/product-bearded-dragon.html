Complete the setup

A baby dragon needs heat, UVB light and live food from day one.

items

Care at a glance care notes for Willem to check

Heat
A basking spot of about 38–42°C for babies, with the cool end around 24–28°C.
UVB
A strong T5 UVB tube across most of the enclosure, on about 12 hours a day, replaced as the maker recommends.
Food
Babies eat mostly live insects (crickets, roaches) daily, dusted with calcium, plus finely chopped greens.
Space
Babies grow fast. Plan for an adult enclosure of about 120 × 60 × 60 cm.
