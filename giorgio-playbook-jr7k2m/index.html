How You Spend Your Day

🎯 Selling — guard this with your life

70% of your week
  • Recurring deals are the priority above everything. Discovery calls, proposals, follow-ups, warm upsells to existing customers.
  • This is the work that pays you every month and makes you a closer, not just a caller.
  • If your calling day is getting eaten by anything else, that is the problem. Protect it.

📦 Operations — keep it tight

30% of your week, in a block
  • Fulfillment, queries, physical orders. All necessary, all important, all lower value per hour.
  • Batch it into one afternoon block. Do it well, then get back on the phone.
  • Never let packing stands push your discovery calls off the day.

The Bar — How We Operate

  1. 01Confirm every meeting 24 hours ahead on WhatsApp. A booking nobody confirms is not a meeting. No-shows are the fastest way to waste a good week. Two slipped on you last week. Zero this month.
  2. 02Strategy and context before you build anything. Who are we talking to, what do they care about, what is the one action we want. Source it, do not invent it. Send me the thinking, I sign off, then you build.
  3. 03Closed means paid. A deal is not won until the setup invoice clears. The second they say yes, send the contract and the invoice. Do not let it drift.
  4. 04Never cold-call an existing ReviewTap customer. Scrub every list against our customers first, every time.
  5. 05Sell the standard package. Anything outside it carries a higher price, and the setup fee has to cover the work so month two onward is profit. Keep the offer simple.
  6. 06Keep the main thing the main thing. Side projects and shiny tools are for after hours. During the day, revenue first.
  7. 07Look professional, every time. CC me and your reviewtap address on client comms. No em dashes. No internal notes showing on calendar invites. Title meetings "ReviewTap and [Company]".
  8. 08Friday update, every Friday. Hours, calls, discoveries, deals moved, commission. Measured against this scoreboard. No surprises.
