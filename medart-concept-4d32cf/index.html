Treatments

Treatments offered

Injectable treatments are prescription medical procedures. Suitability is decided by the doctor at consultation, and not every treatment is right for every patient.

Start here

Consultation and assessment

A medical history, a facial assessment and a discussion of your goals, the options that may suit you, their risks, costs and recovery.

Injectables

Anti-wrinkle injections

Prescription injections used to soften dynamic lines, such as those on the forehead and around the eyes. Administered only after a medical consultation.

Injectables

Dermal fillers

Hyaluronic acid based fillers used to restore volume or contour, including lip treatments. Planned around your facial anatomy.

Injectables

Collagen stimulators

Injectable treatments that work gradually by encouraging the skin's own collagen production, usually over a course of sessions.

Injectables

Injection lipolysis

Injections that target small, localised areas of fat. Your doctor will advise whether you are a suitable candidate.

All medical procedures carry risks, and individual results vary. No outcome is guaranteed. Fees are discussed at consultation.

Your consultation

  1. BookCall or WhatsApp the practice to arrange a consultation. Appointments are recommended.
  2. AssessDr Marli takes your medical history and assesses your face and skin.
  3. DiscussYou talk through suitable options, risks, aftercare and fees, with time to ask questions.
  4. DecideTreatment goes ahead only once you have given informed consent. There is no pressure to proceed.
