12The hard answer about tournaments

You asked what you can enter. Here it is, straight.

You cannot legally enter a single official Fortnite tournament until 17 June 2028. Not one. Not even the free ones inside the game.

Epic's rule is 13 and over for every competitive event, and that includes the in-game Cash Cups you can see in the Compete tab. ACGL, who run the South African tournaments, are also 13+, with a parent consent form needed under 16.

So that is 22 months with no official competition available. I am not going to dress that up. It is the single most annoying fact in this whole plan.

And here is the bit that really matters

The Compete tab is right there in the game. You will see tournaments in it. You might even be able to click into one.

Do not enter one. Epic's rule says anyone competing who does not meet the age requirement is disqualified from that event.

It is the same logic as the Twitch rule. Getting flagged now, on your real account, the one with 824 hours and 14,100 kills on it, to play one tournament you cannot win anything in, would be a genuinely terrible trade.

14FNCS, explained properly

The Fortnite Champion Series. Epic's official world series.

This is the big one, and it is what you unlock at 13. Here is how it actually works, because most people your age have no idea.

The ladder

  1. Divisional CupsEveryone starts in the lowest Division. Do well and you climb toward Division 1, which is where the strongest players are and where the cash sessions happen.
  2. MajorsOnline tournaments run in stages: Play-In, then Heats, then Last Chance, then Finals. Survive each stage to reach the next.
  3. The LANThe top duos from the Major fly out and play in person. In 2026 that was Düsseldorf, Germany, for a $1,000,000 prize pool.

Verified training maps

Every code below was checked as live. Read the warning at the bottom.

Read this before you Google for more maps

We tested 12 map codes from articles called things like "best Fortnite training maps of 2026". Nine of them were dead. Disabled, deleted, or the code pointed at a completely different map.

So here is a skill worth more than any code list. Before you trust a map code, check it yourself:

  1. Go to fortnite.gg and paste the code inIt shows every island.
  2. Look at PLAYERS RIGHT NOWZero players on a 1v1 map means you cannot use it.
  3. Look at UPDATEDIf it was last updated two years ago, the building physics have changed since. Skip it.

Checking your sources instead of believing them is genuinely one of the most useful things you will ever learn, and it applies to a lot more than Fortnite maps.

How to review a death

Ten minutes. One death. Five questions. Out loud.

Most people watch a clip back and go "yeah he was just better". That is not review, that is watching. Ask these instead, in order:

  1. Where was I, and should I have been there at all?Half of all deaths are a positioning mistake wearing a costume.
  2. What were my mats and shield when it started?If you went in on low mats or no shield, the fight was lost before it began.
  3. Did I take this fight, or did it take me?A fight you chose is a mistake you can fix. A fight that found you is a rotation problem.
  4. When did this death really start?Almost always 20 seconds earlier, not 2. Rewind further than feels necessary.
  5. What is the ONE thing I would do differently?Say it out loud. Out loud works better than thinking it, which sounds fake but is not.

That fifth answer becomes your next 14 day weakness. That loop is the entire engine.
