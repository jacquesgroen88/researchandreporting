Highlights

Four months in, the engine is built and running. Here is what is working well.

A steady stream of enquiries

230 travel enquiries have come through the website and WhatsApp, running 13 to 18 a day at roughly R33 each. Cheap, and clearly interested travellers.

Quotes out the door, fast

219 quotes produced across 16 destinations, with turnaround around 20 minutes from enquiry to quote. The quoting side of this is genuinely quick.

6 bookings, R322,755 in travel

Six holidays booked through the partnership so far, from Mauritius and Zanzibar to the Maldives and New Zealand, totalling over R322,000 in travel value.

R5,500 of free ad credit secured

Google credits are stretching the advertising budget, so the roughly R5,000 a month is buying closer to R10,000 of live advertising.
