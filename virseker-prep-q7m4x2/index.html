02The company

Group, brand, products, money flows and what the brand already does.

The group: TIH

  • Telesure Investment Holdings (TIH) is the holding company. Brands on the JD cover: Auto & General, Budget Insurance, 1st for Women, Dialdirect, Virseker, Hippo.co.za (comparison), Renasa (intermediated insurer), TIH Advisory and Wealthport.
  • Short-term insurers, a long-term insurer, health insurance and a comparison platform. About 6,100 employees (Tracxn, Oct 2026). Certified Top Employer.
  • The engine is direct insurance through contact centres: marketing creates contacts and leads, sales agents quote and close, retention keeps the book. That is why the JD's KPIs read like a call-centre funnel.
  • Hippo is in the group. It is both a channel (aggregator) and a competitor for attention. Expect "aggregators" to mean Hippo plus external comparison sites.
  • This role reports into, or supports, the Head of Marketing A&G / Executive Head: Marketing. Virseker is one brand in a portfolio, so expect shared group services (media buying, data, creative, digital journeys) that you influence but do not own.

The brand: Virseker

What it is
  • "A product of Auto & General Versekeringsmaatskappy Beperk." Launched around 2011/2012 as SA's first short-term insurance product only in Afrikaans.
  • Promise: insurance in your language. Policies, service, claims and documents all in Afrikaans.
  • Tagline on site: "Saam verseker ons Afrikaans!"
  • Proof points: "100% of your valid claims paid", "Ons bel jou" (we call you).
  • Marketing lead named in Feb 2026: Morné van Emmenes ("bemarkingshoof", Maroela Media). Find out tactfully whether this is a backfill, a new level above, or a restructure.
What it sells
  • Car: comprehensive, third party fire and theft, third party, Veldbakkie (4x4/SUV), Kompak.
  • Car add-ons: paint and dent, tyre and rim, warranty, Motor Plus, vehicle value protection.
  • Other vehicles: motorbike, caravan, boat, trailer, golf cart.
  • Home: contents, buildings (incl. solar panels), portable possessions.
  • Business: 700+ business types, vehicles up to 10 tons, credit shortfall.
  • Extras: pet, personal accident, funeral, legal.

The Virseker Trust: the brand's real differentiator

  • Founded 2011. A share of every policyholder's monthly premium goes to the Trust (percentage not published).
  • Funds Afrikaans education: Virseker Studiefonds (interest-free study loans, 208+ students), Akademia, Solidariteit's Skoleondersteuningsentrum (SOS), Weet (Maroela Media's online encyclopaedia), teacher training, schools. Also disaster relief (KZN water, Jagersfontein, children's homes).
  • Totals quoted: R32M (2018), R130M "over 12 years" (homepage), R154M (Solidariteit, around 2024). Target: almost R1 billion by 2030.
  • Chair: Dr Dirk Hermann, also head of Solidariteit. The Trust and the Solidariteit Movement are the brand's strongest partner network.
Why this matters for youMost insurers buy "meaning" with advertising. Virseker has it built into the product: buying a policy funds your community's schools. The JD lists "relevance, differentiation, meaning, salience". The Trust is the meaning and the differentiation. Salience is the job.

03The market

What is happening in SA short-term insurance and in the Afrikaans audience.

Hard market, price-sensitive buyers
  • Renewal increases widely reported at 8% to 15% for clean drivers in 2026 (comparison-site figure, unverified). Drivers: parts priced in dollars and euros, theft, hijacking, weather claims.
  • AARTO demerit points expected to start feeding into premiums from mid-2026.
  • Switching is month to month. Quote gaps of R700+ a month for the same profile are quoted. Retention is a live battle.
Competitors that matter
  • OUTsurance: the benchmark. Earnings up about 20%; running a "R2,500 if we can't beat your premium" offer (Nov 2026). Best problem resolution in the CX Index (78%).
  • King Price: premiums that drop as your car depreciates. Strong Afrikaans cultural presence (Pretoria roots, rugby).
  • MiWay, Discovery Insure: lead the broker/hybrid channel in CX.
  • Naked, Pineapple: app-first, Naked raised US$17M Series B.
  • Santam: market leader, about 23% of non-life premiums, broker-led. Sanlam/Santam carry Afrikaans heritage without being Afrikaans-only.
Structural trends
  • EY 2026: digital-first players and broker consolidation are reshaping competition.
  • Flexible and usage-based premiums are growing (Insight Survey).
  • Direct insurers have taken personal-lines share from brokers for a decade.
  • AI answer engines are a new front door. In your index OUTsurance was "the answer" and TIH brands barely showed up.

The Afrikaans audience

  • Afrikaans is the home language of about 1 in 10 South Africans (Census 2022, roughly 10.6%, around 6.5 million people). Check the exact figure before quoting it.
  • The Afrikaans-speaking population is not one segment. A large share are coloured South Africans, concentrated in the Western and Northern Cape. Virseker's partner network (Solidariteit, AfriForum, Maroela) reaches mainly one part of that market.
  • Media: kykNET (DStv), Maroela Media (about 1.5M to 2M monthly unique readers in older figures), Netwerk24/Rapport/Huisgenoot (Media24), RSG, Jacaranda/OFM/Algoa for regional radio, Afrikaans music and festivals (KKNK, Aardklop, Innibos), school rugby and church networks. Daily Afrikaans print is down to 20,000 to 30,000 copies.
Handle with careThe brand sits close to a cultural and political movement. That is a strength (loyalty, meaning, a ready-made partner channel) and a ceiling (people outside that movement may not see themselves in the brand). If asked about growth beyond the core, talk about "every Afrikaans-speaking household" and the language promise, and ask how leadership sees it before offering a view. Do not criticise the partnerships.

04The job, decoded

What the spec is really asking for, and the vocabulary you need to speak fluently.

The one-line readA commercial marketing leader who owns Virseker's growth number, not a brand custodian. They want someone who can run the media, partners and sponsorships, read a contact-centre funnel, defend the budget to EXCO, and build the team and agencies to do it. The phrase "not only one discipline" in the requirements says previous candidates were either pure brand or pure performance.

Use those words back. It signals that you know how brand trackers are built and read.

Also listed: the regulatory frame

  • POPIA (consent, direct marketing opt-in, data from partners), CPA (competitions, fair marketing), and financial services conduct rules: FAIS, Treating Customers Fairly, the Policyholder Protection Rules, FSCA advertising rules. Competitions and "premium back" offers must carry clear terms.
  • Partner leads (Solidariteit, AfriForum, Maroela) raise a POPIA question: who collected consent for the call? Knowing to ask that is a strong signal.

"Running my own businesses taught me what most corporate marketers never feel: every rand of media comes out of a P&L I'm accountable for. That's the discipline this role is asking for. How to answer "the biggest budget you've managed?"

Then: "Virseker's budget will be larger than any single budget I've owned. 08Questions they are likely to ask

With the angle to take. Answer in two minutes or less, end with a number.

What do you know about Virseker?

Insurance in your language; A&G product in TIH; car, home, business and extras; Ons bel jou; the Gatsakbonus; the Trust (R130M+, R1bn by 2030 target); Solidariteit, AfriForum, Maroela; Noordvaal-beker to 2028; tied second in the CX Index. Close with: "And from the outside, the opportunity I see is salience: the brand means a lot to people who know it, but it isn't top of mind or top of answer often enough."

Sales are down 10% this quarter. What do you do?

Walk the funnel, do not jump to media. "Is it contacts, lead quality, closing or cancellations? I'd split by source and by week. If contacts are down, is it spend, price competitiveness or seasonality? If contacts are flat but sales are down, it's closing or capacity, so I'm in the contact centre with Sales. Then I separate structural (price, proposition) from executional (creative fatigue, a broken form) and pick the two or three actions that move the most."

How do you balance brand and performance spend?

Long-term brand building drives future demand; activation harvests it. Reference the Binet and Field work (a brand-heavy split is the usual starting point, tuned by category and growth stage). For a niche brand with strong meaning but low salience, I'd protect brand reach in Afrikaans media and sponsorship, and hold performance to cost per net sale. Then I'd test, because the right split for Virseker is an empirical question.

How would you make the Noordvaal-beker sponsorship pay?

200+ schools means parents: households with cars, homes and teenage drivers. Activate it: match-day QR quote offers, a "school fund" mechanic where a policy sold through a school's link funds that school (it extends the Trust story), SuperSport Schools pre-rolls with a quote CTA, player and coach content on TikTok and Instagram, parent WhatsApp communities, results reported in contacts and sales by school, not only impressions.

How would you manage our agencies?

Clear scopes, a single brief template, a quarterly scorecard covering output quality, speed, cost and business outcome. Agencies get access to the funnel data, so they are judged on what matters. You've been on the agency side, so you know that bad briefs make bad work.

Present our performance to EXCO in five minutes.

One page: the number vs target, the three drivers, what we are doing about each, what we need from the room. No channel jargon. Bring a printed example from a past client (anonymised).

A partner wants more money and you can't prove the return.

Agree measurement before the renewal: tracked quote links (they already have vdn codes), unique offers, and a test period. Then negotiate on performance, with a share of fee linked to results. Keep the relationship, because these partners are part of the brand.

How do you build a team you've inherited?

Listen first: one-on-ones, look at the work, map skills against the plan. Keep what works. Then a capability plan: coaching, clear ownership, and exposure to commercial numbers so everyone knows how their work moves sales.

Why are you leaving your own business?

"I've built and run marketing on my own brands and for 25+ clients. What I want now is one brand, real scale and a team, and a category I know from the media side. Virseker is that. I'm winding down my consultancy, so this would be my full focus."

What would you do in your first 90 days?

Use section 10 of this page. Short version: listen and diagnose for 30 days, agree priorities and quick wins in 60, commit to a plan and scorecard by 90.

Tell us about a failure.

Pick a real one with a lesson. Candidates: a paid channel you scaled before tracking was clean; the Ensinger insourcing (you learned to build for handover); a campaign judged on platform ROAS that didn't hold up against revenue.

Salary expectations?

Grade 26 is a senior band. Let Korn Ferry give you the range first. Your floor is R70k a month CTC; for a function head at this grade, ask what the band and bonus structure are before naming a number. Have your current package view ready.

10Growth ideas to have in your back pocket

Hypotheses from the outside. Offer them as questions, not as a plan, because you haven't seen the data.

1. Make the Trust visible at the point of sale

"Your premium has funded R130M+ of Afrikaans schools." A personal impact counter in the app and renewal letters. Meaning becomes a retention tool.

2. Sell the Gatsakbonus harder

Get your first year's premiums back is a strong, simple offer. Test it as the lead message in acquisition, and remind existing clients how close they are to it (a retention lever in a hard market).

3. Activate the Noordvaal-beker

School-linked quote links that fund the school, parent communities, SuperSport Schools pre-rolls with a quote CTA, results reported in sales by school.

4. Win the Afrikaans search and answer engines

Own Afrikaans insurance search terms and content, and make sure ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews name Virseker when asked in Afrikaans. Your own index showed TIH brands barely appear.

5. Partner channel as a measured engine

Solidariteit, AfriForum and Maroela already send tracked leads. Compare cost per net sale vs paid media, then scale what works with member-only offers.

6. Cross-sell the book

Pet, funeral, legal, business and add-ons to existing car and home clients. Cheaper than new acquisition and grows active book value.

7. Speed to lead

"Ons bel jou" lives or dies on how fast. Check callback time vs closing rate by hour. Your Izi Travel story is exactly this.

8. Referral

No visible referral programme. In a community-driven brand, "bring a neighbour, fund another student" could be cheap acquisition. Check compliance first.

11The first 90 days

Listen, diagnose, then commit. Keep the business running while you learn; don't change the mix in week two.

Days 1 to 30

Listen and learn the machine

  • One-on-ones with every team member, your boss, Sales, Retention, Product, Finance, Data, Compliance, each agency and each partner.
  • Sit in the contact centre for a day. Listen to sales calls and callbacks in Afrikaans.
  • Buy a policy yourself, end to end. Make a claim journey walkthrough.
  • Get the numbers: 24 months of contacts, leads, sales, net sales, cancellations, cost per sale by source; the brand tracker; the P-factor definition.
  • Read every agency scope and every partner and sponsorship contract.
  • Output: a one-page "what I've learned" to your boss. No recommendations yet.
Days 31 to 60

Diagnose and take quick wins

  • Funnel diagnosis: structural vs executional issues, ranked by rand impact.
  • Two or three quick wins with clean measurement (e.g. speed to lead, Gatsakbonus messaging test, Noordvaal-beker activation pilot).
  • One performance dashboard that Marketing, Sales and Finance all agree on.
  • Team capability map; agency scorecards set up.
  • Output: diagnosis and priorities presented to your boss and agreed.
Days 61 to 90

Commit to the plan

  • The Virseker marketing plan (or the next-year plan, depending on the budget cycle): objectives, budget split brand vs acquisition, channel mix, calendar, business cases.
  • Partnership and sponsorship measurement framework.
  • Team structure and hiring or development plan.
  • First MANCO/EXCO narrative in your format.
  • Output: the plan signed off, and a monthly scorecard everyone reports against.
Say this in the interview"In my first month I won't change the media mix. I'll learn how the machine works, listen to the contact centre, and make sure we agree on the numbers. By day 60 I'll bring you a diagnosis and two or three quick wins. By day 90, a plan you can hold me to."
