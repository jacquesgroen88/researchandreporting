04The plan

Three phases, 90 days.

Each phase opens up the next one. The site fix comes first because nothing else gets read without it.

Weeks 1 to 3

Readable and consistent

Fix how the site is read, give every page its own title and description, agree one set of numbers, and switch on measurement.

Weeks 3 to 8

Own the questions

Publish six answer pages, turn three Pulse reports into readable pages, and get two named case studies live.

Weeks 4 to 12

On the reading list

Trade press, directories, the Dig Insights listing and client reviews, so the engines find Innovatr on pages they already read.

Phase 1 · Readable and consistent

  • Pre-render every page so a plain fetch returns the real text. Your site is a React app on Google Cloud, so this is a contained build change, not a redesign
  • A unique title and description on every page: Test24, Test24 Pro, research, each case study and each Pulse report
  • Company profile in structured data (registered name, founder, Johannesburg address, LinkedIn, Dig Insights) so engines stop confusing you with other Innovatrs
  • One fact sheet you sign off: panel size and source, markets, studies run, starting price. Published at a single URL and repeated everywhere else
  • Google Analytics 4 and Search Console, with AI referrals (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot) tracked as their own channel

Phase 2 · Own the questions

  • "24-hour concept testing in South Africa": how Test24 works, what you get, what it costs
  • "What concept testing costs in South Africa": your published prices next to the alternatives. Claude currently says no one publishes them
  • "AI personas and AI qual in South Africa": your method, and where it should and shouldn't be used
  • "The Innovatr consumer panel": size, source and coverage, stated so a buyer can check it
  • "Innovatr or a global agency?": an honest guide to when each is the right call. This page takes on the quick-and-cheap framing
  • "Consumer research across African markets": multi-market reach through the Dig Insights partnership
  • Three Pulse reports rebuilt as readable pages with their numbers, plus two named client case studies (with permission)

Phase 3 · On the reading list

  • Bizcommunity press office, and two releases built on Pulse data
  • GreenBook, GoodFirms and The Manifest listings; ZoomInfo and RocketReach profiles corrected
  • Dig Insights: a listing on their partner page and a note in Research Live or MrWeb (we draft it, Dig publishes it)
  • Reviews from five clients on the platforms the engines check
  • LinkedIn: Pulse findings posted with their numbers. Google already cites your LinkedIn page

Throughout · The scoreboard

  • The same 15 questions re-run every month on all five engines, three runs each, so movement is measured, not guessed
  • A one-page monthly report: share of answer, position, accuracy, sources, and AI-referred visits and leads
  • Wrong facts flagged when they appear, and fixed at the source the engine used
05From answer to booked call

More leads, from the right people.

Visibility on its own doesn't pay. What pays is the buyer who asks the question, sees Innovatr named, and ends up on a call with you. Here is how the plan gets there, and how you'll see it happen.

01

The question picks the buyer

Someone asking "which South African companies can test product concepts in 24 hours?" has a launch on the calendar and a research problem today. That is your buyer: a marketing head, brand manager or insights lead. LinkedIn finds people by job title. A question finds them by need, at the moment they need it.

What it meansFewer, warmer conversations, not more names on a list.
02

The answer page does the selling

Each Phase 2 page answers the question first, then shows the price, the turnaround and a named example, and ends with one step: book a 15-minute scoping call, or start a Test24. The forms ask for a work email, which took your LinkedIn leads from about 27% to 91% work emails.

What it meansA buyer who arrives from an AI answer can act without waiting for a proposal.
03

A person calls back fast

Your LinkedIn report found the gap: 52 leads and 1,811 nurture emails, with strong opens and no genuine replies. "The handoff to a human is the one missing step." Every AI-referred enquiry gets a call within one business hour, tagged in your CRM with the question that brought it.

What it meansLeads from this channel become meetings, not email statistics.
04

You see what it produced

AI referrals are tracked as their own channel in Google Analytics. Every form asks "Where did you hear about us?" in the buyer's own words, because many people who meet you in an AI answer will search your name or type the address instead of clicking. Each month you get: share of answer, AI-referred visits, enquiries, calls booked and tests sold.

What it meansYou'll know whether it's working from your own numbers, not from ours.

Two buyers, two journeys.

Most of the research budget in a big company isn't spent by the CMO. Brand and marketing managers run smaller studies from their own budgets, without needing anyone to sign off, and they need answers fast. The plan is built for both buyers.

The brand or marketing manager

  • Has a question this month. A pack test, or a read on one region. About R25,000 of research, already inside their brand budget
  • Asks an AI engine: "who can test a concept in 24 hours in South Africa, and what does it cost?"
  • Sees Innovatr named, with a price. The answer page shows how Test24 works, what it costs and a named example
  • Starts a Test24 or books 15 minutes. No brief, no pitch meeting, no CMO sign-off
  • Has results in 24 hours, and comes back. Ten of these a month adds up to about R3 million a year

The CMO or head of insights

  • Has a big launch and needs a shortlist of research partners
  • Asks for "the best concept testing agencies in South Africa". Today that's Kantar, Ipsos and Ask Afrika. The plan is built to put Innovatr on that list
  • Does due diligence: "Who is Innovatr? Is the panel real? Who do they work with?" With the plan in place, the engines find one set of facts, the Dig Insights partnership and named case studies
  • Or arrives through their own team: a brand manager who has already run a Test24 brings the results in
  • Meets Richard with the trust already built
06What we will and won't promise

The engines decide. We control the inputs.

We will

Do every item in the plan

The site fix, the pages, the fact sheet, the listings and the press work, on the timeline above, with you signing off every fact before it goes out.

We will

Measure the same way every month

The 15 frozen questions on five engines, three runs each, reported as counts. You can re-run any question yourself and check us.

We won't

Promise rankings or traffic

No one controls what an AI engine says. Answers vary between runs and change week to week. Anyone guaranteeing "page one in ChatGPT" is guessing.

We won't

Inflate your claims

Engines already doubt the 44M figure. Every number we publish for you will be one a buyer can check, because that is what earns a citation.
