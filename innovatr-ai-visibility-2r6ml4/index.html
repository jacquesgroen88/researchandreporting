06Why

The engines can't read your website.

innovatr.co.za is built so the page only appears once a browser runs its code. People see the full site. AI crawlers such as OpenAI's, Anthropic's and Perplexity's mostly don't run that code, so they get an empty page. We fetched it the way they do:

innovatr.co.za, as an AI crawler sees itSame on /research, /test24-pro, /case-study/discovery
Innovatr
That is the entire readable text. 8 characters. The 37M+ panel, Test24, the Dig Insights partnership, the pricing, the case studies and the 13 Pulse insight reports on your homepage aren't there.

Everything people see on your site is the evidence the engines are looking for. Take the Pulse report "Insurance & Digital Banking in South Africa" (1,172 consumer voices): engines can't read it, so they can't cite it. Meanwhile Market Instinct's pricing guide, Yazi's "200 Gen Z interviews in under 24 hours" case study and Kantar Marketplace's concept-testing page are all readable, and they are what gets quoted.

The numbers also don't agree with each other. Your site and deck say 37M+ panellists, 78 markets, 200+ studies and from R5,000 per concept. Your note to us says a 1.5M South African panel and from R1,000 per test. ChatGPT says 44M. We traced that one: it comes from your own earlier LinkedIn ad ("Fast. Smart. Affordable.: 24 hour insights, from R3k per concept, a 44M consumer panel"), which is described on a former marketer's portfolio page. So the engines are repeating you, just an older version of you. That leaves three panel sizes (1.5M, 37M+ and 44M) and three starting prices (R1,000, R3,000 and R5,000) in public. Engines discount a company whose facts conflict, and Claude now tells buyers to question the panel figure.

One more detail: every page on the site has the same title ("Innovatr") and the same description. Even where a search engine does render the page, nothing tells it that /test24-pro is about 24-hour concept testing and /research is about the panel.

07What moves the number

Seven fixes, in order.

These marks show who controls each fix: you (Innovatr can do it alone), influence (needs someone else to act) or earn (takes time and third parties). The engines decide the outcome, so these are inputs, not promises.

1
You

Make the website readable

Have the site render its text on the server (or pre-render each page) so a plain fetch returns the real content. This one change unlocks every page, case study and Pulse report at once. It's the biggest single lever on this list, and nothing below works without it.

2
You

One set of numbers, everywhere

Agree the panel size, market count, study count and starting price. Then make the website, deck, LinkedIn and every directory say exactly that. Retire the 44M and R3k figures from your old LinkedIn ads, and ask the portfolio page that describes them to mark them as out of date. Only claim "largest in South Africa" with a figure someone can check: Kantar publicly owns "South Africa's biggest ever consumer panel".

3
You

Tell the engines which Innovatr you are

Add a full company profile in the site's structured data (registered name, founder, Johannesburg address, LinkedIn, the Dig Insights partner page) and a short "About" page. That separates you from INNOVATR by KTH Tech and innovatr.com.

4
You

Publish a page for each question you lose

"24-hour concept testing in South Africa", "How much concept testing costs in South Africa" (your R5,000 per concept is a real answer, and almost nobody else publishes one), "AI personas and AI qual in South Africa", and "Multi-market research across Africa". Write them as straight answers with numbers, not brochure copy.

5
Influence

Get the Dig Insights partnership on the record

Google already knows the partnership through Insight Platforms. ChatGPT and Claude can't find it, and Claude looks for it in Research Live and MrWeb. Ask Dig to list Innovatr on its partner page and announce it in those two trade titles.

6
Influence

Be on the pages the engines already read

A Bizcommunity press office carrying your Pulse findings, a GreenBook listing, and entries on GoodFirms and The Manifest. Correct the employee and revenue data on ZoomInfo. Keep posting Pulse numbers on LinkedIn, which Google already cites.

7
Earn

Independent proof

Engines hold back because there's "limited independent customer feedback". Named client case studies (your site lists Discovery Bank, Rain and Namibian Breweries, but engines can't read it), trade press coverage and reviews on platforms buyers check close that gap. This takes quarters, not weeks.

Separately: you told us there is no Google Analytics or Search Console on the site. Without them nobody can see which pages get found or what traffic AI sends you. Setting them up takes about an hour and should happen alongside fix 1.

09Method and limits

How this was measured.

Questions: 15, written from your answers about buyers and services, and frozen so they can be re-run exactly. 12 don't mention any brand. 3 do: Innovatr vs Kantar, "What does Innovatr do?", and Dig Insights' African partner.

Engines: ChatGPT (logged out, web search, 3 fresh runs per question). Gemini (temporary chat with no history, 3 fresh runs per question; two runs were discarded and redone after an input error). Claude (claude.ai incognito chat with web search, 3 fresh runs per question). Google AI Overviews (google.com set to South Africa, 1 run per question). Perplexity isn't included: it now refuses every question without a signed-in account, so we'll add it from a clean account at the first re-measure.

Scoring: a company counts as named when the answer text mentions it. The Visibility Score (16/100) adds four parts. Presence: named in 21 of 150 answers, 5.6 of 40. Position: never named on an open question, 0 of 20. Accuracy: 9 of 21 mentions carried a wrong, outdated or conflicting fact, 8.6 of 15. Source ownership: 5 of about 73 sources cited in answers that named Innovatr were Innovatr's own, 1.7 of 25. AI answers change between runs and week to week, so treat every figure as a count on 18 November 2026, not a permanent verdict. This report doesn't estimate traffic or sales.
