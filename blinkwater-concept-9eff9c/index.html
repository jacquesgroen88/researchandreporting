Welcome to Blinkwater

Comfortable rooms, quiet farm nights

Blinkwater sits on Farm Hardekoolbult just outside Koedoeskop, close to local shops and the game farms of the area. Every room has its own entrance, a private bathroom, air-conditioning and a TV.

Rates include breakfast, the restaurant is on the property, and there is free Wi-Fi and secure parking for the bakkie or the company car. Pets are welcome by prior arrangement.

The restaurant

Dinner without driving

Blinkwater's restaurant is right on the property, so after a long day on the road or in the veld supper is a short walk from your room. Breakfast is included in every stay and further meals are by arrangement.
