Journey two of three

The agent

Estate agents are personal brands. Their reputation follows them between listings, between offices and between agencies. So we give each of your agents something worth carrying, and then we count what it does.

1

A card with their name on it

Every agent gets a physical branded card and a digital business card at their own link. At a show house they tap a buyer's phone or hand the card over. The buyer lands on the page beside this.

2

The buyer saves them, not the office

One tap saves the agent to the buyer's contacts, with the photo, the office and the direct WhatsApp. No app, no typing, nothing to lose in a handbag.

3

Every tap is counted against their name

Each card carries its own tracked link. Every scan, tap and click passes through our redirect before it lands, so scans, saves, review clicks and WhatsApp opens are all attributed to that agent rather than to an office average.

4

Reviews land under their name

The review prompt sits on their card. A seller who was looked after well reviews the agent who did it. That is how Plettenberg Bay goes from one review to twenty without anybody running a campaign.

5

They get their own scoreboard

Each agent logs in and sees their own work and nobody else's: enquiries handled, how fast they answered, viewings booked, cards scanned, reviews collected. Good agents want a scoreboard. It is attribution, not surveillance.
