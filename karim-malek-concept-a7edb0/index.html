How to book

Seeing a doctor

Consultations are by appointment.

  1. Phone the roomsCall 011 486 4494 during consulting hours and the reception team will find you a time.
  2. Or send a requestUse the appointment request form below or email karimmalek@mweb.co.za. The rooms will contact you to confirm.
  3. Bring with youYour ID, your medical aid card if you have one, and a list of any medication you take.

Payment: credit card, debit card and tap-to-pay are accepted. For medical aid questions, please ask when you book.
