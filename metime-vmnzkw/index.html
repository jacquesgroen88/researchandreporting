Welcome to MeTime

A home base for the whole family.

MeTime is an owner-run guest house in the heart of Hartenbos. Every room is private with its own en-suite, and everyone shares a fully equipped kitchen, a lounge and a garden with two braai areas under a big old tree. The beach, the waterpark and the Seafront Mall are all within walking distance.

  • Secure parking with CCTV
  • Free Wi-Fi everywhere

All questions and contact

What are the check-in and check-out times?+

Check-in is from 14:00 to 18:00 and check-out is by 10:00. Arriving later? Let Rico know and he will help.

Is MeTime self-catering?+

Yes. Every room has a bar fridge, kettle and welcome coffee and tea, and all guests share a fully equipped open-plan kitchen, a lounge and dining area, laundry facilities and two garden braai areas.

Are children welcome?+

All children are welcome. Three of our five rooms are family rooms with a queen bed and a bunk bed. Each guest using an existing bed is charged as a full guest, and there is no space for extra beds.

How far is the beach?+

Hartenbos beach is about a 7-minute walk. The Seafront Mall, the Hartenbos waterpark, Spur and Ocean Basket are about 800 m away.

Is there backup power during load shedding?+

Yes. MeTime has a solar backup system, so the lights and Wi-Fi stay on.
