Welcome

A calm base in Freemanville

Letsema is a small guest house in Freemanville, Klerksdorp, a few minutes' drive from the Rio Hotel Casino and Convention Resort. Our guests are mostly here for work, with couples and friends visiting too.

Each suite has its own bathroom, air conditioning and free WiFi. The rooms open onto a shaded walkway and a lawn with a fountain bed, and the whole property sits behind an electric fence and a remote-controlled gate, so your car stays safe overnight.

Breakfast is available in the dining room each morning, buffet or full English.
