Stay compliant

When does your team's card run out?

Under the Driven Machinery Regulations, nobody may operate a lifting machine without a valid certificate and carry card. A card is valid for 24 months. After it expires there are 90 days to re-license, after which the operator must redo the full course.

  • We remind you before every card in your team expires
  • Re-licensing is shorter and cheaper than starting over
  • Your whole operator register lives in your client portal, ready for an inspector

Questions we hear every week

Good to know

How long is an operator certificate valid?

The carry card is valid for 24 months from the date the operator is found competent. Once it expires, the operator has 90 days to re-license. After that, the full course must be done again. We track this for you and remind you well before the date.

Can you train on our premises, on our machines?

Yes, most of our training happens on client sites across Gauteng and the Vaal. We can also train at our own centre in Three Rivers, Vereeniging, on our own machines.

How many people can you train at once?

The National Code of Practice allows up to 12 learners per facilitator for theory and no more than 4 learners per machine for practical training. For bigger teams we split into groups or bring more machines.

What does each learner receive?

A certificate of competence and a carry card showing the machine code, capacity, restrictions and expiry date, both with a serial number and QR code that anyone can verify.

How do I know where my certificates are?

Every booking has a live status in your client portal, from learner documents through assessment and moderation to the final certificate.
