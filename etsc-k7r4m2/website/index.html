Questions

Good to know before you book

Still unsure? Call 086 133 3208 or WhatsApp us and a trainer will help.

How long is an operator certificate valid?

The carry card is valid for 24 months from the date the operator is found competent. After it expires there are 90 days to re-license. Past that, the full course must be done again.

Can you train on our premises, on our machines?

Yes. Most of our training happens on client sites across Gauteng and the Vaal. We can also train at our centre in Three Rivers, Vereeniging, on our own forklift.

How many people can you train at once?

Up to 12 learners per facilitator for theory and no more than 4 per machine for practical. For larger teams we run several groups or bring more machines.

What does each learner receive?

A certificate of competence and a carry card showing the machine code, capacity, restrictions and expiry date, each with a serial number and QR code.

Do you help with skills development records?

Yes. Attendance registers, results, certificates and training spend are all available in your client portal for your WSP, ATR and B-BBEE files.
