Where AI goes

Where AI earns its place, and where it shouldn't touch

You asked for judgement on this observed. My rule of thumb: automate anything that's research, first drafts or plumbing, and keep a person on anything a prospect reads as personal. my approach

AI does it

  • Account and contact research before outreach, summarised in the CRM record
  • First drafts of posts, emails and landing page variants from the founder's own notes and calls
  • Turning one webinar recording into a month of posts, clips and a nurture email
  • Enrichment, deduplication and routing
  • The weekly report, pulled from the platforms and written up

A person does it

  • The final edit on anything under the founder's name
  • The first reply to someone who raised their hand
  • Deciding who gets a personal message versus a sequence
  • Offers, pricing language and anything a customer could quote back
  • Pausing a workflow when it starts producing the wrong kind of lead

The point isn't fewer people. It's that the founder's hour goes on conversations, not on drafting and admin.

A workflow I built

An AI workflow I built and use

You asked for one example. This one runs my own B2B product's reporting.

The jobGetting ad results, store sales and CRM conversations into one picture was a manual job, and each platform reported its own version of the truth.
The toolsClaude Code as the operator, the Meta Ads API, Google Ads through Make, the Shopify store, and our CRM's conversation data, written into a branded report.
How it connectsOne command pulls each source, reconciles orders against ad-reported conversions, and writes the report. A person reads it and decides.
What it improvedThe first run showed Google was overstating its return by 47%, because purchases were being counted twice. Once that was fixed, the channel on 6% of the budget turned out to be producing 22% of revenue. That changed where the money went.
The bigger pictureI run three businesses on a setup like this: 20+ integrations and 10 scheduled routines, with a written rule for anything that touches a live ad account. These notes were researched and built the same way.
