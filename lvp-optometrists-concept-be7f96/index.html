Our practice

An East Rand practice since 1940

The practice has passed from optometrist to optometrist for more than eight decades, and moved to Elridge Corner in Bartlett in 2009.

1940

Deryck Humphriss opens the practice in Benoni.

Later

Jan Lombard joins as partner after his studies. The practice becomes Humphries and Lombard Optometrists.

1977

Beulah Verster qualifies and joins full time, after working weekends as a student.

1990

Georg Pheiffer joins full time after qualifying at the then RAU.

1995

The practice is renamed Lombard Verster & Pheiffer.

2009

The practice moves to Elridge Corner, Bartlett, Boksburg.

Urgent eye problems

Phone us during opening hours if you have:

  • a sudden change in your vision
  • flashes or a shower of new floaters
  • a painful red eye

For an eye injury, chemicals in the eye or sudden loss of vision, go to the nearest hospital emergency unit straight away.
