About this course

First Aid is offered at three levels, complying with the Occupational Health and Safety Act (Act 85 of 1993). The courses are intended for employees for whom First Aid is a mandatory job requirement. Levels can be taken concurrently over six days, or individually at roughly 2.5 days each.

Who it is for

  • Employees for whom first aid is a mandatory job requirement
  • Health and safety representatives
  • Employers carrying statutory first aid cover

What you will learn

  • Introduction to first aid
  • Patient management — shock, unconsciousness and level of consciousness
  • Adult and child CPR
  • Bleeding and wound care
  • Medical conditions
  • Secondary survey
  • Burns and skeletal injuries
  • Triage

Worth knowing

The compliance question

Act 85 of 1993 puts the duty on the employer, not the employee. If you are staffing a site and are not sure which level you are required to hold, ask us before you book.

Run it on your site

This is the course we most often deliver at the client premises. Six days, your staff, your building, no travel cost.
