About this course

First Aid runs at three levels and complies with the Occupational Health and Safety Act, Act 85 of 1993. The courses are for employees who carry first aid as a job requirement. Levels can be taken together over six days, or individually at roughly two and a half days each.

Who it is for

  • Employees who carry first aid as a job requirement
  • Health and safety representatives
  • Employers carrying statutory first aid cover

What you will learn

  • Introduction to first aid
  • Patient management: shock, unconsciousness and level of consciousness
  • Adult and child CPR
  • Bleeding and wound care
  • Medical conditions
  • Secondary survey
  • Burns and skeletal injuries
  • Triage

Worth knowing

The compliance question

Act 85 of 1993 puts the duty on the employer. If you are staffing a site and are unsure which level you must hold, ask us before you book.

On your site

This is the course we most often run at client premises. Six days, your staff, your building, no travel cost.
