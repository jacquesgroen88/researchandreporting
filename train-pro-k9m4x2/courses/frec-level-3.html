About this course

First Responder Emergency Care Level 3 is endorsed by the Faculty of Pre-Hospital Care of the Royal College of Surgeons of Edinburgh and meets the PHEM skills framework at descriptor level D. It has been accepted by employers in the international arena, in austere environments such as the Republic of Iraq.

Who it is for

  • Close protection officers
  • Event medical responders
  • First responders
  • Pre-hospital professionals

What you will learn

  • Scene safety
  • Patient assessment
  • Manual handling
  • Catastrophic bleeding management
  • Airway management
  • Life support
  • Haemorrhage and shock management
  • Recognition of medical emergencies
  • Recognition of trauma emergencies
  • Recognition of sepsis

Worth knowing

PHEM descriptor level D

The PHEM skills framework is published and public. Mapping to descriptor level D is the technical reason this award reads the same way in Johannesburg and in Basra.

Holding your CPD

The award runs three years. Graduates complete a BLS course each year to keep CPD points current. We schedule those with you.
