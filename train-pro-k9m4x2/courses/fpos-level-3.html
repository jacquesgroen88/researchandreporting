About this course

FPOS Level 3 (Enhanced) is designed specifically for those in specialist roles such as Close Protection Officers, hostile environment team medics and firearms officers. It carries an internationally accredited qualification endorsed by the Faculty of Pre-Hospital Care of the Royal College of Surgeons of Edinburgh.

Who it is for

  • Close Protection Officers
  • Hostile environment team medics
  • Firearms officers
  • Specialist security and risk roles

What you will learn

  • Scene safety and triage
  • Patient assessment
  • Principles of manual handling
  • Catastrophic bleeding management
  • Airway skills
  • Respiratory assessment
  • Life support
  • Haemorrhage and shock management
  • Recognition of medical emergencies
  • Recognition of trauma emergencies
  • Recognition of sepsis
  • Assisting healthcare professionals
  • Paediatric life support

Worth knowing

Why the endorsement matters

The Faculty of Pre-Hospital Care endorsement is what makes this qualification portable. It is recognised by employers who will not accept an in-house certificate.

Keeping it current

The qualification is valid for three years. We will flag your renewal window before it closes rather than after.
