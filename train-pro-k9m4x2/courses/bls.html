BLS for Healthcare Providers

About this course

A video based, instructor led course covering single rescuer and team basic life support. It prepares healthcare providers to recognise life threatening emergencies, give high quality chest compressions, deliver appropriate ventilations and use an AED early. The course runs to thirty-six lessons and includes a student manual.

What it counts toward

+15CPD points
+1 000Discovery Miles
+2SAIOSH points

Who it is for

  • Registered healthcare professionals
  • Healthcare providers holding resuscitation currency
  • Candidates with prior first aid knowledge

What you will learn

  • CPR and choking for adults, children and infants
  • Ventilation with barrier devices
  • Pocket masks
  • Bag valve mask devices with supplemental oxygen
  • Automated External Defibrillators
  • Thirty-six structured lessons with student manual

How you are assessed

Candidates demonstrate CPR and AED proficiency and score at least 84 percent in the final written evaluation.

Worth knowing

What it counts toward
