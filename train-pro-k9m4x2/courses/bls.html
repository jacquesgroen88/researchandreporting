Basic Life Support

BLS for Healthcare Providers

About this course

A video-based, instructor-led course that teaches both single-rescuer and team basic life support. It prepares healthcare providers to promptly recognise several life-threatening emergencies, give high-quality chest compressions, deliver appropriate ventilations and provide early use of an AED. The course runs to 36 lessons and includes a student manual.

What it counts toward

+15CPD points
+1 000Discovery Miles
+2SAIOSH points

Who it is for

  • Registered healthcare professionals
  • Healthcare providers holding resuscitation currency
  • Candidates with prior First Aid knowledge

What you will learn

  • CPR and choking for adults, children and infants
  • Ventilation with barrier devices
  • Pocket masks
  • Bag-valve-mask devices with supplemental oxygen
  • Use of Automated External Defibrillators
  • 36 structured lessons with student manual

How you are assessed

Candidates must demonstrate CPR and AED proficiency and achieve at least 84% in the final written evaluation.

Worth knowing

What it counts toward

The course carries 15 CPD points, 1 000 Discovery Miles and 2 SAIOSH points.
