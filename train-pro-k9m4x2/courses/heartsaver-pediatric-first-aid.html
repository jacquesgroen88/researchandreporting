About this course

An American Heart Association course for people who work with children, covering accident prevention, medical emergencies, trauma injuries, environmental emergencies, CPR, choking and AED use, taught through hands-on practice in a home setting.

Who it is for

  • Parents
  • Caregivers
  • Guardians
  • Domestic workers
  • Teachers, crèche employees, preschool educators and aftercare tutors

What you will learn

  • Accident prevention
  • Medical emergencies
  • Trauma injuries
  • Environmental emergencies
  • CPR
  • Choking
  • Use of an AED
