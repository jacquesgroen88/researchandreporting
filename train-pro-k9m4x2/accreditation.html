Why it matters

A certificateand aqualification

A training company can print anything. What an employer, a client or an overseas contractor checks is the awarding organisation and the centre number behind it.

  • Registered centre status permits Train Pro to deliver and certificate Qualsafe qualifications in South Africa, under centre number 0907253.
  • FPHC endorsement from the Royal College of Surgeons of Edinburgh is why FPOS and FREC Level 3 read the same way abroad.
  • PHEM descriptor level D is a published skills framework. FREC Level 3 maps to it.
  • A Pearson BTEC is a regulated award. The CPO Level 3 Certificate is one.
