Home/About

Medical and security interlink. So we teach them together.

The founder

David Spangenberg

A bachelor's degree in law enforcement. Moderator with various SETAs. Qualified bodyguard and instructor with Education and Training certification in the UK. Further studies in Occupational Health and Safety and in Project Management.

Before Train Pro was registered, he taught First Aid Levels 1 to 3 in partnership with EMFT, American Heart Association courses from Basic Life Support through CPR for Everyone, and the full Resuscitation Council of South Africa range.

The Train Pro International team is drawn from security, health care and education-and-training, with experience in the private and public sector, nationally and internationally.

How it happened

From one idea to eleven accredited courses.

2014

David Spangenberg sets out to build a company that brings diverse training modes under one roof, on the view that medical and security interlink in too many ways to keep teaching them separately.

2015

Train Pro International (Pty) Ltd is registered. Before this, David taught First Aid Levels 1 to 3 in partnership with EMFT, American Heart Association courses from Basic Life Support through CPR for Everyone, and the full Resuscitation Council of South Africa range.

2017

Partnership with the Professional Bodyguard Association begins, first with First Person on Scene Level 3, then Pearson BTEC First Person on Scene Level 4.

2019

Internationally accredited CPO Level 3 launches, both RPL and full courses. Late in the year Train Pro takes over Accident Scene Management in South Africa, in partnership with Road Guardians International.

Today

A residential training centre in Villiers with accommodation for learners, and a course range spanning security professionals, healthcare professionals, workplaces and families. More courses continue to be added.
