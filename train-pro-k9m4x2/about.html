The founder

DavidSpangenberg

A bachelor's degree in law enforcement. Moderator with various SETAs. Qualified bodyguard and instructor with Education and Training certification in the UK. Further studies in Occupational Health and Safety and in Project Management.

Before Train Pro was registered he taught First Aid Levels 1 to 3 with EMFT, American Heart Association courses from Basic Life Support through CPR for Everyone, and the full Resuscitation Council of South Africa range.

The Train Pro team is drawn from security, health care and education and training, with private and public sector experience nationally and internationally.

How it happened

2014

David Spangenberg sets out to build a company bringing several training modes under one roof, on the view that medical and security work interlink too closely to keep teaching them apart.

2015

Train Pro International (Pty) Ltd is registered. Before this David taught First Aid Levels 1 to 3 with EMFT, American Heart Association courses from Basic Life Support through CPR for Everyone, and the full Resuscitation Council of South Africa range.

2017

The partnership with the Professional Bodyguard Association begins. First Person on Scene Level 3, then Pearson BTEC First Person on Scene Level 4.

2019

Internationally accredited CPO Level 3 launches, RPL and full course. Late in the year Train Pro takes over Accident Scene Management in South Africa with Road Guardians International.

Today

A residential training centre in Villiers with accommodation for learners, and eleven courses across security, healthcare, workplaces and families. More are being added.
