Home/Training centre

Two ways to run it.

At your premises, which is how most of our work is delivered. Or at our residential training centre in Villiers, with accommodation for learners.

At your premises

Your building, your staff, your shift pattern. No travel cost, no lost days, no coordinating a group across a province. For statutory First Aid across a team, or a BLS refresher across a clinical department, this is almost always the right call.

  • Group delivery
  • Your schedule
  • No learner travel
Ask about on-site delivery →

At the Villiers centre

Our own residential training centre with accommodation for learners. Practical scenario space rather than a hired boardroom. This is where the long-format security courses run — a 15-day close protection course is not something you deliver out of a meeting room.

  • Accommodation on site
  • Scenario space
  • Multi-day formats
Ask about the centre →

Residential

Beds, not just a venue.

A close protection course runs fifteen days. FPOS Level 3 runs five. The statutory First Aid block runs six. For anyone travelling in, accommodation is the difference between attending and not.

  • On-site learner accommodation
  • Practical space for scenario work and casualty simulation
  • Equipment on hand — immobilisation, airway, AED, BVM
  • Villiers, Free State — reachable from Gauteng and the Free State

What it looks like

Real courses, real scenarios.
