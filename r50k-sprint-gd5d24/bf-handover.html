5. Step by step: the GHL build

All of this is in the ReviewTap sub-account.

1Import the two lists

  1. Contacts → Import (top right) → upload bf26-buyer-IMPORT.csv.
  2. Map the columns: email → Email, first_name → First Name, last_name → Last Name, phone → Phone, company → Company Name, tags → Tags.
  3. Choose Create and update contacts, matched on email. Most of these people are already in GHL. This adds the tag without creating duplicates.
  4. If GHL asks, don't let the import overwrite existing names that are filled in, and leave existing tags alone (add, don't replace).
  5. Repeat for bf26-subscriber-IMPORT.csv.
  6. Check: Contacts → filter Tag is bf26-buyer should show about 606, and bf26-subscriber about 262. A few under is fine (GHL merges duplicates). Write the two real numbers down for Jacques.

The workflows don't exist yet when you import, so importing sends nothing.

2Create two helper tags

  • bf26-ordered: anyone who buys during the promo gets this tag, and it stops their emails (Step 8).
  • bf26-test: for your test contacts only.

3Check the sender

Open the existing Smart Card workflow from August and look at its Send Email step. Use the same From name, From email and Reply-to in every Send Email step below. That address already has a sending history, and replies have to reach a monitored inbox. If you are unsure, ask Jacques before you publish.

4Workflow A: "BF26 Buyers"

Automation → Workflows → Create → Start from scratch.

  1. Trigger: Contact Tag → tag added → bf26-buyer.
  2. Wait → until specific date/time → Sun 22 Nov 2026, 09:00 (SAST). This lets you add everyone the evening before without anything going early.
  3. Send Email → template BF26 B1 → subject and preview text from section 2.
  4. Wait → until Fri 27 Nov 2026, 09:00.
  5. If/Else → contact has tag bf26-ordered → Yes: End. No: continue.
  6. Send Email → BF26 B2.
  7. Wait → until Mon 30 Nov 2026, 09:00.
  8. If/Else → has tag bf26-ordered → Yes: End.
  9. Send Email → BF26 B3.

Workflow settings:

  • Allow re-entry: OFF
  • Stop on response: ON (if they reply, they stop getting sales emails and you answer them personally)
  • Time zone: Africa/Johannesburg
  • Drip mode: ON, 100 contacts every 2 hours. This spreads B1 out over a morning instead of hitting 600 inboxes at once, which protects the sending domain.

Publish it. Publishing sends nothing, because a tag trigger only fires for tags added after the workflow is live, and everyone was tagged at import.

6.2 The full workflow, on fast-forward (30 min)

This proves the sequence, the first-name merge, the stop-on-order rule and the stop-on-reply rule, without waiting a week.

  1. Create two test contacts with your own addresses (Gmail plus-addressing works, for example you+bf1@gmail.com and you+bf2@gmail.com). Give them first names "TestOne" and "TestTwo" and tag them bf26-test.
  2. Duplicate Workflow A and name the copy "BF26 Buyers TEST". In the copy, change the trigger to tag bf26-test, and change all three Wait steps to wait 3 minutes. Publish the copy.
  3. Remove and re-add the tag bf26-test on TestOne (or use Add to Workflow on just that contact).
  4. Check: B1, B2 and B3 arrive about 3 minutes apart, in that order, and each says "Hi TestOne,". Screenshot your inbox.
  5. Now TestTwo: enroll it, and as soon as B1 arrives, add the tag bf26-ordered to TestTwo. Check that B2 and B3 never arrive. This proves buyers stop getting emails.
  6. Stop-on-reply: enroll TestOne again (remove it from the test workflow first if needed), reply to B1, and check that B2 doesn't follow. Also check your reply shows up in GHL Conversations.
  7. Do the same with a copy of Workflow B ("BF26 Subscribers TEST"): S1, then S3, then S2 should arrive in that order.
  8. When everything passes, delete both TEST workflows and the two test contacts, so nothing stray can fire later.

8. While it runs (22 to 30 Nov)

GHL can't see Shopify orders (the Shopify plan blocks order sync), so this is manual and takes about 2 minutes:

  1. Shopify admin → Orders → filter date from Sun 22 Nov.
  2. For each customer email in that list, find the contact in GHL and add the tag bf26-ordered.
  3. Do it on Thu 26 Nov (before the 27 Nov emails) and again on Sun 29 Nov (before the 30 Nov emails). Claude can pull the list for you if you ask Jacques.

Replies

  • Replies land in GHL Conversations. Answer within 15 minutes in working hours, as with every post-sale reply.
  • "Can you check my old design?": yes, ask for their previous order number and pass it to the design side.
  • "5 or more stands" or "group price": thank them, say Jacques will send a group price today, and send it to Jacques.
  • Unsubscribes and complaints: leave GHL to handle them. Never re-add someone who unsubscribed.

Daily (2 min)

Check each workflow's stats: delivered, opened, clicked, bounced, unsubscribed. Send Jacques one line a day: "B1: 590 delivered, 41% opened, 23 clicks, 2 unsubscribes, 0 errors."

After 30 Nov

Both workflows end on their own after the last email. On Tue 1 Dec, send Jacques the final stats per email.

What the new email (S3) is for

Subscribers who have never bought used to get only 2 emails: a launch email on 23 Nov and "ends tonight" on 30 Nov, with nothing for a whole week in between, including Black Friday itself. S3 fills that gap on Black Friday with the three questions that stop people ordering: does it work on my customers' phones, is there a monthly fee, and what if I don't like the design. Its answers are no app, no monthly fee, and design proof before print.
