Black Friday emails: before and after

All six emails rewritten on 23 Nov. The goal: on a phone, the reader sees what they get, the price and the button without scrolling. The dashed orange line marks roughly where the first screen ends on a phone. Nothing has been sent, and GHL still holds the old versions.

Offer images. Built from real product and customer photos. The prices are also in the email text, so the offer still reads with images switched off.

What changed and why

  • The price, a picture of the 4 cards and the button are now the first thing on screen. Before, they came after two paragraphs and a photo.
  • The subject names the product and the price instead of a general Black Friday line.
  • One offer. The Counter Kit is now a single P.S. line.
  • Added a WhatsApp route: a prefilled message for anyone who would rather order through us than through a checkout.
  • Kept the reorder shortcut (previous order number in the notes).
  • The price is corrected to R1,048. That is what the cart actually charges (4 x R262). The old email said R1,049.
