The 15-minute call

  1. Two minutes on them. "How do enquiries reach you after dinner, and who answers them?" Let them tell you about the late-night gap in their own words.
  2. Play their demo on screen. Pause on the special-request answer and say: "That answer came from your website. If it's wrong, that's the first thing we'd fix."
  3. Show the morning view and the review request. Every guest gets the same Google link, and unhappy guests are not filtered out.
  4. Run through the "Confirm on the call" list on their card (rates, breakfast, parking), so the live setup is right.
  5. Say the price and the start date in one sentence, then stop talking.
  6. Where the rebuild case is "Strong", offer the website as a second step, not the first.

Don't claim: booking confirmations (the assistant passes requests to the team), review counts you'll get them, or anything their site doesn't publish.
