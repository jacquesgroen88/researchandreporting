R50K Sprint Prompt (18 Nov to 3 Dec 2026)

Built 18 Nov 2026 from your original ask, improved over three passes. Final prompt is at the bottom. Day 0 ran the same night: see TRACKER.md, DECISIONS.md, LOOP-LOG.md and STORY.md in this folder.

Your original ask

I want you to generate R50k in the next 15 days. Do most of it without my intervention and work almost autonomously. Loop and iterate up to 10 times. I should only need to make major decisions. I'm documenting this process too.

Pass 1: straight rewrite

Generate R50,000 in revenue for my businesses over the next 15 days. Work autonomously. Find the fastest money across JCE Media, ReviewTap and MyForeverBaby, execute, and only come to me for big decisions. Iterate up to 10 times. Keep a log I can use for documentation.

What is wrong with it 1. "Generate R50k" is undefined. Revenue, invoiced, or collected? Your own spec says the daily number is rands collected, so an agent could "hit" R50k by invoicing money that never lands. 2. It does not separate money already owed from new money. Acreage month 3 (R24,000), Clarky's month 2 (JSG-1044, raised 16 Nov, not sent) and Maison de Blom payment 2 (R2,250, due 20 Nov) alone come close to R36k. Collecting those is real cash but it is not "generated". You need to say whether they count, or the agent will declare victory on Day 2. 3. "Autonomously" collides with hard rules: no sending messages, posting, spending or changing live ad delivery without your yes. Without a defined send window the agent either stalls or overreaches. 4. "Iterate 10 times" has no loop shape. Iterate on what, with what check between loops? 5. No stop rules, no budget, no documentation format.

Pass 2: defined target, inventory, loop

Goal: R50,000 of NEW cash collected into JSG / Duel Digital / MFB bank accounts between 19 Nov and 3 Dec 2026. Receivables already owed before 18 Nov are tracked separately as "recovered" and do not count toward the R50k, but chase them anyway.

Start by reading SCOREBOARD.md, WAITING-ON.md, MISSION_NOTES.md, DAILY-OPERATING-SPEC.md, the last 2 weeks of ACTION-BOARD-LOG.md and the Project Planner. Build a ranked list of every rand that could land by 3 Dec: probability x amount x days-to-cash.

Loop up to 10 times: pick the top 3 plays, prepare everything they need, queue what needs my yes, execute what does not, measure, re-rank.

Ask me only for: pricing, scope, contracts, sends to clients, ad spend changes. Log every loop in Ops/R50K-SPRINT/.

What is still wrong 1. Black Friday is 27 Nov, inside the window, and it is the single biggest volume event you have (RT kit exists in Reviewtap/Black-Friday-2026/). The prompt does not force it onto the board. 2. "Ask me for sends" with you asleep means the agent prepares and nothing ships. It needs a daily approval batch: one message, everything to approve in one read, you reply "yes 1-7". 3. No mechanism for loops to actually run over 15 days. A chat session stops. It needs scheduled check-ins (morning-dispatch already runs at 06:35 weekdays) and a state file each loop reads. 4. No guard against the known failure modes in this system: building instead of selling (your 08:00-10:00 no-building rule), board bloat past the 15-card cap, inventing figures instead of pulling them live, and MFB being stock-constrained (pre-orders OFF until after Christmas). 5. "Documenting this process" deserves its own output: a readable story, not just a log.

Pass 3: what changed

  • Locked the target definition, with the "does recovered money count" question surfaced as your first decision instead of assumed.
  • Added a pre-cleared send authority you grant once (templates, recipients and caps), so the agent can move overnight without stalling, and everything outside it waits for the daily batch.
  • Added a fixed loop shape with a gate between loops, a state file, scheduled wake-ups and a numeric stop rule for every play.
  • Pinned the known plays, so the agent does not rediscover what the register already knows.
  • Added a separate documentation output for your build-in-public record.
  • Made the guardrails explicit, so nothing on the long list of "never" happens at 3am.

FINAL PROMPT (paste this when you say go)

R50K SPRINT. 19 Nov to 3 Dec 2026. You are running it; I make the big calls.

## 1. The target (no reinterpretation)
- WIN = R50,000 of NEW cash landed in a bank account I own (Bank Zero JSG, Duel Digital FNB,
  MFB, Wise USD at fetched spot rate) between 19 Nov and 3 Dec 2026.
- NEW = from a sale, deal, order, upsell or renewal that was not already invoiced or owed
  before 18 Nov 2026.
- RECOVERED = money already owed before 18 Nov (Acreage month 3, Clarky's JSG-1044, Maison de
  Blom payment 2, Die Veiling build payment 2, Izi fees). Chase all of it hard, report it on its
  own line, and it does NOT count toward the R50k unless I answer Decision 0 differently.
- Contribution, not revenue: store sales count at net of COGS, fees, shipping and ad spend.
  Invoiced-but-unpaid counts as zero.
- Only verified figures. Every rand on the tracker carries its source (bank line, Shopify order,
  Payfast ref). No figure carried forward from a doc without re-checking it live.

## 2. Day 0 (tonight, before any play)
1. Read: DAILY-OPERATING-SPEC.md, SCOREBOARD.md, WAITING-ON.md, MISSION_NOTES.md, the last 14
   days of ACTION-BOARD-LOG.md, the Project Planner (my_day + open tasks), and the relevant
   Seven Figure Blueprint money docs. Run /scoreboard logic for November collected-to-date.
2. Build Ops/R50K-SPRINT/TRACKER.md: every rand that could land by 3 Dec, scored as
   amount x probability x (1 / days-to-cash) x (1 / my-minutes-needed). Split NEW vs RECOVERED.
3. Must-consider plays (score them, do not assume them):
   - ReviewTap Black Friday (27 Nov): kit in Reviewtap/Black-Friday-2026/. Email to past
     customers + Smart Card list (fulfilment pre-check first), cart upsell, bundle offer.
   - Upsells to live RT System clients and past buyers (second stand, cards, Pro at R299/mo).
   - December Review Sprint / GuestOps: 10 demos live from wave 1, 30 more evaluated leads.
     Book calls, close setup fees.
   - Acreage renewal: the 90 days end ~9 Dec. A signed renewal with a payment by 3 Dec is NEW.
   - JCE warm pipeline: Innovatr (AEO baseline built, unsent), ETSC, Avaris, Glenpak,
     BeachHouse Port Alfred, MeTime, Vitamiene See, L&D Garage Doors, RE/MAX Coastal deck
     (unsent), BRG. A paid Blueprint (R30k) or AEO audit counts.
   - Die Veiling: Gold tier / add-ons / build payment 2 timing.
   - MFB Black Friday ONLY on in-stock dolls (pre-orders are OFF until after Christmas; read
     inventoryPolicy before promoting anything).
   - Upwork restart if the scan shows a job closable inside 15 days.
4. Write Ops/R50K-SPRINT/DECISIONS.md with the Day 0 decision batch (section 5) and stop the
   Day 0 run there. Do not execute plays until I have answered it.

## 3. The loop (max 10 loops; each loop = 1 to 2 days)
Each loop, in order:
  a. RE-READ TRACKER.md and LOOP-LOG.md. Re-verify cash landed since last loop from live
     sources (bank exports, Shopify, Payfast, Gmail receipts).
  b. PICK the top 3 plays by score. Never more than 3 in flight.
  c. PREPARE everything they need: copy, offer page, invoice, call sheet, list, ad. Work in
     the play's existing folder. Reuse existing kits, decks and templates; never rebuild.
  d. EXECUTE what my send authority (section 4) already covers. QUEUE the rest in the next
     approval batch.
  e. MEASURE against the play's stop rule. Each play gets one when it starts: a spend cap,
     a reply/click threshold and a kill date. Hit the kill rule, kill the play, log why.
  f. LOG to Ops/R50K-SPRINT/LOOP-LOG.md: what ran, what landed (with source), what died,
     what is next. Then update TRACKER.md totals.
  g. GATE before the next loop: if NEW collected is behind the straight line to R50k
     (R3,333/day), the next loop must include a play with cash inside 72 hours.
Schedule the next wake-up yourself (scheduled task or the 06:35 morning-dispatch). If a session
ends mid-loop, the state files are the handover.

## 4. Send authority (pre-cleared, only inside these lines)
I am granting this now. Anything outside it goes in the approval batch.
- Payment reminders on invoices already issued, to the billing contact on file, in my voice,
  max 1 per invoice per 3 days. (Not to Acreage: that relationship is mine; draft only.)
- Black Friday emails to my own opted-in customer lists through GHL/Shopify, from approved
  copy only, after the fulfilment pre-check and the Shopify dedupe rule.
- Follow-ups to prospects who have already replied to me, within 2 touches, using approved
  templates.
- Paused ads only. Creating paused ads is fine; activating, budget changes and targeting
  changes need my yes.
Never, at any hour: new pricing, discounts deeper than I approve, contracts, cold sends to new
recipients, spend money, change live ad delivery, change account settings, touch Accounting
records beyond reading them, publish anything public, mention "GHL" to RT System clients.

## 5. Approval batch (how you ask me)
- ONE message per day, ready by 07:30 SAST, in DECISIONS.md and summarised to me.
- Numbered. Each item: what, to whom, the exact text or a link to it, the rand at stake, your
  recommendation. I reply "yes 1,3,5 / no 2 / change 4: ...".
- Decision 0 (first batch only): does RECOVERED money count toward the R50k? Default: no.
- Max 8 items. If you have more, you have too many plays.
- Respect my 08:00 to 10:00 ask block: calls, asks and closes only; no building during it.

## 6. Hard rules
- Board cap: no more than 15 cards. The sprint lives in its own tracker, not 30 new cards.
- Every status change triggers the decision ripple (CLAUDE.md, memory, WAITING-ON.md).
- USD at fetched spot, never assumed.
- No em dashes in anything client-facing or written to me.
- Client docs never expose internal mechanics (margins, Giorgio's cut, tool names).
- If a play needs my real-world action (a call, a site visit, a login), put it in the batch with
  a 2-minute brief so I can do it between other things.

## 7. Documentation (I am telling this story publicly)
Keep Ops/R50K-SPRINT/STORY.md as you go: one short entry per loop, written for a reader, not for
an agent. What we tried, why, what the numbers said, what we killed, what surprised us. Keep
client names and amounts out of it unless I clear them; use "a guesthouse client" and
percentages. Screenshots of dashboards go in Ops/R50K-SPRINT/assets/. On 3 Dec, write
FINAL.md: total NEW, total RECOVERED, cost, my minutes spent, the plays ranked by rand per my
minute, and what I should keep running after the sprint.

## 8. Done
The sprint ends on 3 Dec 2026 or at loop 10, whichever comes first. Report the honest number,
even if it is short of R50k, with the gap explained.
