What it costs to win an order: Meta's recent cost per purchase is R487 (28 days to 8 Nov) and R543 last week. The six-month blended paid CAC is R382.

The three facts the offer is built on:

  1. Discounting a single stand loses money. A R649 stand earns ~R455, and the ads cost ~R490 to get it. Any rand off a single unit is a rand of loss. The promo must raise the order value instead.
  2. Hardware is nearly free to make. An extra card costs R33 and sells at R449. Adding product costs almost nothing, while cutting the price costs real money. Give more, don't charge less.
  3. The cheapest sale is to someone who already bought. There are 840 past buyers and only 11.3% have ever bought again. Nobody has asked them, because CRM email was broken from 19 Aug to 7 Nov. They cost R0 to reach, so the past-buyer offer is where most of the profit sits.

The R1,000 free-delivery rule stays exactly as it is (Jacques, 3 Jul: no shipping changes). Every BF offer is built to land over R1,000, so free delivery comes from the existing rule and not from a new one.

5+ stands (groups)

No new offer. The existing 5+ tier (R549 each) stays, with "ask us for group pricing" pointed at the Bulk Orders page. Groups go to Jacques or Giorgio as a quote, not a checkout.

Honest-pricing rule (Consumer Protection Act)

The "was" price in every ad, email and page is today's real selling price (R1,487 / R2,036 / R1,676). It is never the inflated compare-at (R869 / R599 per item). The compare-at prices were set 30-35% above price on 21 Oct, so quoting them as "was" would overstate the saving.

Hard end

The discounts are scheduled to end in Shopify at 30 Nov 23:59, so the deadline is real. After that, prices go back to normal and no extension is offered.

Delivery promise

Custom orders take 5-7 business days. Ordered by 30 Nov means delivered by about 10 Dec, before the December holiday rush. That is the urgency line for hospitality, and it ties in with the December Review Sprint Giorgio is already working.
