Signs it's a spring or a cable

A loud bang from the garage

Torsion springs break with a bang. You'll often see a gap in the spring above the door.

The door is suddenly heavy

The springs do the lifting. Without them the door is too heavy to lift by hand.

A cable is hanging loose

It has come off its drum, and the door can twist in the tracks.

The door hangs crooked

One side is pulling harder than the other. We rebalance it.

Squealing or jerky movement

Worn pulleys and rollers. New ones make the door run smoothly again.

Common questions

How do I know if the spring is broken?

A loud bang, a gap in the spring above the door, or a door that suddenly feels very heavy.

Can I still open it with the motor?

Please don't. Without spring support the motor can strip its gears or pull the door off the track.

Do you replace both springs?

On doors with side springs, yes, we replace them as a pair so the door stays balanced.

How soon can you come?

We're open 24 hours. Phone 061 663 1330 and we'll tell you when we can be there.
