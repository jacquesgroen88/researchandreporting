Until we get there

Keep people, pets and cars away from the door, especially if it's crooked or a spring has snapped. Don't try to force it up or down. If it's safe, take a photo or a short video and WhatsApp it to us so we bring the right parts.

Nights and weekends

Night and weekend call-outs are part of the job for us. Ask about the call-out cost when you phone and we'll tell you before we leave.

When parts are needed

The first thing we do is get the door closed and locked. If the full repair needs parts we don't have with us, we leave it secure and come back to finish.
