What we fix in Westdene

It won't open or close

Usually a broken spring, a cable that has come off, or the motor. We check which one before we quote.

It's off its track

Don't force it. A door off its track can drop. We rehang it and straighten or replace the tracks.

A spring or cable snapped

Heard a bang and the door is suddenly very heavy? That's normally the spring.

It's noisy or jerky

Worn rollers, dry hinges or loose bolts. A service often sorts it out.

A panel is dented

On a lot of doors we can replace the damaged panel instead of the whole door.

The motor or remote stopped

We test the remote, receiver and motor before we replace anything.
