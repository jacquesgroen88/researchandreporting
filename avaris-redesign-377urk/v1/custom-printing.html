What we need from you

What to send us for a quote

The more of this you have, the faster we can price it. Missing something? Send what you have and we will fill the gaps together.

Send your spec
  1. Bag type: courier, shopping, feed, ice, bakery, refuse or something else.
  2. Size: width x length in mm.
  3. Thickness: in micron. Not sure? Tell us what goes in the bag.
  4. Print colours: plain, 1 colour, 2 colours or 3+.
  5. Quantity: roughly how many you need.
  6. Artwork: your logo or design file.
  7. Delivery town: where the bags must go.
How it works

How a printed order works

  1. Step 1

    Tell Us What You Need

    Send the checklist above by form, phone or email. If you are starting from scratch, a photo of a bag you like is a good start.

  2. Step 2

    We Create Your Solution

    We match the bag, size and thickness to the job and come back to you with a price.

  3. Step 3

    Fast Production & Delivery

    Once you approve the price and the artwork, we make your bags and deliver them.
