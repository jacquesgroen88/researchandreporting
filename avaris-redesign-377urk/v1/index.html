Custom & branded bags

Branded bags, printed to your specification.

Your logo on a bag that is the right size and thickness for what goes in it. Made by the manufacturer, not passed on by a middleman.

What to send us for a quote

  1. Bag type: courier, shopping, feed, ice, bakery, refuse or something else.
  2. Size: width x length in mm.
  3. Thickness: in micron. Not sure? Tell us what goes in the bag.
  4. Print colours: plain, 1 colour, 2 colours or 3+.
  5. Quantity: roughly how many you need.
  6. Artwork: your logo or design file.
