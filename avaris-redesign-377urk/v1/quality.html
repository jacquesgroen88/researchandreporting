The standard

What ISO 22000 means for your bags

ISO 22000 is an international standard for food safety management. It sets out how a business should identify and control food safety risks across its work.

For packaging, that matters most where the bag touches food: ice, bread, deli goods and anything else people eat.

Avaris holds a Certificate of Compliance to ISO 22000:2018, issued by ISO Logic.
