Who we are

About Avaris (PTY) LTD

A proudly South African packaging manufacturer in Germiston.

Our story

Avaris Group (Pty) Ltd began in 2016 with humble beginnings and a strong commitment to customer excellence.

Welcome to Avaris, a proudly South African company bringing innovation, reliability and a fresh perspective to the packaging industry.

We manufacture flexible packaging. We are not a reseller. When you order from us, the people who quote your bag are the people who make it.

Our vision

At Avaris, our vision is to become a leading manufacturer of flexible packaging by consistently exceeding customer expectations, upholding ethical standards and contributing to the sustainable development of our employees, communities and stakeholders.

What we invest in

Our commitment to excellence is reflected in our investment in customer service, advanced equipment, automated systems, continuous innovation and, above all, our people, the heart of our business.
