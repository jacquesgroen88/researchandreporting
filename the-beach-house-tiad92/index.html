Welcome

A quiet house on the dunes.

The Beach House sits on West Beach Drive with nothing between you and the Indian Ocean but a strip of dune. Watch the boats come past from the deck, swim in the pool, or walk straight onto miles of beach that, as one guest put it, you'll often have all to yourselves.

Halfway between Gqeberha and East London, it's an easy stop on a Sunshine Coast road trip, and a proper reason to stay a week.

Good to know

Before you book.

Anything else, WhatsApp Isabeau on 082 662 5720. We reply fast.

Is breakfast included?+

Yes. Every room stay is bed and full English breakfast, served daily in the dining room that opens onto the pool deck. The 180° Ocean View flat is self-catering, with a full kitchen.

Are rates per person or per room?+

Per room, per night, for two adults. Rooms start from R2,390 a night and sea-view rooms with a bath from R2,890. Rates change with the season, so check your dates for live prices.

Can we bring children?+

The Beach House is a quiet retreat for guests aged 12 and over. Travelling with younger children? Ask us about the self-catering flat or taking the whole house.

King or twin beds?+

Three rooms have a queen bed and three can be made up as a king or two singles. Mention king or twin in the special requests when you book.

Is there parking and Wi-Fi?+

Yes to both. Ample safe and secure off-street parking, and free wireless internet throughout.

How do I book?+

Pick your dates above to see live availability and book instantly, or send us a WhatsApp or email and we'll come back to you as soon as possible.
