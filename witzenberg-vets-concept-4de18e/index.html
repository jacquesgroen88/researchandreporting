Veterinary care for pets, horses and livestock in the Witzenberg.

Witzenberg Vets is a mixed animal practice with two branches: our clinic at The Corner Hub in Ceres, and our Tulbagh practice on Van Der Stel Street, which also runs mobile large animal services.

What we do

One practice for the whole valley: small animals at the clinic, and horses, cattle and other farm animals at the clinic or on site.

Consultations

General consultations and routine wellness check-ups for your animals.

Vaccinations and preventive care

Appointments

How to book

Choose your branch

Ceres at The Corner Hub, or Tulbagh on Van Der Stel Street. For a farm visit, choose Tulbagh.

Phone or send a request

Call the branch during hours, or use the form below and the team will phone you back.

Bring the history

Bring vaccination cards and a list of any medication your animal is on.

Appointment request

Concept form, not connected. On the live site this would go to the branch you choose. For anything urgent, phone instead.

Emergencies

If your animal is injured, struggling to breathe, bleeding, or you think it has eaten poison, phone the branch before you set off so the team can be ready for you.
