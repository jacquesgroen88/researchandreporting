The shift

Your buyers have stopped searching. They ask.

A growing share of people choosing car insurance no longer scroll through ten blue links. They type a full question into ChatGPT, Claude or Google and get back one answer, often with a shortlist of three to five names. The brand at the top of that shortlist gets the click. The others mostly get ignored.

That answer isn't bought, and it isn't built from your website alone. The engine assembles it from what it trusts: press coverage, review platforms, comparison sites, rankings, and whatever it can read about you. As of October 2026, OpenAI has also opened sponsored placements inside ChatGPT in South Africa, so the organic answer and the paid one now sit side by side.

Nobody at MiWay can see this today. It doesn't show up in Google Analytics, search rankings or your media reports. The audit makes it visible, measures it and shows you what moves it.

What we already observed

MiWay is in the answer. OUTsurance is the answer.

The engines have put the two brands in different boxes. OUTsurance owns "best", "best reputation" and "good at paying claims", and backs it with things the engines can quote: the News24 award, the DataEQ index and its ombud complaint record. MiWay owns "cheaper". Asked which insurers are good at paying claims, Claude named OUTsurance first in 3 of 3 runs and never named MiWay. All three ChatGPT runs of "Is MiWay a good insurance company?" warned of "delays, rejected claims and problems coordinating repairs".

There's real upside too. Asked about MiWay directly, Claude calls it "one of South Africa's better-regarded short-term insurers". MiWay comes up on every engine for young drivers and for price. And when a customer asks what to do about an 18% premium increase, no engine recommends any insurer. That question is open ground. The audit measures all of this properly, across four engines and both product lines, and turns it into a plan.

What we haven't proven

Four things we won't promise you

We could leave these out and you might never notice. We'd rather you hear them from us before you sign.

  • We can't tie AI answers to sales precisely. When someone reads an AI answer and later visits your site, analytics mostly records that as "direct" traffic. No one can measure this cleanly yet. What we can measure, honestly and repeatably, is your share of the answers and where you rank in them.
  • We can't buy you a place in the answer. Organic AI answers can't be paid for. We improve the evidence the engines use. The engines decide.
  • The answers move. Engines update weekly. The audit is a dated snapshot, and its value is as a baseline you can re-measure against.
  • Some fixes take quarters, not weeks. Industry awards, independent rankings and press coverage have to be earned. We'll tell you which fixes are which so nobody sells you a slow one as a quick one.
