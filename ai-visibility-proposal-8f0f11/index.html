01The shift

Your customers stopped searching. They ask.

People choosing car insurance no longer scroll through ten links. They type a whole question into ChatGPT, Claude or Google and get one answer back, usually a shortlist of three to five names. The first name gets the call. The rest mostly don't.

That answer can't be bought, and it isn't built from your website. The engine assembles it from what it trusts: press, review platforms, rankings and comparison sites. Since October 2026 ChatGPT also sells sponsored placements in South Africa, so the paid answer and the earned one now sit side by side. None of it shows up in your analytics. Nobody at MiWay can see it today.

09What we haven't proven

Four things we won't promise.

We could leave these out and you might never notice. We'd rather you hear them from us before you sign.

One

We can't tie AI answers to sales precisely.

When someone reads an AI answer and later visits you, analytics mostly logs it as "direct". Nobody can measure this cleanly yet. What we can measure, honestly and repeatably, is your share of the answers.

Two

We can't buy you a place in the answer.

Organic AI answers aren't for sale. We improve the evidence the engines use. The engines decide.

Three

The answers move.

Engines update weekly. The audit is a dated snapshot, and its value is as a baseline you can measure against.

Four

Some fixes take quarters.

Awards, rankings and press have to be earned. We'll mark which fixes are which, so nobody sells you a slow one as a quick one.
