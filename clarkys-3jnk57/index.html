Why Dullstroom

The Highlands, two hours from Johannesburg.

Rolling mist, cold clear trout dams and fireplaces lit by four o'clock. Fish in the morning, play a round on a championship course, ride the mountain bike trails, or spot the cranes. Then come back to a hot tub under the stars.
