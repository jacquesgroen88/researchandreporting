Lodge, pub & grill · Alldays

Bushveld days, poolside evenings.

Clarky's Pride, known to locals as the "Ou At se Gat", is a bushveld lodge, pub and grill in Alldays. Air-conditioned rooms, a pool for the afternoon heat, and a fire and a cold one when the sun goes down.
