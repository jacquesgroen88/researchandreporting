The Business

ReviewTap sells NFC and QR-enabled hardware (stands + cards) that help SA businesses collect Google reviews on the spot. Customers tap the stand, land on Google, and leave a review in under 30 seconds. A separate monthly SaaS subscription layer — Rep Suite — manages review monitoring and automated response for multi-location businesses. A conversational AI agent handles inbound sales questions and closes purchases directly through WhatsApp, and a separate bot manages after-sales support — both running without human intervention. The business has been live since February 2025, has processed 764 orders across a B2B SME customer base, and carries R15,548/month in recurring SaaS revenue transferable to a buyer. ReviewTap Pro — a self-serve SaaS tier at R399/mo — is in active pre-launch and will increase ARR materially before close.

The Dormancy Explained

Ads were paused Jan–Apr 2026 while the owner directed all capital and attention to a concurrent eCommerce brand during its peak season. The instant May revival (R80k from zero) confirms the demand-generation machine is intact — this was a resourcing pause, not organic decline. A buyer who leaves the ads running faces no equivalent gap.

Refund record: 1 refund across 764 orders. Remaining "returns" were customers voluntarily upgrading from standard to custom units and covering return postage themselves — exchanges, not refunds.

What's Next For The Brand

WhatsApp AI Review Engine

A WhatsApp AI flow that automatically requests a review after every purchase, then filters by sentiment: happy customers are sent straight to Google, while anyone unhappy is intercepted into a private feedback space before it ever reaches the public rating. It lifts the public star count and captures every review opportunity, fully automated.

ReviewTap Pro (Recurring SaaS)

ReviewTap Pro, a self-serve subscription tier at R399/mo, is built and ready to launch. Every asset is already in place: landing pages, onboarding hub, full launch kit, and the GHL + PayFast + Make automation. It turns one-time hardware buyers into recurring MRR, the single biggest lever on valuation.

Proof: Guesthouse, Strand

A guesthouse client bought hardware, grew to 1,200+ Google reviews, and converted to a R1,899/mo subscriber. One customer chain that shows exactly how these steps compound: hardware in, reviews up, recurring revenue out.
