Why this decides your bookings

Reviews decide whether a guest finds you, picks you, and books you direct.

01

Found

Google ranks local results on relevance, distance and popularity. In its own words: "More reviews and positive ratings can help your business's local ranking." You can't move your distance. Reviews are the part you can grow every week.

02

Picked

68% of people want at least a 4-star rating, and 74% look for reviews written in the last 3 months. Old reviews stop counting, so the asking can't stop.

03

Booked direct

A guest who doesn't find you on Google, or isn't convinced, often books you through a booking site instead. You pay commission on a guest who was already looking for you.

Sources: Google Business Profile Help, "Tips to improve your local ranking on Google". BrightLocal Local Consumer Review Survey 2026 (international survey).

How it works

From checkout to a Google review, on its own.

After checkout

A thank-you

The guest gets a WhatsApp thanking them for their stay and asking how it was.

Thanks for staying at Rockview! How was your stay? Tap a star 🙂
Happy guest

Straight to Google

One tap opens your Google review page. The review goes up the same day.

Unhappy guest

You hear first

A guest who rates 3 or lower is asked privately what went wrong, so you can put it right. They still get the Google link, because Google requires it.

Every review

Answered

A reply goes up under every review within a day, so the next guest reading it sees an owner who listens.

Every guest gets the Google link. Asking only happy guests breaks Google's rules and can get reviews removed.

Sell the unhappy-guest step as "you hear first", never as "unhappy guests can't post." If they want proof, offer to WhatsApp them the guest's-eye view from a test run, not TO Strand screenshots.
