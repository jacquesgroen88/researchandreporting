What this means for MiWay

🟢 inside MiWay's control · 🟡 can be influenced · 🔴 has to be earned. These are inputs. The engines decide the outcome.

1

🟢 Publish dated, crawlable claims proof

The engines already quote MiWay's 5% overturn ratio, but they set it beside OUTsurance's 3% and its fewest-complaints-per-1,000-claims record. MiWay needs a plain-text, dated claims page the engines can quote: turnaround times, payout volumes and repair-coordination performance. It has to answer the "delays and rejected claims" caveat directly.

2

🟢 Own the "my premium went up" question

Not one engine recommends an insurer for this question. The answers come from the ombud, broker blogs and a generic "get three quotes" list. A loss-of-trust moment that no brand answers is the cheapest open ground in the category.

3

🟡 Get into the sources the engines read

Trade press (BusinessTech, Moonstone), Hippo, Hellopeter and the roundup guides decide the shortlists. OUTsurance's sponsored News24 content is cited as evidence, and Pineapple and Naked get cited for their own guides. MiWay needs a definitive buyer guide, placed coverage of its claims numbers, and a Hellopeter response programme.

4

🔴 Earn third-party proof

OUTsurance's lead rests on the News24 award, the DataEQ index and its ombud complaint record. These can't be bought. They have to be earned, and that takes quarters.

OUTsurance's exposure: it is missing from the young-driver question on all three engines (0 of 7 runs) and from the older-car question on Claude (0/3). It owns trust but cedes price.

The gap, in one line

MiWay is named in 60% of ChatGPT and Claude answers but recommended first in 4 of 70 runs. OUTsurance is recommended first in 21 of 70. MiWay wins the head-to-head in none.

Method and limits

Questions: 14 full-sentence buyer questions across the eight archetypes, frozen in prompts.md. Share of answer uses the 10 questions that don't name a brand. ChatGPT: logged out with web search, 3 fresh sessions per question, 42 runs. Claude: claude.ai incognito chats (no memory or history), Opus 5.5 with web search, 3 fresh chats per question, 42 runs. Google AI Overviews: 1 run per question on google.com set to South Africa, from a signed-in Chrome profile, so some personalisation is possible. Perplexity: stopped at its logged-out limit after 5 searches, 3 valid runs kept. The limit was not worked around. Recording: an insurer counts as named when the answer text mentions it. Source chips (Hippo, Hellopeter, Reddit) are counted as sources, not recommendations. AI engines are non-deterministic, so read every figure as a frequency, never a verdict. This report does not estimate traffic, leads or revenue. AI-referral attribution is not reliably measurable today.
