Year one, all in: roughly R124,000. That is the R64,000 build plus about ten months of the licence. Year two runs at R72,000 with nothing new to build. No VAT is added to any figure on this page.

Included in the investment

  • A strategy session with you to settle the packages, the prices and the founding intake, using your real cost per drink
  • A working session with each of your four bar managers, at their bar
  • An inspection of the till setup at each bar, and a plan for any adaptations the weekly export needs per site
  • The baseline: how each bar trades today, measured before a single membership is sold, because it can never be taken afterwards
  • QR sign-up from the table: choose a package, four fields, pay by card or instant EFT, membership card on the phone in about 90 seconds
  • Digital membership card with a code that changes every 30 seconds, so it cannot be shared or screenshotted
  • The allowance engine: what each package gets, when it resets, what carries over, all changeable by you without a developer
  • Bar screen for your staff: scan or type the code, green or red, capture the till slip, pour
  • Free welcome drink on Silver and Gold, poured automatically on the first visit
  • Family package with each child's treat, released on one scan
  • Your dashboard across all four bars: member search, leaderboard, retention and usage, the no-spend report
  • Role-based access: each manager runs his own bar and sees only his own numbers, while you see all four
  • Weekly sales upload from your manager's normal CSV export, matched on slip number, so you see what members actually spend
  • Monthly draws, with every visit an entry
  • POPIA-compliant consent and data handling from day one

Optional add-on: marketing and launch messaging

The club works without this. It fills faster with it. We set up the launch campaign and five automatic member campaigns: the Tuesday special, the monthly draw announcement, the day-14 win-back, birthdays, and renewals. SMS from day one, WhatsApp once Meta approves the business account.

What is not included

  • Payfast transaction fees on membership payments. Those come off the membership income.
  • Printing of the table talkers and posters. We design them, you print them.
  • Changes to your till systems. There are none, deliberately. The only contact is the weekly export your managers already run.

The protection on your side

The second payment only falls due when you are live. Half now, and the other half on the day members can actually join at all four bars, not before.

A stop button at day 90. Ninety days after go-live, if the numbers do not support carrying on, cancel the licence on thirty days' notice. No penalty. We hand over every member, every membership and the full history in a format anyone can use.

Founding intake is capped at roughly 150 members a bar until the day-45 review, which caps what you owe in beer while the model proves itself.

To start

Say yes and we invoice the first R32,000. We book the strategy session and the bar manager visits for the first week, and we need your real cost per drink including wastage and your call on whether Bronze and the Family package are paid. The build runs from there to opening night.
