Die Veiling Klub · how members pay

Which payment route keeps the most money in the till.

The membership fee repeats every month, so the fee you pay to collect it repeats too. On a recurring charge the difference between a card swipe and a debit order is not small, it is most of the cost. Here is the comparison, modelled on the real Klub prices.

01 · The one number that decides it

3% of a card charge vs a few rand flat.

R45 per member vs R4 per member, every month
A card fee of about 3% on a R1,499 Silver membership is roughly R45 a month. The same charge collected by debit order is a flat fee of about R4. Across 150 members at one bar that is R6,750 a month in card fees versus about R600 by debit order.

So the rule is simple: card is the easy way to sign someone up, debit order is the cheap way to keep charging them. The best setup uses both, which the processors below all support.

03 · The recommendation

Card to join, debit order to stay.

At sign-up → card

A new member taps their card once and is in. Pay the ~3% on that first charge, it buys an instant, zero-friction join at the bar. Payfast or Peach both do this cleanly, and it is what the sign-up screen is already built for.

Every month after → debit order

Move the recurring charge to a DebiCheck debit order (Peach, Netcash or Stitch). The fee drops from about R45 to about R4 a member, and the money arrives on the same day each month without the member doing anything.

For the yearly option, card is fine. An annual membership is a single charge, so the ~3% is paid once, not twelve times. There the convenience of card outweighs the saving of a debit order.
What this needs from Harry. Debit orders run off a DebiCheck mandate, which the member approves once at sign-up (a quick confirmation on their banking app). It also needs the merchant account in Die Veiling's name, the same account the card sign-ups pay into. Both are set up once, per the go-live checklist.
