Die Veiling Klub · how members pay

Payment options.

The membership fee repeats monthly, so the fee to collect it repeats too. Card is the easy way to sign someone up; a debit order is the cheap way to keep charging them.

Key detail
Card ~R45 a member · debit order ~R4 a member
That is the monthly collection fee on a typical membership: about 3% on a card versus a flat few rand on a debit order. Over a full bar of members it is thousands a month versus hundreds.

The plan

Card first, debit order later.

Phase 1 · launch

Card

Members join and pay by card from day one, on the same merchant account as the sign-up. No extra setup, so this is what we launch on.

Phase 2 · later

Debit order

Switch the monthly charge to a debit order once there is volume, to cut the fee. Setup is more involved (a provider account, FICA, a mandate per member), so it is a phase-two move, not a launch blocker. The call to make here is which processor is the best long-term fit.

Yearly memberships stay on card either way: one charge a year, so the ~3% is paid once, not twelve times.
