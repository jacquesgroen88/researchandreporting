02 · Monthly and yearly

Two ways to pay, with the best deal on yearly.

Offer both. Monthly is the easy yes; yearly locks the member in for twelve months and brings the cash in upfront, so it carries the better deal. The standard move is pay for ten months, get twelve, which is two months free. You can make the yearly saving bigger or smaller, that dial is yours.

Example. If Ram is R500 a month, the yearly price at "two months free" is R5,000 for the year (R6,000 of monthly value), and it reads on the sign-up as "save R1,000." Members on yearly almost never cancel, and you hold their fee from day one.
03 · Setting the price

How to work out what to charge.

The price is yours to set. Here is the simple way to land on a number you can defend, for any tier. Every price sits above what the free drinks cost you and at or below what they are worth to the member. Where you put it between those two is your margin.

  1. Count the free drinks in a month. Ram gives 2 a week, so about 9 a month. Bul gives 1 a day, about 30 a month.
  2. Put two numbers on each drink. What it costs you (a draught is roughly R13 from the keg) and what a customer pays for it (about R42). Those two numbers are your floor and your ceiling.
  3. Pick your price in between, then add for the perks. The hat, the VIP upgrades and the draws add value for the member but cost you little, so they let you price nearer the ceiling.

One more lens, if it helps. You pay R1,500 per bar a month for the platform. Price Ram so that roughly one member a bar covers that fee, and everything above it is yours. That is the simplest way to know the programme pays for itself from the very first member.
Remember the once-off costs. The supporter hat costs you a few rand each (roughly R50–R80). Treat it as a sign-up cost and either absorb it or build it into the first month, your choice.
