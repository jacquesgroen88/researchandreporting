Under the bonnet

What the system keeps
about each member

Who he is

Name, cellphone, birthday, home bar, and how he found you. Plus a record of what he agreed to be contacted about, and when he agreed to it.

What he bought

Which package, what he paid, when it started and when it runs out. We store a payment reference and never a card number.

What he has taken

Every drink, with the bar, the barman, the time, the slip number and what that drink cost you on the day.

That last one matters more than it looks. Keg prices move. If we did not store the cost at the moment of the pour, a price increase in March would quietly rewrite what January looked like.

Keeping it honest

Why the code moves

A fixed code would not survive a month

If every member had one permanent barcode, somebody screenshots it and puts it in a WhatsApp group. Within a fortnight you are pouring free beer for people who never paid.

So it changes every 30 seconds

The code on his screen is only good for half a minute and can only be used once. A screenshot sent to a friend is expired by the time it arrives.

  • Your barman does the scanning. The member holds up his phone and never hands it over, and never taps anything himself.
  • The system knows who poured it. Every drink carries the barman's name, which is what makes the weekly check on the last panel possible.

Behind the bar

The five seconds that pay for the whole thing

One extra field on that green screen: the till slip number. It is the only thing your staff do differently, and it is what turns a record of what the club costs into a record of what it earns.

What it costs them

Four digits off the slip already in their hand. The keypad is already open when the green screen appears.

What it gives you

Every free drink joined to what that table actually spent. Without it you know your cost and are guessing at your revenue.

If a bar stops doing it

The dashboard shows the capture rate per bar per week, so you see it slipping at Meyerton before it becomes a habit.

If one bar simply will not keep it up, we move that bar to sampling on two shifts a week instead of losing the number altogether.

Built in from the start

Two things that are cheaper
to build now than to fix later

The licence

Your barmen can refuse a redemption and record the reason, there are hard ceilings on how much anyone can take in a day regardless of what they paid, and no drink offer can ever attach itself to a child's record.

Sasolburg falls under the Free State and the other three are Gauteng, so the rules can be set differently per bar if your attorney comes back with different answers.

Personal information

Consent for WhatsApp is captured at sign-up with the date attached. For children we store a first name and a birthday, nothing else, no photographs, and never any marketing aimed at them.

If a member asks for his data or asks to be deleted, that is a button rather than a project.
