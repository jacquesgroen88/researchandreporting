A paid membership programme for the Die Veiling bars: members carry a digital card, staff pour against it in seconds, and every benefit and every rand is tracked. This page shows what is already running, what comes next, and why it matters.

01 · The idea in one minute

Regulars become members. Members become measurable.

Members pay a monthly fee and get a weekly drink allowance plus perks at their local. The business gets predictable monthly revenue before anyone orders a round, more frequent visits, and, because every pour is linked to the till slip, proof of what members spend over and above their benefits. That last part is the engine: the system does not just reward loyalty, it measures whether the programme pays for itself.

03 · Built and verified

What is done under the hood.

  • Membership database and rules engine, running on production infrastructure Every pour is a permanent ledger entry. Balances are calculated, never guessed.
  • Rotating member codes with one-time use A code works once. Reuse, expiry and fakes are all refused with a clear message to staff.
  • Weekly allowances that reset every Monday, plus the welcome drink on first visit
  • Pour, slip capture and manager reversal, tested end to end at the bar screen
  • Role-based access The owner sees all four bars. A manager manages their own bar. Bar staff can only pour.
  • Till-slip linking for spend measurement The slip number typed at pour time joins to the POS sales export, so member spend beyond the allowance is measurable per bar.
  • Public sign-up that issues a card on the spot Pick a membership, enter details, and the digital card is live immediately. The payment step is ready to connect to the merchant account.
  • Owner and manager dashboard Members and fees per bar, points leaderboard, and member search, scoped by role.
04 · Next up

The road to launch.

  1. Switch on card payment The sign-up already collects everything and issues the card. The last step is connecting the merchant account so the monthly fee is charged automatically. Waiting on the account approval.
  2. Sales data connection Weekly POS sales data per branch feeds the spend reports. Access to the POS systems is being arranged now.
  3. Baseline measurement A few weeks of normal trading recorded before memberships go on sale, so the programme's real effect can be proven, not guessed.
  4. Launch on the Klub's own address The same system, live at klub.dieveiling.co.za, with a capped founding-member intake at all four bars.
The member-facing and owner-facing software is built and running. What remains is mostly the two outside pieces: the payment merchant account approval and POS data access, both in progress.
05 · Why it pays

The value behind it.

Revenue before the first round

Membership fees arrive monthly whether it rains or not. It is the most predictable rand a bar can earn.

More visits, proven

A member with drinks waiting at their local has a reason to walk in. Every visit is logged, so frequency is a number, not a feeling.

The spend around the benefit

The free draught is the invitation. The food, the second round and the friends who came along are the business case, and the slip link measures exactly that.
