  • We stretched her ad budget. R5,500 of free Google credit is being layered on top of her spend, so her R5k/month is buying closer to R10k of live advertising.
The conversion gap (the real issue)
  • ~140 clients quoted, only 6 booked. The leak is entirely at quote-to-booking, not at the top of the funnel.
  • 30 quotes are sitting cold in follow-up, most untouched for two weeks, and 37 have already been lost at that exact stage.
  • Follow-up is the lever, not more leads. Propose the automated follow-up sequence and enrolling the 30 parked quotes this week. Every extra booking is roughly R3,000 to R4,000 to JCE.

  • Credits are half gone and expire mid-August. Once used, her monthly ad cost jumps from ~R5k to ~R10k unless spend is throttled. Flag it before she gets a surprise statement.
