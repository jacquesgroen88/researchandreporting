Worth saying first

Five refunds. In five years. Across 998 orders.

You ship live insects and frozen rodents across South Africa and almost nothing goes wrong. That is genuinely hard, and most of your competitors make a marketing feature of getting it right.

The opportunity in that

A 0.4% refund rate is the strongest trust signal in the business, and it currently appears nowhere a customer can see it. Neither does the 4.6 Google rating, the shop you have run since 2006, the reptile exhibition, or the petting zoo.

What sells

The store runs on two different engines

Consumables make the customer

Crickets, frozen rodents, worms, substrate. Crickets alone: R26,671 from 126 different people, 43% of whom bought again. The highest repeat rate of anything you sell.

Animals and hardware make the money

An order containing a live animal averages R1,213. A cage order averages R1,297. Against a store average of R731.

The connection nobody is making

Of 190 orders containing a live animal, only 50 included any food and only 31 included a cage. Someone buys a bearded dragon from Reptilia and then buys everything it needs somewhere else. Closing that gap is the clearest single opportunity in the data.

The one number to take away

1,105 people a quarter reach your checkout page and do not finish. That is about 4,400 a year. They found you, they found the product, they put it in the basket, and they walked at the last step. Only 4.4% of filled baskets become orders. A healthy store runs 30% to 40%.

The likeliest single cause: R160 delivery is not mentioned anywhere on the site until that exact screen. On a median basket of R532 it lands as a surprise.

Two patterns worth planning around

January to March is the season

Those three months carry 30% of all revenue off 25% of orders, so bigger baskets too. School holidays, first pets, full setups.

April and October are the troughs, both around 40% below January.

Everything needs to be live by 1 December. The season is decided before it starts.

Already done

Two of these are closed

Old product links recovered Live

590 redirects built and published, then independently re-tested against the live site. 65.6% of the revenue behind old links now lands on a working page, with zero misdirections. Customers arriving on an old bookmark or an old Google result get where they were going.

Logo weight cut 94% Live

The logo was being sent to every visitor as an 870 KB file and displayed at 97 pixels wide. Replaced with a 52 KB version at identical quality, checked on screen before it went live. Every page on the site is lighter for it.

Next in the queue, both quick: turning on compression across the server, worth roughly 600 KB less on every single page load, and switching product reviews on.
