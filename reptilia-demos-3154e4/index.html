1WhatsApp assistant

Every WhatsApp gets an answer in seconds, even at 9 at night

Most of your customers message before they buy. Right now, an evening message waits until someone in the shop picks up a phone the next day, and some of those buyers go to the next shop on Google. The assistant answers the common questions straight away and passes everything else to your staff.

  • Answers in seconds, day and night, from your own WhatsApp Business number
  • Handles the questions you get every day: how to order, delivery, collection, what's in stock
  • Takes animal reservations from the website's Reserve button and puts them in one list for the shop
  • Sends one polite follow-up when a customer goes quiet, and never more than one
What it doesn't do
  • It doesn't pretend to be a person. Anything it can't answer goes straight to your staff
  • It doesn't confirm a live animal on its own. Only the shop does that
  • It doesn't message anyone who hasn't messaged you first

It runs on the same system that already sends your Google review requests, so there's no new app for the shop to learn. Your staff see every chat and can step in at any point.

2Abandoned cart reminders

Someone fills a cart and leaves. Three reminders bring them back.

People start a checkout, get interrupted, and forget. Right now the store doesn't follow up, so those sales just disappear. This sequence sends a short reminder with their cart in it, and stops the moment they pay.

Starts when: a customer enters their email or number at checkout and hasn't paid an hour later.
Stops when: they pay, they unsubscribe, or after the third message. Live animals are never chased; they go through reservations instead.

The example cart uses prices from your store's recent orders. Every recovered order shows up by order number in your weekly update, so you can see exactly what it brought back.

3What it takes

Two to three weeks to switch on, once you say yes

From youYour opening hours, the answers to your ten most common questions, and who in the shop confirms reservations.
From usSet-up on your WhatsApp Business number, the messages written in your voice for you to approve, and a WhatsApp opt-in tick box at checkout.
The rulesWe only message people who contacted you or started a checkout, and every message has a way out. This keeps you on the right side of POPIA and WhatsApp's own rules.

The cart reminders come with the Starter package and up. The WhatsApp assistant comes with Growth, together with social media and Google Ads. They also work with the sales tracking we have just switched on, so every message can be tied to the order it brought in.
