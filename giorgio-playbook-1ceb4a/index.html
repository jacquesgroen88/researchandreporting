What we sell

Ask. Answer. Reply.

Ask

Every guest gets asked for a Google review the day they check out.

Answer

Every review gets an answer, so the owner never has to write one again.

Reply

WhatsApp and website chat answer guests' questions, Sundays included.

For you, not for prospects

The bonus

As a bonus, we add a review stand for their reception, and they get a discount on additional units (more stands or cards). I bring it up on the call, after the price. Never put it in a message or email.

The line for an owner: "We make sure every guest gets asked, every review gets answered and every WhatsApp gets a reply."

On the call

Two lines you can use

Scarcity"We're only taking two more guesthouses before mid-December."
Urgency"To be live and ready for December, we'd need to start by 13 November."

After 13 November, the urgency line becomes: "Let's get you set up so February doesn't stay quiet."

H · Hook

Open with what you found

Getting to the owner"Hi, it's Giorgio. Could I speak to the owner, or whoever looks after your Google reviews? It's about how the guesthouse shows up on Google Maps. Two minutes."
The opener"Hi [name], it's Giorgio from ReviewTap. I'll be quick. I was looking at guest houses in [town] on Google Maps and noticed something about [guesthouse]. Have you got two minutes?"
What you found (Rockview example)"When someone searches 'guest houses in Port Edward', you come up fourth. EIGHT PALMS comes up first with 103 reviews. Your rating's great at 4.6, but you've had 2 new Google reviews this year. Google says more reviews can help your ranking, so the place that gets reviewed more gets seen first."

Only use the numbers from the check. Never round them up, and never say reviews are the only thing that counts. Google ranks on relevance, distance and popularity.

O · Open question  ·  W · Walkthrough

Ask, listen, then book

Open question"What happens at the moment after a guest checks out? Does anyone ask them for a Google review?"

Let them answer. Most will say "sometimes". Then ask: "How many came in last month?" The gap between what they think happens and the real number does the selling for you. Somewhere in here, ask how many rooms they have.

Book it"We set that up for guesthouses so every guest gets asked. I'd like to show you your Google listing next to [competitor] on a 20-minute call with Jacques, who runs the setup. Is Tuesday at 10 or Wednesday at 2 better for you?"

When they push back

Six answers

"Send me some information."

"Happy to. The most useful thing is your own Google page, and I'll WhatsApp it now. It makes more sense with someone walking you through it, though. Shall I pencil in Thursday at 10? If it's not useful, you can cancel."

"How much is it?"

"Every place is different. We have guesthouses spending about R500 a month and others spending R8,000 a month. Where you'd sit depends on your rooms and what we find, and that's exactly what the 20 minutes is for." Then back to the two times.

"We're too busy, it's nearly December."

"That's exactly why. December is when you have the most guests to ask. To be live and ready for December, we'd need to start by 13 November."

"Most of our bookings come through Booking.com."

"How many would you say? And what does that commission cost you in a year?" Let them work out the number. Don't promise to fix it.

"We already ask for reviews."

"Great. Google shows [N] new ones this year. Is that about what you'd expect?"

"Not interested."

"Fair enough. Can I ask, is it that reviews aren't a priority, or just the timing?" If it's the timing, ask when to call back and put it in the CRM.

Can't get hold of them

Three calls, then write

  1. Call three times, at different times of day: morning, after lunch, late afternoon.
  2. After the third try, send email 1 (next slide) and the WhatsApp below, with the link to their page.
  3. Four days later, if they haven't replied, send email 2 as a reply in the same thread.
  4. No reply after that? Mark it in the CRM and move on. Don't chase a third time.
The WhatsApp"Hi [name], Giorgio from ReviewTap. I tried to call about how [guesthouse] shows up on Google Maps. Here's what I found: [their page link]. Worth a 20-minute chat?"

Keep us honest

Never say

"We'll get your price showing on Google."

We can't do that. If they ask: "That's set up through your booking system. We'll check yours as part of setup."

TO Strand's "8 reviews a week".

That's a big resort. A 5-room guesthouse gets maybe 5 to 9 a month.

"Reviews are what Google ranks on."

It's one of three things. Say "can help your ranking".

Anything from your internal list, or the bonus in writing.

Scores and offer groups stay with us. Prospects only ever see their own page.
