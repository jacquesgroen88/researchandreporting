What we sell

Ask, answer, reply

Ask

Every guest gets asked for a Google review the day they check out, plus a stand at reception.

Answer

Every review gets an answer, so the owner never has to write one again.

Reply

WhatsApp and website chat answer guests' questions, Sundays included.

That's the line for an owner: "We make sure every guest gets asked, every review gets answered and every WhatsApp gets a reply." The price, the guarantee and the extras are mine to present on the walkthrough, so you don't need to learn them.

The Google check

Why you check before you call

The sheet guesses who the competitor is. Google Maps shows who's actually beating them.

What the sheet said about Rockview

Competitor: The Edwardian, with 349 reviews.

What Google actually shows

Search "guest houses in Port Edward": Rockview comes 4th. EIGHT PALMS is 1st, with 103 reviews. Rockview has had only 2 new Google reviews this year.

If you open with the wrong competitor, the owner can prove you wrong in one search. Always use what the check found.

The Google check

Let your Claude do it

  1. Open the check tool.
  2. In the first box, paste tomorrow's list, one per line: Name, Town.
  3. Click Copy instructions for Claude and paste them into Claude in Chrome.
  4. Claude searches Google Maps for each place, saves the results and makes each guesthouse's ReviewTap page. At the end it gives you a table with the line to open each call with, plus every page link.
  5. Once a day, click Export for Jacques and send me the file, so I can see what you checked.

First time: do one guesthouse and check it against Google yourself. If it matches, give it the whole list from then on. If Google shows "I'm not a robot", stop and do that one by hand. The check tool has the links.

How to book the walkthrough
SHOWS

What we want from every call is a walkthrough the owner actually shows up to. Don't call to sell. Call to tell them something about their own Google listing. An owner will give you two minutes for that.

After every call, ask yourself which letter went wrong. That's the one to work on.

SSearch
The day before, run the Google check. Before you dial, have three numbers ready: where they come up, who comes up first, and how many Google reviews they've had this year.
HHook
Open with what you found on their listing.
OOpen question
"What happens after a guest checks out?" Then stop talking.
WWalkthrough
Book it with two specific times, never "when suits you?"
SSend their page
WhatsApp the confirmation with their Google page, then a reminder on the morning of the call.
H · Hook

Open with what you found

Getting to the owner"Hi, it's Giorgio. Could I speak to the owner, or whoever looks after your Google reviews? It's about how the guesthouse shows up on Google Maps. Two minutes."
The opener"Hi [name], it's Giorgio from ReviewTap. I'll be quick. I was looking at guest houses in [town] on Google Maps and noticed something about [guesthouse]. Have you got two minutes?"
What you found (Rockview example)"When someone searches 'guest houses in Port Edward', you come up fourth. EIGHT PALMS comes up first with 103 reviews. Your rating's great at 4.6, but you've had 2 new Google reviews this year. Google says more reviews can help your ranking, so the place that gets reviewed more gets seen first."

Only use the numbers from the check. Never round them up, and never say reviews are the only thing that counts. Google ranks on relevance, distance and popularity.

O · Open question  ·  W · Walkthrough

Ask, listen, then book

Open question"What happens at the moment after a guest checks out? Does anyone ask them for a Google review?"

Let them answer. Most will say "sometimes". Then ask: "How many came in last month?" The gap between what they think happens and the real number does the selling for you. Somewhere in here, ask how many rooms they have.

Book it"We set that up for guesthouses so every guest gets asked. I'd like to show you your Google listing next to [competitor] on a 20-minute call with Jacques, who runs the setup. Is Tuesday at 10 or Wednesday at 2 better for you?"
S · Send their page

  2. On the morning of the call, send the reminder.

It's a 20-minute video call, and I've sent the invite to [email]. Here's the one-page look at how [Guesthouse] shows up on Google: [link to their page] Have a look before we chat. We'll go through it together.

Their own page is the best thing we have for getting people to show up, because it's about them, not about us. You can send a prospect their own page. Never send them your list.

Before you send a link, open it and check two things

When they push back

Six answers

"Send me some information."

"Happy to. The most useful thing is your own Google page, and I'll WhatsApp it now. It makes more sense with someone walking you through it, though. Shall I pencil in Thursday at 10? If it's not useful, you can cancel."

"How much is it?"

"It depends on the size of your place. There's a once-off setup and then a monthly fee. Jacques will give you exact numbers on the call, and it only takes 20 minutes." Then back to the two times.

"We're too busy, it's nearly December."

"That's exactly why. December is when you have the most guests to ask. To be live for December we need to start by 13 November."

"Most of our bookings come through Booking.com."

"How many would you say? And what does that commission cost you in a year?" Let them work out the number. Don't promise to fix it.

"We already ask for reviews."

"Great. Google shows [N] new ones this year. Is that about what you'd expect?"

"Not interested."

"Fair enough. Can I ask, is it that reviews aren't a priority, or just the timing?" If it's the timing, ask when to call back and put it in the CRM.

Can't get hold of them

Three calls, then write

  1. Call three times, at different times of day (morning, after lunch, late afternoon).
  2. After the third try, send email 1 (next slide) and a short WhatsApp with the same link.
  3. Four days later, if they haven't replied, send email 2 as a reply in the same thread.
  4. No reply after that? Mark it in the CRM and move on. Don't chase a third time.
The WhatsApp"Hi [name], Giorgio from ReviewTap. I tried to call about how [guesthouse] shows up on Google Maps. Here's what I found: [their page link]. Worth a 20-minute chat?"

Keep us honest

Never say

"We'll get your price showing on Google."

We can't do that. If they ask: "That's set up through your booking system. We'll check yours as part of setup."

TO Strand's "8 reviews a week".

That's a big resort. A 5-room guesthouse gets maybe 5 to 9 a month.

"Reviews are what Google ranks on."

It's one of three things. Say "can help your ranking".

Anything from your internal list.

Scores and offer groups stay with us. Prospects only ever see their own page.
