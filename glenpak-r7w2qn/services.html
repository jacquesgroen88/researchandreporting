      Paper reel splitting (slitting)

      Reels cut to the width you need

      Glenpak offers a direct reel slitting service on a mill-graded unit. We cut widths from about 70mm to just over 3000mm, on reels up to about 1650mm in diameter and over 2.5 tonnes in weight.

      For general splitting the unit converts around 30 tons a day. Each split has an 18mm cut loss, so allow for it when you plan your widths.

      • 70 to 3000mm+Cut widths, about
      • 1650mmReel diameter, up to about
      • 2.5t+Reel weight, over
      • 30 tonsGeneral splitting a day, around

      Papers we split

      • Newsprint
      • Kraft
      • Bond
      • Other heavy-duty papers

      2-colour reel-to-reel flexo printing

      Repeat print on paper reels, with food-graded ink

      Our in-house, high-speed 2-colour flexo printer prints reel to reel, with a repeat print up to about 1000mm wide. It uses food-graded ink on greaseproof, newsprint, kraft, board and other papers.

      We supply the printed paper back as reels, repeat sheets, or counter and table rolls.

      • 2 coloursReel-to-reel flexo
      • 1000mmRepeat print width, up to about
      • Food-gradedInk
