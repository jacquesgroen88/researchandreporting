        About Glenpak

        A family packaging business in Newtown

        Glenpak was founded by Mr Glen Restall. It started in Langlaagte, on the edge of Johannesburg's CBD, grew into Newtown, and now works from permanent premises on Gwigwi Mrwebi Street.

        His sons and daughters joined the company over the years: today Glenpak is truly a family business, supplying products from nearly every field of packaging, from plastic tubing and bags to corrugated board, tapes, labels and packaging machines.

        BBBEE Level 251% black owned

        How we got here

        1. LanglaagteStarted on the edge of Johannesburg's CBD.
        2. NewtownGrew into the heart of Johannesburg's arts and culture centre.
        3. Gwigwi Mrwebi StreetLarger, permanent premises after a further eight years of growth.
        4. TodayA family business, with the next generation continuing the founder's work.

        How to order

        Three steps from price to delivery

        1. Browse prices online

          Over 1,400 products are listed with prices Excl VAT, so you know the cost before you ask.

        2. Order on WhatsApp, email or the online shop

          Send the product and quantity to our sales team on WhatsApp or to info@glenpak.co.za. Pay by card or EFT; accounts by application, 30 days from statement.

        3. Collect in Newtown or get it delivered

          Collect free from our Newtown warehouse, or delivery is R90 Excl VAT in Johannesburg under R3,000 and free in Gauteng over R3,000 Excl VAT. We courier nationwide. Please allow 3 to 5 working days for us to fill your order.
