      What it does

      The DKX4540 erector forms flat cartons and seals the bottom. The FXJ5050Z sealer folds and tapes the carton, and the DFXS-7050 seals the side edges for an H-tape finish. The DBA-200 fully automatic strapping machine straps the sealed carton on a powered roller table.

      Each unit is listed with its own power, size and weight below, so you can plan floor space and supply.

      Before you order

      What is the lead time?

      Ask us whether your machine is in stock. Some machines are imported when out of stock, so ask for the current lead time before you order.

      How much is delivery?

      Machine prices exclude delivery. Machines ship on pallets via cargo companies nationwide; we quote delivery with your machine. You can also collect from our Newtown warehouse.

      How can I pay?

      Pay by card (Payfast, Yoco) or EFT. Lay-buy on machines: no credit checks, no interest, up to 12 months to pay. All prices are Excl VAT; 15% VAT is added.

      What support is there after the sale?

      After-sales technical support and spare parts are available: ask our sales team for details on your machine.

      Have these ready for your quote

      • Your product: what you pack and its consistency.
      • Output: how many packs per hour or per day.
      • Pack size range: smallest and largest.
      • Power and air: 220V or 380V on site, and whether you have a compressor.
      • Delivery town: so we can quote pallet delivery.
