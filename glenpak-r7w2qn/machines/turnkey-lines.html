      How it works

      What a turnkey line is

      A turnkey line is a set of machines chosen to run together as one production process, from the empty bottle or bag to a packed, coded product. You buy it as one order at one price. Our team helps you check that it fits your product, space and power supply.

      1. Enquiry

        Tell us what you want to make, the pack size and your target output.

      2. Specification

        We confirm the line, what is included and what you need to supply.

      3. Supply

        Pay in full or on lay-buy. Some sections may be imported when out of stock.

      4. Delivery and setup questions

        We quote pallet delivery nationwide. Ask us about setup.

      Before you order

      Turnkey line questions

      What is the lead time on a turnkey line?

      Ask us for the current lead time on your line. On the BF2000 and MONO water lines, some sections may need importing if out of stock: an 80% deposit and a 10 to 16 week import period may apply, subject to import delays.

      Is delivery included?

      No. Machine prices exclude delivery. Machines ship on pallets via cargo companies nationwide; we quote delivery with your line.

      Can I use lay-buy on a line?

      Lay-buy on machines has no credit checks, no interest and up to 12 months to pay. Ask our sales team how it works for a full line.

      What is not included?

      On the BF2000 and MONO water lines, the filtration line and storage tanks are sold separately: you need a filtered water supply to suit. Bottles, caps, labels and shrink film are consumables, ordered separately. Ask us about setup.

      Planning a production business?

      Tell us what you want to make and your target output. We will match a line and quote delivery.
