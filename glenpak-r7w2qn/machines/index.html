      Buying a machine

      How buying a machine works

      1. Tell us what you pack and how many per hour

        Use the quote form or WhatsApp. Pack size and power supply help too.

      2. We confirm the machine, price and delivery cost

        Prices are Excl VAT. Delivery is quoted per pallet with your machine.

      3. Pay, or use lay-buy over up to 12 months

      4. We arrange pallet delivery anywhere in South Africa

      Questions

      Machine buying questions

      Do machine prices include delivery?

      No. Machines can weigh a ton or more, so they ship on pallets via cargo companies that deliver nationwide, charged per pallet. We quote delivery with your machine.

      Do prices include VAT?

      No. All prices on this site are Excl VAT. 15% VAT is added to your quote and invoice.

      Can I pay a machine off over time?

      Yes. Lay-buy on machines has no credit checks and no interest, with up to 12 months to pay. Choose the machine, add it to the cart on glenpak.co.za and select Lay-buy at checkout. You get a monthly statement by email, payable by EFT, and once it is settled our sales team arranges delivery anywhere in South Africa at a pre-determined courier fee. Non-SA citizens are welcome.

      Are machines in stock?

      Ask us whether your machine is in stock. Some machines and line sections are imported when out of stock, so ask for the current lead time before you order.

      Do you offer support after the sale?

      After-sales technical support and spare parts are available: ask our sales team for details on your machine.

      Tell us what you pack. We will match the machine.

      Send the product, pack size and output you need. Prices are Excl VAT and we quote pallet delivery with your machine.
