      About Glenpak

      A family packaging business in Newtown, Johannesburg

      Glenpak started in Langlaagte, grew into Newtown and now works from permanent premises on Gwigwi Mrwebi Street. Today we supply packaging, packaging machines and complete start-up lines from our Newtown warehouse.

      BBBEE Level 2 51% black owned Glenpak CC

      Our story

      From Langlaagte to Gwigwi Mrwebi Street

      1. Where it started

        Langlaagte

        Glenpak CC started in Langlaagte, on the edge of Johannesburg's CBD.

      2. Growing

        Newtown

        Rapid growth took the business to Newtown, in the heart of Johannesburg's arts and culture centre.

      3. Settling in

        Gwigwi Mrwebi Street

        After a further eight years of growth, Glenpak moved into larger, permanent premises on Gwigwi Mrwebi Street, Newtown.

      4. Today

        The Packaging Family

        Founded by Mr Glen Restall. His sons and daughters joined the company over the years: today Glenpak is truly a family business.

      • A proudly 51% black owned company with a BBBEE Level 2 rating.
      • Experts in small and large packaging and machinery.
      • Our Newtown warehouse in Johannesburg, where you can collect free of charge.

      What we do

      Nearly every field of packaging, under one roof

      Glenpak manufactures, supplies and deals in products from nearly every field of packaging: plastic tubing and bags, corrugated paper, tapes, labels and many types of packaging machines.

      • Packaging suppliesOver 1,400 products with prices online Excl VAT: boxes, bags, pouches, tape, egg trays, bottles and void fill.
      • Machines and turnkey linesPackaging machines from hand sealers to complete start-up lines, with lay-buy on machines up to 12 months.
      • Services: reel splitting and printingPaper reel splitting from about 70mm to just over 3000mm, reel recovery, 2-colour and 4-colour flexo printing.
