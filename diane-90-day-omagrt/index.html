How we win the new business: the growth playbook

The short version. The business costs about R93,000 a month to run. Recurring client income covers about R54,000 of that after Giorgio's share, and the two stores swing between nearly nothing and R40,000. October proved the business can clear its costs. The next 90 days are dangerous for three reasons at once: Acreage's 90-day retainer ends on 9 December (it is 43% of our recurring income), December and January are the slowest months for clients and for ReviewTap, and MyForeverBaby is limited by stock through Christmas.

So the plan has one job: keep every rand we have already earned, keep every client we already have, and add about R40,000 a month of new recurring income by the end of February. Jacques sells. Diane runs everything that keeps money coming in and clients staying, so Jacques has the hours to sell.

2. The three priorities, in order

Priority 1. Keep what we have

Losing one client hurts more than failing to win a new one. Acreage first, then every Giorgio client whose onboarding is stuck (Clarky's, TPI, Iso'Bella, Maison de Blom). A client who has not had their system working for a month does not renew.

Priority 2. Collect everything we are owed, on the day it is due

Every invoice raised on the day, every chase on schedule, every paid-but-not-invoiced item cleaned up (TPI month 3, Thompsons) so the books match the bank. No duplicate invoices: the old Invoiless schedules for Reptilia and Top Speed must be stopped before they fire again on ~25 Nov.

Priority 3. Add about R40,000 a month of new recurring income by the end of February

From the places most likely to say yes: upsells to clients who already pay us, Giorgio's December Review Sprint for guesthouses, two Seven Figure Blueprints from warm contacts, the Die Veiling licences, and the Upwork restart. Jacques owns this; Diane makes sure nothing he sends sits unanswered.

The rule for the 90 days: if work will not bring in cash, protect a client, or cut a cost before February, it waits. That includes new internal builds and side projects. Jacques's 08:00 to 10:00 block is for selling and asking, never building.
