The quickest way to see it

The NFC row matters more than it reads. A QR code asks the guest to unlock the phone, open the camera, aim it, wait, and tap a notification. NFC is one tap on a card already in their hand while you are taking payment. Same guest, same goodwill, completely different number of people who finish.

The training row matters more here than it would anywhere else. These are franchised restaurants. You can set a standard centrally but you cannot stand in 34 kitchens. Every site gets the same short onboarding video, so a new waiter in Kimberley is told exactly what a waiter in Camps Bay is told, and you are not relying on 34 owners to each explain it their own way. Cards that arrive in a box with no explanation end up in a drawer, and that is the most common way a rollout like this quietly fails.
