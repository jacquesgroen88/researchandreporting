Where they are the same

A printed card with a code on it. 3@1 are a good print business and they can produce a smart looking card with your branding on it. I am not going to pretend otherwise, and if the card itself is all you need, theirs is the cheaper way to get one.

So the question is not which card looks better. It is what happens after somebody taps it.

The quickest way to see it

Your Willowbridge franchisee has been running the 3@1 card for a while now. Ask them three questions. It is one phone call and it will settle this faster than anything I can write.

  1. How many people tapped or scanned your card last month?
  2. How many of those actually left a review?
  3. Which of your reviews this month came from the card, and which would have come anyway?

They will not be able to answer any of the three. Not because they are not paying attention. Because a printed code has no way of knowing. It opens a page and forgets it ever happened.
