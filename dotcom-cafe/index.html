Our Story

A hidden garden in the heart of Berea

Nestled between picturesque gardens and water features, with a cosy fireplace for cool East London days.

You'll find us at no.7 Scherwitz Road, with safe and secure parking from the Scherwitz Road entrance. We serve delightful breakfasts, lunches and sweet treats — with a bit of retail shopping thrown in for good measure.

Free WiFi for those working out of the office, a much-loved dog-friendly zone, and our wood-fire pizza oven. We also host private functions with in-house catering to suit your budget.
