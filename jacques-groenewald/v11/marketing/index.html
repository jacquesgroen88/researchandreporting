CASE 01

AI visibility index for South African car insurance

11 insurers, including Auto & General, Dial Direct, 1st for Women, OUTsurance and MiWay · October 2026

Data and insightInsuranceAI search
99AI answers analysed across ChatGPT, Claude, Google AI Overviews and Perplexity
21 vs 4times OUTsurance vs MiWay was the first insurer recommended, in 70 runs
0 of 30ChatGPT answers that named Dial Direct

The problem

Car insurance buyers now ask ChatGPT, Claude and Google which insurer to choose before they reach a comparison site or a call centre. No insurer could see how often it was recommended in those answers, where it ranked, or which sources the engines trusted.

What I did

  • Wrote 14 real buyer questions across eight buyer types: a young driver, an older car, a slow claim, a premium increase, a woman driving alone at night, price and head-to-head comparisons, set in Johannesburg, Cape Town, Pretoria and Durban.
  • Ran each question three times in fresh sessions on ChatGPT and Claude, once on Google AI Overviews and as a Perplexity sample: 99 valid answers.
  • Scored share of answer, first recommendation and average position for 11 insurers, and mapped the sources each engine built its answers from.

What it found

Being named is not being chosen. MiWay was named in 60% of ChatGPT and Claude answers but recommended first in only 4 of 70 runs, while OUTsurance was first in 21. The engines justify OUTsurance with third-party proof they can quote: awards, ombud complaint ratios and sponsored News24 content. Among the TIH brands, 1st for Women led the question about a woman driving alone at night, but Dial Direct appeared in none of 30 ChatGPT answers and Virseker was mentioned only in passing. Hippo.co.za was one of the few sources all four engines drew on. The prompt set is frozen, so a monthly re-run becomes the scoreboard.

CASE 05

The Seven Figure Blueprint method

SaaS, travel tech, manufacturing, proptech and services companies in the UK, US and South Africa · 25+ engagements

Growth and GTMStrategy
25+companies, including AeroCRS, WorldTicket, Ensinger UK and Club 720
3 weeksfrom first workshop to a dated 90-day plan
2engagements that became three-year fractional leadership roles

The problem

Founder-led companies usually know they want growth but not where it is leaking. Strategy decks built on assumptions get shelved because nobody can act on them on Monday morning.

What I built

  • A productised engagement in three workshops: diagnostic, ICPs, revenue leak map, offer and pricing, channel plan, money model and a 90-day plan.
  • Every leak costed and ranked by value recovered per hour of effort.
  • Where the CRM was the bottleneck, fixed it as part of the engagement: for Adept Cloud (UK), optimised their Zoho CRM alongside the strategy.
  • In 2026, a RevOps layer: maturity score, lifecycle SLAs, LTV:CAC, cost of poor quality, an A3 summary and a change plan.

What changed

Clients leave with one ranked list and a plan with dates and owners. Ensinger UK and Club 720 both started as blueprints and ran for about three years as fractional leadership roles.

CASE 06

Lead-to-quote engine for a travel franchise

Travel agency franchise, South Africa · 2026, ongoing

Revenue operationsSystems
2 minto prepare a quote, down from 1 hour
< 1 minto respond to every new lead
95%quote rate: 230 enquiries became 219 quotes in 30 days

The problem

Enquiries arrived through five web forms and WhatsApp. Each quote took about an hour to retype by hand from supplier PDFs, prices sometimes drifted in the retyping, and pipeline stages were moved by hand, so the CRM showed most quoted deals at zero.

What I built

  • Every form and the WhatsApp bot route into one CRM pipeline, with qualification fields and an urgency flag on departure date.
  • An AI quote tool that reads the supplier document, checks every price against the source, and moves the deal and its value when the quote is sent.
  • Booking confirmation and invoice tools on the same record, so each later stage updates itself.
  • When the data showed follow-up, not quoting, was the bottleneck, a follow-up engine triggered on stage entry, with daily tasks and five templates.

What changed

Quoting stopped being the constraint. A quote now takes about 2 minutes instead of an hour, every new lead gets a response in under a minute, and almost every enquiry was quoted within the month, at about R33 a lead. The diagnosis then moved the work to the right place. 54 warm quoted leads had no follow-up cadence behind them, so the next system was built around them instead of around faster quoting.

Method

How I work

The same four steps sit under every case above.

Diagnose from data

Pull the real numbers from the store, CRM and ad accounts before forming a view. Most leaks hide in data nobody has grouped.

Cost every leak

Put a money value on each problem and rank by value recovered per hour of effort.

Build the system

CRM stages, automation, SLAs and tracking, built hands-on so the fix does not wait on a vendor.

Run the cadence

A short weekly scorecard, a monthly review and a quarterly re-run, so the gains hold.
