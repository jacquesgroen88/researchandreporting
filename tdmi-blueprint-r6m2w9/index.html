What we've already noticed Before onboarding

From the outside only, on 14 October 2026. Each of these gets confirmed or ruled out once we have access.

Google Ads may be running without conversion tracking

The new site loads a Meta pixel, but we couldn't find a Google tag, Google Analytics or a Google Ads conversion tag on the pages we checked. If that's right, Google has had no enrolment data to optimise against since the move, and the ad spend is running without feedback.

The Meta pixel only records page views

The pixel on the homepage fires a page view. We'll check whether "add to cart", "start checkout" and "purchase" events are firing, because those are what Meta uses to find more people who enrol.

Old Shopify links now go nowhere

Shopify-style addresses (such as /products/... and /collections/...) return "not found" on the new site. Any Google rankings, saved links or ads still pointing at the old addresses are leaking visitors unless they redirect to the new pages.

Google is only being told about one course

The new sitemap lists 11 pages, and only one of them is a course page, although the homepage shows at least four courses. Courses Google isn't told about are slower to appear in search.

Prices show "Price TBC" on the homepage

The featured courses on the homepage show "Price TBC". A student comparing training providers often stops at that point. Worth checking whether that's intended.

Since 2009 TDMI has built a strong offer: online access, practical assignment feedback, clear assessment requirements and course support. The aim is to make sure the marketing gets that in front of the right electricians, and that the website turns them into enrolments.
